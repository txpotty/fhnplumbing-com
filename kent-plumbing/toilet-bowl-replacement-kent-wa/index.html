    Bowl Replacement Versus Full Toilet Replacement in Kent, WA

    Bowl-only swaps make sense when the tank is newer and the bowl is still made, since a matched bowl often costs $100 to $350 while a new basic toilet starts around $200.     Cracked Bolt Hole Repair in Kent

    Hairline cracks near the bolt holes often start when someone tightens the closet bolts too far, and they spread a little with every flush and every sit. Sealants do not hold for long.     Restaurant and Gym Bowl Replacement in Kent, WA

    Gyms and restaurants break more bowls than offices do, usually at the rim or the bolt holes, and a broken bowl there means a restroom out of service. Work gets scheduled around your hours. We also check the carrier on wall-hung bowls, since a loose carrier cracks the next bowl too.

    Freeze-Cracked Bowl Replacement in Kent

    Hard freezes are infrequent on the west side of Washington, but short cold snaps catch many homes with uninsulated pipes off guard. Water left sitting in a toilet in an unheated cabin or vacant house can freeze, and the ice cracks the bowl at the trap where you cannot see it. Toilets in unheated spaces should be drained. After replacing the bowl, we show you how to winterize it, which means shutting the stop, flushing and adding RV antifreeze to the bowl and tank.

    Emergency Toilet Bowl Replacement in Kent, WA

    A bowl that splits open can dump the tank and every refill onto the floor until someone closes the angle stop. Shut the valve behind the toilet first. Once the water is off, we remove the broken bowl in pieces if needed, wrap the shards and set a replacement or a full new toilet as fast as we can get one.

    Tank-to-Bowl Leak Repair and Replacement in Kent

    Drips from the bolts between the tank and bowl usually mean a flattened gasket or rusted bolts, but sometimes the porcelain around a bolt hole has cracked. We look closely before quoting. For a worn gasket, a new bolt kit fixes it, and a cracked bolt hole means a new bowl with stainless or brass hardware.

    Pressure-Assist Toilet Bowl Replacement in Kent

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the tank, and the bowl is matched to that vessel's flush rate, so a bowl-only swap means finding the exact paired model. Many are not sold separately. If the pair is unavailable, a full replacement is the clean answer, and we will price both.

    Old Toilet Upgrades in Kent, WA

    Bowls from before 1994 were built for 3.5 gallon flushes or more, and pairing one with a new low-flow tank does not work because the trapway and rim are sized for the larger volume.
