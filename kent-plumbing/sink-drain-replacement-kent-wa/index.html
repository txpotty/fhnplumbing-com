    Bathroom Drain Upgrade in Kent, WA

    Old bath drains often carry decades of hair and scum. Replacing the pop-up body and the trap removes the rough inside walls that grab hair, and a smooth new 1-1/4 inch path drains a full bowl in seconds instead of minutes. Clogs come back slower. A hair catcher keeps it that way.

    Push-Button Drain Installation in Kent, WA

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. A press on the cap opens and closes them, and a slotted body only goes in sinks with an overflow. They clean easier too. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.

    Cost of Sink Drain Replacement in Kent, WA

    Scope sets the price. Bath pop-up and trap swaps cost the least, and kitchen drains or rusted wall stubs cost more. Designer finishes add some. The quote lists each part.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Kent

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, or water leaks out the slots. Mismatched drains are common. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Choosing Drain Material in Kent, WA

    PVC is the default choice under most sinks. It does not rust, it stands up to drain cleaners, and it costs less than brass, while polished 17-gauge brass makes sense under a pedestal sink or any trap you can see. ABS works the same way. We match what you have, with a proper transition where they meet.

    Corroded Drain Pipe Replacement in Kent, WA

    Galvanized drain arms rust from the inside. An old steel stub can crumble at the threads, so we cut it back and join new PVC with a shielded coupling. Cast iron gets the same treatment. Sometimes that means opening the wall.

    A trap that falls apart sends a sink of water into the cabinet. Shut off the faucet, put a bucket under it, and avoid running water elsewhere if another drain is backing up. A plumber comes out with trap kits. Most get fixed for good on the first visit.
