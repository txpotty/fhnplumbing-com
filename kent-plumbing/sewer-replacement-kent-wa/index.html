    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Kent, WA

    Plenty of older houses still drain through their first sewer pipe. Clay joints open up and let roots in, cast iron scales and cracks from the inside, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid 1900s, slowly flattens until almost nothing passes. Replacement means PVC or HDPE.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Kent

    Cast iron sewer pipe lasts a long time, though not indefinitely. The inside rusts and scales until the bottom of the pipe thins out and cracks. We replace it with PVC, and where cast iron remains under the house we use a shielded no-hub coupling at the transition. Clean seal.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Kent

    Bursting works best on clay, cast iron and Orangeburg. A hydraulic rig at the receiving pit pulls the bursting head through the old line, splitting it into the soil, while a continuous length of fused HDPE follows behind which leaves a single joint-free pipe in the ground. Roots cannot get into a joint that does not exist.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Kent, WA

    Pipe bursting needs two pits. One sits at the house, the other at the far end of the run. A cone-shaped head is pulled through the old line, breaking it outward, while fused HDPE pipe follows right behind it, which puts new pipe in the same spot without digging the yard. Driveways stay put.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Kent, WA
