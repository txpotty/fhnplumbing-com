    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Kent, WA

    A sound pipe with a grease or root clog just needs cleaning. Broken pipe needs repair. Offset joints, cracks and collapsed sections keep clogging no matter how often they get cleared, so the camera footage decides whether you spend a few hundred dollars on cleaning or more on a spot fix, liner or replacement. You see the footage first.

    Residential Clogged Sewer Lines in Kent

    Most home sewer clogs come from three things: roots, grease and wipes. Often all three together. Grease coats the pipe, wipes snag on the coating and roots growing through a joint hold the whole mass in place until the line stops. We clear it with a sewer machine, then check on camera to see the main cause.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Kent, WA

    Flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. They stay intact for weeks. Mixed with grease that hardened in the main, they make a plug that a cable can only partly clear. Jetting scours the walls and flushes the debris downstream. Toss wipes in the trash.

    Kitchen grease cools in the sewer line and sticks to the walls. Layer after layer builds up.
