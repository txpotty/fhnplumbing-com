    Essential Plumbing Maintenance Services in Kent, WA

    Emergency Plumbing Readiness in Kent, WA

    While maintenance aims to prevent emergencies, being prepared is still smart. Our services include checking for common causes of urgent issues, like exposed pipes that can freeze or early signs of slab leaks. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and covering hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. Finding and fixing these weak spots during routine maintenance greatly reduces the chance of needing an emergency plumber or costly water damage restoration down the line.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Kent

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get swapped out. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility right away.

    Repiping and Polybutylene Replacement in Kent, WA

    Homes with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipe face a higher risk of leaks and low water pressure. During maintenance, we check pipe materials and look for signs of failure. If repiping makes sense, we explain PEX and copper options and give a detailed estimate. Repiping can be done in stages to fit your budget. New pipe brings back strong pressure and peace of mind.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Kent

    Water softeners fight hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance prolongs its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing costly scale damage.

    Preventative Leak Detection in Kent, WA

    Proactive leak detection is a key part of our plumbing maintenance. Housing in Washington ranges from early 1900s homes to new construction, with plumbing materials that change a lot from one decade to the next.
