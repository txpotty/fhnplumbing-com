    Constant Pressure Pump Systems in Kent

    Constant pressure systems use a variable frequency drive to adjust pump speed and keep pressure even. Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive systems are common choices. They eliminate the pressure swings of traditional systems. They can also cut wear on the pump. We help you decide if the extra cost is worth it.

    Submersible Well Pump Replacement in Kent

    Submersible pumps are common for deeper wells, as they operate submerged within the well casing. When these heavy-duty pumps fail due to wear, electrical issues or sediment, replacement requires specialized tools and expertise. Our technicians safely retrieve the old pump, inspect the well and install a new, high-efficiency submersible unit. We ensure proper depth and electrical connections for homes in Kent.

    Well Pump Diagnosis and Sizing in Kent

    Accurate diagnosis is key to effective well pump replacement. We do not just replace a pump, we assess your entire well system, including the well depth, yield and your household's water demand.     Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Kent

    Old galvanized drop pipe can corrode and leak, causing pressure loss. Damaged wire can cause motor failure. When we pull the pump, we inspect both. We replace them with durable materials like schedule 120 PVC or poly pipe as appropriate. This helps the new pump last for years.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Kent

    The cost of well pump replacement in Kent, WA typically ranges from $1,500 to $3,500, though it can vary. Factors influencing the price include the type of pump, well depth, horsepower and any additional components needed like pressure tanks or wiring upgrades. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after a careful assessment.

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up from shallower wells, usually less than 25 feet. Submersible pumps are placed directly inside the well casing, submerged in water, and are used for deeper wells, pushing water up to the surface. Each has specific applications and benefits.

    Well pump replacement is a complex job involving electrical wiring, heavy lifting and specialized tools, especially for submersible pumps. It is strongly recommended to hire a professional plumber for safety, proper installation and compliance with all codes, avoiding costly mistakes.

    Low pressure, sputtering faucets, no water and high electric bills can signal pump trouble. Frequent cycling is another warning sign.

    FHN Plumbing installs and services a wide range of trusted well pump brands known for their durability and performance. These commonly include Grundfos, Franklin Electric, Goulds Pumps and Sta-Rite. We can recommend the best brand and model to suit your well's requirements.

    If your pressure tank is old or waterlogged, replacing it at the same time can protect the new pump. A failed tank causes rapid short cycling, which is one of the most frequent reasons submersible motors burn out early.

    Most standard well pump replacements can be completed within a single day, typically 4 to 8 hours. However, complex installations involving very deep wells, difficult access or additional system repairs may take longer. Our team works quickly to restore your water as soon as possible.

    Most new well pumps come with a manufacturer's warranty, typically ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. FHN Plumbing also backs our installation work with our own labor warranty for complete peace of mind.
