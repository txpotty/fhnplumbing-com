    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Kent

    Hard water can cause scale buildup in tankless heat exchangers and heating elements. Scale reduces efficiency and can trigger error codes. Soft water can be slightly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. We recommend a water softener or scale inhibitor to protect your unit, and we install service valves for periodic descaling. Regular maintenance helps extend the life of your tankless heater.
