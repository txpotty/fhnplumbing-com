    Everything in the trap comes out, from the floating grease cap down to the sludge of food solids on the floor. Haulers call it brown grease. Yellow grease, the used fryer oil in your collection bin, is a separate product that gets recycled and should never go into the trap.

    It is the most common pumping standard. When grease and solids reach a quarter of the depth, it is time to pump. Beyond that level, water passes through too quickly to separate, and grease leaves through the outlet even though the trap seems fine.

    Zurn, Schier, Watts and Big Dipper units turn up across Washington every week, along with Rockford traps and older steel boxes from makers that are long gone. Parts are usually available.
