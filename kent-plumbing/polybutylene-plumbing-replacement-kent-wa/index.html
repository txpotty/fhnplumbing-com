    How We Replace Polybutylene in Kent, WA

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Kent

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. Copper is stiff, heat tolerant and long proven. It costs more. Some water can pinhole copper over time, so we look at your water and any past leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Kent, WA

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow. Every fixture, appliance and the water heater gets new pipe from the main, and most houses are finished in two to four days with water on each evening.

    Polybutylene to PEX Transitions in Kent, WA

    Sometimes the yard service line coming into the house is not polybutylene, or it was replaced already. When the service line is already sound, we tie new PEX into it at the main with a listed transition fitting and skip the trenching. We check the service line first. If it is blue or black poly, we will recommend replacing it too.

    Polybutylene Replacement Costs in Kent, WA

    Replacing polybutylene with PEX in an average home often costs $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size and bathrooms. Copper systems cost more. A yard service line typically adds $1,500 to $4,000, and that is still usually less than repairing damage from one burst.

    Polybutylene Leak Repair in Kent

    Repairs on polybutylene are temporary by nature, because the pipe next to the repair is the same age. We still make them. We cut out the failed piece and splice in PEX with listed fittings, stopping the leak and giving you time to plan the full job.

    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Kent, WA

    Some homeowners in Kent, WA want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, and join it with lead-free solder or press fittings. Expect a higher price and a slightly longer job with copper, in exchange for good heat and UV tolerance and a long history.
