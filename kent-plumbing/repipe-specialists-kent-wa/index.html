    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Kent

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. Our crews use Uponor or similar PEX-A, where the pipe end gets expanded and shrinks back over the fitting for a full-diameter connection. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Two or three days covers most houses.

    Residential Repiping Plans in Kent

    Every house is piped a little differently, so a repipe starts with a walk-through. We count fixtures, check attic and crawlspace access, look at where the main comes in and ask about plans for future remodels before we draw a route. Then we size the lines. Bigger homes may need a 1-inch trunk so the shower does not drop when the dishwasher fills.

    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Kent, WA

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. Copper commonly costs 20 to 40 percent more than PEX, and some owners prefer it anyway for its long history. Both prices include permits, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Kent

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Clogged aerators are a clue. Replacing galvanized with PEX or copper ends the rust, brings pressure back and stops the leaks that start at the threaded joints where galvanized is thinnest. Old pipe stays in walls when pulling it would wreck finished surfaces.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Kent, WA

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Low pressure, brown water and leaks at threaded joints are the typical signs. Old galvanized gets removed or abandoned and replaced with PEX or copper, and most homes see better pressure and clearer water right away.

    Commercial Repiping in Kent, WA

    Commercial buildings in Kent, WA often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day.     Type L Copper Repiping in Kent

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. We use Type L, not the thinner Type M, and we support it with the right hangers so it does not rub or rattle in the walls. All joints use lead-free solder. Copper can pinhole in aggressive water, so we ask about your water and any past leaks before recommending it over PEX.
