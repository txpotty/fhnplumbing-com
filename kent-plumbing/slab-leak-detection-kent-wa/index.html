    Slab Leak Reroutes in Kent

    A reroute abandons the leaking line under the floor. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, so there is no concrete work and the cost is often lower than going through tile.     Slab Leaks in Commercial Buildings in Kent

    Commercial slabs are thicker and carry larger pipe, so leaks can move more water and be harder to hear from the surface with a standard ground microphone. Our approach changes for those.     Thermal Scans on Large Floors in Kent, WA

    Large open floors can hide a leak. A thermal camera scans an entire living area in a few minutes, showing temperature differences of a degree or less across tile or concrete, so we can rule out half the house before setting up the microphone. That saves real time in bigger houses across Kent, WA.

    Camera Inspection for Under-Slab Drains in Kent, WA

    Drain lines under the slab can leak too.     Tracer Gas Leak Detection in Kent

For those, we drain the line and push in a safe hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas, which rises through the slab and gets picked up by a sniffer at the floor surface, often through grout joints and hairline cracks. It finds leaks acoustic gear misses in Kent homes.
