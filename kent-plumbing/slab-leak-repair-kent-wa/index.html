    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Kent

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. The new pipe runs through the attic. For older Kent homes, this works well with aging copper or galvanized pipe, since it bypasses every foot of buried line on that run. We keep drywall cuts small at fixture walls.

    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Kent, WA

    Copper and PEX fail in different ways under a slab. Copper pinholes. Old copper wears through where it touches gravel or reacts with the water, while PEX usually leaks at a fitting that was crimped short or chewed by rodents near an access point. Trucks in Kent, WA stock fittings for each type.
