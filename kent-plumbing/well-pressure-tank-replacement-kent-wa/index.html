    Residential Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Kent

    For homeowners experiencing issues like a constantly running pump, short cycling, or inconsistent water pressure, a residential well pressure tank replacement is often the solution. Our team at FHN Plumbing quickly diagnoses the problem, whether it is a ruptured diaphragm, waterlogging, or corrosion, and replaces your old tank with a new, properly sized model. We ensure the new tank is installed correctly, with appropriate pre-charge pressure and system calibration, to restore reliable water service to your home.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Kent, WA

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop, typically at 30/50 or 40/60 psi. Pitted contacts, insect nests and a clogged sensing nipple are common causes of failure in well houses. We replace worn switches with Square D or similar models and clean or replace the nipple below it. A low-pressure cutoff switch can also be added to stop the pump if the well runs dry. We set the switch to suit your new tank's pre-charge.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Kent, WA

    While our primary service is well pressure tank replacement, FHN Plumbing also emphasizes the importance of preventative maintenance for your well system in Kent, WA. Regular checks of your pressure tank's air charge and overall condition can help identify potential issues before they lead to a complete failure. During a maintenance visit, we can top off the air charge, inspect for early signs of corrosion or leaks, and advise on steps to maximize the lifespan of your current tank, potentially delaying the need for replacement.

    Large Capacity Tanks for Ranch and Commercial Wells in Kent

    Properties with livestock, irrigation or more than one home on a well need more storage than a single residential tank. We install multiple tanks manifolded together or large capacity models over 100 gallons. Proper sizing keeps big three-phase pumps from cycling too often. We also check the check valves and pressure ratings on the distribution lines. Work is scheduled to keep water downtime brief for animals and occupants.

    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Kent

    Bladder tanks hold water inside a flexible butyl bag surrounded by pressurized air. When the bag ruptures, water fills the whole tank, there is no air cushion and the pump short cycles. A quick test is pressing the air valve on top, since water spitting out means the bladder has failed. We replace the tank, set the new pre-charge and confirm the pump runs a full cycle. Most bladder tank swaps take about two to three hours.

    Bladder-Style Pressure Tank Replacement in Kent, WA

    Most modern well pressure tanks are bladder-style, separating air and water for better efficiency. When the bladder fails, you'll experience symptoms like short-cycling of the well pump. FHN Plumbing carefully replaces these bladder-style tanks across Kent, WA, ensuring proper pre-charge pressure and system integration for long-lasting performance.

    A well pressure tank usually lasts between 7 to 15 years, depending on water quality, usage, and maintenance. Tanks in areas with harder water or higher demand may have a shorter lifespan. Regular inspection can help identify potential issues before complete failure.

    Both bladder and diaphragm tanks use an air cushion to pressurize water, but they differ in how the water and air are separated.
