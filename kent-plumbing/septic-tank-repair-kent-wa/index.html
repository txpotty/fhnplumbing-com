    Septic Tee Repair in Kent

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer drifts into the laterals and plugs the gravel. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Kent

    Old concrete lids crack, and a cracked lid over a 1,000-gallon tank is a real danger to kids and pets. We replace failing lids and add risers that bring access up to the lawn. Future pumping gets cheaper. Each lid we install gets screws or a lock so a child cannot lift it.

    Commercial Septic Repair in Kent, WA

    Churches and small offices on septic run more water through the tank than any house, and a commercial kitchen adds grease on top of that. We size repairs to the actual daily flow, not a residential guess. Grease interceptors and lift stations are part of it. Downtime costs you, so we schedule around open hours.

    Repair Versus Replace in Kent, WA

    Most septic systems that fail can be repaired for a fraction of the cost of a new one. We price the repair and the replacement side by side, and we tell you which one we would pick for our own house. A new field is sometimes the right call. Often it is not.

    Septic Repair for Businesses in Kent, WA

    Daycares and small shops on septic put more flow through the tank, more often, than a typical three-bedroom house with four people in it.     Septic Line Clog Clearing in Kent

    A slow toilet and a gurgle in the tub usually point to a clog between the house and the tank. We run a camera down the line first, then clear it with a jetter set up for a 4-inch building sewer.
