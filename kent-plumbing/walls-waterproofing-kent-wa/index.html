    Wall Waterproofing Work We Handle in Kent, WA

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Kent

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Constant moisture west of the mountains promotes mold, rot and corrosion around leaks, so small drips should not be ignored. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also means less musty air upstairs.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Kent, WA

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. The difference is that the water no longer ends up on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and makes sense when a patio or driveway sits against the foundation.

    Commercial Walls Waterproofing in Kent

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and mechanical rooms in Kent take on water the same way houses do, just with more at stake. A wet electrical room can shut a business down. We schedule around operating hours, work with property managers on access, and follow the waterproofing specs written into the building plans for below-grade walls. Downtime stays short.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Kent

    Restaurant kitchens, gym locker rooms and salon wash stations put water on walls all day.     Waterproofing for Older Homes in Kent, WA

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Mid-century homes often have galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains, while some 1980s and 1990s homes were plumbed with gray polybutylene. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. We repoint failed mortar joints before sealing.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Kent, WA

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the waterline and check it a day later. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a two-piece clamping drain set on the liner. We flood test the new pan before any tile goes down.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Kent

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that picks up the cooler wet area, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. We replace the leaking fitting or pipe section. Wet insulation comes out, and fans dry the cavity before we close it up.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Kent

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, a membrane over cement board or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. We reinforce corners, niches and valve penetrations with fabric or preformed pieces.
