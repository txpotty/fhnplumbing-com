    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Kent, WA

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install their M53 and M98 models often. Backup and combo units are available too. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Kent, WA

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Kent

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Landlords like them. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Kent, WA

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. Their 250 and 280 series cover most residential lifts, and the vertical float version fits narrow pits where a wide tethered float would rub. Parts are easy to find. A small weep hole in the discharge pipe keeps the pump from air locking.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Kent, WA

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. People with carpet downstairs buy these. They are worth it anywhere a few inches of water would mean new drywall.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Kent, WA

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. A swap in an existing pit is the low end. Battery backups and alarms add to the total. Your written quote shows each option and its price.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Kent

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. That saves some labor. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Kent, WA

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. We set a basin at the lowest point, run perforated drain pipe to it where needed and seal the vapor barrier around the lid so the space stays dry. Clearance is usually under two feet. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Kent

    A pump swap in an existing pit usually runs $600 to $1,500. A new pit with slab cutting and digging often runs $1,500 to $4,000, depending on how deep we go and what we hit. Add $500 to $1,500 for a backup.

    Doing it yourself is possible but risky. Pit depth, discharge routing and wiring all have to be right. We do it daily.

    A straight replacement often needs no permit. We let you know if your job needs one, usually only for new pits or wiring.

    Most standard installs take three to six hours. If we have to cut the slab, dig a new pit and patch the concrete, plan on most of a day, with the patch curing overnight. Swaps go faster.

    A submersible sits in the pit under water, so it runs quieter and takes no floor space.     Swapping a pump in an existing pit takes about two to three hours. A new pit can take a full day.
