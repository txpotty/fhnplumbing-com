    Main Water Line Replacement in Kent

    The main water line is the primary pipe delivering water from the municipal supply to your home. When this critical line fails due to age, shifting soil, or severe leaks, a full replacement is often the most sensible long-term solution. We expertly excavate, remove the old line, and install new, durable piping, ensuring proper connection to your home's plumbing system and the city's main.

    Damage Assessment and Replacement in Kent

    Diagnosing the exact cause and extent of water line damage is essential before replacement. Our technicians use modern leak detection equipment and camera inspections to pinpoint issues like tree root intrusion, pipe corrosion, or ground shifting. For properties in Kent, especially those with older foundations or significant tree growth, accurate assessment ensures we recommend the most fitting and effective replacement strategy.

    Water Line Upgrades and Code Compliance in Kent, WA

    Beyond simple replacement, we also offer water line upgrades to improve water flow or ensure compliance with current plumbing codes. Older homes in Kent might have undersized or outdated galvanized steel pipes that restrict flow and cause rust-colored water.     Copper Service Line Installation in Kent, WA

    Type K copper is a tough option for underground water lines. It resists punctures and lasts for decades. Copper costs more than PEX but is favored by some homeowners. We install copper with proper bedding to cushion it. We can also install sleeves where it passes through foundations.

    Commercial Water Service Replacement in Kent

    Commercial properties often have larger service lines and backflow assemblies. We replace commercial water lines and coordinate with the utility when needed.     Traditional Water Line Excavation in Kent

    Sometimes, traditional open-trench excavation is the most appropriate method for water line replacement in Kent, especially for severe damage, complex pipe layouts, or when the existing pipe material is incompatible with trenchless techniques.     PEX Water Line Installation in Kent, WA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a modern, flexible, and very popular choice for water line replacement. It resists corrosion, scaling, and chlorine, making it very durable for most water conditions. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and protecting hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. PEX can expand and contract without bursting, which is a significant advantage when a cold snap hits Kent. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings and faster installation.
