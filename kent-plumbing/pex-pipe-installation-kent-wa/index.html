    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Kent

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Either one lasts for decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Kent, WA

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Kent, WA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. Every one is listed for potable water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Kent
