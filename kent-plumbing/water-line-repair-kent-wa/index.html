    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Kent, WA

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Two or more leaks on galvanized is a replacement.     Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Kent, WA

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground.     Most replacements get Type K copper or PEX. For pulled lines we use HDPE. We recommend one based on your soil, line length, budget and what your local code allows in Kent.

    A full service line replacement usually needs a plumbing permit. Small repairs often do not. The rules come from your city or county, and they can differ even between neighboring towns in the same county. We pull the permit and schedule the inspection when it is required.

    We dig a pit at each end of the old line. First, a cable is threaded through.
