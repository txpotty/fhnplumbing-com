    Shower Pan Replacement in Kent, WA

    Cracked pans damage subfloors, walls and nearby rooms. With slab floors, water can move under flooring into the next room, so we trace it first. Then the pan comes out. We install the new pan with slope and waterproofing, then flood-test it.

    Shower Replacement Options and Costs in Kent, WA

    A valve-only swap costs far less than a full rebuild. It only makes sense when the pan and walls are sound, though, because a new valve in a shower with a failing pan just moves the leak to a different spot. Pans usually mean tile removal. The condition decides the fix.

    Shower Unit Replacement in Kent

    Cracked fiberglass units leak at the base first. The crack usually starts where the base was never fully supported, flexing a little every shower until the gel coat splits and water gets under the unit into the subfloor. We pull the old unit. Then we check the subfloor and set the new base on a full mortar bed.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Upgrades in Kent

    Sometimes the shower only needs its guts replaced. If the pan and walls are sound, we can swap an old valve, diverter and trim without tearing out tile, which fixes drips and temperature swings for a fraction of a full rebuild. It is a big upgrade. Half a day, usually.

    Custom Tile Shower Replacement in Kent, WA

    Custom tile replacement starts with careful demolition. We take the old tile and pan down to the studs and subfloor, replace any damaged wood and install a new waterproofed pan and walls, then set the valve and drain to the finished tile depth. The slope has to be right. We flood-test before tile.

    Showerhead and Trim Upgrade in Kent, WA

    If the valve is sound, new trim and a new head can refresh the shower without opening the wall. A trim kit matched to your valve, a new head and an optional slide bar update the look without demo. Easy-clean nozzles help with scale. It costs the least.

    Commercial Shower Replacement in Kent

    Gyms and hotels need showers that survive heavy use. Commercial replacements get heavy-duty valves, tamper-resistant trim and properly sized drains to code. We phase the job. That way some showers stay open while others are replaced.
