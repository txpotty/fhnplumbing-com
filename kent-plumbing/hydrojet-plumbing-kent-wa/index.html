    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. The debris flushes away as normal waste, leaving nothing behind to harm pipe or septic bacteria.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Kent, WA

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. The jet is the bigger bill. Fewer repeat visits often make jetting cheaper over a few years.

    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in Kent

    Properties with chronic drain issues do better on a jetting schedule. Commercial kitchens often jet quarterly. Homes with root-prone sewers usually jet every year or two, and we keep a camera record from each visit so you can see whether the roots are coming back slower, faster or the same.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Kent

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. A cable cuts a channel through it. Jetting with a spinning nozzle strips the grease from the full circumference so it flushes downstream instead of re-forming a plug at the next fitting.
