    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Kent

    The cost of water heater replacement in Kent, WA typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of unit (gas, electric, tankless or hybrid), its capacity and any needed plumbing modifications. Our team will provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your needs and existing setup.

    Most new water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components, depending on the model and brand. FHN Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, so your investment is covered.

    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take longer.

    Generally, if your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down or has a major issue like a leaking tank, replacement is often more cost-effective in the long run. For newer units with minor, easily fixable issues, repair might be a viable option. Our technicians can provide an honest assessment.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but usually have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater annually is recommended in most homes. Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from damaging heating elements.

    Tank water heaters often last about 8 to 12 years, and hard water and sediment buildup can shorten that. Soft water can be slightly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. Annual flushing and anode rod checks can help extend that life.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line changes.
