    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Kent, WA

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. The valve's age decides it. An old valve on new resin is a short-lived fix.

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in Kent

    Restaurants and laundromats run softeners hard, and a commercial unit often needs a 1.5-inch or 2-inch valve and twin tanks to keep up with flow. We size it to peak gallons per minute. Installs happen before opening or after close.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Kent

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. They cost more.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Kent, WA

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. We check those first and test hardness before and after the unit, so you know whether it is softening at all. Obsolete valves are another story.

    Emergency Softener Replacement in Kent, WA

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. Put the unit in bypass first. Most stuck or leaking systems get replaced in a day or two, sooner if the right size is in stock.

    Softener Loop Installation in Kent, WA

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.

    Whole-Home Softener Placement in Kent, WA

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. We typically leave the hose bibs on hard water. Why soften water for the lawn?

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Kent

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. We replace timer units with metered valves that regenerate on gallons used. You will buy less salt. We set the brine refill to your hardness so the system is not dumping salt down the drain every few days.
