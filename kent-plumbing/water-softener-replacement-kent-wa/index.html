    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Kent, WA

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more economical than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes more sense.

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and proven solution for hard water in Kent.     Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Kent

    A standard single tank softener replacement usually costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can raise the cost.

    Salt-free conditioners reduce scale buildup but do not remove hardness minerals. A true softener removes them, which is more effective for soap and fixtures.

    New water softeners typically come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years on different components, such as the control valve and resin tank. FHN Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work for your peace of mind. Exact warranty details will be provided with your quote.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had significant mineral buildup restricting flow. However, a water softener's main job is to remove hardness minerals, not to boost pressure. If low water pressure is your main concern, we can investigate other causes.

    FHN Plumbing installs a wide range of trusted water softener brands, including Fleck, Autotrol, Culligan, Pelican and Watts. We select brands known for their reliability, efficiency and long-term performance, so you get a quality system that meets your needs.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is generally not required for a direct like-for-like water softener replacement if no significant changes to the existing plumbing are made. However, if new water lines or drain lines are being installed or relocated, a permit may be required. We will advise you on any permitting requirements.

    Most softener replacements take two to four hours. New loops or drain lines can take a bit longer.

    While it might seem like a straightforward task, water softener replacement involves working with water lines, drain connections and sometimes electrical components. Improper installation can lead to leaks, inefficient operation or even water damage. Hiring a professional ensures the job is done correctly, safely and to code.
