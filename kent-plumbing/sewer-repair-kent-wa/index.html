    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Kent

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and we always jet a line before lining it. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Kent

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Minor sags clog quickly under that load.     Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Kent, WA

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Per foot, lining runs higher. It avoids excavation, though, and on long runs under driveways or patios the savings on concrete and landscaping often make it the cheaper total job. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. We price all three so you can compare.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Kent, WA

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line with a jetter first. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. On a four-inch residential line, that rarely matters.

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. So does an extra-green strip of lawn.     Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Kent

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. The crack lets soil in and sewage out.
