    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Maintenance in Kent

    The cost of water softener maintenance in Kent, WA generally ranges from $100 to $250 for a standard service. This can vary based on the system's age, brand and any specific repairs needed. For a precise quote, please contact FHN Plumbing to assess your unit.

    Once a year is a good schedule for most homes. High hardness and heavy water use can justify service every six to nine months.

    A standard water softener maintenance service typically takes about 1 to 2 hours. This can vary depending on the system's condition, age and whether any unforeseen issues are discovered during the inspection.

    Generally, routine maintenance and minor repairs on an existing water softener do not require a plumbing permit in Kent. However, if major modifications or a full replacement of the system are needed, a permit might be required. We always ensure compliance with local codes.

    Common signs include a return of hard water symptoms such as mineral spots on dishes, dry skin and hair, or scale buildup on fixtures. You might also notice your softener using more salt than usual, odd noises or the system not regenerating properly. Don't wait for a complete failure.
