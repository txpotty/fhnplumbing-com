    What Our Gas Line Plumbers Fix in Kent, WA

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Kent

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged. Every repair gets a soap test and a system pressure test before we leave.

    Emergency Gas Line Repair in Kent

    If you smell gas, leave the building, skip the light switches and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Then call us. After the gas company secures the meter, our plumber finds the leak, repairs it and runs the test the utility needs to see before turning gas back on. We relight pilots when service returns. Most emergency repairs are done in a single visit with parts from the truck.

    Gas Shut Off by the Utility in Kent

    When your utility shuts off gas for a leak, you may be without hot water, heat or cooking until a licensed plumber fixes it. We respond quickly, locate the leak, make the repair and run the pressure test the utility needs to see before it will restore service. Most are one-visit repairs. We stay until the system holds its test pressure and the meter is ready to be unlocked.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Kent, WA

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. We install the branch line, add an accessible shutoff valve and a sediment trap where the code calls for one, then connect the appliance with a new coated flexible connector. If the existing appliance line is leaking or damaged, we fix it on the same visit.

    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Kent, WA

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Uncoated brass connectors from decades ago crack at the flare fittings and should be replaced on sight. New coated connectors go in. We also add a reachable shutoff and a drip leg where needed, and confirm the new connector is rated for that appliance's BTU input.

    Honest Gas Line Repair Pricing in Kent, WA

    Gas leaks are usually a surprise expense, so the price should not be another surprise. You get a written estimate covering the work, the parts and any permit fees before anything begins. No hidden fees. If spot repair and replacement are both options, we price each so you can weigh cost now against the age of what is left in the ground.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Kent

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. We replace them with new coated stainless connectors sized for the appliance and add an accessible shutoff valve if the old one is missing or seized. Usually under an hour per appliance. Ranges, dryers, water heaters and furnaces all need proper connections, and most also need a sediment trap ahead of the appliance gas valve.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Kent, WA

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. Many tankless heaters are rated at 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, which can be more than double what an old tank heater used, so the line feeding it often has to be upsized back toward the meter. We calculate the full load first. The new pipe gets sized so every burner in the house still has full pressure at once.
