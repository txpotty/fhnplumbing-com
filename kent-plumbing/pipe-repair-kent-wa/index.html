    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Kent

    Threaded steel pipe rusts from the inside until flow drops and threaded joints start to leak. It can be patched. The trouble is that every fitting you disturb on old galvanized can start a new leak, so we replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric fittings and talk through a full replacement plan when the rest is close behind.     Pinhole Leak Repair in Kent

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water chemistry causes most. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with new copper or a PEX transition. One usually means more are coming.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair in Kent, WA

    Slab leaks start when pipe under the concrete wears through or gets stressed by soil movement. Steep slopes and wet soils can shift over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. A hot spot underfoot is common. We isolate the leaking line with pressure tests and find it with acoustic and thermal equipment, then choose between a small slab cut at the leak and rerouting the line through the walls or attic. We quote each way.

    After-Hours Pipe Repair in Kent

    Pipes like to burst at night and on holidays. We still answer. An after-hours plumber in Kent can stop the water, find the failed section and fix it the same night using parts already on the truck. Larger repairs get scheduled for morning.

    PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Kent

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. It still fails. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, and we cut out the bad fitting and replace it with a crimp, clamp or expansion connection rated for the pipe. Most take under an hour.
