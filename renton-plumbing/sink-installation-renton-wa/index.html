    Frequently Asked Questions About Sink Installation in Renton

    Drop-in and pedestal sinks usually run $250 to $550 for labor. Undermount and vessel sinks typically cost $350 to $800 because of clips and cure time, and adding a second sink or moving a drain can reach $900 to $1,800. The fixture is not included.

    A same-spot swap usually does not need one. New drain or supply work, like a second bowl or a relocation, usually needs a permit and inspection. We pull the permit if it is needed.

    Cracks are the big one. A crack through porcelain or fireclay, glaze chipped down to the base, a stainless bowl dented around the drain, or a drain body rusted into the basin all mean a repair will not last. Stains alone are not a reason.

    Every common style. We install every mount type for kitchens and baths, plus utility and commercial sinks. Material does not limit us.

    A vanity top swap is a fair weekend project. The hard parts are getting the trap arm lined up without strain and sealing the drain flange so it does not weep over time. We can fix it if it leaks.

    All the major ones. Kohler, Elkay, Blanco and Kraus sinks are routine, with Moen, Delta or Pfister faucets. Lesser-known brands work as well.

    Laminate pairs best with drop-in sinks. Stone and solid surface take undermounts since the edge polishes, while butcher block usually gets a drop-in or apron sink. Ask before you order.

    Two layers of coverage. Our written labor warranty covers leaks and loose mounting from our work, while the sink and faucet makers cover defects in the fixtures, and some faucet brands cover finish and cartridges for life. Registering the faucet helps with claims.
