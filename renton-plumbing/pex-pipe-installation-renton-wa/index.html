    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Renton, WA

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is known for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Many insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Renton, WA

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Renton

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, usually using crimp or compression fittings. Our plumbers in Renton can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    PEX Main Water Line Installation in Renton

    The main water line brings water into your home, and a failing one can cause serious issues. We provide expert PEX main water line installation, replacing old, damaged lines with durable PEX. This upgrade improves water flow, reduces the risk of future leaks and offers superior resistance to ground shifting and corrosion. Our team ensures proper trenching and the connection to your municipal supply.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Renton

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer dependable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to verify a proper seal.
