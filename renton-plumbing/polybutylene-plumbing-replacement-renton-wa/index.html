    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Renton

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Check the heater first. It also shows up under sinks, at the main and through garage walls, and we follow it into the attic to see how much of the house it feeds.

    Full Home Polybutylene Repiping in Renton

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Partial jobs leave risk behind. New pipe goes from the main to every fixture and appliance, each one gets a new quarter-turn stop and the system is tested before any wall is closed.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Renton, WA

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Faster install means lower labor cost. Attic PEX stays out of UV, clear of flues and can lights and supported as the maker specifies, which protects the pipe and the warranty.

    Copper Replacement Option in Renton

    Type L copper appeals to some homeowners for its long history and rigid feel. Joints are soldered or pressed. Since copper is pricier and slower to install, we walk through cost, water conditions and your plans for the house before you decide.
