    Jetting a Sewer Line in Renton, WA

    Grease and scale are jetting work. Cables leave them behind. The rear jets drive the hose forward while the spray scours the wall, so the line ends up much closer to its original size.

    Root Masses in Renton Sewer Laterals

    Mature trees and old clay laterals do not mix. Roots find the joints. Our root cutting heads attach to the sectional cable and saw through the mass in passes, and a camera run afterward shows how much growth is left and where the roots are coming in.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Renton, WA

    Grease hardens as it cools. It shrinks the opening a little every week. A greasy main slowly loses diameter until it cannot carry a full washer load or a bathtub draining at once, and jetting is the right fix.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Renton

    No cleanout means pulling a toilet. That adds time. We can install a two-way cleanout near the foundation so the line can be cleaned in either direction, which makes every future cleaning and camera inspection faster and cheaper, and the work is done to local code with any required permit.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Renton, WA

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. We would rather look than guess. A waterproof color camera on a push rod shows the inside of the line live on a screen, so we can see the clog, measure its distance from the cleanout and spot any crack or sag.

    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Renton, WA

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. Every drain shares that one pipe. After the line is open, a camera run tells us if the blockage was debris or something structural that will keep catching waste.

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Renton

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Renton, WA
