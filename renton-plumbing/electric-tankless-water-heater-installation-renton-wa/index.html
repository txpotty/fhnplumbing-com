    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Renton

    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes. Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. A softener or scale filter helps, and the service valves we install make yearly descaling simple. It is a short service.
