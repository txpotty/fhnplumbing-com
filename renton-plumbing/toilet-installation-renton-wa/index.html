    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Renton

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. They need at least 20 psi to work right.     ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Renton

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. That spacing leaves room for a grab bar.     Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. They recover instantly between flushes.
