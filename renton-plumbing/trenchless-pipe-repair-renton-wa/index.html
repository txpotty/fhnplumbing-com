    Frequently Asked Questions About Trenchless Pipe Repair in Renton

    Most residential trenchless jobs run $80 to $250 per foot. Footage matters most. Pipe diameter, the type of damage and whether we line or burst all move the price, so a camera inspection comes first and the written estimate from we follow from what the footage shows.

    A properly installed CIPP liner can last 50 years or more. That beats most old pipe. The cured liner resists corrosion, roots and chemical damage, so it frequently outlasts the pipe it was installed in, especially when that pipe was clay or cast iron to begin with.

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. Bursting replaces it. A CIPP liner cures inside the host pipe, and bursting fractures the old pipe while dragging new HDPE in behind it.

    CIPP lining jobs usually take one to three days. Bursting can run longer. Pipe bursting may take slightly more, depending on length and access, but either one is much faster than open trenching, which can stretch to weeks once excavation and restoration are counted.
