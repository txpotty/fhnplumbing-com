    Pipe Bursting Services in Renton, WA

    For severely damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, breaking apart the old pipe at the same time. This provides a completely new, durable pipe, often HDPE (high-density polyethylene), which resists corrosion and leaks. It's an effective long-term fix for widespread issues in Renton, WA.

    Sectional Point Repair in Renton

    When damage is confined to a specific section of pipe, sectional point repair is an ideal trenchless solution. We target only the damaged area, installing a patch or liner to seal the leak or reinforce the pipe. This method is cost-effective and limits the scope of work, which suits isolated issues in Renton without affecting the entire line.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Renton

    CIPP lining is a popular trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is well suited for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Renton.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Renton, WA

    Main water lines can also be replaced with trenchless methods. We can pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through the old path using bursting or a directional boring tool. This avoids digging across lawns, driveways and walkways. The new line is pressure tested before service is turned back on.
