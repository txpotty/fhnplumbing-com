    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Repair in Renton

    Restaurants and laundromats in Renton often run two to six tankless units on a common manifold.     Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Renton

    Scale is calcium and magnesium carbonate that drops out of hard water when it heats, and a tankless exchanger heats water fast through narrow passages. Many rural Washington homes rely on private wells, and some carry iron, manganese or other minerals that call for testing and treatment. Our tech connects a pump to the isolation valves and runs descaler through the exchanger for close to an hour. Then we flush with clean water. The inlet screen gets cleaned too.

    Electric Tankless Element Repair in Renton, WA

    Electric units fail in their own way. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart models use two to four heating elements in separate chambers, so a single burned element leaves the heater behind at high flow, while a failed flow switch means no heat at all. We check each element's resistance, measure voltage under load and check amperage on each leg, then confirm the panel and wire size match the unit's rating.
