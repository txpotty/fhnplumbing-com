    Whole-House Water Softener Installation in Marysville

    Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is typically soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. Softener sizing comes down to hardness times daily gallons, which tells us how many grains the resin has to remove between regenerations. A unit sized too small regenerates constantly and wastes salt. We set the drain line with an air gap. Programming follows, set to your hardness, and we run one test cycle.

    Expansion Tank and Sewage Ejector Installs in Marysville, WA

    A closed water system has nowhere for heated water to expand, so pressure climbs every single time the heater cycles. The expansion tank takes up that swing so the relief valve stops weeping. A PRV usually means you need one. Fixtures below the sewer line get an ejector system with a sealed basin, a check valve and a vent.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Marysville, WA

    Navien and Rinnai tankless units hang on a wall and free up the floor space a tank used to take. Gas models usually need a bigger gas line and new stainless or PVC venting. Electric models need amperage. We size by gallons per minute and the temperature rise from your incoming water. Washington has two very different climates, with long, wet and mild seasons in the west and hot, dry summers and cold winters in the east. Isolation valves go on every unit for descaling.

    Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Marysville

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. They are different pumps for different water, and one cannot stand in for the other. Sizing comes first. We match pump capacity to how fast the pit fills, put the pump on its own circuit and run the discharge with a check valve.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Marysville, WA

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. The fix is a sealed basin with an ejector pump that lifts waste to the main. Pump and basin are sized to the fixtures, the basin is vented to code, and the discharge gets a check valve and a shutoff so the pump can come out for service. We usually install Liberty or Zoeller pumps.

    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Marysville, WA

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. Our licensed plumbers size each run from the appliance BTU ratings and the developed length, then choose black iron or CSST to suit the route. CSST must be bonded. Without that bond, a lightning strike nearby can arc through the tubing and start a fire.

    Backflow Preventer Installation in Marysville

    Irrigation systems and fire sprinkler lines are the usual reasons a home needs a backflow preventer. The device stops lawn water and fertilizer from siphoning back into the drinking supply when a main break or hydrant flush drops street pressure. Type matters. We pick the assembly for the hazard level and local requirement and test it on install day.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Marysville

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. Repiping in PEX stops the cycle. We run either a manifold with home runs to each fixture, which balances pressure, or a trunk-and-branch layout when the walls make home runs impractical. We cut small, clean access holes and patch them, or hand off to your drywall contractor if you prefer.
