    Running Toilet Fixes in Marysville

    Phantom refills, the tank topping itself off for a few seconds in the middle of the night, almost always mean a slow leak at the flapper. The level falls a little until the fill valve opens back up. Sometimes the cause is a refill tube pushed too far down the overflow, which siphons the tank. We clip it above the water line.

    Clogged Toilet Solutions in Marysville, WA

    Paper towels, wipes, dental floss and toys cause most of the repeat clogs we clear, and none of them break down the way toilet paper does. The closet auger usually hooks them and pulls them out. If the toilet clogs again within a week, the problem is further down the line. We check the branch drain and the main next.

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Marysville, WA

    Most clogs sit in the trapway, the curved channel cast into the porcelain, and a closet auger with a rubber-sleeved head clears them without scratching the bowl. Plungers miss some. If the auger comes back clean and the toilet still backs up, the blockage is further down the drain or in the main line. We run a cable from the cleanout or put a camera in.

    Sensor and Flushometer Repair in Marysville

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. Before replacing parts, we test the sensor and the solenoid. Batteries are the cheap fix. If the valve flushes on its own, the sensor range or a dirty lens is usually to blame.

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Marysville, WA

    The rubber parts in a toilet, the flapper, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the bolt washers and the wax ring at the floor, all break down with age and chlorine. They get stiff. Hardened rubber stops sealing and lets water seep out slowly where you will not see it until the floor stains. We replace the whole set of tank seals at once when one is gone.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Marysville, WA

    Braided supply lines last years, but the plastic nut on the toilet end can crack and a bulge in the braid means the inner hose is about to burst. Every visit includes a look at the supply line. Bulges get replaced on the spot. If you still have a vinyl or solid plastic line, we change it to braided stainless before it lets go.

    Flush Valve Seat Repair in Marysville

    Scale on the flush valve seat is the reason a brand-new flapper sometimes keeps leaking, because the rubber cannot seal against a rough, crusted ring. Feel the seat with a finger. A scrub pad and some vinegar clean most seats, and if it is pitted we install a seat repair kit or a new flush valve. Then we set the float so the water stops about an inch below the overflow.
