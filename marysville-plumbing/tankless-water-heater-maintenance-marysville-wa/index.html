    Noritz Descaling in Marysville, WA

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Marysville

    Grit gets in. The small screen on the cold inlet catches sand and loose scale after a water main repair. On units with an air intake filter, dust and lint load it up and the burner runs rich or throws a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Maintenance for Every Tankless Brand in Marysville, WA

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Marysville, WA

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. Gas pressure at the inlet gets read with a manometer at full fire and checked against the rating plate.     Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Marysville, WA

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. We check gas pressure with all units firing at once. Each heater gets its own flow and temperature reading. Then we compare them.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Marysville, WA

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. Rinnai LC codes point to scale, Navien E003 is a failed ignition and Noritz 90 points at combustion air or exhaust trouble. First we read the code history. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Service Valve Kit Installs in Marysville, WA

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. We add a pair of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines. Most kits include a relief valve port as well. After that, every flush takes minutes to set up.
