    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Marysville

    Most well pressure tank replacements cost $900 to $2,200 installed, depending on tank size and whether the switch and tank tee are replaced. Large capacity or multi-tank setups can run $2,500 or more. We give a written price before starting.

    A like-for-like tank replacement usually does not require a permit in most counties. Electrical changes or new piping may, and we check local rules for you.

    Both bladder and diaphragm tanks use an air cushion to pressurize water, but they differ in how the water and air are separated. Bladder tanks have a replaceable rubber bladder that holds the water, completely isolating it from the air. Diaphragm tanks use a fixed rubber diaphragm. Bladder tanks are generally preferred for their longevity and ease of maintenance.

    Regular maintenance for a well pressure tank involves checking the air pressure in the tank at least once a year, ensuring it's properly pre-charged. You should also listen for frequent pump cycling and visually inspect the tank for any signs of corrosion or leaks. We can include this check during routine well system servicing.

    We install a variety of trusted well pressure tank brands, including Well-Rite, Flex-Lite, Amtrol, and Goulds. Our technicians can recommend the best brand and model for your specific well system and budget.

    Most new well pressure tanks come with a manufacturer's warranty, usually ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. FHN Plumbing also stands behind our labor with our own service guarantee, ensuring peace of mind.

    If your switch is more than a few years old or the contacts are pitted, replacing it with the tank is inexpensive insurance. It also lets us match the switch settings to the new tank.

    Most pressure tanks last 10 to 15 years. Hard water, sand and frequent cycling can cut that, especially when the pre-charge is never checked.
