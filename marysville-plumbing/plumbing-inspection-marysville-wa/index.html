    Gas Connection Safety Check in Marysville, WA

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance.     Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Marysville, WA

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. Too much pressure wears parts. Anything over 80 psi cuts the life of hoses, fixtures and heaters, and we note the supply pipe type, from copper and CPVC to poly, Kitec and galvanized.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Marysville, WA

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. We look at backflow assemblies, grease interceptors, commercial water heaters, flush valves and the building sewer. Inspectors like documentation. The written report helps facility managers plan and budget repairs before a health inspector or the water provider writes them up.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Marysville

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Rental and Commercial Property Inspections in Marysville

    Landlords and property managers use our inspections between tenants or before buying an investment property. We document each unit.     Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Marysville

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue. Early slab leak finds save floors and foundations.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Inspection in Marysville

    A standard plumbing inspection in Marysville, WA usually costs $150 to $350. Size changes the price. The base price covers pipe, fixtures, the heater, drains and pressure, and the sewer camera is usually a separate line since it adds time and equipment. We quote it on the phone.

    High water bills, repeat clogs, slow drains and discolored water are common signs. Banging pipes count too.     We can include estimated repair ranges in the report and firm quotes if you want us to do the work. You are never required to hire us.

    For an older home, it usually pays for itself. Age brings hidden issues. Homes with galvanized supply pipe, clay or cast iron sewers or original water heaters often have problems that are invisible from the outside, and an inspection lets you plan repairs instead of reacting to a failure.

    Watching your water meter, looking under sinks and checking for leaks are all things you can do yourself. A professional inspection adds camera video, pressure testing and expert analysis.

    Our full inspection covers supply pipe, drains, fixtures, shutoff valves, the water heater, gas connectors and the sewer line. Pressure gets measured.     Plan on one to two hours for most residential inspections. Big homes and long sewers add time.
