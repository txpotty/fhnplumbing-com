    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Marysville

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a especially challenging issue. Newer homes in Marysville usually use PEX supply lines, and many older houses have been partially repiped over the years. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to major structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to pinpoint slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak quickly in Marysville is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Marysville, WA

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is generally soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and ends repeat leaks.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Marysville

    Copper pipes are known for their durability and long life, but they can still develop leaks due to corrosion, excessive water pressure, or physical damage. In Marysville, we often see pinhole leaks in older copper lines.     Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Marysville

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins.     Water Line Repair at the Meter in Marysville, WA
