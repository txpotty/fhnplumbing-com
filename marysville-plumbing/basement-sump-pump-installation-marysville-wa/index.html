    Sump Pump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Marysville

    A complete sump pump system needs a properly installed basin and discharge line. We handle the excavation and placement of the sump basin, setting it at the lowest point of your basement floor so it collects water well. We also install the discharge line, ensuring it is correctly sloped, sealed and routed away from your foundation to a suitable drainage area, so water does not find its way back into your Marysville property.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Marysville

    Pedestal sump pumps are an good choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level. This design makes them easier to maintain and can often offer a longer lifespan because the motor stays out of the water. FHN Plumbing can expertly install pedestal sump pumps in your Marysville home, with the right sizing and setup for effective water removal without crowding your pit.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Marysville

    Office buildings, parking structures and multi-unit properties with below-grade levels often need duplex sump systems with two pumps and an alternating control panel. The panel switches lead and lag pumps on each cycle so wear stays even, and a high water alarm notifies building staff. We coordinate electrical with your electrician and follow manufacturer specs for basin size and float settings. Property managers get a simple maintenance schedule to keep both pumps exercised.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Marysville, WA

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Marysville

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by finding the low point where water collects.
