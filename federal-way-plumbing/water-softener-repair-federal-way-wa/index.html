    Control Valve Repair in Federal Way

    Water that runs to the drain all day usually means the control valve is stuck between cycles. FHN Plumbing rebuilds stuck valves with the correct seal and spacer kit for your model. Timing problems get fixed, whether mechanical or electronic. Common parts are on the truck, so most valve repairs are finished the same day without a second trip to the supply house.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Federal Way, WA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    Hard Water Symptoms & Diagnostics in Federal Way

    Scale on faucets means trouble. FHN Plumbing checks hardness at the tap, inspects every component and gives you a repair plan for your Federal Way home. A hardness test strip tells the story in seconds, and we run one before the repair and again after so you can see the drop in grains per gallon.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Federal Way, WA

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. A minor drip still wastes water and damages floors and cabinets. O-rings and fittings get replaced and pressure tested. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Federal Way

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. The usual causes are a dead timer motor, a bad board, a stuck meter or settings wiped by an outage. Each part gets tested. After the repair, the valve is programmed for your household size and hardness so you have soft water by the next cycle.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Federal Way, WA

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. If scale comes back or pressure drops, the media or the prefilter is usually the problem. FHN Plumbing repairs these units in Federal Way, WA. The media wears out in a few years.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Federal Way, WA

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. Installing a new quality softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,500. Sound tank and valve, repair wins. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense than chasing used parts online.
