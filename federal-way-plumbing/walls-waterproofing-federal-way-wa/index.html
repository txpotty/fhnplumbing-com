    Foundation, Shower and Block Wall Sealing in Federal Way, WA

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Federal Way

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing to carry the water away from the wall. Steep slopes and wet soils can shift over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Federal Way

    Mid-century homes often have galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains, while some 1980s and 1990s homes were plumbed with gray polybutylene. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with real hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and install drain tile where water pools along the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Some basements never need a sump.

    Drainage System Integration in Federal Way, WA

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Exterior footing drains, interior drain tile and surface French drains all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a gutter dumping at the corner. Downspouts get extended at least four feet out.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Federal Way

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and often add a dehumidifier to keep relative humidity below 60 percent. East side homes near Federal Way and beyond face bigger temperature swings, which put extra stress on pipes, fittings and outdoor plumbing. The joists stop rotting. Drier air under the floor also means less musty air upstairs.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Federal Way, WA

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and tie it into a sump pit so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. The difference is that the water no longer ends up on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Federal Way, WA

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer piped to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. The wall behind them can take a moisture-resistant board and sealer.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Federal Way, WA

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head aimed at the house instead of the bed, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. After that the wall gets cleaned, dried and coated with a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.
