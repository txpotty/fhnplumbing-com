    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Federal Way, WA

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This typically involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Federal Way

    Restaurants, gyms, salons and offices often have restrooms, locker rooms and kitchens with constant water exposure. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with durable membranes. Work can be scheduled after hours to reduce downtime. We also repair the plumbing behind those walls when leaks are discovered.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Federal Way

    Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier. This not only waterproofs the walls but also controls humidity, preventing mold growth and wood rot in the crawl space. Constant moisture west of the mountains encourages mold, rot and corrosion around leaks, so small drips should not be ignored. An encapsulated crawl space can noticeably improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency by keeping moisture from moving up into the home. We use durable, reinforced poly sheeting for walls and floors, sealed at all seams and penetrations.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Federal Way

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most reliable method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Crawlspaces and basements are both common in Washington homes, and a damp crawlspace is often the first sign of a drainage or plumbing problem. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also added to divert water away.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Federal Way, WA

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are common culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry for good.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Federal Way

    Newer homes in Federal Way typically use PEX supply lines, and many older houses have been partially repiped over the years. Wherever a home has a basement or below-grade walls, heavy rain and snowmelt can push water through foundation walls and cracks. We seal cracks with injection products, apply interior or exterior waterproofing and install drainage where needed. A sump pump may be added if groundwater collects.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Federal Way

The damaged section of pipe or valve is replaced, and wet drywall is cut out and dried. Once the cavity is dry, we restore the wall and seal any wet-area surfaces.

    Older homes, especially those built before modern waterproofing standards, are particularly prone to water intrusion.
