    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Replacement in Federal Way

    The cost of plumbing replacement in Federal Way, WA varies widely based on the type of service. A water heater replacement can range from $1,200 to $3,500, while a sewer line replacement might be $3,000 to $15,000 or more. Whole-home repiping can cost between $4,000 and $15,000. We provide detailed, transparent estimates after an on-site assessment.

    We install water heaters from Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith, tankless units from Rinnai and Navien, and quality softeners and well pumps from trusted manufacturers.

    We primarily use PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) and copper piping for water supply lines. For sewer and drain lines, we use durable PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene) pipes, based on the application and local codes.

    While some minor plumbing repairs can be DIY, major replacements like water heaters, sewer lines or repiping should always be handled by licensed professionals. These jobs involve complex systems, potential safety hazards (gas, high water pressure) and strict code compliance. Improper installation can lead to leaks, damage and even severe safety risks, costing more in the long run.

    For water heater replacements, we mainly recommend and install reliable brands such as Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien. These manufacturers are known for their durability, energy efficiency and excellent warranty coverage, providing long-term value for your Federal Way home.

    Replacing old pipes with PEX or copper offers many benefits. PEX is flexible, corrosion-resistant and less prone to bursting in freezes, while copper is durable and long-lasting. Both improve water quality, eliminate leaks from old pipes and can significantly boost water pressure and flow throughout your Federal Way home.

    Many sewer line replacements take one to three days depending on length, depth and method. Trenchless options can shorten the timeline.
