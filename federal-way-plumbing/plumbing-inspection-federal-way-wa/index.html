    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Federal Way

    Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is typically soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. You get options, not a pitch. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.
