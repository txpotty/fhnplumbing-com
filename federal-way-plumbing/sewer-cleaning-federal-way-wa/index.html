    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Federal Way, WA

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust. Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. Flow slows a little each year. A cable can punch a hole through the crust, but a jetter cleans the walls back to full size and leaves less for new grease to grab onto, and the method depends on pipe material and condition.

    Commercial Sewer Cleaning in Federal Way, WA

    Commercial lines take a beating. Kitchens put grease into the line daily. Offices and retail buildings deal more with paper towels and foreign objects flushed in public restrooms, which snag on joints and catch everything after them, and our heavy jetters handle 6 inch commercial pipe without much trouble.

    Root Masses in Federal Way Sewer Laterals

    Mature trees and old clay laterals do not mix. Clay joints let roots in. We cut roots in several passes, then look on camera to judge the damage and talk about lining or a spot repair.

    Scheduled Sewer Cleaning in Federal Way, WA

    History predicts the next backup. Repeat lines get a schedule. Homes with past root problems, older cast iron or a lot of kitchen grease do well on a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the growth or buildup closes the line.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Federal Way

    No cleanout means pulling a toilet. It makes every call slower. We can install a two-way cleanout near the foundation so the line can be cleaned in either direction, which makes every future cleaning and camera inspection faster and cheaper, and the work is done to local code with any required permit.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Federal Way

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Both have weak spots. Roots love clay joints, while Orangeburg fiber pipe tends to squash into an oval and eventually collapse, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Residential Sewer Cleaning in Federal Way

    Household sewers clog slowly. It takes months of buildup to stop a main. Each flush adds a little more to the pile at a rough joint or a low spot, until the 4 inch line is down to a trickle and the lowest drain in the house starts to back up, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.

    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Federal Way, WA

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. Every drain shares that one pipe. We clear it with a sectional cable or a jetter, then camera the line to see whether the cause was roots, debris or a structural problem like a broken joint or an offset section.
