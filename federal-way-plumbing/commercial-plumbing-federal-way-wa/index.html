    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Federal Way

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Glacial till and dense hardpan are common in western Washington, and they drain poorly enough to keep water against foundations and in crawlspaces. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. We price both so you can compare.
