    Dependable Storm Drain Cleaning Services in Federal Way, WA

    Yard and Area Drain Cleaning in Federal Way

    Yard drains and area inlets collect runoff from lawns, rock landscaping and patios. Over time the grates fill with leaves and the pipes fill with silt and small gravel. We pull the grates, vacuum the inlet box and jet the pipe toward its outlet. This restores the line so it can carry a full storm. We also check that grates are secure and level.

    Catch Basin Cleaning Services in Federal Way

    Catch basins are key parts of many storm drainage systems in Federal Way, designed to trap debris before it enters the main pipes. Over time, these basins can fill with leaves, trash, and sediment, reducing their effectiveness. Our service includes fully cleaning out catch basins, removing built-up material so they work correctly and prevent blockages further down the storm drain line, especially important in areas with mature trees.

    Root Removal from Storm Drains in Federal Way

    Tree roots are a common cause of severe storm drain blockages in Federal Way, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees. Roots seek out moisture and can work into drain pipes through tiny cracks or loose joints, growing and eventually completely blocking water flow. FHN Plumbing uses dedicated root-cutting attachments on our drain snakes and hydro jetting equipment to cut through and remove root intrusions. After removal, we can suggest further steps to prevent future root problems.

    Dry Well Cleaning and Inspection in Federal Way, WA

    Dry wells let storm water soak into the ground instead of flowing to a street. They are found on commercial sites, HOA common areas and some residential lots. Silt slowly seals the settling chamber and the drainage rock, slowing how fast water drains. We vacuum the chamber, inspect the inlet and outlet, and check drain-down time. If the dry well no longer drains properly, we explain options for repair or replacement.

    Preventative Storm Drain Cleaning in Federal Way, WA

    Proactive cleaning is key to avoiding major issues with storm drains in Federal Way, WA.     Hydro Jetting for Storm Drains in Federal Way, WA

    Hydro jetting is a proven solution for deeply clogged storm drains in Federal Way, WA. This method uses high-pressure water streams to wash out stubborn obstructions like compacted soil, heavy silt, roots, and grease buildup.
