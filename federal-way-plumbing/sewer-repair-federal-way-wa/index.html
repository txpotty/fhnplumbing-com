    Sewer Pipe Relining in Federal Way, WA

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. On a four-inch residential line, that rarely matters.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Federal Way, WA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Steep slopes and wet soils can shift over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Federal Way

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Small defects turn into big backups.
