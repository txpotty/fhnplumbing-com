    Descaling Tankless Units in Federal Way

    Error codes often mean scale. Scale in the exchanger cuts flow and trips the unit's safety shutoff. We descale through the service valves. Yearly descaling is standard advice for hard water.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Federal Way

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Scale on the tank bottom traps heat and causes water to pop and rumble as it heats. We flush until it runs clear. Heavy buildup may take several cycles.

    Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Federal Way

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is typically soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. We run a hose from the drain valve, open it up and let the tank drain until the water runs clear, then refill and check the anode rod, the relief valve and every connection for drips or corrosion. Takes about an hour. Less sediment means more years.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Federal Way

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Depending on your water chemistry, a rod that should last five years might be spent in three, so we pull it on every visit instead of assuming it is fine because the tank is still young. We swap worn ones. It keeps rust off the tank lining.

    Commercial Tank and Tankless Service in Federal Way, WA

    Commercial heaters work harder than home units. High demand speeds up scale and wear, so commercial units need service on a tighter schedule. Our techs know these systems. Hot water stays on for your business.

    Tank Flush and Inspection in Federal Way

    Sediment forms from minerals in the water. We flush the tank, test the relief valve, check the anode and look over each fitting. Efficiency comes back. Recovery speeds up.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Federal Way, WA

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We lift the lever, confirm water discharges and the valve reseats without dripping, and check that the discharge pipe runs full size to an approved spot within a few inches of the floor or outside. We swap bad ones. We check the expansion tank too.
