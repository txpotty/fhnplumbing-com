    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Federal Way, WA

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Washington homeowners.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Federal Way, WA

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is especially useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add decades of service.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Federal Way

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot get into. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their shape.
