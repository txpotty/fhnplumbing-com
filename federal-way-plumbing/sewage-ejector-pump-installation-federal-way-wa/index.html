    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Federal Way

    The cost for sewage ejector pump installation in Federal Way usually ranges from $2,500 to $5,000.     Signs that you might need a new sewage ejector pump include unusual noises from the pump or pit, frequent cycling on and off, slow drainage from basement fixtures or a sewage odor coming from the area.     Avoid flushing wipes, paper towels, feminine products, grease and cat litter. These items clog pumps and cause early failure.

    Regular maintenance for a sewage ejector pump involves periodically checking the basin for debris, confirming the check valve works correctly and testing the float switch.
