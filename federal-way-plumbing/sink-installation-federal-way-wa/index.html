    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Federal Way

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Old basket strainers fuse to the bowl, the disposal has to come off its mounting ring, and the dishwasher drain hose needs to be rerouted to the new disposal or tailpiece with a high loop. After that, the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.

    Commercial Sink Installation in Federal Way, WA

    Commercial kitchens need sinks the health inspector will sign off on. A three-bay sink drains indirectly through an air gap to a floor sink, and hand sinks need tempered water near each prep area. We read the plan review first. Mop basins and grease interceptor ties are part of it.

    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in Federal Way, WA

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking. ADA layouts call for a rim at or under 34 inches, open knee space, and insulated pipes below. Offset drains help with knee space. Lever or sensor faucets finish it off.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Federal Way, WA

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim covers small flaws in the cutout, so a laminate counter can take a new self-rimming sink with only a check of the hole size and a bead of silicone or putty as the maker specifies. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Federal Way, WA

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. The faucet holes have to match the faucet you picked, whether that is a single hole, a 4 inch centerset or an 8 inch widespread, and the overflow has to line up with the pop-up drain. The spec sheet gets read first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Commercial Restroom Sink Installation in Federal Way

    Public restroom lavatories take a beating. Wall-hung or countertop lavatories go on carriers with sensor faucets and mixing valves where tempered water is required. We can fit tamper-proof parts. Spec sheets go to your general contractor.

    Sink Installation Cost Factors in Federal Way, WA

    Labor is the main cost driver. Moving the drain or adding a second bowl adds hours, and a straight drop-in swap is the cheapest. We quote labor apart from the sink. You can buy it or we can.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Federal Way, WA

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. The supply stops and the trap sit right behind the column, so we center the drain stub, use chrome or polished brass trap parts, and set the stops tight to the wall where the pedestal can hide them. Blocking in the wall is a must. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.
