    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Federal Way, WA

    Tankless installation costs more upfront than a standard tank because of the unit price, gas line upgrades and venting changes. Over time, lower standby energy losses and a longer expected lifespan make up for part of that cost. Tankless units often last 15 to 20 years with proper descaling, compared with about 8 to 12 years for many tanks in hard water areas. We give you a side-by-side quote so you can decide what suits your budget.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Federal Way

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to deliver hot water to distant fixtures. A recirculation pump or dedicated return line keeps hot water close to faucets, reducing wait time and water waste. Some units have built-in pumps with smart scheduling. We design the system to suit your plumbing layout and usage patterns.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Federal Way

    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units. A 40 gallon tank heater uses roughly 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need up to 199,000 BTU, so the gas line usually needs to be upsized. Venting changes from a metal flue to PVC or stainless, and the unit moves to a wall location. We remove and dispose of your old tank and patch the old flue penetration.
