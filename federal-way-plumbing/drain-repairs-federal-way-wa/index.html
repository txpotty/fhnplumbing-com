    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Federal Way, WA

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. The bottom goes first. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, and we replace the bad length with plastic pipe joined to the old iron with shielded couplings. If the whole stack is failing, we price it separately.

    Broken Drain Pipe Repair in Federal Way, WA

    Broken drain pipe usually comes from settling, roots or age. Sometimes it is a bad glue joint. We find the break with a camera and a locator, then cut out the damaged length and install new PVC or cast iron with transition couplings sized to both pipes. The repair gets a flow test.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Federal Way, WA

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. Most go unnoticed for months. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then cut out the damaged section and replace it with matching PVC, ABS or cast iron. Every repair is done to code.

    Outdoor Drain Repair in Federal Way

    Downspout lines and yard drains fail without much warning. Water pools at the house. We clear the line, camera it for crushed or disconnected sections, and replace the damaged pipe so roof water reaches the outlet instead of soaking the soil next to your foundation. The discharge point gets checked as well.

    Preventive Drain Maintenance in Federal Way

    Scheduled cleaning keeps buildup from turning into a repair. Once a year is common. For older homes in Federal Way with cast iron or a history of kitchen clogs, we jet the main branches and camera the line so we can catch a cracked fitting or a growing sag early.     Restaurant and Office Drain Repair in Federal Way

    Vent Pipe Repair in Federal Way
