    Slab Leak Repiping in Federal Way

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Glacial till and dense hardpan are common in western Washington, and they drain poorly enough to keep water against foundations and in crawlspaces. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed.     Copper Repiping in Federal Way, WA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity.     Galvanized Pipe Removal in Federal Way

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. That is often the first sign. New PEX or copper stops the rust, restores pressure and ends leaks at the threads, which is where galvanized fails first. Old pipe stays in walls when pulling it would wreck finished surfaces.

    Type L Copper Repiping in Federal Way

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. We use Type L, not the thinner Type M, and we support it with the right hangers so it does not rub or rattle in the walls. All joints use lead-free solder. Copper can pinhole in aggressive water, so we ask about your water and any past leaks before recommending it over PEX.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Federal Way

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Many homes built from the 1980s through the early 2000s have one or the other. The pipe at the water heater usually tells us which one you have, and we replace the full system so the home is easier to insure and to sell.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Federal Way
