    Efficient PEX Repiping in Federal Way, WA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly popular choice for repiping projects in Federal Way, WA. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also very resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a dependable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Federal Way

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Federal Way

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us what you have.
