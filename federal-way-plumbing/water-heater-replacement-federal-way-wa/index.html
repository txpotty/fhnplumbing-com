    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Federal Way, WA

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Federal Way, WA homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Every joint gets checked.
