    Swapping Out Old Disposals the Right Way in Federal Way, WA

    InSinkErator Replacement in Federal Way

    Most InSinkErator units share the same Quick Lock mount, so a like-for-like swap can go fast when the old mount is in good shape. If the old ring is corroded or the flange is leaking, we replace both, because a new disposal hung on a worn mount will start dripping within months. The old cord rarely transfers. We bring the right cord kit for the new model.

    Mount and Flange Replacement in Federal Way

    During every replacement we inspect the sink flange and mount. If they are corroded, scratched at the seal or leaking, we replace them with new parts, which prevents leaks at the top of the disposal and lets the new unit seal tight to the sink. Old putty gets scraped off.

    Upgrading During Replacement in Federal Way, WA

    Replacement is the easiest time to step up in size, from an old 1/3 HP to a 1/2 or 3/4 HP unit. Larger units are taller, so we measure the cabinet and check that the discharge outlet will still sit above the trap, since a disposal outlet below the wall stub leaves water standing in the chamber. Insulated models run much quieter. Most people notice it the first night.

    Disposal Housing Leak Replacement in Federal Way

    A body leak usually means the seal inside the unit is gone. This cannot be repaired. We remove the leaking unit, inspect the cabinet for water damage, install a new disposal on new mounting hardware, and then test it with water and check every connection for drips.

    Disposal Replacement Cost and Value in Federal Way, WA

    The model and any wiring or drain changes set the price. A basic swap is the least expensive, while upgrades to quieter, stronger models cost more but usually last longer, grind better and carry longer warranties. The choice is yours.

    Leaking Disposal Replacement in Federal Way

    Water from the bottom of the housing means the internal seal has failed, and that seal is not a replaceable part. We pull the unit, dry the cabinet, check the floor for soft spots and install a new disposal with a new flange and gaskets so every seal on the assembly is fresh. Swollen cabinet bases need repair. You will hear about it from us.

    Like-for-Like Disposal Swap in Federal Way

    If the replacement uses the same mount as the old unit, the swap is quick and the trap usually lines up without changes. We still replace the flange gasket and putty if they have hardened, and we check the discharge tube for cracks, since old plastic tubes split right at the gasket. Figure on an hour. We haul the old one away.

We check the circuit, the cabinet space and the trap height before ordering, because a bigger unit draws more at startup and sits lower in the cabinet. Usually it fits without trouble.
