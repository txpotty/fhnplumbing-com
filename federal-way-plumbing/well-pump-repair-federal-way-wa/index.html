    Deep Well Pump Repair in Federal Way

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up, run quietly and last, but motors, impellers and splices still wear out. After pulling the pump, we test each part and replace only the failed one. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Federal Way, WA well.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Federal Way

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, a clogged sensing tube and ant nests are the usual reasons one fails. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. This is among the least expensive well repairs.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Federal Way, WA

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. The air charge gets checked and the bladder inspected, then the tank is recharged or replaced so the pump runs a full minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.

    Well Pump Maintenance and Repair Cost in Federal Way, WA

    Maintenance costs far less than an emergency pull. A typical well pump repair in Federal Way runs $400 to $1,500, and a yearly check costs a small fraction of that. FHN Plumbing prices both up front. We check tank precharge, switch contacts, amp draw and the well cap, which catches most problems early.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Federal Way

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. When the bladder fails, the tank fills with water and the pump starts and stops every time someone opens a faucet. We inspect the bladder, the switch and the tank connections. Proper sizing adds years to a pump.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Federal Way, WA

    Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. A tripped breaker is often the first sign. Licensed technicians test these parts safely with the power locked out, replace what failed and tighten every connection in the box. Wiring meets Washington code when the job is done.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Federal Way

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. The usual signs are no water at all, weak pressure or a hum with no flow. We test the motor from the surface first. If it has to come up, a pump hoist lifts the pipe safely, and we replace the burned motor, worn impellers or a bad check valve before setting it back.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Federal Way, WA

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. Common Franklin parts are on the truck. Replacing a failed motor usually costs less than a rewind once shipping is counted.
