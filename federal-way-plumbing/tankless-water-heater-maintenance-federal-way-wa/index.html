    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Federal Way

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate.     Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Federal Way, WA

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. Rinnai LC codes point to scale, Navien E003 is a failed ignition and Noritz 90 points at combustion air or exhaust trouble. First we read the code history. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    What Tankless Maintenance Saves in Federal Way

    Scale on an exchanger acts like insulation. A unit with a thick layer burns noticeably more gas for the same shower than a clean one, and the extra cost adds up month after month. You get the maintenance price beside the likely repair cost, and the decision is yours.

    Tankless Vent Inspection in Federal Way

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. On a condensing unit the PVC or polypropylene vent should pitch back toward the heater. We check every joint and test for carbon monoxide near the unit. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Federal Way

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. We wash the air intake filter and check the flame rod for carbon. On recirculating models we also check the pump schedule, because a wrong setting burns gas for nothing.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Federal Way, WA

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. We read gas pressure at the inlet with a manometer while the burner is at full fire and compare it to the rating plate. The flue gets a look for obstructions. A written report with every reading goes in your file for next year's comparison.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Federal Way

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. The pump pulls solution from a bucket, through the cold port, out the hot port and back again. After the cycle we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Commercial Tankless Maintenance in Federal Way, WA

We pull and clean the inlet screens and air filters. Downtime is the real cost. So we schedule the work before opening or after close.
