    Water Heater Maintenance in Burien

    Regular water heater maintenance is crucial to preventing sediment buildup. Many rural Washington homes rely on private wells, and some carry iron, manganese or other minerals that call for testing and treatment. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, boosts efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Burien

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers call for annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.
