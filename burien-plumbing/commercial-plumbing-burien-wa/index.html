    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Burien

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. We carry Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits for that reason.
