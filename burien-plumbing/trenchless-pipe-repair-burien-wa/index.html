    Pipe Bursting Services in Burien, WA

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Burien, WA soil.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Burien
