    Common Toilet Repairs in Burien, WA

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Burien

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the china that compresses air with incoming water pressure, and the cartridge inside that vessel eventually wears out. You will not find most of these parts at a hardware store. We carry Flushmate cartridges and dual-flush seals for the common TOTO and American Standard models, and we check house pressure, since these units need steady line pressure to flush properly. Low line pressure gives you a weak, half-hearted flush.

    Running Toilet Repair in Burien

    A tank that keeps hissing and refilling every few minutes is usually leaking past the flapper or overfilling into the overflow tube. It adds up fast. We look at the chain, float, flapper and seat before touching a part, then replace only what failed. Most running toilets are quiet again in about thirty minutes.

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Burien, WA

    Most clogs sit in the trapway, the curved channel cast into the porcelain, and a closet auger with a rubber-sleeved head clears them without scratching the bowl. Plungers miss some. If the auger comes back clean and the toilet still backs up, the blockage is further down the drain or in the main line. At that point we run a cable from the nearest cleanout or send a camera down to see it.

    Leaky Toilet Repair in Burien

    Food coloring in the tank is the cheapest test we run. If color shows up in the bowl within fifteen minutes without a flush, water is leaking past the flapper or the flush valve seat, and the toilet is quietly refilling itself all day and night. A new flapper fixes most of these.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Repair in Burien

    Restaurant restrooms get the hardest use we see, and wall-hung bowls there can loosen on their carriers until the seal leaks behind the wall. We tighten or rebuild the carrier and reseal the bowl. That one needs care. Leave a loose wall-hung bowl alone long enough and the china can crack at the mounting holes.

    After-Hours Toilet Repair in Burien, WA

    Shutting off the angle stop buys you time, but an old multi-turn stop valve can crumble or leak at the stem when it has not been moved in fifteen years. Do not crank on it hard. The house main shutoff is your backup if the angle stop will not budge, and we can replace the stop with a quarter-turn valve once the water is off.

    Toilet Flush Repair in Burien, WA

    Two or three tries to get a full flush points to a flapper closing too early, a chain hooked in the wrong hole, scale in the rim jets, or a cracked overflow tube letting water bypass the bowl. Each one has a tell. Our plumber watches a complete flush with the tank lid off and replace the flush valve if the overflow tube is cracked, since it cannot be patched.

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Burien, WA

    The rubber parts in a toilet, the flapper, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the bolt washers and the wax ring at the floor, all break down with age and chlorine. They get stiff. Hardened rubber stops sealing and lets water seep out slowly where you will not see it until the floor stains. We replace the whole set of tank seals at once when one is gone.
