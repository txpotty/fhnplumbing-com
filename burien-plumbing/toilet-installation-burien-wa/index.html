    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Burien

    Labor to install a customer-supplied standard toilet typically runs $200 to $400. Including a mid-range toilet, most installs total $450 to $900, while wall-hung systems with carriers range from $1,500 to $3,500 or more. We give you a flat price up front.

    A one-piece toilet has the bowl and tank integrated into a single unit, offering a sleek look and easier cleaning. A two-piece toilet has a separate bowl and tank, which are bolted together during installation. Both types are easy to find and perform well.

    FHN Plumbing installs all major toilet brands, including Kohler, Toto, American Standard, Delta, and Gerber. We can help you choose a model that fits your budget, style preferences, and water efficiency goals.

    Replacing a toilet in the same location generally does not need a permit, but new bathrooms, additions and drain relocations typically do. Requirements vary by city, and we check with the local building department for your project.

    FHN Plumbing stands behind our workmanship. We offer a warranty on all our toilet installations, covering labor and making sure your new toilet works as expected. Specific warranty details will be explained at the time of service.

    While it's possible for a homeowner to attempt a DIY toilet installation, it's usually best left to professionals.     Toilets carry manufacturer warranties, often 1 to 5 years on parts and longer on porcelain. We also guarantee our installation workmanship and will come back to fix any leak caused by the install.

    The rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, typically 12 inches but sometimes 10 or 14. Buying a toilet with the wrong rough-in means it will not fit or will sit too far from the wall.
