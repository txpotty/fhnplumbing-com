    Jet Pump System Repair in Burien, WA

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. FHN Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Burien operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is essential for jet pump function.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Burien, WA

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps prematurely. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives more even pressure at the tap.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Burien, WA

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. River flooding and soaked ground can push water back through sewer lines, so backwater valves and sump pumps are worth considering. Lightning and power surges often damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help prevent repeat failures.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Burien, WA

    Control and pressure switch repairs usually cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500. Pulling and replacing a submersible pump can range from $1,800 to $5,000 or more, depending on depth, horsepower and wire. If your pump is older and already needs pulling, replacing it at the same time can save a second pull later. We lay out both options with clear numbers.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Burien

    Submersible pumps are widely used in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms usually include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use dedicated equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, ensuring the integrity of your well system.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Burien

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.
