    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Burien

    The cost for tankless water heater maintenance in Burien, WA typically ranges from $150 to $300, depending on the unit's age, condition and the extent of descaling required.     Neglecting maintenance can lead to several problems, including reduced heating efficiency, higher utility bills, decreased hot water output and eventually, costly breakdowns. Mineral buildup can seriously restrict water flow and damage the heat exchanger, potentially leading to premature unit failure and expensive repairs or replacement.

    While some basic tasks like cleaning external filters might be possible for a homeowner, complete maintenance, especially descaling the heat exchanger and inspecting gas lines, should be left to a licensed professional. Improper servicing can damage the unit, void warranties and create safety risks related to gas and carbon monoxide.

    Tankless water heater maintenance focuses mainly on descaling the heat exchanger and checking electronic components for efficiency. Hot water tank maintenance usually involves flushing sediment from the bottom of the tank, checking the anode rod for corrosion and inspecting the pressure relief valve. Both aim to prolong life but address different system needs.

    The descaling process involves shutting off the unit, isolating the water lines and connecting a circulating pump to flush a specialized acidic solution through the heat exchanger. This solution dissolves mineral deposits like calcium and magnesium, which are common in hard water, improving heat transfer efficiency.
