    Preventive Leak Inspections in Burien, WA

    A yearly leak check catches small problems early. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves. Older homes with galvanized pipe or original water heater connectors benefit most from a yearly check, because those parts often fail together. It is a short visit.

    Slab Leak Detection in Burien

    Concrete slab homes have supply lines buried in sand below the floor, and a pinhole there can run for months before anyone notices. A warm tile is the classic sign. We pressure test the hot and cold sides separately, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak through the concrete and confirm with a thermal camera when the leak is on the hot side.

    Pool Leak Detection in Burien

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation probably has a leak. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, use an underwater hydrophone to listen, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain to find the exact crack or fitting that is losing water.

    Commercial Building Leaks in Burien, WA

    Restaurants, offices and apartment complexes in Burien pay for every hour a leak keeps running. Sprinkler lines, cooling tower makeup water and long copper mains are common sources, and a leak in any of them can run under a floor or above a drop ceiling for weeks. Each zone gets isolated and tested, then mark leaks for repair. Most tenants never see us.

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Burien, WA

    Copper leaks are loud on a ground microphone. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. We handle both every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX top the list of what we find.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Burien, WA

    The buried line from your meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. A green stripe in a dry yard is a hint. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, switching to tracer gas on plastic lines that do not carry sound.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Burien

    Our trucks carry acoustic correlators and ground microphones, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Every tool covers a different case. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, and tracer gas finds leaks that make no sound at all on plastic pipe.
