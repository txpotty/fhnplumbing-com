    What Goes Into a Repipe in Burien, WA

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Burien

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, which can cause insurance headaches. Replace it.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Burien, WA

    We often leave old galvanized pipe in the wall. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, which keeps drywall damage low. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Burien

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. Scale and rust do not affect it. Many rural Washington homes rely on private wells, and some carry iron, manganese or other minerals that call for testing and treatment. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, choosing a layout that fits the house. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Burien, WA

    Drain lines fail too. Cast iron cracks and scales. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, checking slope and venting so the new drains flow freely. Camera first.

    Full Repipes for Older Homes in Burien

    Homes from the 1950s and 1960s often still have original galvanized pipe. It is near the end. A full repipe brings new PEX or copper to every fixture, improves pressure, clears up discolored water, and removes the leak risk from aging threads. Worth it.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Burien, WA

    Copper still has fans. It is durable and heat tolerant. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, and support every run with hangers at the spacing code requires, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Pipe Replacement for Multi-Unit Buildings in Burien

    Apartments and condos share risers. One leak can affect several units. We plan riser replacement in phases, one stack at a time, keeping each unit informed. Organized work.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in Burien

    Water under a slab shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a sound of running water, or a meter that spins with everything off. We locate the leak first. Then you have a choice between breaking the slab to fix that spot or rerouting the line through walls or the attic, and rerouting often costs less and avoids future slab leaks on that line. We explain both.
