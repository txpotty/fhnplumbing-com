    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Burien, WA

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and covering hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. Close the main valve. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, so a pipe that froze overnight may start spraying the next afternoon once the house warms up. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Insulation goes on after.

    Preventive Copper Pipe Maintenance in Burien, WA

    Copper gives warning signs before it leaks. They are easy to overlook. Green crust at fittings, a stain on a basement ceiling and high static pressure all point to trouble ahead, so we look over the pipe you can see and test the pressure. A pressure valve can slow it down.

    Underground Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Burien

    Buried copper service lines can leak for months before anyone notices. The meter gives it away. We locate the leak with acoustic gear and tracer gas, and either spot repair it or swap in a new service line with minimal digging. We test before backfill.

    Thin-Wall Copper Pinhole Repair in Burien

    Older copper, especially Type M, pinholes more than heavier pipe. Thin walls wear through first. We locate the leak precisely and either solder in a new section or use a press fitting near framing, with new pipe that is heavier than the original. Nearby pipe gets a look.

    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Burien, WA

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Heat speeds it up. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, so we repair leaking hot lines and can add a timer or adjust the pump to cut down on wear. It slows the next pinhole.

    Copper Slab Leak Repair in Burien

    Many slab homes have copper water lines running under the concrete. Listen for running water.     Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Burien

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot water speeds it up. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with Type L copper or a press fitting. One usually means more are coming.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Burien, WA

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original work, thermal expansion or vibration. Look for white or green crust. A leaking joint usually weeps for a while before it drips, so we either resolder it or replace the fitting and a short length of pipe. Then we pressure test it.
