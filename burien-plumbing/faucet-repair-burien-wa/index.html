    What We Fix on Faucets in Burien, WA

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Burien

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Turn off the stops under the sink. A new faucet can usually go in on the same visit, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Burien, WA

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. We can check whether yours qualifies. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard use their own cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Burien

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. A flush of the lines usually fixes it. On pull-down faucets, the check valves in the hose can stick too, and we clean or replace those.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Burien, WA

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. There are slip-on and threaded spouts. The pipe stub gets measured so we bring the right spout, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Burien, WA

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. A diverter costs very little. Touch and touchless faucets add a solenoid and batteries to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Burien

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Over time the washer hardens and the seat wears rough. We replace the washer and dress or replace the seat with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Burien

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. The hose usually splits near the quick-connect fitting. Brand-matched hoses, heads and weights go in, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Burien

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and covering hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. We replace packing washers and seat washers on standard hose bibs, and we install frost-free sillcocks with the valve seat inside the warm wall when an old one has split. A vacuum breaker goes on every new one.
