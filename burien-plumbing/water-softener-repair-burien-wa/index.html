    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Burien, WA

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. Installing a new quality softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,500.     Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Burien

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not. We retest hardness and reprogram the valve, or upgrade the head to a metered control that only regenerates when the resin is actually close to exhausted. Salt bills drop.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Burien, WA

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. FHN Plumbing gives honest advice based on both. A ten-year-old unit with a sound tank and a rebuildable valve is usually worth fixing, while an obsolete head with no parts available is a different conversation. You get a clear breakdown.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Burien

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Control Valve Repair in Burien

    Water that runs to the drain all day usually means the control valve is stuck between cycles. FHN Plumbing rebuilds stuck valves with the correct seal and spacer kit for your model. Mechanical or electronic, we fix the timing. Common parts are on the truck, so most valve repairs are finished the same day without a second trip to the supply house.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Burien, WA

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. A minor drip still wastes water and damages floors and cabinets. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.
