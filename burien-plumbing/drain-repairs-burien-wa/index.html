    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Burien

    Clearing a clog usually runs $150 to $400. Structural fixes are higher. Main line repairs can be anywhere from $800 to $5,000 or more, especially when the pipe is buried and has to be excavated to reach the damaged section. We price it once we know the cause.

    Call us if a plunger or a small hand snake has not cleared it. Same goes for repeat clogs. Multiple slow drains, sewer smell or water coming up in a different fixture than the one you are using usually mean the problem is deeper in the line than a hand tool can reach.     Keep grease out of the kitchen sink. Use hair screens. Flush only toilet paper, run hot water after greasy dishes, and have the main branches jetted every couple of years on older homes. Hard water homes may need it more often.

    Clearing a simple clog takes an hour or two. Repairs run longer. Replacing a section of main line or opening a wall to swap a cast iron stack can take a full day or more, depending on access and whether a permit inspection is needed. The quote includes a time estimate.

    Hydro-jetting scours the pipe with water at several thousand psi. The whole inside gets cleaned. A snake only punches a hole through a clog, while a jetter removes grease, scale and fine roots from the full circumference of the pipe. Fragile pipe gets checked before jetting.

    Every repair is warrantied. Workmanship is covered in writing. Replacement parts carry their manufacturer warranties, and we explain what is and is not covered at the time of the visit. No fine print.

    Small fixture drain repairs usually do not. Stack work typically does. Replacing drain stacks, relocating drains or adding new lines often needs a permit, depending on the city and how much of the system is being changed. The permit is on us if needed.
