    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Burien, WA

    The gas control valve regulates temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something goes wrong. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or approved universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Burien, WA

    Atmospheric gas water heaters rely on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and verify draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.

    Burner Assembly and Venting System Repair in Burien

    A malfunctioning burner assembly can result in poor heating or unusual noises. We carefully inspect the burner for blockages, corrosion or wear. We also check the venting system for proper draft and obstructions, which is essential for safe operation and preventing carbon monoxide buildup in your Burien home. Proper venting is a key safety consideration.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Burien

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane instead of natural gas. Propane units use different orifices and regulator pressures, and incorrect conversions cause sooting and poor performance. We confirm the correct parts are installed and repair pilots, valves and burners. We also check tank regulators for proper pressure.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Burien

    Odd noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can speed up sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, improving efficiency and quiet operation.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Burien

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it senses vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and trip the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as required.

    Tank Leaks & Pressure Problems in Burien

    Even gas water heaters can develop tank leaks, often due to corrosion from hard water or high water pressure. Soft water can be slightly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. While some leaks might require a replacement, minor leaks or pressure relief valve issues can often be repaired. Our team assesses the extent of the damage, inspects the anode rod and addresses pressure imbalances to prevent further complications in your Burien home.
