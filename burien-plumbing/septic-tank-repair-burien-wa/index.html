    Effluent Pump Replacement in Burien, WA

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. A pump that hums but moves no water has a stuck impeller or a bad capacitor. Our tech pulls the old unit and checks the discharge before the new one goes in. Floats get replaced at the same time.

    Septic Pump and Alarm Repair in Burien

    Pumped systems move effluent uphill or to a far field, and the pump sits in its own chamber with a float tree and an alarm. An alarm going off means the water rose past the high-level float. We check the breaker and meter the pump draw before replacing anything. Zoeller and Liberty parts ride on the truck.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Burien, WA

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones corrode from the acid that sewer gas forms and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Emergency Septic Repair in Burien

    Sewage in the shower pan on a Sunday is an emergency, and we treat it like one. Our dispatcher asks if the tank has a riser at grade, because digging for a buried lid adds an hour to the call. Stop using water until the tech arrives. Then we find the blockage, clear it and tell you what caused it.

    Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Burien

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Press the silence button and cut back on water. We check whether the pump runs and whether a float is hung up on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.
