    Why Choose FHN Plumbing for PEX Pipe Installation in Burien, WA?

    What Goes Into a PEX Job in Burien, WA

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Burien, WA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Burien, WA

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting is pushed in while the pipe is open, and the plastic's memory shrinks it back around the barb, sealing tighter during the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. Joints get time to set before we pressurize.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Burien

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Both last decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Burien

    Radiant floor loops are PEX too, but a different kind you drink from. Heating loops need oxygen-barrier PEX, which has a coating that keeps oxygen from migrating through the wall and rusting the boiler, the pump and any iron in the system. Loops have length limits. Too long and the far end runs cold.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Burien, WA

    Price on a repipe comes down to a few things: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX almost always comes in below copper because the material is cheaper and a crew can run a house in two to four days instead of a week. Drywall patching may be separate. Ask and we will price all three, PEX-A, PEX-B and copper, side by side.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Burien, WA

    A single bad section is not always a reason to repipe. We can cut out a split or corroded stretch of copper, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and have the water back on in an hour or two. It is fine for an isolated leak. Once you are on the third patch, we will tell you straight whether the rest of the copper is heading the same way.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Burien

    Your service line runs from the meter or well to the house, often a foot and a half to three feet underground. A PEX main comes off a coil in one piece, so there are no buried joints between the meter and the foundation wall. Glacial till and dense hardpan are common in western Washington, and they drain poorly enough to keep water against foundations and in crawlspaces. We bed it in sand. Sharp rock against plastic pipe is how a new line fails early.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Burien

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. The tech expands the pipe and ring together, pushes the fitting in, and the plastic shrinks back around the barb over the next few seconds, tighter than when it started. There is no crimp gauge to check. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.
