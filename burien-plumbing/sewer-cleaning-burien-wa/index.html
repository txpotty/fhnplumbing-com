    Jetting a Sewer Line in Burien, WA

    Grease and scale are jetting work. Cables leave them behind. The rear jets drive the hose forward while the spray scours the wall, so the line ends up much closer to its original size.

    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Burien, WA

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust. Soft water can be slightly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. Over years the opening shrinks. Cabling opens a path, while jetting strips the crust off the walls so the line stays open longer, and the method depends on pipe material and condition.

    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Burien, WA
