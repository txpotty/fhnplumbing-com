    Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Burien

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Soft water can be slightly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines.     Electric Element Service in Burien, WA

    Two elements, two thermostats. Sediment coats the lower element first, it runs hot and burns out, and that is the most common reason an electric tank suddenly gives you only half a tank of hot water. We test with power off. We only replace what failed.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Burien
