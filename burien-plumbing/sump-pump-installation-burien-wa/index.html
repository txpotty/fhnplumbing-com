    Sump Pump Installation Services in Burien, WA

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Burien

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Burien

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Burien, WA

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Burien

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. Install time is shorter too. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Burien, WA

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install their M53 and M98 models often. They make matching backup units. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Burien, WA

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. Our trucks carry 1/3 and 1/2 HP submersibles, check valves and fittings, so in most homes we can pull the old pump and set a new one in one visit. Figure on two hours for a typical swap. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Burien, WA

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Burien

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Rentals need these. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    A pump swap in an existing pit typically costs $600 to $1,500. New pits, with saw cutting, excavation and a concrete patch, run about $1,500 to $4,000. Add $500 to $1,500 for a backup.

    For some homeowners, swapping a pump in a working pit is doable. Anything involving a new pit, a new discharge route or new wiring is a job for a professional.

    Plan on seven to ten years for a well-kept pump. Heavy use shortens that. We can check yours.

    A submersible sits in the pit under water, so it runs quieter and takes no floor space. The pedestal type mounts the motor on a column above the water, which makes it easier to reach for service and usually cheaper to buy, though you will hear it. Both work.     Seven to ten years is typical. Cast iron pumps with mechanical switches tend to outlast plastic ones, and a pump that rests longer between cycles lasts longer. Yearly cleaning stretches it.
