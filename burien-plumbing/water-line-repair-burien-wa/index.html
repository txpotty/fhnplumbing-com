    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Burien, WA

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a simple spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades.     While some very minor, accessible leaks might seem DIY-friendly, we do not recommend attempting water line repairs yourself, especially for underground or main lines. Professional plumbers have the specialized tools, experience, and knowledge of Washington plumbing codes to ensure a safe and lasting repair.

    Most standard water line repairs can be completed within a few hours to a full day, depending on the severity and accessibility of the leak.     Our plumbers are experienced with all common water line materials found in Burien, including PEX, copper, PVC, and older galvanized steel pipes.     In most communities the water provider owns the meter and the pipe on the street side of it. The homeowner is responsible for the service line from the meter to the house. We check which side the leak is on before quoting you.

    Preventative measures include regular plumbing inspections, being mindful of tree roots near your water lines, and protecting outdoor pipes from extreme temperatures. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and protecting hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. For older homes with galvanized pipes, planning a full replacement can prevent ongoing issues. We can advise on preventative steps suited to your property.

    Trenchless water line repair is a method of repairing or replacing underground pipes without extensive digging.
