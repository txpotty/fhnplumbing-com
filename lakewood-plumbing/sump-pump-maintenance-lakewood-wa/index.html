    Sump Pump Maintenance in Lakewood, WA: What We Check

    Float Switch Inspection in Lakewood, WA

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Lakewood

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. Our plumber drains the basin and digs the sediment out by hand. With the pit clean, the float swings without catching. The pump runs cooler too.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Lakewood

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. We lift the unit out to look at the seals and the cord. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. We also clean the intake screen and check the housing for corrosion, which shows up fast on pumps that sit in standing water for years.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Lakewood

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. A failed valve sends that water back down for the pump to move again. Motors wear out faster. We test the valve, look for a weep hole between the pump and the valve, and check every joint up to the wall.

    Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in Lakewood

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, leaves and gravel settle at the bottom of the basin. That debris gets pulled into the intake and jams the impeller. Our plumber pumps the basin down, rinses the screen and inspects the liner for cracks that let dirt seep in through the walls. Takes about twenty minutes.

    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in Lakewood, WA

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. Those units need more frequent checks than a house pump. Our crew tests the controls, the alarm panel and each pump in a duplex station, and records amp draw every visit so a slow decline gets caught. Downtime costs money.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Lakewood

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. A backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or quits. We load test the battery, check the charger and the alarm, and run the backup by itself with the main pump unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Lakewood, WA

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Each start heats the motor windings. We swap worn valves for quiet, spring-loaded models and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose couplings all the way to the exit point. The water should exit well away from the slab or foundation so it does not soak back toward the pit.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Maintenance in Lakewood

    Most Lakewood, WA homeowners pay $100 to $250 for a standard maintenance visit, depending on the pump type and how long since it was last serviced. That covers the inspection, pit cleaning and a full water test. Parts and repairs are quoted separately before we start.

    Grinding, rattling, nonstop running, a pump that will not start, rust on the housing or damp spots on the floor near the pit. Each of those is reason enough to book a visit before the next storm.

    Without a working battery or water-powered backup, the pit keeps filling during an outage and eventually spills over onto the basement floor. In a hard storm, that takes under an hour. Water-powered backups need city water pressure, so they do not work on a private well.

    Most visits run 45 minutes to an hour. Swapping a check valve or float on the same trip adds a little time.

    Most local codes prohibit sump discharge into the sanitary sewer, since storm water can overload treatment plants. We route it to the yard, a dry well or another approved spot.

    Our crew services Zoeller, Liberty, Wayne, Little Giant, Superior and most other residential and commercial pumps. We can also size an upgrade for a pit that cannot keep up.

    Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. In hard-water areas, scale builds up on the impeller, the float rod and the basin walls. Over a few years that crust slows the pump down and can lock a vertical float in place so the pump never starts. We scrape it off during maintenance.

    A quality pump with yearly service usually lasts 7 to 10 years. Skip maintenance and it can give out years sooner.
