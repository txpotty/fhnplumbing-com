    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Damage Restoration in Lakewood

    The cost of water damage restoration in Lakewood varies widely depending on the extent of the damage, the category of water, whether clean, gray, or black water, and the materials affected. Minor clean water incidents might range from $1,500 to $4,000, while extensive black water damage involving structural repairs could be $10,000 or more. We provide a detailed estimate after a thorough assessment.

    Drywall soaked by clean water can sometimes be dried in place if addressed quickly. Drywall that has been wet for a long time or exposed to contaminated water usually must be replaced. We test moisture levels to decide.

    Household fans can help with small surface spills but rarely dry wall cavities, subfloors or cabinets fully. Hidden moisture can lead to mold and structural damage. Professional equipment and moisture monitoring give better results.

    While you can handle very minor spills, professional water damage restoration is highly recommended for anything more serious. DIY efforts often miss hidden moisture, leading to mold growth, structural damage, and ongoing issues. Professionals have professional equipment like hygrometers and thermal cameras to confirm complete drying and prevent long-term problems.

    Water damage can stem from any plumbing brand. We commonly see issues with water heaters from brands like Rheem or Bradford White, washing machines from Whirlpool or Samsung, dishwashers from Bosch or GE, and supply lines from many faucet brands. Our focus is on repairing the damage, regardless of the brand.

    Mold prevention is a key part of our process. We achieve this through fast water extraction, thorough drying with air movers and dehumidifiers, and applying antimicrobial treatments to affected areas. Our moisture monitoring confirms all hidden moisture is removed before mold can take hold.

    For minor water damage, you might be able to stay in your home. However, for extensive damage, especially involving contaminated water or significant structural drying, it may be safer and more comfortable to temporarily relocate. We will let you know based on your situation.

    FHN Plumbing offers 24/7 emergency response for water damage. Our goal is to arrive at your Lakewood property within a few hours of your call, as fast action is critical in limiting damage and preventing further issues like mold growth.
