    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Lakewood, WA

    Crawlspaces and basements are both common in Washington homes, and a wet crawlspace is often the first sign of a drainage or plumbing problem. A section of sewer pipe that sinks forms a belly. Waste settles in the low spot. Over time that standing water collects paper and grease, and the line clogs at the same spot every few months no matter how often it gets cleaned. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. A spot repair restores the slope.

    Residential Clogged Sewer Lines in Lakewood

    Most home sewer clogs come from three things: roots, grease and wipes. Often all three together. Grease coats the pipe, wipes catch on it and roots lock everything into a plug. We clear it with a sewer machine, then check on camera to see the main cause.

    Commercial Clogged Sewer Line Service in Lakewood

    Commercial sewer lines in Lakewood carry far more than a house does. Kitchen grease is the big one.     Tree Root Invasion Removal in Lakewood

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture in the soil. A hairline gap is enough. Once inside, they grow into a dense mat that catches toilet paper and waste until the whole line stops flowing. We cut them out with a root saw on a sectional machine, and check the joints on camera afterward. Lining or replacement seals the joints for good.

    Identifying and Repairing Damaged Pipes in Lakewood

    A clog that keeps coming back usually means broken pipe. Cleaning will not fix that. Our camera finds the crack, offset or collapse, while the locator marks it so the repair can be quoted right. FHN Plumbing gives you options from a single dig to full replacement.

    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Lakewood, WA

    One slow drain is a branch clog. Several slow drains is the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, which is why we ask which fixtures are acting up before we decide where to open the line. The right entry point saves time.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Lakewood, WA

    Flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. Not even close. Mixed with grease that hardened in the main, they form a dense plug that a cable can poke a hole through but not fully remove from the pipe. Jetting scours the walls and flushes the debris downstream. Wipes belong in the garbage.
