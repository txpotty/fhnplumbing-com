    Pop-Up Drain and Stopper Repair in Lakewood, WA

    When the stopper will not close, will not open or lets water slowly escape, the pop-up linkage is usually to blame. The pivot rod nut can leak, the clevis strap can slip, and hair wraps around the stopper body. We clean or replace the assembly and adjust it so the lift rod works freely. If the drain body itself is corroded, we replace it with a matching finish.

    Supply Line and Shutoff Valve Repair in Lakewood, WA

    Braided supply lines and angle stops are rated for years, not decades, and the rubber inside breaks down in warm cabinets. A failing line can burst without warning and flood a bathroom. We replace worn lines with stainless braided connectors and swap stuck multi-turn valves for quarter-turn stops. It is a small job that prevents a very expensive one.

    Sink Trap and P-Trap Repair in Lakewood, WA
