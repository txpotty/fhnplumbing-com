    Expansion Tank Installation in Lakewood, WA

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and pipes.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Lakewood

    The cost of water heater installation in Lakewood, WA usually ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of water heater (tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any necessary plumbing or electrical modifications. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your particular needs.

    Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional inspection.

    Most standard water heater installations, particularly for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours. However, a conversion from a tank to a tankless system, or installations requiring significant plumbing or electrical modifications, might take a full day or longer. We will provide an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency. If your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down, or the repair cost is more than 50% of a new unit, replacement is often the more cost-effective choice. Newer models also offer much better energy efficiency.

    An expansion tank absorbs excess water pressure created as water heats up inside the tank. Many homes in Lakewood, WA, particularly those with closed-loop plumbing systems, are required by code to have an expansion tank to protect the water heater and plumbing system from damage.

    Yes, we stand behind our work. Our water heater installations come with a labor warranty, and the water heater unit itself will have a manufacturer's warranty, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years, depending on the model.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, water heater installation involves working with gas lines, high voltage electricity and water connections, all of which carry significant risks if not handled correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, gas explosions, electrical fires and carbon monoxide poisoning. It can also void your warranty, and local codes typically require a licensed plumber for this work.

    We install Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, Navien, Rinnai and other trusted brands. We recommend models based on reliability, warranty and budget.
