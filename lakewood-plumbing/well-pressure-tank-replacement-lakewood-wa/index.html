    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Lakewood, WA

    Diaphragm tanks use a flexible sheet clamped inside the shell. Air sits above it, water below. When it fails, symptoms match a bladder tank, with short cycling, pressure swings and water spitting from the air valve. Replacement is the usual answer.

    Well-Rite Pressure Tank Installation in Lakewood, WA

    Well-Rite tanks use a replaceable diaphragm design in many models. We install them in the common residential sizes. Like any tank, the key is setting pre-charge before it fills, then confirming the gauge, switch and drawdown line up with the pump you have. A good tank installed badly fails early.

    Emergency Pressure Tank Replacement in Lakewood, WA

    If the pump is running nonstop or the taps are dry, call (425) 491-3131. A ruptured tank or a stuck switch can burn out a pump, so we isolate the system, diagnose it and swap the tank, often the same day. Turn off the breaker first.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Lakewood

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Old ones leak at threads. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, and a corroded valve may not open when needed. Whole set, one visit.

    Commercial Well Pressure Tank Services in Lakewood, WA

    Commercial wells often run several tanks in parallel. Multi-tank setups are common. We check each tank's pre-charge separately, since one bad tank in a bank can still cause short cycling, while planning the work so the building keeps water. Larger tanks need two people.

    Whole-House Tank Replacement on Older Wells in Lakewood

    Older wells sometimes have a tank, switch and plumbing that were all installed together decades ago. A new tank can reveal tired fittings. We look at the whole tank area, from the check valve to the house shutoff, so you can choose a tank-only swap or a fuller refresh. Your call.
