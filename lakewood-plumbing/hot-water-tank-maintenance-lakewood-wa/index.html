    Gas and Electric Tank Checks in Lakewood, WA

    Anode Upgrades for Odor in Lakewood, WA

    A rotten egg smell from the hot side only is almost always sulfate-reducing bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. Swapping the rod usually fixes it.     Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Lakewood

    Street pressure over 80 psi is hard on every fixture in the house, and plumbing codes generally call for a pressure reducing valve above that number. We check static pressure at a hose bib.
