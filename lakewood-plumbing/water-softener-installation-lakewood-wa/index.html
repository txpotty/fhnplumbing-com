    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Lakewood

    Testing is what makes the settings right. Our crew checks hardness, iron and chlorine, then sets the regeneration and salt dosage to match. Iron adds to the setting. You get a walkthrough of the settings.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Lakewood

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. The savings add up. Several models are available.

    Water Softener Replacement in Lakewood, WA

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Salt use drops. You will notice soft water the same day.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Lakewood

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning. We test the water, ask about daily use and choose a capacity that lets the system regenerate roughly once a week. That keeps salt use reasonable. One visit covers the full install.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Lakewood

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. Bring your own unit. Programming is part of the job.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Lakewood, WA

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. If you bought a Pelican system for your home, we install it with secure connections, a proper bypass and the startup steps the manual lists for flushing the media. Skipping the flush causes trouble. Every step gets done.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Lakewood, WA

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. Taste improves fast. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Softener Loop Installation in Lakewood

    No loop? A loop is the plumbing that routes the main line through a softener, and many older homes were built without one. We add the loop. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.
