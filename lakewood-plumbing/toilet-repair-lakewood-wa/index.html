    Flush Mechanism Repair in Lakewood, WA

    A weak flush usually means the tank is not filling to the fill line, the chain has too much slack, or the flapper is closing before the tank empties. We bring the water up to the line stamped on the overflow tube and adjust the chain to about half an inch of slack. Handles break too. A loose or cracked handle is a five-minute swap.

    Toilet Flush Repair in Lakewood, WA

    Two or three tries to get a full flush points to a flapper closing too early, a chain hooked in the wrong hole, scale in the rim jets, or a cracked overflow tube letting water bypass the bowl. Each one has a tell. Our plumber watches a complete flush with the tank lid off and replace the flush valve if the overflow tube is cracked, since it cannot be patched.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Lakewood, WA

    A rocking bowl breaks the wax seal a little more every time someone sits down, and eventually the leak shows up as a stain or a soft spot.     Older Toilet Brands We Service in Lakewood, WA

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Lakewood, WA

    Braided supply lines last years, but the plastic nut on the toilet end can crack and a bulge in the braid means the inner hose is about to burst. We check every supply line on every visit.     Running Toilet Fixes in Lakewood

    Phantom refills, the tank topping itself off for a few seconds in the middle of the night, almost always mean a slow leak at the flapper. The water level drops until the fill valve kicks on again. Sometimes the cause is a refill tube pushed too far down the overflow, which siphons the tank. We clip it above the water line.

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Lakewood

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the china that compresses air with incoming water pressure, and the cartridge inside that vessel eventually wears out. Hardware stores rarely stock those parts. We carry Flushmate cartridges and dual-flush seals for the common TOTO and American Standard models, and we check house pressure, since these units need steady line pressure to flush properly. Low pressure makes them sound weak.
