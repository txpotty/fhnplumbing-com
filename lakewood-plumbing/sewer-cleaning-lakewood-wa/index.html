    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Lakewood

    Roots come back after cutting. Usually within a year or so. Putting a root-prone main on a yearly cleaning schedule keeps the growth small enough that it never stops the line, and it costs far less than an after-hours emergency call.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Lakewood, WA

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. It only removes what is inside. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera usually shows a belly, a crack or a collapsed section, and we compare the yearly cost of cleanings to a spot repair, a liner or a replacement, with the numbers in writing.

    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Lakewood, WA

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust. Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is generally soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. Flow slows a little each year. Cabling opens a path, while jetting strips the crust off the walls so the line stays open longer, and the method depends on pipe material and condition.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Lakewood

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. Cabling just opens a hole. Water at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes it all toward the street, which leaves the pipe close to its original diameter.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Lakewood, WA

    Grease hardens as it cools. Then it narrows the pipe. A greasy main slowly loses diameter until it cannot carry a full washer load or a bathtub draining at once, and jetting is the right fix.
