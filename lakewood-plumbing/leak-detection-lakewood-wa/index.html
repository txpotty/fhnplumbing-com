    Irrigation and Yard Leak Detection in Lakewood, WA

    Main Water Line Leak Detection in Lakewood

    The service line between your meter and house can leak underground for months, especially in older galvanized or copper lines. Wet spots in the yard, oddly green grass or a sinking area near the front of the house are clues. We use line tracers to map the pipe and ground microphones to hear the leak. That keeps excavation to a small, focused hole.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leak Detection in Lakewood

    Leaks inside walls and ceilings often show up as stains, bubbling paint, soft drywall or a musty smell. We use thermal cameras to find temperature differences caused by water, then confirm with moisture meters. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line. Pinpointing the source lets us cut a small, clean access opening.
