    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Lakewood

    Residential hydro jetting in Lakewood usually costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    FHN Plumbing uses professional-grade hydro jetting equipment from established manufacturers like Spartan Tool and MyTana. These brands are known for their reliability, powerful performance, and safety features, so we can deliver the most effective cleaning for your pipes.

    No permit is needed for jetting. If the inspection reveals damage that needs repair, permits may be required and we handle them.

    For most homes in Lakewood, hydro jetting every 1 to 3 years can be a good preventative measure, especially if you have recurring drain issues or older pipes. Businesses, particularly restaurants, may need quarterly or twice-yearly hydro jetting to manage grease buildup. We can recommend a schedule based on your needs.

    While hydro jetting fully removes existing tree roots from your sewer line, it does not prevent new roots from growing back. For a long-term solution to recurring root intrusion, we might recommend trenchless sewer repair or pipe replacement, which we can go over during our assessment.

    For most modern and well-maintained pipes, hydro jetting is very safe. However, for very old, corroded, or severely damaged pipes, the high pressure could cause further damage. That is why FHN Plumbing always performs a camera inspection first to check your pipe's condition and confirm hydro jetting is appropriate.

    A standard residential hydro jetting service in Lakewood typically takes between 1 and 3 hours. This includes the initial camera inspection, the jetting process itself, and a final inspection. More extensive commercial jobs or severe main line blockages might require more time.

    Repeat clogs in the same line, slow drains after snaking, sewer odors and known grease or root problems are solid reasons to consider jetting.
