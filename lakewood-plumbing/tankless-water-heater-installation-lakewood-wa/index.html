    Propane homes in Lakewood are good candidates for tankless, using a model built or converted for propane.     Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Lakewood

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job because the old tank spot is not always the best place.     Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Lakewood, WA

    Switching from a tank is more than a swap. A typical 40-gallon gas tank uses around 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the gas line from the meter almost always gets upsized. The vent changes as well. Both are on the estimate.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Lakewood, WA

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Many rural Washington homes rely on private wells, and some carry iron, manganese or other minerals that call for testing and treatment. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. Flush it yearly. Service valves make it easy.
