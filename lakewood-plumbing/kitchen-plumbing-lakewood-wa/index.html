    Sinks, Disposals and Drains in Lakewood, WA: How We Handle Them

    Grease Trap Installation in Lakewood, WA

    Under-sink grease traps work for small kitchens, while full restaurants often need an in-ground interceptor sized to the local sewer authority's rules. We read the requirement before we quote. Trap size comes from fixture flow rates, and a trap that is too small fills quickly and lets grease slip into the sewer.

    Grease Trap Installation and Cleaning in Lakewood

    Grease traps are sized by flow, and a three-compartment sink typically calls for a trap rated at 20 gallons per minute or more. We set the trap level, install the flow control fitting and vent it so it does not siphon. Cleaning schedules depend on volume. A kitchen running fryers all day may need service every couple of weeks.

    Recurring Kitchen Clogs in Lakewood, WA

    Grease, rice, pasta and coffee grounds build up in the trap and branch line over months, not days. We clear the line with a cable sized for kitchen pipe and can camera it when the same clog returns. The pipe is sometimes the cause. A sag in the branch line or a drain arm with too little slope will hold grease no matter how often it gets cleaned.

    Disposal Jams and Hums in Lakewood, WA

    Humming with no grinding means the motor is getting power but the plate is stuck. That is often ten minutes. We free the plate from below with a hex key, clear the hopper with pliers, never a hand, and reset the overload before we test it with water. No sound at all points us to the switch, the outlet or the breaker.

    InSinkErator Models We Service in Lakewood

    Badger units are the basic line, with a single grind stage and a 1/3 to 3/4 horsepower motor. Evolution models add grind stages and sound insulation, which you notice in an open floor plan, and some carry longer in-home warranties than the basic line does. Common models are on the truck. We also service older InSinkErator units when the motor is still sound.

    InSinkErator Installation in Lakewood, WA

    Many homeowners ask to step up from an older InSinkErator to an Evolution because the newer units are quieter and grind finer. The mount often carries over. When the old Quick Lock mount is sound, we can reuse it, which saves time and leaves the sink flange alone, and we register the warranty for you.

    Burst Supply Lines in Lakewood, WA

    Plastic supply lines with plastic nuts crack more often than braided stainless, especially after years of pressure swings. A failed line under the sink can put several gallons a minute into the cabinet and the floor below before anyone notices it. Both supply lines get replaced. The second line is just as old as the one that failed.

    Disposal Repairs That Make Sense in Lakewood, WA

    Jams, tripped resets, loose mounts and leaking sink flanges are all fixable in under an hour. Motor and seal failures are not. When the repair would cost more than half of a new unit, we tell you so and give you a replacement price on the spot instead of selling a part that will not last.
