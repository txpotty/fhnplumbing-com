    Dependable Tankless Water Heater Repair Services in Lakewood, WA

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Lakewood

    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace failed parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are correct.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Lakewood

    Gas tankless water heaters require precise gas pressure and proper ventilation for safe operation. Issues can range from gas valve failures to carbon monoxide sensor errors. Our licensed technicians in Lakewood perform thorough diagnostics on gas lines, igniters and exhaust systems, so your unit is both functional and safe.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Lakewood, WA

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and verify proper drainage. PVC venting is also inspected for slope and sagging.

    Tankless Water Heater Error Code Diagnostics in Lakewood, WA

    Modern tankless water heaters display error codes to indicate specific problems, from flow rate issues to combustion errors. Our experts in Lakewood, WA are trained to interpret these codes accurately, using dedicated diagnostic tools to pinpoint the exact component failure.     Ignition Failure Repair in Lakewood, WA

    If your tankless unit will not fire, the cause may be a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. We test each component and measure gas pressure at the inlet. Undersized gas lines are a common hidden cause when a tankless unit replaced a tank. We correct the issue so the unit lights reliably.
