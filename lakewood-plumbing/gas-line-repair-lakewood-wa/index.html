    Cost of Gas Line Repair in Lakewood, WA

    Gas line repair prices in Lakewood, WA depend on where the leak is and how much pipe has to change. Tightening or replacing a single fitting or connector is usually a few hundred dollars. Trenching and replacing a buried line often lands between $1,000 and $5,000, with length, soil and any concrete in the path setting the final number. We put the price in writing first.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Lakewood

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. With every appliance off, a moving test dial on the meter tells us gas is escaping somewhere, and then we isolate branches to narrow it down. An electronic detector does the rest. A spray of soap solution on the fitting shows bubbles at the leak, and we fix that joint or section and retest the whole system.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Lakewood, WA

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. After the coating breaks down, corrosion starts, and constant moisture from irrigation or poor drainage makes it worse. We call 811 before digging. We lay polyethylene with tracer wire or coated steel where exposed, then backfill and clean up after the test passes.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Lakewood

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Lakewood building officials. We file the permit, schedule the inspection and are on site when the inspector checks the work and the test gauge. Inspectors often come within the week. Once it passes, the utility can restore service and we come back to relight appliances if needed.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Lakewood, WA

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Vibration and settling loosen threaded joints over time, and pipe in damp garages or outside walls can rust through from the surface. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Lakewood

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. We add up the BTU load of every appliance on the system, measure the longest run and pick a pipe size that keeps pressure up at the farthest appliance when everything runs together. After that comes routing, the tie-in and the test.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Lakewood

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. We tighten, reseal or replace fittings and sections with new pipe and gas-rated thread sealant. Rusty runs get swapped out. Heavily rusted pipe in garages, crawlspaces or outside walls is replaced before it fails, and we pressure test the full system afterward.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Lakewood, WA

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. Pool heaters are the big load, often 250,000 to 400,000 BTU, so a line that was fine for a grill can starve the heater and the house appliances at the same time. We size outdoor lines for the full load. New and repaired lines get tested, buried to the required depth and laid with tracer wire.
