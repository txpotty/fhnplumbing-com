    Deep Well Pump Repair in Yakima

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up, run quietly and last, but motors, impellers and splices still wear out. After pulling the pump, we test each part and replace only the failed one. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Yakima, WA well.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Yakima, WA

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Most failures start with an air leak somewhere in the suction line. FHN Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and watches it reach cut-out pressure. A jet pump that runs nonstop without reaching pressure has almost always lost prime or sucked air through a cracked fitting.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Yakima

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, a clogged sensing tube and ant nests are the usual reasons one fails. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank, typically 40 and 60 psi. This is among the least expensive well repairs.

    Low Water Pressure Repair in Yakima

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. Common causes include a plugged sediment filter, a low switch setting or a yard line leak. In some cases the pump is fine and the well just cannot keep up with demand. We test each one.

    Submersible Pump Replacement in Yakima

    Sand wears impellers down over time, and a pump that once filled the tank in a minute starts taking three. Other common failures are the motor, the splice and the check valve. We test the motor and wire from the surface before deciding whether to pull. Replacement pumps get sized to the yield and depth of the well.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Yakima, WA

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. Common Franklin parts are on the truck. Replacing a failed motor usually costs less than a rewind once shipping is counted.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Yakima

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. When the bladder fails, the tank fills with water and the pump starts and stops every time someone opens a faucet. We inspect the bladder, the switch and the tank connections. Proper sizing adds years to a pump.
