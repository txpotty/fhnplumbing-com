    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Yakima

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. What worked for three people can run out of capacity in a few days with five. Sizing is based on current usage.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Yakima

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break them up and clean out the mush at the bottom. We replace cracked tanks and bad safety floats. Pellets bridge less than rock salt.

    Whole-Home Softener Placement in Yakima, WA

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. Outdoor hose bibs usually stay hard. Why soften water for the lawn?

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Yakima

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico runs them without electricity, using water flow to drive the valve. They cost more.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Yakima

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. We replace the media or the whole system, depending on age. Following the install specs keeps the warranty intact.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in Yakima, WA

    Swapping a salt-based softener in Washington homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. We pressurize and check every joint. The drain line gets an air gap.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Yakima, WA

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. They do keep scale from sticking inside pipes and water heaters. No salt, no drain. Swapping to a salt-based unit is an option too.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Yakima, WA

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. Hardness gets tested on both sides of the unit, and the cheap fixes get tried before we talk replacement. Discontinued valves usually mean replacement.

    Local Water Softener Replacement in Yakima, WA

    Most softener jobs around Yakima are finished in an afternoon. We carry common 32,000 and 48,000-grain systems, so a failed unit can often be swapped the same day you call. Larger units may need a day.
