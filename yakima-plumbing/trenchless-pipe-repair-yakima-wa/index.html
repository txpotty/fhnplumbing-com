    Pipe Bursting Services in Yakima, WA

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE resists corrosion and leaks in Yakima, WA soil.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Repair Cost in Yakima, WA

    Per foot of pipe, trenchless can cost more than open trenching. That's not the whole bill. After adding drive, patio, landscaping and sprinkler repairs to the trench bid, trenchless usually wins on price and speed. We compare both with real numbers for your home.

    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Yakima

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Prep is half the job. Before lining, Picote heads mill out roots and scale, and after curing they reopen the lateral connections so each drain flows. Good prep makes a good liner.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Yakima

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's rated for sewer lines. Its liners cure into a structural pipe that holds even if the old host pipe keeps falling apart around it. They hold up in tough conditions.
