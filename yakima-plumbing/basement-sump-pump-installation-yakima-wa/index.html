    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Yakima

    River flooding and saturated ground can push water back through sewer lines, so backwater valves and sump pumps are worth considering.     Watch for constant running, rapid cycling or grinding noises. Rust stains and a float that sticks are warning signs too, as is water sitting above the switch level without the pump starting. Do not wait for the storm.
