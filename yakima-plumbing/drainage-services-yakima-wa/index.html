    Residential Drain Cleaning in Yakima

    Downspout and Roof Drain Solutions in Yakima

    Gutters concentrate a lot of roof water into a few points, often right next to the foundation. Glacial till and dense hardpan are common in western Washington, and they drain poorly enough to keep water against foundations and in crawlspaces. We pipe downspouts underground in solid PVC to a pop-up emitter or dry well well away from the house, with a cleanout at the base so the line can be flushed.     Commercial Drainage Services in Yakima

    Retail centers and office buildings need floor drains, roof drains and parking lot catch basins that keep working. Property managers can set up regular service with us.     Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in Yakima, WA

    Sewer laterals fail from roots, settling, or plain age. Older clay and cast iron sewers crack at the joints, which lets roots in. We camera the line, locate the damage from the surface, and price a spot repair, a full replacement, or trenchless lining where the pipe is a good candidate. You pick.

    Sump Pump Services in Yakima, WA

    A sump pump protects a basement or crawlspace from groundwater. We test the float, check the discharge line and check valve, and replace pumps that hum, run nonstop, or cannot keep up during a hard rain. Discharge must go outside. A frozen or buried discharge line is a common reason a working pump still floods the pit.

    French Drain Installation in Yakima

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects groundwater and carries it away. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so silt does not clog it. It needs steady fall to an outlet, and the outlet gets a grate to keep critters out.

    Sink, Tub and Toilet Drain Clearing in Yakima

    Kitchen sinks clogged with grease and bathroom drains slowed by hair make up most of our fixture calls. We pull the trap when we can. For a toilet, we use a closet auger first, and if that fails we pull the toilet to clear the line from the flange, then reset it on a new wax ring.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Yakima

    A hydro jetter sends water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full pipe wall. Cables punch through clogs, but jetting removes the grease and scale coating the pipe, which is what keeps causing them. Restaurants need it most. A camera check comes first to confirm the pipe can take it.
