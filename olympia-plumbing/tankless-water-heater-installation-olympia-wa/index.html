    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Olympia, WA

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Soft water can be slightly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. Flush it yearly. We install isolation valves for that.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Olympia

    Sizing a home unit comes down to two numbers: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. It shortens the list quickly.
