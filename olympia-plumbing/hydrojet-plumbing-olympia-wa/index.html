    Why Choose FHN Plumbing for Hydrojet Plumbing in Olympia, WA?

    A cable snake opens a hole through a clog, but buildup on the pipe wall stays behind and catches the next clog. Jetting removes that buildup.     • Longer Results, A jetted line stays open much longer than one that was only cabled.

    Residential Hydrojetting in Olympia

    Most home jetting calls involve a greasy kitchen line or a root-filled sewer. Whenever possible, we work from the outside cleanout and keep the hose out of the house. Kitchen lines may need access under the sink. Floors get protected.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Olympia

    Preventive jetting makes sense when the same line clogs more than once a year. Camera findings set the interval. A line with heavy grease might need jetting every six months, while a sewer with a few root entry points might only need it every 18 to 24 months, and the footage tells us which.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Olympia, WA

    Older cast iron drains corrode and build scale on the inner wall, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. Jetting can remove that buildup. Thin or cracked cast iron gets gentler settings or a lining recommendation instead.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Olympia, WA

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Soft water can be slightly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. A jetter with a scouring nozzle strips that scale from the walls, and on heavily scaled cast iron we may follow up with a descaling chain head to finish the job. The difference shows on camera.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Olympia, WA

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Eastern Washington deals more with drought and heat, while spring snowmelt can still lift creeks and groundwater in low areas. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. Pop-up emitters get checked too.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in Olympia

    Hotels, apartment complexes and shopping centers need drains that keep up with heavy use every day. A backup closes rooms or stores. We schedule around slow hours and keep video records for property managers.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Olympia, WA

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.
