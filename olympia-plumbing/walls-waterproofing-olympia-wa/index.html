    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Olympia

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Steep slopes and wet soils can shift over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints.
