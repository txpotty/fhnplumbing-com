    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Olympia

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. Pressing it sends air down a tube. A control box under the sink reads that pulse and flips the power, keeping live wiring well away from the water. We drill the counter or use the spare faucet hole.

    Hard Water and Disposals in Olympia

    Many rural Washington homes rely on private wells, and some carry iron, manganese or other minerals that call for testing and treatment. Scale builds up on grind rings and drain tailpieces over the years. Scale makes a disposal noisy. Drainage slows too, since crust narrows the tailpiece and trap, and we check those parts before tying a new unit into them. Heavily scaled parts get replaced the same visit.

    Disposal Installs for New Sinks in Olympia, WA

    A new sink changes the drain math. Deeper undermount or farmhouse bowls are common in remodels. With the bowl lower, the disposal drops too, and the discharge can land below the old trap arm, which means lowering the stub. Better to sort that out mid-remodel than after the tile is up.

    Badger Replacement for Olympia Homes

    Builder-grade kitchens almost always started with a Badger, and a worn one usually gets replaced with another. First the old unit comes off.     New Construction Disposal Installs in Olympia

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Olympia

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. We drive the plug out and fish it out of the chamber.     Standard InSinkErator Installation in Olympia

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. We take the old disposal down, scrape the old putty off the flange and set a new flange with fresh putty. Power comes from a cord kit or the existing hardwire, checked for ground before the breaker goes back on.
