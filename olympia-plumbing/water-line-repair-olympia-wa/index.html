    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Olympia, WA

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a quick spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with actual information.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Olympia

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Olympia

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We confirm the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to find the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before we backfill.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Olympia, WA

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.
