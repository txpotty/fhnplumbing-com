    To extend the life of your new garbage disposal, avoid putting hard items like bones, fruit pits, or fibrous vegetables down the drain. Always use plenty of cold water while operating the disposal and run it regularly, even if there's no waste, to prevent rust and keep components moving.

    To maintain your new garbage disposal, always run cold water before, during, and after grinding food waste. Avoid putting hard items like bones, fibrous vegetables, or grease down the drain. Occasionally grind citrus peels or ice cubes to help clean the blades and freshen the unit.

    Continuous feed disposals operate with a wall switch and can run while you're adding food waste.
