    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Olympia, WA

    An air button on the sink deck or counter turns the disposal on. The button pushes air through a tube to a control box in the cabinet, which keeps electricity away from the button and avoids a new wall switch. They are common on islands.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Setup in Olympia

    The stopper lid acts as the switch on a batch-feed unit. We install the unit, then test the lid in every position to make sure the motor only runs when the lid is fully seated and stops the moment it is lifted out. It is the safety feature. Families with kids and anyone nervous about a hand near the hopper tend to choose these in Olympia homes.

    Disposal Upgrades in Olympia, WA

    Upgrading from an old 1/3 HP unit to something quieter is a common request. We disconnect and remove the old disposal, check the trap and drain arm for corrosion, and install the new unit with a new mount, so the upgrade does not inherit the old unit's worn parts. Old drain arms often need replacing. Galvanized arms rust from the inside.

    InSinkErator Model Selection in Olympia, WA

    Choosing between Badger and Evolution is where we start based on how you cook, how much noise bothers you and how much cabinet room you have. Badger units are compact and simple, while Evolution units are taller and heavier, with more grind stages and better sound insulation. Cabinet size often decides it. We measure before you buy.

    Standard Disposal Installation in Olympia

    Most households in Olympia do well with a 1/3 to 1/2 horsepower continuous-feed unit. We remove the old unit if there is one, set a new flange in fresh plumber's putty, hang the disposal on a new mount and connect it to the trap with a new discharge tube and gasket. Wiring and testing come last. We listen for a smooth spin and watch the drain clear.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Olympia, WA

    Going from batch-feed to continuous-feed means adding a way to switch it on. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. An air button skips the wall wiring. The drain usually stays the same.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Olympia, WA

    Busy kitchens get the most from a 1 HP unit, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model. We confirm the circuit can carry the motor and the drain can handle the flow, because a strong unit pushing into a flat trap arm will still clog. These units weigh more. They get supported during mounting.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Olympia

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. We also check the circuit. A larger motor pulls more current when it starts.
