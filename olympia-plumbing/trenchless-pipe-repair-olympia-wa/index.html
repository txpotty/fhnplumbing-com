    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Olympia

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Olympia yard.

    Cost-Effective Underground Pipe Repair in Olympia, WA

    People are often surprised at the final number. Trenchless can cost less. Per foot, lining can look pricey, but once you add the cost of tearing out and replacing a driveway, a patio and the landscaping above an open trench, the trenchless bid usually comes out ahead.

    Sewer Lateral Lining in Olympia

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Clay and Orangeburg fail often. Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots over time, so we jet the line clean, inspect it by camera and choose lining or bursting based on what we see. The new line restores full flow and resists roots.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Olympia

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's built for sewer use. The liners and resins are structural, meaning the cured pipe can stand on its own even if the old pipe around it keeps deteriorating, which is why we trust them for local lines. They handle harsh soil and flow.

    Sectional Point Repair in Olympia

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. That calls for a sectional liner. We position a short resin-soaked sleeve over the damaged area with a camera, inflate it and let it cure, so you pay for a few feet of liner instead of the whole run. It suits isolated damage in Olympia sewer lines.
