    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Olympia

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. The trim comes off first. Worn O-rings or a loose bonnet nut are the usual cause.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Olympia, WA

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Shut the main, or use the shower's integral stops if your valve has them behind the trim or an access panel. Next, call us. FHN Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Olympia, WA

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. We check all of it. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

    Commercial Shower Repair in Olympia, WA

    Gyms and apartment buildings run showers dozens of times a day. Commercial valves get heavy-duty cartridges, and we schedule around your busy hours so the showers stay open when people need them. We stock tamper-resistant heads. Every repair is set to scald-limit code.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Olympia

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. Water that seeps from the arm connection runs back along the pipe into the wall, and you only find out when the paint on the other side starts to bubble. We reseal the threads. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Olympia, WA

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. We test both sides. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Olympia

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. The fix is in the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Olympia, WA

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. We replace the spout or stem. It is a short job.
