    Backup Pump Battery Service in Olympia

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. The backup takes over when the primary loses power or simply stops. Our plumber load tests the battery, tests the charger and alarm, then runs the backup alone with the primary unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Olympia

    Brand matters less than people think, but parts do not cross over cleanly between Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and Little Giant. We match the float and seals to the model number on the label. A submersible Zoeller and a pedestal Wayne need different checks. We know both.

    Wayne Sump Pump Service in Olympia, WA

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. We check that intake and the float mechanism for buildup. Loose discharge fittings show up on every brand. Easy fix.

    Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in Olympia

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, leaves and gravel settle at the bottom of the basin. Sooner or later the pump sucks it into the screen and locks up the impeller.     Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Olympia, WA

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Each start heats the motor windings.
