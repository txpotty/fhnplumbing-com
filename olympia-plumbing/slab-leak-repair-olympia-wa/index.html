    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Olympia, WA

    Two or three slab leaks in a few years point to the pipe, not bad luck. A repipe may be cheaper long term. PEX bends with the structure, won't corrode and runs overhead, so a whole-house repipe leaves nothing under the slab. It suits many Olympia homes.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Olympia, WA

    Spot repairs fit leaks in otherwise good pipe. It is a single small opening. The floor gets opened, the pipe replaced and tested, and the trench compacted and patched, ready for your flooring contractor.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Olympia

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. Rerouting skips it. We abandon the leaking run in place and install new pipe through walls or the attic, so the problem section is gone from service and the water travels through fresh line that you can reach later. Olympia homes with original plumbing are good candidates.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Olympia, WA

    Direct repair is often the most efficient fix for a single leak. We cut a small square. Once the pipe is exposed, we replace the bad section, test the line and backfill in compacted lifts before patching. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.
