    Grease Line Jetting in Olympia

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. It builds up in rings, thickest at the bottom of the pipe. A snake cuts a narrow path through and the line plugs again within weeks, while a jetter with a rotating nozzle peels the grease off the whole pipe wall. We run a camera after to confirm.

    Residential Grease Trap Cleaning in Olympia, WA

    Homes rarely have a grease trap, though a few large custom kitchens and some home-based catering setups in Olympia, WA put a small unit under the sink. Most are 10 to 20 gallon boxes.     Odor Control and Trap Inspections in Olympia

    A trap that stinks between pump-outs usually has a flattened lid gasket or a vent problem. We check both on every visit. A gasket costs little to replace, while a missing or broken vent can let sewer gas into the kitchen and also slow the trap's drainage, so that one is worth tracking down. Odors are often fixed the same day.

    Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Olympia, WA

    In-ground interceptors need a vacuum truck and usually a pressure wand to break up the cap. We open every compartment and pump each one to the floor. While the tank is empty, we look at the inlet and outlet tees, the baffle wall, the risers and the lid seals, because a cracked riser lets groundwater and dirt in. Lids go back on tight.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Cleaning in Olympia

    Gravity interceptors in the parking lot often hold 750 to 2,000 gallons across two or three compartments. We pump each compartment on its own. On big tanks we break the grease cap with a pressure wand first, since a cap that has set up hard will plug the suction hose and leave half the grease behind. No one goes inside without confined-space gear.
