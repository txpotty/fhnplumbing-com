    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Olympia

    Well pump replacement usually costs $1,800 to $4,500 depending on well depth and pump size. Deep wells or constant pressure systems can cost $4,000 to $8,000.

    Well pump replacement is a complex job involving electrical wiring, heavy lifting and specialized tools, especially for submersible pumps. It is strongly recommended to hire a professional plumber for safety, proper installation and compliance with all codes, avoiding costly mistakes.

    New well pumps typically come with a manufacturer's warranty, usually ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. FHN Plumbing also stands behind our installation work with our own labor warranty. We will discuss warranty details for your chosen pump during the quoting process.

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up from shallower wells, usually less than 25 feet. Submersible pumps are placed directly inside the well casing, submerged in water, and are used for deeper wells, pushing water up to the surface. Each has specific applications and benefits.

    The lifespan of a well pump varies widely depending on usage, water quality and pump type, but most last between 10 and 20 years. Regular maintenance and proper sizing can extend its life, but eventually wear and tear will call for replacement.

    Low pressure, sputtering faucets, no water and high electric bills can point to pump trouble. Frequent cycling is another warning sign.

    FHN Plumbing installs and services a wide range of trusted well pump brands known for their durability and performance. These commonly include Grundfos, Franklin Electric, Goulds Pumps and Sta-Rite. We can recommend the best brand and model to suit your well's requirements.

    In many areas, a plumbing or well permit is required for well pump replacement to confirm compliance with local building and health codes. FHN Plumbing handles all required permitting on your behalf, so your installation is fully compliant and safe.
