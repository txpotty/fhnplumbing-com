    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Olympia

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged. Every repair gets a soap test and a system pressure test before we leave.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Olympia

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Glacial till and dense hardpan are common in western Washington, and they drain poorly enough to keep water against foundations and in crawlspaces. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Olympia, WA

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. We install the branch line, add an accessible shutoff valve and a sediment trap where the code calls for one, then connect the appliance with a new coated flexible connector. Leaking existing lines get repaired during the same trip.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Olympia

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Olympia building officials. We pull the permit, book the inspection and meet the inspector at the gauge so you do not have to. Inspection usually happens within a few days. Once it passes, the utility can restore service and we come back to relight appliances if needed.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Olympia, WA

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. We reseal or replace the bad sections. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Olympia

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. New coated stainless connectors go on, sized to the appliance, with a reachable shutoff valve if the old one is stuck or missing. Usually under an hour per appliance. Ranges, dryers, water heaters and furnaces all need proper connections, and most also need a sediment trap ahead of the appliance gas valve.

    Cost of Gas Line Repair in Olympia, WA

    Gas line repair prices in Olympia, WA depend on where the leak is and how much pipe has to change. Tightening or replacing a single fitting or connector is usually a few hundred dollars. Replacing a buried line that needs trenching across the yard can run $1,000 to $5,000 or more, depending on length, digging conditions and whether the driveway or a patio is in the way.     Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Olympia, WA

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. We size outdoor lines for the full load. Repairs and new runs are tested, buried at code depth and marked with tracer wire.
