    Upsizing Water Lines for Better Pressure in Olympia, WA

    Commercial Water Service Installation in Olympia

    Restaurants, retail suites and small commercial buildings need service lines sized for higher demand and often require backflow protection at the meter.     New Construction Water Service Lines in Olympia

    New homes, guest houses and additions need a correctly sized service line from the meter to the building. We review the plans, confirm meter size with your water provider and choose a line diameter that matches the fixture load. Most homes need a 3/4 inch or 1 inch line, while larger homes with pools and big irrigation systems often benefit from 1 1/4 inch. We coordinate the rough-in timing with your builder so the line is ready when the inspector arrives.

    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Olympia, WA

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and holds up against cracking in expansive clay. We use approved compression or fusion fittings and add tracer wire so the line can be found with a locator later. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it with care on every job site.

    Water Line Installation Cost Comparison in Olympia, WA

    Cost depends largely on run length, soil conditions and pipe material. A short PEX run through soft soil is the most affordable option, while long copper runs through rocky ground cost more in both labor and materials. Trenchless methods cost more per foot but can save thousands in concrete and landscape repair. We provide a written quote that itemizes trenching, pipe, fittings, permits and restoration.
