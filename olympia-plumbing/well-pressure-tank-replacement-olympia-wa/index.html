    What Goes Into a Well Tank Swap in Olympia, WA

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Olympia

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. Fittings tend to line up. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, since many original tanks were undersized from the start. Check before swapping.

    Whole-House Tank Replacement on Older Wells in Olympia

    Older wells sometimes have a tank, switch and plumbing that were all installed together decades ago. A new tank can reveal tired fittings. We look at the whole tank area, from the check valve to the house shutoff, so you can choose a tank-only swap or a fuller refresh. Your call.

    Cost-Effective Well Pressure Tank Upgrades in Olympia, WA

    Upgrading a size often costs less than people think. Going one size larger adds only a little. A larger tank means fewer pump starts per day, and fewer starts is the biggest single thing you can do for pump life, so we often recommend it during a replacement.

    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in Olympia

    Drawdown is the usable water between cut-out and cut-in. Small tanks give surprisingly little. We divide pump flow by drawdown to see how many starts per hour the pump will face, aiming for at least one minute of run time per cycle. That often points to a bigger tank.

    Pressure Tanks for Well Houses and Barns in Olympia, WA

    Tanks in outbuildings face heat, cold and damp. Moisture eats steel tanks from the bottom. We often recommend a composite tank in those spaces, raise it off the floor on a stand, while protecting the switch and pipes from freezing. Simple protection.

    Galvanized Steel Pressure Tank Upgrades in Olympia

    Old galvanized tanks have no bladder at all. Air and water share the same space. Over time the water absorbs the air, the tank waterlogs, and someone has to drain and recharge it every few months. A sealed bladder tank ends that chore.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Olympia

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Threads on old tees weep. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, so we replace a crusted or corroded one rather than trust it. Whole set, one visit.

    Commercial Well Pressure Tank Services in Olympia, WA

    Commercial wells often run several tanks in parallel. Some run four or more on a manifold. We check each tank's pre-charge separately, since one bad tank in a bank can still cause short cycling, while planning the work so the building keeps water. Larger tanks need two people.
