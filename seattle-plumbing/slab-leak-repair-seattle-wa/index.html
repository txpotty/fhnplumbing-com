    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Seattle, WA

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most efficient repair method. This involves carefully cutting a small section of your concrete slab to access and repair or replace the damaged pipe section. Our team makes sure the repair is fully compliant with Washington plumbing codes and the slab is properly restored, preventing future issues. We take real care to contain dust and debris during this process in your Seattle home.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Seattle, WA

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel. Mineral scale and constant recirculation can wear the pipe wall faster. We repair the leak or reroute the hot line, and we can adjust recirculation pumps to reduce wear. Warm floors and higher gas or electric bills are common signs.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Seattle

    Locating a hidden slab leak requires specialized tools. Our plumbers use sensitive electronic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to pinpoint the exact location of the leak under your concrete foundation. This precise detection reduces the need for extensive concrete removal, saving you time and money. We can detect leaks in both hot and cold water lines, as well as sewer lines, for a complete diagnosis of your Seattle home.

    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Seattle, WA

    Whether your home in Seattle, WA has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our plumbers are proficient in repairing both. Pinhole leaks are common in older copper due to corrosive soil conditions and water chemistry, while PEX can sometimes suffer from installation issues or rodent damage. We carry a full range of parts and tools for both materials, ensuring a correct and durable repair no matter your pipe type.

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in Seattle

    Our approach to effective slab leak repair includes investing in modern diagnostic technology. We use equipment such as thermal imaging cameras to detect temperature changes caused by leaking hot water lines, and very sensitive acoustic sensors to listen for the sound of escaping water. This technology allows us to locate leaks without guesswork, even in the varied mix of buildings across Seattle.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Seattle, WA

    When the leak is isolated and the rest of the pipe is in good shape, a spot repair through the slab is a sensible option. We cut a small opening, dig down to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The trench is compacted and the concrete is patched. Flooring is then restored by your preferred contractor.
