    Commercial Slab Leak Repair in Seattle, WA

    Commercial slabs in Seattle carry longer pipe runs and more branches. It means more line to test. Branches get isolated one by one, and the cutting is booked after closing or on a weekend so customers never notice. Work meets Washington code and stays safe and compliant.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Seattle

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. A reroute bypasses it. We abandon the leaking run in place and install new pipe through walls or the attic, so the problem section is gone from service and the water travels through fresh line that you can reach later. Seattle homes with original plumbing are good candidates.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Seattle, WA

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Heat is the reason. Each time hot water runs, the pipe expands against concrete and gravel, and recirculation pumps keep that cycle going all day, which grinds the pipe wall thin at the rub points. We repair or reroute the line and can adjust the pump to cut wear.

    Thermal and Acoustic Tools in Seattle

    A thermal camera shows the warm streak a hot water leak leaves on the floor. Cold leaks need the mic. For those, an acoustic sensor listens through the concrete for the sound of water escaping, and we mark the spot where it peaks, whether the house is an older ranch or a newer two-story. Both tools ride on every Seattle truck.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Seattle, WA

    Leaks under a load-bearing wall or a kitchen island are hard to reach from inside. Tunneling solves that. From a pit by the foundation, the crew hand-digs under the slab, shoring as they go, and your floors and cabinets never get touched. Many Seattle, WA homes are good candidates.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Seattle, WA

    Direct repair is often the most efficient fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. Once the pipe is exposed, we replace the bad section, test the line and backfill in compacted lifts before patching. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Seattle

    Locating comes before cutting. We use a ground mic and a thermal camera. The mic picks up the hiss of water escaping through concrete, and the camera shows warm water spreading from a hot line, so together they narrow the leak to a spot about a foot across. We test hot lines, cold lines and drains in your Seattle home.
