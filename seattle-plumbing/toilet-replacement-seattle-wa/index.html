    Choosing a Toilet Brand in Seattle, WA

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield all make solid toilets, and each line has a few models we steer people away from because of weak flushes or hard-to-find parts. You get our honest take on which models to skip. What fits your bathroom comes down to rough-in and bowl shape, and we check both before you buy.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Seattle

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips. No plumbing changes are needed. We often pair it with an elongated bowl and a soft-close seat, and the swap takes the same couple of hours as any other.

    Emergency Toilet Replacement in Seattle

    A toilet that cracked overnight and is still seeping into the floor cannot wait for next week, so we bring a standard round and elongated model on emergency calls. Shut the angle stop first. Most of the time the new toilet goes in that same day, and we check the subfloor for soaked plywood before setting it.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Seattle, WA

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Take the measurement from the wall to the closet bolts, not from the baseboard trim. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. An offset flange can correct an inch or two without moving the drain.

    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Seattle

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in. You can pick battery or hardwired sensors. Work can happen after hours, and we check that the new fixtures meet accessibility requirements.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Seattle

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. Some carriers need new bolts. The new bowl gets matched to the carrier already in the wall whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    Toilet Brands We Replace in Seattle

    Brand matters less than model, because every major maker sells a great toilet and a mediocre one at nearly the same price. We look at the flush valve size and MaP score. Parts availability counts too. Proprietary flappers and fill valves mean that ten years from now, a simple fix becomes a special order.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Seattle, WA

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are loud. Replacement means matching the vessel brand for parts later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines.
