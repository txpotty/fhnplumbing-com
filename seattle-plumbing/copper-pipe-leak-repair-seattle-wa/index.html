    Underground Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Seattle

    Buried copper service lines can leak for months before anyone notices. The meter gives it away. We locate the leak with acoustic gear and tracer gas, and either spot repair it or swap in a new service line with minimal digging. We test before backfill.

    Commercial Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Seattle

    Commercial buildings often run larger diameter copper for domestic water. Shutdowns cost money. We repair copper supply lines in offices, restaurants and apartment buildings, using press tools on large lines to shorten the shutdown and skip the torch. We schedule around business hours.

    Under Slab Copper Leak Repair in Seattle, WA

    Copper lines under a slab can wear through where they rub concrete or gravel. A hot spot underfoot is common. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests and locate the leak with acoustic and thermal equipment, then choose between a small slab cut at the leak and rerouting the line through the walls or attic. Both options get a price.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Seattle

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot water speeds it up. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with Type L copper or a press fitting. More pinholes often follow.

    Copper Fitting and Valve Repair in Seattle

    Angle stops, shutoff valves and copper fittings under sinks and behind toilets can weep or seize. Scale locks them up. Hard water buildup inside a valve can keep it from closing when you need it most, and quarter-turn valves go in to replace the old multi-turn stops. Every valve gets checked.

    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Seattle, WA

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and covering hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. Shut the main first. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many bursts are found hours after the cold. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Insulation goes on after.

    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Seattle, WA

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Temperature drives it. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, so we repair leaking hot lines and can add a timer or adjust the pump to cut down on wear. It slows the next pinhole.

    Preventive Copper Pipe Maintenance in Seattle, WA

    Copper gives warning signs before it leaks. They are easy to overlook. Green crust at fittings, a stain on a basement ceiling and high static pressure all point to trouble ahead, and a walkthrough of exposed copper in the basement, crawlspace or utility room catches many of them. A pressure valve can slow it down.
