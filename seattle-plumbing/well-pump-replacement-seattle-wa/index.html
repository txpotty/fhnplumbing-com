    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Seattle

    The cost of well pump replacement in Seattle, WA typically ranges from $1,500 to $3,500, though it can vary.     The lifespan of a well pump varies widely depending on usage, water quality and pump type, but most last between 10 and 20 years. Regular maintenance and proper sizing can extend its life, but eventually wear and tear will require replacement.
