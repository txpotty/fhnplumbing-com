    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Seattle

    Traps leak at the joints first. The beveled washers inside the slip nuts flatten and harden over the years, and a trap that has been taken apart several times to clear clogs often has stripped plastic threads or a cracked nut. A new trap kit costs little. Labor is most of the bill.

    Kitchen Drain and Disposal Tie-In in Seattle

    Disposals change the kitchen drain. The outlet on the disposal has to sit above the trap inlet, the dishwasher hose needs a high loop or an air gap, and the trap has to line up without the slip joints pulling sideways. We set them all at once. Then the disposal runs with water while we watch.

    Corroded Drain Replacement in Seattle, WA

    Drain cleaners speed up corrosion. Caustic products sit in the bottom of a metal trap between uses and eat at the chrome plating and the thin brass underneath, which is why so many old traps we pull have pinholes right at the bottom of the bend. Plastic traps resist that damage. We recommend it for most cabinets.

    Commercial Lavatory Drain Replacement in Seattle
