    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Seattle

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in homes built before the 1970s. Over time, it corrodes inside and narrows the pipe. This causes low pressure and discolored water. We replace galvanized lines with modern PEX or copper. The difference in water pressure is often noticeable right away.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Seattle

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a popular and budget-friendly choice for water line replacement in Seattle. It's flexible, resistant to freezing, and less prone to scaling and corrosion than some traditional materials. Its flexibility allows for easier installation, often requiring fewer connections, which reduces the potential for leaks. PEX is also a good option for homes with corrosive soil conditions, offering a reliable alternative to metal pipes.

    Traditional Water Line Excavation in Seattle
