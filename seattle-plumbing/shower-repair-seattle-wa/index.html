    Shower Temperature Problem Repair in Seattle

    If your shower turns cold when someone flushes, the pressure-balance spool may be stuck. If you cannot get enough hot water, the anti-scald limit stop may be set too low or the cartridge may be worn. We repair or replace pressure-balance and thermostatic valves and adjust limit stops. Your shower will hold a steady, consistent temperature.

    Leaking Shower Valve Repair in Seattle

    A shower that drips after it is turned off usually has a worn cartridge or seals. Hard water scale scratches cartridge surfaces and breaks down O-rings faster than soft water does. We replace the cartridge with a genuine part and lubricate the valve with silicone grease. This stops the drip and makes the handle turn smoothly again.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Seattle, WA

    Leaks under the shower pan or behind tile can cause rot, mold and stains on ceilings below. We use moisture meters and flood tests to find failed grout, cracked pans or leaking pipe joints. Then we repair the source, whether that means resealing a drain, replacing a valve or rebuilding a pan. On slab homes, these leaks can damage flooring in nearby rooms.

    Emergency Shower Leak Repair in Seattle

    A severe shower leak can quickly cause extensive water damage to walls, floors, and ceilings. If you have a sudden, uncontrollable shower leak in your Seattle home, call right away. Our emergency plumbing team is available to respond quickly, locate the source of the leak, and perform immediate repairs to prevent further damage.     Clogged Shower Drain Solutions in Seattle, WA

    Slow or standing water in your shower is a clear sign of a clogged drain. These clogs are typically caused by a buildup of hair, soap scum, and sometimes mineral deposits. Our team uses professional drain snakes and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the most stubborn blockages without damaging your pipes. We also offer advice on preventive measures to keep your shower drains flowing freely.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Seattle, WA

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This restores full flow to the showerhead.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Seattle, WA

    Weak shower pressure is often caused by mineral buildup in the showerhead or flow restrictor. It can also come from a clogged cartridge or a failing pressure regulator. We clean or replace the showerhead, check the cartridge and test whole-home pressure when needed. Most low-pressure issues are fixed quickly.
