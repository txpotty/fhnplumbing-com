    Proven Walls Waterproofing Solutions in Seattle, WA

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Seattle, WA

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already passed through the foundation walls. This typically involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Seattle

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Steep slopes and wet soils can move over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also added to divert water away.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Seattle

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra attention before new tile is set.

    Drainage System Integration in Seattle, WA

    Effective walls waterproofing often requires a robust drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Seattle, WA properties, so water is managed well and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Seattle

    Housing in Washington ranges from early 1900s homes to new construction, with plumbing materials that change a lot from one decade to the next. Wherever a home has a basement or below-grade walls, heavy rain and snowmelt can push water through foundation walls and cracks. We seal cracks with injection products, apply interior or exterior waterproofing and install drainage where needed. A sump pump may be added if groundwater builds up.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Seattle

    A slow drip from a shower valve, supply stub or drain fitting can soak a wall for months before you notice. We locate the leak with moisture meters, thermal imaging and pressure tests. The damaged section of pipe or valve is replaced, and wet drywall is cut out and dried. Once the cavity is dry, we restore the wall and seal any wet-area surfaces.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Seattle

    Even minor cracks in foundation walls can allow significant water intrusion. Our services include crack repair using epoxy or polyurethane injection to seal these entry points permanently. This method fills the entire depth of the crack, restoring strength and preventing water penetration. We address both vertical and horizontal cracks, knowing that horizontal cracks can signal structural issues that need a thorough assessment before sealing. Addressing these early prevents further water damage and expensive future repairs.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Seattle, WA

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to verify the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test proves the pan is watertight before tile.

    When professionally installed using high-quality materials, walls waterproofing can last for decades, often 20 to 50 years or even longer.     Damp proofing is a less durable solution, typically a spray-on asphalt coating that resists soil moisture but isn't designed to withstand hydrostatic pressure.
