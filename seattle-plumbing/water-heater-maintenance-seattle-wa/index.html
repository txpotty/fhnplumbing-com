    Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Seattle

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Many rural Washington homes rely on private wells, and some carry iron, manganese or other minerals that call for testing and treatment. We run a hose from the drain valve, open it up and let the tank drain until the water runs clear, then refill and check the anode rod, the relief valve and every connection for drips or corrosion. Takes about an hour. A clean tank heats faster and lasts longer.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Seattle

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Yellow or lazy flames mean poor combustion, so we clean the burner and combustion chamber, check the thermocouple or flame sensor, and confirm the vent has a proper draft and tight connections. Bad venting is dangerous. We test the gas connection too.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Seattle, WA

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We test the valve, confirm it reseats and check that the discharge pipe is routed correctly. We swap bad ones. We check the expansion tank too.

    Businesses cannot run without hot water.     Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Seattle, WA

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. We check for gas leaks at the valve and connector, watch the flame, clean the burner and confirm the flue pulls exhaust up and out instead of spilling it into the room. Carbon monoxide is the concern. We also test the thermocouple.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Seattle, WA

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. We also check the igniter, the fan and the exhaust vent for safe operation.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Seattle

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Water chemistry can wear a rod out in a few years, so we check it every time. We swap worn ones. It keeps rust off the tank lining.
