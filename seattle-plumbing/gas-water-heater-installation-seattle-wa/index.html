    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Seattle

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. Each install gets a new gas connector and drip leg, a vent connector pitched upward and a relief line piped where code allows. We strap it if code calls for it. Your household sets the size.

    Proper Sizing and Venting in Seattle, WA

    Sizing and venting decide whether a gas heater runs safe and efficient. We figure your household peak demand from bathrooms and people, then make sure the vent diameter, rise and total length match the manufacturer table and local code. Undersized vents spill CO. Draft gets tested on every job.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Seattle, WA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. That means the heater can go in a spot with no chimney or vertical flue, and the vent can run sideways out an exterior wall as long as it stays within the length limit. A 120-volt outlet is required. Termination clearances follow the manual.

    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Seattle

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. Most conversions need a bigger gas line from the meter, a new vent through the wall or roof, a condensate drain and isolation valves for future descaling. It takes most of a day. Our licensed plumbers handle all of it to code.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Seattle

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Most run without electricity. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.

    Brands We Install in Seattle

    Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith, Navien and Rinnai make up most of the gas heaters we install in Seattle homes and businesses. We compare warranties, recovery and venting across brands so the choice is easy. Budget matters too. We install all of them.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Seattle, WA

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Because the exhaust comes out cool and wet, they vent through PVC and need a condensate drain, which changes where the heater can go and what the install involves. Energy Star models qualify for some rebates. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.
