    Leaking Toilet Repair in Seattle

    Toilet leaks can cause significant water damage to your bathroom floor, subfloor, and even the ceiling below. Leaks can start from the base of the toilet, the tank, or the supply line. Common culprits include a worn-out wax ring, loose bolts, or cracked porcelain. We carefully inspect all potential leak points, replacing seals, tightening connections, or recommending bowl replacement if the porcelain is damaged, preventing further water intrusion.

    Running Toilet Fixes in Seattle

    A toilet that constantly runs or refills itself intermittently is not only annoying but also a major water waste. This issue in Seattle is usually caused by a faulty flapper valve, an improperly adjusted float, a worn-out fill valve, or sediment buildup. Our technicians diagnose the specific component causing the problem and replace it with durable, long-lasting parts. We ensure proper alignment and adjustment of all internal mechanisms to restore your toilet's efficient operation and stop wasted water consumption.

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Seattle

    Pressure-assist toilets from Sloan Flushmate and dual-flush models from TOTO and American Standard use parts that hardware stores seldom stock. We carry cartridges, flush buttons and seals for the common models and can order specialty parts quickly. These systems need correct pressure settings to work well, so we test incoming water pressure as part of the repair.

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Seattle, WA

    If your toilet needs two flushes or clogs often, the problem could be scale in the rim jets, a partial blockage in the trapway or a flush valve that does not open fully. We auger the trap, check the vent if gurgling is present and clean the jets with a descaling treatment. Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you plainly if replacement is the better fix.

    Running Toilet Repair in Seattle

    A running toilet is not just annoying, it can waste hundreds of gallons of water a day, leading to much higher water bills. This common issue is usually caused by a faulty flapper, a misaligned fill valve, or an improperly adjusted float. Our plumbers quickly identify the exact cause, whether it's mineral buildup from hard water affecting the flapper seal or a worn-out component, and replace the necessary parts to stop the continuous flow and restore efficiency.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Seattle

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank usually points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet much quieter.

    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Seattle

    Cracks in a toilet tank or bowl in Seattle can lead to significant water leakage and require immediate attention. While small hairline cracks might be temporarily sealed, larger or structural cracks often necessitate replacement of the affected component. Our technicians assess the severity of the crack and advise on the most cost-effective and durable solution. We can perform careful tank or bowl replacements, ensuring a watertight seal and proper function, preventing further water damage to your flooring or subfloor.
