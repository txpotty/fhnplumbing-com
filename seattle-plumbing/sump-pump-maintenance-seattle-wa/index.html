    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Seattle

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Seattle

    River flooding and saturated ground can push water back through sewer lines, so backwater valves and sump pumps are worth considering. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Seattle, WA

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Many homes with crawlspaces or deep window wells use small pumps to clear runoff.     Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Seattle

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements.     Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Seattle, WA

We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil near the slab or foundation.

    We recommend having your sump pump professionally maintained at least once a year. If your home is in an area prone to heavy rainfall or has a high water table, twice-yearly maintenance might be more beneficial. Regular checks confirm it's ready for peak seasons.
