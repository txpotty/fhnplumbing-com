    Identifying Signs of Sewer Line Clogs in Seattle, WA

    Several drains backing up at once is the main sign. Listen for gurgling. Water coming up through a drain when you flush, a sewage smell in the yard or basement and slow drains throughout the house all point to a sewer line that needs cleaning.

    Pre-Purchase Sewer Line Cleaning and Inspection in Seattle, WA

    Most home inspections skip the sewer line. It can be costly. After cleaning, we record the entire lateral and write up the findings, which helps buyers and sellers alike.

    Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Seattle, WA

    Sewer backups pick bad times. Holidays are common. An after-hours tech arrives ready to clear the line and start cleanup, so the damage stops spreading.

    Residential Sewer Line Cleaning in Seattle

    Household laterals collect grease and hair. It takes months to close a line. Over time, grease and hair coat the pipe and catch more debris, especially in older cast iron with a rough inner wall, and we clear it with a cable or a jetter sized for the pipe.

    Preventative Sewer Line Maintenance in Seattle

    Scheduled cleaning beats emergency calls. Roots grow back. Properties with older plumbing or nearby trees do well with periodic cleaning, often jetting, which clears buildup before it turns into a full blockage and extends the life of the line.

    Roots in Clay and Cast Iron in Seattle

    Older clay and cast iron invite roots. Joints are the weak spot. We clear root growth with cutters and jetting and talk about lining or repair to stop the next round.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Seattle, WA

    Jetting cleans the pipe wall. A snake punches a hole. High-pressure water strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes them toward the street, and with the right settings it is safe for PVC and older clay alike.

    Sewer Line Cleaning for Rentals and HOAs in Seattle

    Rentals see more clogs from wipes and grease. Turnover adds to it. We offer scheduled cleanings for landlords and property managers, and for HOAs we clean shared lines and document each building so the budget can be planned, coordinating access with tenants.
