    What Our Gas Line Plumbers Fix in Seattle, WA

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Seattle

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. We shut off every appliance and watch the meter's test dial, then close branch valves one at a time to find which section is leaking. An electronic detector does the rest. A spray of soap solution on the fitting shows bubbles at the leak, and we fix that joint or section and retest the whole system.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Seattle, WA

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. A pool heater can pull 250,000 BTU or more, which can rob gas from the furnace and water heater if the line was sized for a grill. Sizing is the first thing we check. Repairs and new runs are tested, buried at code depth and marked with tracer wire.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Seattle, WA

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. We install the branch line, add an accessible shutoff valve and a sediment trap where the code calls for one, then connect the appliance with a new coated flexible connector. If the existing appliance line is leaking or damaged, we fix it on the same visit.

    CSST Repair in Seattle

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Common damage comes from nails, rodents or lightning, and repairs require cutting out the bad section and using fittings from the same manufacturer. Bonding gets checked on every system. A bonding clamp and conductor tie the CSST to the home's grounding system, following the maker's instructions and code.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Seattle, WA

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Vibration and settling loosen threaded joints over time, and pipe in damp garages or outside walls can rust through from the surface. We reseal or replace the bad sections. Rusted pipe that runs outside gets painted or replaced with coated steel so it does not come back as the same problem.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Seattle, WA

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. Many tankless heaters are rated at 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, which can be more than double what an old tank heater used, so the line feeding it often has to be upsized back toward the meter. We calculate the full load first. The new pipe gets sized so every burner in the house still has full pressure at once.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Seattle

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. Our plumbers reseal or replace the leaking fittings and sections using new pipe and approved sealant. Rusty runs get swapped out. Pipe in a garage, crawlspace or exterior wall that shows heavy rust gets replaced before it turns into a leak, and the whole system gets a pressure test after the repair.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Seattle

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Seattle building officials. We pull the permit, book the inspection and meet the inspector at the gauge so you do not have to. Inspectors often come within the week. Once it passes, the utility can restore service and we come back to relight appliances if needed.
