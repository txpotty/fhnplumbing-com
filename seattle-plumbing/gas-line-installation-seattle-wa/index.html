    Reliable Gas Line Installation Services in Seattle, WA

    Pool and Spa Heater Gas Lines in Seattle

    Pool heaters are among the largest gas appliances in a home, often rated at 250,000 to 400,000 BTU. They usually sit at the equipment pad far from the meter, so line size and length are crucial. We may recommend a larger main line or a meter upgrade from your gas utility. A properly sized line lets the heater reach its rated output and heat the water efficiently.

    New Gas Appliance Hookups in Seattle

    Adding a new gas range, dryer or water heater requires a dedicated gas line. Our plumbers can install new black iron pipe or corrugated stainless steel tubing, known as CSST, from your main gas supply to your new appliance. We confirm proper pipe sizing for adequate gas flow and perform all necessary connections and pressure tests for a safe, functional setup in your Seattle home.

    Flexible Gas Line (CSST) Installation in Seattle, WA

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing offers a flexible, practical option for gas line installation. FHN Plumbing is experienced with installing CSST, which can simplify routing and reduce the number of fittings compared to traditional black iron pipe. We ensure CSST is properly bonded and grounded, as Washington code requires, to protect against lightning and electrical surges in your Seattle home.

    Gas Range and Cooktop Line Installation in Seattle

    Switching from an electric range to gas is one of the most frequent reasons homeowners call us. We run a dedicated branch from the nearest adequate supply, install a shutoff valve behind the range and connect with a new approved flexible connector. Professional-style ranges can need 60,000 BTU or more, so we confirm the branch and main can handle the load. The appliance is then checked for proper burner flame and ignition.

    Gas Water Heater and Tankless Line Upgrades in Seattle, WA

    Tankless water heaters often need up to 199,000 BTU, far more than the 40,000 BTU a typical tank heater uses. Many existing 1/2 inch lines cannot supply that demand, which causes error codes and lukewarm water. We calculate the full house load and upsize the line to 3/4 inch or larger as needed. This upgrade is one of the most critical steps in a successful tankless conversion.

    CSST Gas Line Installation in Seattle, WA

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing offers flexibility and can be installed with fewer fittings, usually reducing installation time. Our team is certified in CSST installation, including the bonding and grounding code requires. CSST is a common choice for new gas lines or extensions because it routes easily and tolerates building movement, making it a viable option for many Seattle, WA homes.

    Commercial Gas Line Installation in Seattle

    Restaurants, laundromats and light industrial spaces need gas piping sized for high-demand equipment like fryers, ovens, dryers and rooftop units. We install schedule 40 black iron or approved commercial systems, add regulators where elevated pressure is supplied and coordinate inspections. Work can be scheduled outside business hours to prevent downtime. Final documentation is provided for your landlord, insurer or fire marshal.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Line Installation in Seattle

    The cost for gas line installation in Seattle usually ranges from $500 to $2,500, depending on the length of the run, the pipe material, black iron or CSST, and the complexity of the installation.     Gas line installation is extremely dangerous and should only be performed by licensed professionals. Improper installation can lead to gas leaks, explosions, fires and carbon monoxide poisoning. It also voids warranties and often fails local inspections, so it is never a DIY project.
