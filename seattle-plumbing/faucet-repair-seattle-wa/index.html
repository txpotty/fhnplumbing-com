    Ball Faucet Repair in Seattle, WA

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's likely time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are usually the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Seattle, WA

    Beyond standard kitchen and bathroom fixtures, we handle repairs for specialty faucets such as pot fillers, utility sink faucets, and outdoor spigots found on many homes. These often require specific tools and knowledge due to their unique designs and usage. Whether it's a garden hose bib that won't stop dripping or a filtered water faucet under your sink, FHN Plumbing has the expertise to diagnose and repair it correctly.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Seattle

    Compression faucets, often found in older Seattle homes, are known for their separate hot and cold handles. When these faucets leak, it's typically due to worn-out rubber washers or packing. Our team is skilled in disassembling these classic fixtures, replacing the necessary seals and washers, and reassembling them to stop leaks effectively. We respect the character of older homes and make sure these repairs are done carefully.

    Leaky Faucet Repair in Seattle

    A dripping faucet is more than just an annoyance, it's a waste of water and money. Fixing a leak promptly is one of the simplest ways to conserve water at home. Our plumbers can identify the cause, whether it's a worn-out washer, O-ring, or a corroded valve, and replace the faulty components. We carry a wide range of common parts so most leaky faucet repairs can be completed in a single visit, restoring your peace and quiet.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Seattle, WA

    A cartridge repair often costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life. However, if the faucet body is cracked, parts are discontinued or it has needed several repairs, replacement may be better value. We give you both prices so you can choose. Many brands like Moen and Delta offer lifetime warranties on cartridges, which can reduce your repair cost.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Seattle

    That constant drip, drip, drip isn't just annoying, it's a sign of a deeper problem. Most dripping faucets are caused by internal component wear, like a deteriorated rubber washer or a cracked cartridge. Our team provides precise solutions, replacing the specific parts that are failing. We work with all types of faucets, including compression, cartridge, disc, and ball varieties, ensuring a quiet and drip-free operation once again.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Seattle, WA

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base points to failed O-rings or a loose mounting nut. If left alone, the leak can soak into the cabinet below and damage the sink base. We replace O-rings, tighten mounting hardware and reseal the base. We also check the deck for water stains.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Seattle

    Weak or sputtering flow is often caused by mineral buildup in the aerator or cartridge. We remove and clean or replace the aerator, flush the lines and check the cartridge for scale blockage. On pull-down faucets, the spray head and check valves can also clog. If flow is low at every fixture, we test whole-house water pressure.
