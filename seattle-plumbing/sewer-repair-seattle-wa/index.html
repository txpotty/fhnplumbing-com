    Sewer Line Backups & Blockages in Seattle

    Sewer backups are usually caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or heavy tree root intrusion. Our team uses advanced sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. We then use hydro-jetting or mechanical snaking to clear the obstruction, restoring proper flow to your system and preventing further damage. This approach is highly effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older Washington homes.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Seattle, WA

    Tree roots are a leading cause of sewer line blockages and damage, especially with the mature trees found in many Seattle neighborhoods. Roots can work into tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog pipes. We use specialized augers and hydro-jetting to cut through and remove roots, followed by inspection to assess pipe integrity. For ongoing issues, we may recommend trenchless repair or pipe replacement to prevent future root problems.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Seattle

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can often use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Sewer repair is complex and best left to licensed professionals due to the specialized equipment required, potential health hazards and strict local plumbing codes. Attempting DIY repairs can lead to further damage, costly mistakes and even safety risks. Always consult with a professional plumber.

    Yes, weather can impact sewer lines. Washington has two very different climates, with long, wet and mild seasons in the west and hot, dry summers and cold winters in the east. Heavy rains can saturate the ground, putting pressure on pipes and widening existing cracks. Dry spells can cause soil to shift, leading to pipe misalignment or breaks.

    Common signs include recurring drain clogs, multiple fixtures backing up, foul odors coming from drains or the yard, gurgling noises from toilets, unusually lush patches of grass in your yard or dips in your lawn.
