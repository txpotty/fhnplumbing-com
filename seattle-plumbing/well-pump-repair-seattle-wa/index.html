    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Seattle, WA

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. A weak motor draws high amps.     Control Box and Electrical Repair in Seattle, WA

    Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. A tripped breaker is often the first sign. With the power locked out, we test and replace the failed parts and tighten every terminal. Wiring meets Washington code before we restart the pump.

    Deep Well Pump Repair in Seattle

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up, run quietly and last, but motors, impellers and splices still wear out.     Pressure Switch Replacement in Seattle

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank, typically 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.
