    Water Leak Repair in Seattle

    Hidden leaks sit inside walls, under sink cabinets and in the buried yard line that runs between the meter and the house. A thermal camera narrows it down. We open just enough drywall or dirt to make the repair. Fixes include pinholed copper, failed PEX crimps, leaking hose bibs and worn shutoff valves.

    Sewer Pipe Repair in Seattle, WA

    Clay and Orangeburg laterals crack and separate as the ground shifts, and roots find those gaps within a season or two. A camera and locator mark exactly where and how deep the break sits. Repair options run from a traditional dig to pipe bursting or a cured-in-place liner. We camera it again before backfilling.

    Well Pump Service in Seattle, WA

    Submersible pumps sit deep in the casing, so diagnosis happens at the surface first. FHN Plumbing checks the pressure switch, the control box capacitor and the tank precharge, then measures amp draw on the motor. High amp draw usually means a worn motor or a seized impeller. Jet pumps are simpler, and a lost prime or a bad foot valve is the usual cause when one stops moving water.

    Drain Line Repair in Seattle

    Slow drains that come back a week after snaking usually mean the pipe itself is damaged. Cast iron that has cracked or a run that sags will hold debris no matter how often it is cleared. We run a camera to find the spot, then replace that section or hydro-jet the line if buildup is the only problem. Grease is the usual buildup.

    Slow Drain Repair in Seattle

    Gurgling toilets and a tub that drains slow often trace to a blocked vent or a sagging drain line. After clearing it, we camera the line and fix whatever is broken. Common fixes include replacing corroded cast iron, cutting out failed ABS joints, rebuilding a broken trap arm and resetting toilets on a new wax ring and flange. No monthly snaking.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Seattle, WA

    A warm spot on tile, a meter that spins with every tap off, or a water bill that jumps for no reason. Those are slab leak signs.     Tank and Tankless Water Heater Repair in Seattle

    Tank and tankless heaters fail in different ways. FHN Plumbing repairs both, across all the major brands, gas and electric. Tank calls usually come down to the thermocouple, the gas valve, an element or the anode. On a tankless unit, the culprit is often scale in the heat exchanger or a dirty inlet filter, so we flush and clean before swapping parts.
