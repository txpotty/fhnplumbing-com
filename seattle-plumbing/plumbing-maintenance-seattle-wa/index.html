    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Seattle

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Seattle

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. We pump descaler through the service valves. We also clean the inlet screen and check venting and gas supply.

    Commercial Plumbing Maintenance in Seattle, WA

    Businesses cannot close for a plumbing failure. Restaurants, offices and retail spaces in Seattle run bigger heaters, more restrooms and grease lines that wear faster and cost more when they fail mid-day. Scheduled visits prevent that. We work around your hours.

    Tank and Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Seattle

    Mineral-heavy water shortens water heater life. We drain sediment, check the anode rod, inspect the burner or elements and confirm the relief valve opens and reseats, whether the unit is a standard tank or a tankless model on the wall. Most failures give warning. We spot the signs early.

    Gas Line and Pipe Material Checks in Seattle, WA

    Gas leaks are the one item we never skip. We check visible gas piping and appliance connectors for corrosion and leaks, confirm the shutoff valves turn, and note any old flexible connectors that should be replaced before they crack. Pipe material matters too. Older pipe types get flagged with repipe choices.
