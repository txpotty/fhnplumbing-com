    PEX and Copper Pipe Repair in Seattle, WA

    PEX and copper fail in different ways. Copper pins.     Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Seattle

    Drain pipe made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. Stains give it away. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet, so we find it, swap the bad length and run water at full volume to prove it. We look at venting as well.

    Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Seattle

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Frozen water cracks it. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many burst pipes are found hours after the cold snap. We repair the split and add insulation. We can add heat cable where needed.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Seattle

    Galvanized steel and old copper both corrode with age. Brown water is a clue. Pressure comes right back.
