    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Richland, WA

    Copper leaks are loud on a ground microphone. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. We handle both every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX top the list of what we find.

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Richland

    Heat and wind can pull a lot of water out of a pool, so it is hard to tell evaporation from a leak. A bucket test settles it. We then pressure test the plumbing lines and dye test around lights, skimmers and return fittings, because a running leak can undermine the soil around the pool.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Richland, WA

    Sprinkler laterals crack and zone valves stick open, and either one can waste thousands of gallons before the bill shows it. Check the yard first. Our tech tests the irrigation system separately from the house, runs each zone and traces buried lines, since sprinkler leaks are the first thing most water departments suggest checking.

    Slab Leak Signs in Richland, WA

    A meter that never rests, cracked tile and warm floor spots are the common signs. So is the sound of water with everything off. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests, then trace and listen through the slab, and once it is located we go over spot repair, reroute and repipe options with prices for each.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Richland, WA

    A cracked drain line leaks wastewater into the soil, which can show up as a sewer smell in the yard, a soggy patch or slow drains. We use a camera for these. The camera goes in at the cleanout and runs to the main, recording cracks, offset joints and root entry in PVC, clay and cast iron, with a locator giving the exact depth and distance.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Richland, WA

    The buried line from your meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. A green stripe in a dry yard is a hint. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, switching to tracer gas on plastic lines that do not carry sound.

    Main Water Line Leak Detection in Richland

    A soft wet patch near the sidewalk or a sinking spot over the line can mean the service pipe is leaking. Old galvanized and copper lines fail most. We map the pipe with a line tracer and listen for the leak with a ground microphone, which keeps the dig to a single hole rather than a trench. Repair options follow.
