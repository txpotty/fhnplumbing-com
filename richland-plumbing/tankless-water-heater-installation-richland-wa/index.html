    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Richland

    Gas tankless units in Richland are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. The catch is gas supply. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or larger. We test the new gas piping before first fire.

    Rinnai, Navien and Noritz Installation in Richland, WA

    We install Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem in Richland, WA homes every week.     Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Richland, WA

    Upfront, a tankless install in Richland, WA costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. Over the long haul, the math narrows. Less standby loss, a life of up to 20 years versus roughly 10 for a tank, and replaceable parts all count over the years. Ask for both prices.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Richland, WA

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Richland, WA homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current. Some models need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, more than a 100-amp panel can usually spare alongside the range and AC. We check the panel first.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Richland, WA

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and parts are easy to get. Their condensing and non-condensing lines both fit different homes, depending on the vent route and the budget you are working with. The non-condensing ones need stainless vent pipe. Condensing units vent in PVC and drain condensate.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Richland

    Conversions in Richland usually take a full day. The old tank gets drained and hauled off, the gas line is upsized and tested, a new vent goes out through a wall or roof, and the unit is mounted, plumbed with isolation valves and fired. You shower that night. The inspector comes after.

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Richland

    Propane homes in Richland are good candidates for tankless, using a model built or converted for propane. Propane runs at a higher pressure than natural gas, so the regulator, gas line sizing and the unit's gas valve all have to match, and the propane supplier may need to change the regulator at the tank. Tank size matters too.
