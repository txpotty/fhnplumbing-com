    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Maintenance in Richland

    A standard sump pump inspection and cleaning typically runs $125 to $250. Replacing a float switch or check valve usually adds $75 to $250 depending on parts.

    Our technicians are experienced with all major sump pump brands, including Zoeller, Wayne, Little Giant, Basement Watchdog, Liberty Pumps and more. We carry common replacement parts for most popular models.

    Most quality pumps last seven to ten years. Pumps that sit dry for long periods can seize sooner, which is why testing matters during dry months.

    Routine maintenance usually does not require a permit. However, if a significant repair involves altering the discharge line or replacing the entire unit, especially if electrical work is involved, a local permit might be necessary. We take care of all permit requirements for you.

    Most visits take 45 minutes to an hour. Replacing a check valve or float on the same visit adds a little time.

    Yes, you can pour a bucket of water into the pit and watch the pump start. A professional visit adds pit cleaning, check valve testing, amperage readings and backup testing.

    Watch out for unusual noises, such as grinding or rattling, that point to debris or a failing motor. If the pump runs constantly, fails to turn on or gives off a foul odor from the pit, it's clearly time for a professional check. Visible rust or corrosion are also red flags.
