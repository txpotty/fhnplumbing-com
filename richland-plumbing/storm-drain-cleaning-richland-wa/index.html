    What Our Richland, WA Storm Drain Techs Clean

    Yard and Area Drain Cleaning in Richland

    Lawn and patio runoff ends up in yard inlets. Gravel and silt follow. We pull the grates, vacuum the inlet box and jet the pipe toward its outlet, restoring the line so it can carry a full storm, and we check that every grate is secure and seated.

    Catch Basin Cleaning in Richland

    Sumps and catch basins trap sediment. Full basins stop working. When a basin fills, sediment flows into the pipes and clogs them, so we vacuum out the basin, remove trash and debris and check the outlet pipe, and we schedule HOAs and commercial lots before and after the rainy season.

    Channel and Trench Drain Cleaning in Richland

    Channel drains catch water at driveways and garage doors. Narrow channels fill fast. We remove the grates, clean the channel and jet the outlet pipe, and cracked or loose grates can be swapped while we are there, which keeps water out of the garage during a downpour.

    Preventative Storm Drain Cleaning in Richland, WA

    Scheduled cleaning beats emergency calls. Debris builds slowly. We clear leaves, silt and sediment on a set schedule throughout the year, which prevents the buildup that leads to flooding during the rainy season and extends the life of the system.

    Storm Drain Video Inspection in Richland

    Buried storm pipe hides its problems. A camera finds them.     Jetting Silt From Storm Pipe in Richland, WA
