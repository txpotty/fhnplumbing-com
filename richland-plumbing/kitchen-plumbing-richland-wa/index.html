    Grease Trap Installation and Cleaning in Richland

    Grease traps are sized by flow, and a three-compartment sink usually needs at least a 20 gallon per minute unit. We set the trap level, install the flow control fitting and vent it so it does not siphon. Cleaning schedules depend on volume. Busy fryer kitchens may need it every few weeks.

    Disposal Jams and Hums in Richland, WA

    Humming with no grinding means the motor is getting power but the plate is stuck. That is often ten minutes. We free the plate from below with a hex key, clear the hopper with pliers, never a hand, and reset the overload before running water through it. No sound at all points us to the switch, the outlet or the breaker.

    Kitchen Drain Clog Removal in Richland, WA

    Water rising in the second bowl when the first drains tells us the clog is past the trap, in the branch line inside the wall. We run a 1/4 or 3/8 inch cable from the cleanout or the trap arm, and on grease-packed lines we follow up with a jetter. Chemical cleaners rarely touch grease. What they do is sit in the trap and weaken the glue joints.
