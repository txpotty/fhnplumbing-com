    Flexible Gas Line (CSST) Installation in Richland, WA

    Flexible tubing simplifies routing in finished homes.     Natural Gas & Propane Line Services in Richland

    Propane runs at a different pressure than natural gas, and the appliances need different orifices. We set the second-stage regulator at the house, size the piping for the lower volume propane needs per BTU, and confirm each appliance is set up for the fuel it will actually burn in Richland. Wrong orifices cause sooting.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Richland

    Every gas appliance we hook up gets a shutoff valve within reach, a sediment trap and a new approved connector. The appliance rating plate tells us how big the branch must be, check it against the manufacturer's inlet pressure, and light the appliance before we leave so you can see a clean blue flame in your Richland home. A lazy yellow flame gets fixed.

    CSST Flexible Gas Piping in Richland, WA

    Heavy, prolonged rain from atmospheric river storms is a major factor in western Washington, raising groundwater and filling crawlspaces and basements. We use 6 AWG copper and a listed clamp at the first fitting, follow the manufacturer's install guide on the rest, and route the tubing through attics and finished walls with fewer joints than iron in Richland, WA.     Gas Line Installation for Generators in Richland

    Standby generators burn a lot of gas when they run.     Gas Line Relocation and Rerouting in Richland

    Kitchen remodels often move the range a few feet, and the gas has to follow. We cap the old line at a fitting, not in the wall, extend the new branch to the new location, and leak test the whole modified section before the cabinets go in at your Richland property. Capped lines get tested too.
