    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Installation in Richland

    Costs depend on the project. A standard tank water heater install usually runs $1,600 to $3,500, a water softener $1,500 to $4,000, and a whole-home PEX repipe $5,000 to $15,000 or more depending on home size.

    Most standard water heater installations, whether tank or tankless, can be completed by FHN Plumbing within 3 to 6 hours. This timeframe can vary based on the complexity of the existing setup, any required modifications to gas or water lines, and if electrical upgrades are needed for electric tankless units.

    Many homes benefit from a softener when the water is hard. Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is generally soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. A softener protects fixtures, appliances and water heaters from scale.

    Installing a water heater or gas line yourself is strongly discouraged due to safety risks and code complexities. Improper installation can lead to gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning, explosions, or severe water damage. These jobs should always be performed by licensed and experienced plumbers, like those at FHN Plumbing, to ensure safety and compliance.

    A water heater or softener install typically takes a few hours. Gas line runs and water line replacements can take a day, while whole-home repipes may take several days.

    A sump pump is designed to remove clear groundwater that collects in a sump pit, typically in a basement or crawl space, to prevent flooding. A sewage ejector pump, on the other hand, handles wastewater containing solids from plumbing fixtures located below the main sewer line, lifting it up to the main sewer or septic system.

    FHN Plumbing installs a wide range of trusted brands, including Moen, Delta, Kohler for fixtures, Rheem, Bradford White, and Rinnai for water heaters, and Zoeller or Liberty Pumps for sump and ejector pumps. We can also install client-supplied products, ensuring they meet quality and code standards.

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible plastic tubing used for water supply lines. It's popular for installations in Richland because it's corrosion-resistant, less prone to bursting from freezing than copper, and often quicker to install. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings, reducing potential leak points and making it a dependable choice for Washington homes.
