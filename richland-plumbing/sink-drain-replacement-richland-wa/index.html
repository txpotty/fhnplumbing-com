    Pop-Up Drain Assembly Replacement in Richland, WA

    A stopper that drops on its own has a worn pivot. The plastic ball on the pivot rod wears flat, the nut that holds it loses its seal, and the rod no longer holds the stopper up, so the sink either will not drain or will not hold water. A new pop-up fixes all of it. Takes about an hour.

    Commercial Lavatory Drain Replacement in Richland

    Public restroom drains take constant use. Offset grid drains and heavy traps go in, with insulated covers where accessibility rules apply. Work can happen after hours. Inspectors get a clean, labeled job.

    Bathroom Sink Drain Replacement in Richland, WA

    Bathroom drains wear out at the pop-up. Pivot nuts leak and drain bodies corrode at the overflow holes, so a sink that will not hold water needs a new pop-up. Patching one piece rarely works. The new drain matches your faucet finish.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Richland

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. The putty under a 3-1/2 inch strainer dries out, the locknut rusts, and water seeps down the outside of the tailpiece where it looks like the trap is leaking even when it is not. Strainer and trap get swapped as a pair. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.
