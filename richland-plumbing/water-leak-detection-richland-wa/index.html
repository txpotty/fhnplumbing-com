    Slab Leak Detection in Richland

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often indicated by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. FHN Plumbing uses dedicated acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is key to preventing major foundation damage.

    Commercial Water Leak Detection in Richland, WA

    Commercial properties in Richland face unique challenges with larger, more complex plumbing systems. Leaks can disrupt business operations, lead to costly downtime and damage inventory. FHN Plumbing offers dedicated commercial leak detection services, using industrial-grade equipment to locate leaks in large facilities, multi-story buildings and specialized systems. We work quickly to minimize impact on your business, providing rapid diagnosis and repair solutions.

    Smart Leak Detection Device Installation in Richland

    Smart shutoff valves like Moen Flo and Phyn monitor water flow and can shut off water on their own when they detect a leak. Small sensors placed near water heaters, sinks and washers send alerts to your phone. We install and set up these systems so you get warnings before small leaks become big ones. Some insurers offer premium discounts for these devices.

    Main Service Line Leak Detection in Richland, WA

    The line from the meter to your house carries full pressure and can leak underground for a long time. Wet soil, soft spots or oddly green patches of yard may be the only clues. We trace the line, listen for the leak and mark it for a targeted repair. Replacing old galvanized or polybutylene service lines is usually recommended.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Richland

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for long periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. Signs include unusually green patches in your lawn, persistent wet spots or a constantly running water meter. FHN Plumbing uses ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection methods to accurately map out and pinpoint leaks in buried pipes, allowing for targeted excavation and repair rather than digging up your entire yard.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Richland

    Drip and sprinkler systems run underground across yards and often account for hidden water loss. Cracked tubing, broken emitters and stuck valves can waste large amounts of water. We isolate the irrigation system, test each zone and trace leaking lines. Repairs restore efficient watering for your landscape.

    Hidden Wall & Ceiling Leak Detection in Richland, WA

    Leaks behind walls or in ceilings can cause significant damage before they become visible, leading to stained drywall, peeling paint or a musty odor.
