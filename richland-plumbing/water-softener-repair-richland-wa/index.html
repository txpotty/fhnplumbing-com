    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Richland, WA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    Control Valve Repair in Richland

    Water that runs to the drain all day usually means the control valve is stuck between cycles. FHN Plumbing rebuilds stuck valves with the correct seal and spacer kit for your model. Timing problems get fixed, whether mechanical or electronic. Common parts are on the truck, so most valve repairs are finished the same day without a second trip to the supply house.

    Commercial Water Softener Repair in Richland, WA

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Richland

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. Even a slow drip wastes water and soaks the floor.
