    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Richland, WA

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Richland

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron changes the math. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Richland

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning. Our residential installs start with a test and a few questions about water use, then we pick a grain capacity that regenerates about once a week for your household. That keeps salt use reasonable. Every detail is handled on one visit.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Richland

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Soft water reaches every fixture. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Richland

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Programming is the last step. We set regeneration overnight.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Richland, WA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. FHN Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. They work best on very hard water.
