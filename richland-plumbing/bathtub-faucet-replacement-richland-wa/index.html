    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Richland, WA

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. A new valve makes sense for discontinued parts, a cracked body or an old faucet with no scald protection. We quote both options. Most people choose repair first.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Richland

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. Single-handle valves can often be updated from the front, while three-handle valves usually get cut out and replaced. The valve gets identified before we price it. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in Richland, WA

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update. Many Delta Monitor valves accept a new cartridge and trim from the front, and the MultiChoice universal rough-in lets you switch trim styles later without touching the pipes in the wall. Popular Delta cartridges are on the truck. Older Delta valves sometimes need full replacement.

    Replace or Keep Repairing in Richland

    Repeated repairs add up. If a faucet has had three cartridges in five years, the valve body is likely worn where the cartridge seals, and replacing the whole valve once costs less than another round of service calls. Your service history helps us decide. Newer valves often come with lifetime cartridge warranties.

    Upgrading Tub Faucets in Richland, WA

    Plenty of people replace a working tub faucet for looks. A dated chrome cross-handle set can become a brushed nickel or matte black lever trim, and if the valve is a newer universal rough-in, the swap happens without opening the wall at all. We can match your sink finish. Delta and Moen make this simplest.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Richland

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. New internals end the drip. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Richland

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard and Pfister each use their own cartridges and trim threads, and a new trim kit from the same maker as the valve usually bolts right on without touching the plumbing. Mixing brands rarely works. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Richland, WA

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.
