    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Richland

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines.     Backflow is when water flows the wrong way through a pipe and carries contaminants into the clean water supply. It can happen from back siphonage during a pressure drop or back pressure from pumps and boilers.
