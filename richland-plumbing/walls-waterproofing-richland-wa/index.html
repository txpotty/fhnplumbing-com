    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Richland

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra reinforcement before new tile is set.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Richland, WA

    Sump pumps are a critical part of many interior waterproofing systems, particularly in homes that see heavy rainfall. We install high-capacity sump pumps designed to handle large volumes of water, keeping your basement dry even during flash floods. Our services include primary sump pump installation, battery backup sump pump systems for power outages, and routine maintenance or repair of existing units. We make sure the discharge line is routed properly so water does not circulate back toward the foundation.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Richland

    Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier. This not only waterproofs the walls but also controls humidity, stopping mold growth and wood rot in the crawl space. Washington has two very different climates, with long, wet and mild seasons in the west and hot, dry summers and cold winters in the east. An encapsulated crawl space can noticeably improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency by keeping moisture from moving up into the home. We use durable, reinforced poly sheeting for walls and floors, sealed at all seams and penetrations.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Richland, WA

    Washing machine hoses, utility sinks and water heaters can all leak into nearby walls. We replace burst-prone rubber hoses with braided stainless hoses and install leak pans and shutoff valves.     Sprinklers and drip lines aimed at the house are the most common cause. Heavy storms and poor grading also send water toward walls. Masonry absorbs that water and leaves mineral deposits as it dries.

    The duration of a walls waterproofing project varies depending on its scope. Minor crack repairs might take a few hours, while an interior drainage system could take 1 to 3 days.     Look for bubbling paint, soft drywall, stained baseboards and musty odors. White crust on block or stucco walls is another sign of moisture moving through. Loose tiles or cracked grout in a shower also suggest water is getting behind.
