    Disposal Installation in Richland, WA: How We Do It

    Standard Disposal Installation in Richland

    Most households in Richland do well with a 1/3 to 1/2 horsepower continuous-feed unit. We remove the old unit if there is one, set a new flange in fresh plumber's putty, hang the disposal on a new mount and connect it to the trap with a new discharge tube and gasket. Then we wire it and test it. A smooth spin and a clean drain tell us it is right.

    Disposal Upgrades in Richland, WA

    Upgrading from an old 1/3 HP unit to something quieter is a common request. We disconnect and remove the old disposal, check the trap and drain arm for corrosion, and install the new unit with a new mount, so the upgrade does not inherit the old unit's worn parts. Old drain arms often need replacing. Galvanized arms rust from the inside.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Richland

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. Power needs a check too. Bigger motors draw more at startup.

    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Richland

    These disposals run only with the stopper lid seated, which adds safety for families with kids. Because they need no wall switch, they suit kitchens where a new switch would mean cutting drywall or a tile backsplash. The drain hookup is the usual setup.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Richland, WA

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. The button pushes air through a tube to a control box in the cabinet, which keeps electricity away from the button and avoids a new wall switch. Islands use them often.

    Garbage Disposal Installation Cost Factors in Richland, WA

    The model and the power setup drive most of the cost. A simple swap on a sink that already has a switched outlet is the least expensive job, while adding power, lowering the drain or reworking a double-bowl layout adds time and shows up as its own line on the quote. The quote covers unit and labor.

    Same-Day Disposal Installation in Richland, WA

    If your old disposal quits with a sink full of water, we can usually install a new one the same day. We carry 1/2 and 3/4 HP units, mounts and cord kits on the truck, so there is no supply house run before your sink drains. Figure on roughly an hour.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Setup in Richland

    On a batch-feed unit, the lid is the switch. We install the unit, then test the lid in every position to make sure the motor only runs when the lid is fully seated and stops the moment it is lifted out. That is the whole point. Families with kids and anyone nervous about a hand near the hopper tend to choose these in Richland homes.
