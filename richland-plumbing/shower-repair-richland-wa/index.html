    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Richland, WA

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. The problem is on the hot side, either sediment in the water heater outlet, a partly closed valve at the heater or a cartridge whose hot port is packed with scale. Each side gets tested. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Richland, WA

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. Each part gets tested. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Richland

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. The spool should shift to keep the mix steady when one side loses pressure, but scale can lock it in one spot. Replacing the cartridge solves it. If you cannot get the water hot enough, the anti-scald limit stop may just need adjusting.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Richland, WA

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Shut the main, or use the shower's integral stops if your valve has them behind the trim or an access panel. Next, call us. FHN Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Richland
