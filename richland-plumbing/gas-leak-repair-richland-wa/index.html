    Propane Gas Leak Repair in Richland, WA

    Propane behaves differently from natural gas because it is heavier than air, so a leak settles into basements and crawlspaces instead of rising. We check both regulators, the one at the tank and the one at the house. Low spots get sniffed first. If the supplier owns the tank, they handle the tank side and we handle the piping from there to the appliances. Rural properties with buried tanks get a line test too.

    Residential Gas Leak Detection in Richland

    A gas leak in a house rarely sits where the smell is strongest, because gas drifts along joists and collects in closets and basements. We start with a gauge on the system to confirm there is a leak at all. Then the sniffer comes out. A combustible gas detector follows the reading from fitting to fitting until it spikes. Bubble solution on the suspect joint gives the final confirmation.

    Commercial Gas Leak Repair in Richland

    Commercial gas piping often runs at 2 psi or higher with regulators at each appliance bank, so a leak test there is not the same as a house test. We check regulator lockup and vent lines along with the joints. Rooftop units leak too. If a shutdown would cost you a dinner rush, we valve off just the bad branch and leave the rest live. Staff get a short walkthrough on the shutoff locations.

    Emergency Gas Leak Repair in Richland, WA

    Gas Line Repair & Replacement in Richland, WA

    Black iron pipe with threaded fittings is still the most common interior gas pipe, and the leak is nearly always at a thread, not in the middle of a length. We take the joint apart, clean the threads and remake it with fresh gas-rated sealant. Cracked fittings get replaced. Badly rusted sections, often near a crawlspace vent, are cut back to clean pipe and replaced. Every remade joint gets a detector check and a gauge test.

    Appliance Connector Replacement in Richland

    Old uncoated brass connectors, the kind made before the mid-1970s, are the subject of consumer safety warnings because the flare nut can separate from the tube. Any of those still in service should be replaced. We see them weekly. The replacement is a coated stainless connector sized for the appliance, with a new ball valve and a sediment trap where one is required. Bubble testing follows before the burner is lit.

    Interior Gas Pipe Repair in Richland, WA

    Inside the house, gas leaks hide at unions in the basement and at the threaded nipple behind the furnace, places nobody looks. Testing runs from the meter inward, one section at a time. Drywall stays intact when possible. Rerouting with CSST past a leak in a finished wall is often cheaper than cutting and patching drywall. Nothing is turned back on until the gauge holds steady.
