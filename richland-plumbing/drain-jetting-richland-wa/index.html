    Kitchen Drain Jetting in Richland

    Kitchen lines are where grease, food particles and dish soap collect and harden. Over time the pipe narrows until the sink drains slowly or backs up into the other basin. We access the line through a cleanout or the trap arm and feed a small jetting hose toward the main. Rear-facing jets scrub the walls while the stream flushes residue away. Your sink and dishwasher drain at full speed once more.

    High-Pressure Water Jetting Equipment in Richland, WA

    We use modern high-pressure water jetting equipment for the most effective drain cleaning in Richland, WA. Our fleet includes truck-mounted and portable jetters that can deliver water at pressures up to 4000 PSI, depending on the application. We use several specialized nozzles, including penetrating nozzles for roots, degreasing nozzles for FOG (fats, oils, grease), and reverse jet nozzles for scrubbing pipe walls. This equipment lets us tackle any blockage with control and power.

    Floor Drain and Utility Sink Jetting in Richland, WA

    Garage floor drains, utility sinks and mop sinks often collect dirt, paint residue and debris. They are easy to forget until they overflow. Jetting clears the line and flushes the trap.     Root Removal with Jetting in Richland, WA

    Tree roots are a frequent problem for sewer lines in Richland, WA, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees. Our drain jetting service includes root-cutting nozzles that slice through even thick root masses, flushing them out of your system.
