    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Redmond

    The cost of water heater replacement in Redmond, WA usually ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation. Tankless units can range from $3,000 to $5,000 or more, depending on the model and needed plumbing modifications. Factors like unit type, capacity, brand and labor complexity influence the final price.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line changes.

    Tank water heaters often last about 8 to 12 years, and hard water and sediment buildup can shorten that. Many rural Washington homes rely on private wells, and some carry iron, manganese or other minerals that call for testing and treatment. Annual flushing and anode rod checks can help lengthen that life.

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith water heaters. Each offers dependable performance and a range of warranties.

    FHN Plumbing installs and services all major brands, including Bradford White, Rheem, AO Smith, Rinnai, Navien and State Water Heaters. We can help you choose the best brand and model that suits your budget and hot water demands.

    Upgrading provides more reliable hot water, improved energy efficiency and lower utility bills, better performance and a reduced risk of leaks or breakdowns. Newer models often have advanced features and longer warranties, improving comfort and safety in your home.

    Manufacturer tank warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor.
