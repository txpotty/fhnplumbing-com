    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Redmond

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, particularly in closed-loop systems. Seismic strapping on water heaters is a common inspection item here, and FHN Plumbing installs to meet local requirements. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to support proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Redmond. This small component offers real long-term protection.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Redmond

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank water heater typically costs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless installations often cost $3,000 to $6,500 depending on gas line and venting needs.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the ideal size.

    Most standard water heater installations, particularly for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours. However, a conversion from a tank to a tankless system, or installations requiring significant plumbing or electrical modifications, might take a full day or longer. We will provide an estimated timeline during our first assessment.

    The typical lifespan of a traditional tank water heater is about 10 to 15 years, while tankless water heaters can last 20 years or more with proper maintenance. Factors like local water quality, maintenance frequency and usage habits can all affect the actual lifespan of your unit. Regular flushing can help extend its life.

    A standard tank replacement typically takes two to four hours. Tankless installs or relocations can take most of a day.

    To improve efficiency, make sure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and reduce energy consumption.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency. If your water heater is over 10 years old, often breaks down, or the repair cost is more than 50% of a new unit, replacement is often the more economical choice. Newer models also offer much better energy efficiency.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. FHN Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, ensuring peace of mind. We will discuss exact warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.
