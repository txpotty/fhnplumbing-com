    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Redmond, WA

    Glacial till and dense hardpan are common in western Washington, and they drain poorly enough to keep water against foundations and in crawlspaces. A section of sewer pipe that sinks forms a belly. Water and solids sit in the dip. Over time that standing water collects paper and grease, and the line clogs at the same spot every few months no matter how often it gets cleaned. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. A spot repair restores the slope.

    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Redmond, WA

    One slow drain is a branch clog. Several slow drains is the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, which is why we ask which fixtures are acting up before we decide where to open the line. The right entry point saves time.

    Tree Root Invasion Removal in Redmond

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture in the soil. They only need a tiny opening. Once inside, they spread into a net that traps everything moving through. We cut them out with a root saw on a sectional machine, then camera the line to see how many joints are letting roots in. Lining or replacement seals the joints for good.

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Redmond

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. The cable is not the fix. Those lines are usually coated in grease or scale, and the cable only bores a narrow path through the middle that closes up again within weeks. Jetting removes the coating down to the pipe wall. The results hold up far better.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Redmond
