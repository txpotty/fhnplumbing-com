    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Redmond

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.

    Food Truck Commissary and Ghost Kitchen Traps in Redmond, WA

    Commissary kitchens and ghost kitchens often have several prep stations and heavy dishwashing loads. We design grease trap installs that handle high flow from multiple sinks and dish machines. Each unit is sized for the combined discharge, not just one fixture. We also install cleanouts and sample ports where the city requires them. This keeps shared kitchens compliant for each tenant.
