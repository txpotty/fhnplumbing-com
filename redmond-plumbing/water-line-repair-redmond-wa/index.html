    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Redmond

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. Trenchless avoids most of that. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, following the old line or cutting a new path beside it. Most replacements finish in a day. The new pipe carries its own manufacturer warranty.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Redmond

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. Most codes cap it at 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, and if it reads high we replace the pressure reducing valve with a Watts or Zurn Wilkins unit. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. That way you can actually shut the water off.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Redmond

    A meter that moves with every fixture off is the clearest sign you have. Confirming it takes about ten minutes. A repair coupling or a new length of pipe goes in, and then we pressure test it.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Redmond, WA

    A full break can flood the yard and leave the house dry at the same time. Shutting the water off comes first.     Main Water Line Replacement in Redmond, WA

    Replacement beats repair once a line has leaked two or three times, or when it is galvanized or polybutylene. Patching that kind of pipe tends to buy months, not years.     Water Line Repair at the Meter in Redmond, WA

    The meter box is where a lot of leaks start. Compression fittings there work loose over time.     Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Redmond

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Newer homes in Redmond usually use PEX supply lines, and many older houses have been partially repiped over the years. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or abandon the old pipe under the floor and run new PEX above it. You get a price on every option.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Redmond, WA

Age matters too, and a line past about 40 years is usually near the end no matter the material. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. We show you the pipe we exposed.
