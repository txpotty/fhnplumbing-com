    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Redmond, WA

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Redmond, WA

    A power outage during a heavy storm can render your primary sump pump useless. Our maintenance service includes thorough testing of your battery backup sump pump system. We check the battery charge, verify the charger's function and confirm the backup pump activates when the main power is interrupted. This dual protection is vital for homes in Redmond, WA where power interruptions can coincide with severe weather.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Redmond, WA

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Cost in Redmond, WA

    Routine maintenance usually costs a fraction of replacing a burned-out pump and cleaning up a flooded basement. A new installed sump pump often runs $400 to $1,200 depending on horsepower and backup options. Most pumps last seven to ten years with care. We will tell you honestly whether yours has more years left or is ready to retire.

    Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Redmond

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. We test duplex systems, alternating controls and high-water alarms on a scheduled basis. Maintenance logs help property managers stay on top of inspections.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Redmond

    Our team is experienced in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in Redmond due to their quiet operation and compact design. Maintenance involves pulling the pump to inspect the watertight seals, clearing the intake screen and checking the internal components for corrosion or wear. We also verify the electrical connections are secure and free from moisture, preventing electrical hazards and supporting longevity.
