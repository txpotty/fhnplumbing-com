    Jams usually trace back to one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller.     Preventive Care and Longevity in Redmond, WA

    Keep cold water running before, during and after use, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap. Celery, artichoke leaves and corn husks belong in the trash.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Redmond

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else.
