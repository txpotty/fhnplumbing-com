    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Redmond, WA

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Certain types need a plan. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Redmond, WA

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Angle stops and the main shutoff valve often seize over the years, and we also listen for gurgling drains that point to venting problems, since small fixes like these are easy to plan once you know about them.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Redmond, WA

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. The visit covers flushing the heater, working the shutoffs, reading pressure and checking for slow drains and small leaks. Plan on an hour or so. Doing this every year or two adds years to a water heater and keeps the shutoffs working for the day you really need them.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Inspection in Redmond

    Most home plumbing inspections run $150 to $350, depending on the size and age of the house. Camera work costs extra. A sewer camera adds to the total, and you hear the full price before we arrive, so the invoice matches the quote.

    A plumbing inspection is a deeper look at one system. Home inspectors cover the basics. Home inspectors run water and flush toilets, and our plumbers add sewer video, pressure tests, pipe identification and code review.

    A standard home inspection takes about one and a half to three hours. Sewer video adds time. Larger homes, long sewer lines or a hidden leak that needs tracing can push it longer, and we will tell you the expected time when you book so you can plan around it.

    We check water supply lines, drain lines, fixtures, the water heater, sump pump, garbage disposal and exposed gas lines. Drainage gets tested. Our plumber also checks for leaks, corrosion and vent issues, measures pressure and writes up the condition of every component.

    Every one to two years works for most homes. Some houses need it yearly. Houses with original pipe, big trees over the sewer or past leaks do better with a yearly check that catches problems while they are small.

    No permit is needed for an inspection. Repairs may need one. If the inspection leads to repairs or replacements that need a permit, we will say so up front and handle it if we do the job.

    We can include estimated repair ranges in the report and firm quotes if you want us to do the work. There is no obligation to hire us for repairs.
