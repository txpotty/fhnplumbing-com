    Causes of Sewer Line Failure in Redmond, WA

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints separate, crack or drop out of grade over the years. Steep slopes and wet soils can move over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Then age finishes the job. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under the weight of soil and traffic.
