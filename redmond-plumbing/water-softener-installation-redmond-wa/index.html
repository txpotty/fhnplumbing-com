    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Redmond, WA

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. When a homeowner has already bought one, we handle the plumbing, the bypass and the startup flush the manual requires. Startup matters. Every step gets done.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Redmond

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. Sizing comes first. Your test result and water use decide the tank size.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Redmond, WA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. FHN Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Redmond

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning. We test the water, ask about daily use and choose a capacity that lets the system regenerate roughly once a week. That keeps salt use reasonable. One visit covers the full install.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Redmond

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Soft water reaches every fixture. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Redmond

    Water softener installation in Redmond, WA usually runs $1,500 to $4,000. Where you land depends on the type and size of the system and whether we need to change any plumbing. Loops add cost. We quote after an on-site test.

    Most homeowners are better off hiring a pro. A wrong connection can leak for weeks or leave the water just as hard as before. Professional work gets sizing, connections and code right.

    Fleck, Culligan, Ecodyne and Kenmore are some of the brands we install. Our plumbers walk you through the options and help you pick a model that fits your water, your household and what you want to spend. Size matters more than brand.

    Control valves usually carry about 5 years, and tanks often 10 or more. Our labor has its own warranty.

    We install Culligan, Pelican, Fleck, Kenmore, Whirlpool and GE, among others. We help you choose the right brand and model for your water and budget.

    Salt use depends on hardness, water use and how efficient the softener is. A family of four with fairly hard water might use 40 to 80 pounds of salt per month, and a metered high-efficiency model uses less. Metered valves save most.

    Soap scum on shower doors, dry skin, dull laundry and white scale on faucets are the classic signs. We can run a hardness test in your home to confirm it, usually in a few minutes with a simple kit at the kitchen sink. No charge for it.

    Adding salt every 4 to 8 weeks is most of the work. A yearly checkup with a plumber catches worn seals, salt bridges in the brine tank and clogged injectors before they cause hard water. Do not let the salt run out.
