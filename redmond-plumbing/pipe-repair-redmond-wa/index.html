    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Redmond, WA

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Both have weak points. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic, while CPVC turns brittle with age and heat and cracks when bumped, so we replace failed fittings and damaged sections with parts matched to each material. Brittle CPVC needs a gentle hand.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Redmond

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water quality is often behind it. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, which we fix by replacing that length with new pipe. One usually means more are coming.

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Redmond

    Threaded steel pipe rusts from the inside until flow drops and threaded joints start to leak. Repairs are possible. The trouble is that every fitting you disturb on old galvanized can start a new leak, so we replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric fittings and talk through a full replacement plan when the rest is close behind. You decide the timing.

    Underground Pipe Repair in Redmond

    Buried pipe takes stress from settling soil, roots and vehicles. You rarely see them. We locate underground leaks with acoustic equipment and tracer gas, and either spot repair at the leak or replace the line by pulling new pipe through. We test before backfill.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair in Redmond, WA

    Slab leaks start when pipe under the concrete wears through or gets stressed by soil movement. Crawlspaces and basements are both common in Washington homes, and a damp crawlspace is often the first sign of a drainage or plumbing problem. A hot spot underfoot is common. We isolate the leaking line with pressure tests and find it with acoustic and thermal equipment, and you pick between a targeted floor repair and a reroute overhead. Both options get a price.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Redmond

    Drain pipe made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. Stains give it away. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet, and we locate the leak, replace the damaged section and test the drain under full flow before closing up. Vents get checked too.

    Pipe Repair vs Repipe Cost in Redmond, WA

    A single pipe repair costs far less than a whole-house repipe. It makes sense on sound pipe. If you are seeing repeated leaks, low pressure or rusty water, the total cost of patching can pass the cost of repiping within a few years, which is why we price both and give you our honest take. The decision is yours.

    Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Redmond

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Frozen water cracks it. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, so a pipe that froze overnight may start spraying the next afternoon once the house warms up. We repair the split and add insulation. Heat tape is an option on problem runs.
