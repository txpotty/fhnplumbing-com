    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Redmond

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber.     Disposal Installs for New Sinks in Redmond, WA

    A new sink changes the drain math. Undermount and farmhouse bowls are often deeper than the old drop-in. With the bowl lower, the disposal drops too, and the discharge can land below the old trap arm, which means lowering the stub. Better to sort that out mid-remodel than after the tile is up.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Redmond

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. Pressing it sends air down a tube. That pulse trips a switch in a control box plugged in under the sink, so no live wiring sits anywhere near the water, which is the main reason people like them. We drill the counter or use the spare faucet hole.

    Evolution Disposal Upgrades in Redmond, WA

    Stringy food is where these units earn their price. Corn husks and celery will stall a light disposal. An Evolution Compact or Excel grinds in stages and has more torque, so those scraps break into small pieces instead of wrapping around the impellers and tripping the overload. We check cabinet clearance first.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Redmond

    Kitchens that never had a disposal need some rework. The sink needs a disposal flange. The tailpiece and trap have to be reworked, and the cabinet needs a switched outlet, which means an electrician if there is no power under the sink already. Drain slope to the stack gets checked as well.

    Hard Water and Disposals in Redmond

    Soft water can be slightly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. Scale builds up on grind rings and drain tailpieces over the years. A scaled unit gets loud. Drainage slows too, since crust narrows the tailpiece and trap, and we check those parts before tying a new unit into them. If they are crusted badly, we swap them while we are there.

    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Redmond, WA

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. Expect the new unit to sit lower in the cabinet. That extra height can put the discharge outlet below the trap arm, so we measure the drop before install day and lower the trap or the arm if the numbers do not work. We also confirm the circuit can carry the larger motor.

The Evolution Septic Assist injects an enzyme solution into the grind chamber each time it runs, which helps break down food waste before it reaches the tank.
