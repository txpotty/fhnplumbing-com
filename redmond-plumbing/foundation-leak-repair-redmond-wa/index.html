    Foundation Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Redmond, WA

    Direct repairs are often the lowest upfront cost but involve breaking concrete and may need flooring repairs. Reroutes cost a bit more but avoid concrete work and future leaks in the abandoned line. A full repipe is the most comprehensive option for homes with multiple leaks or aging pipe. We help you compare total costs, including flooring and drywall repairs.

    Water Line Reroute in Redmond

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This skips breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for easy patching.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Redmond, WA

    When the leak is in an accessible spot and the rest of the pipe is in good condition, direct repair is often the most affordable choice. We cut a small opening in the floor, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The soil is compacted and the concrete is patched to match. Flooring can then be restored by your flooring contractor.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Redmond

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs with steel cables under high tension. Cutting into these slabs requires care and scanning to avoid hitting a cable. We use ground-penetrating radar or other scanning methods before opening the floor. When risk is too high, rerouting the plumbing is usually the safer option.
