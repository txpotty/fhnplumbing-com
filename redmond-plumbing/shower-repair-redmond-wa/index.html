    Fixing Showers in Redmond, WA

    Emergency Shower Leak Repair in Redmond

    Water coming through a ceiling below a bathroom needs fast action. Close the main, set a bucket under the drip and stay clear of any drywall that is bulging, because wet ceilings can drop. Then call us. Our plumber locates the leak, stops it and starts drying the area.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Redmond

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. That spool is supposed to slide back and forth to keep the hot and cold mix even when pressure drops on one side, and scale can freeze it in place. Replacing the cartridge solves it. If you cannot get the water hot enough, the anti-scald limit stop may just need adjusting.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Redmond, WA

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. Usually a new spout fixes it. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Redmond, WA

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. A meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan show whether the leak is the valve, the pan or the drain. Then we fix the source. On slab homes, these leaks can spread into the flooring of the next room before anyone sees them.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Redmond, WA

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. The Moen 1222 runs the Posi-Temp line, and the Delta RP46074 runs the Monitor 13 and 14 series, and both wear out on a schedule you can almost set a calendar by. We stock both. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Redmond, WA

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Redmond, WA

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Shut the main, or use the shower's integral stops if your valve has them behind the trim or an access panel. Then call us. FHN Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Redmond

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. Water that seeps from the arm connection runs back along the pipe into the wall, and you only find out when the paint on the other side starts to bubble. Fresh thread tape fixes it. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.
