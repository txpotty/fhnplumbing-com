    Jammed Disposal Repair in Bellevue

    Jams usually trace back to one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. We press the reset after clearing it. Repeat jams point us to the trap arm.

    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Bellevue

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor. We start at the red reset button on the bottom, then check the breaker, the wall switch and the outlet or junction box under the sink with a meter. Cabinets are damp places.     Cost-Effective Disposal Repair in Bellevue, WA

    A disposal repair that costs half of a new unit rarely makes sense, and we will say so. Plenty of repairs cost little. A reset, a cleared jam, a new switch or a resealed flange usually costs far less than a new unit and can keep a younger disposal going for years.

    Disposal Repair vs Replacement Costs in Bellevue, WA

    Jams and minor leaks usually cost far less than a new unit. Once a unit is eight to ten years old, leaking from the body or losing its motor, replacement usually wins, and we price both when it is a close call. You make the call.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Bellevue

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. A steady whine usually points to failing motor bearings, and the noise keeps building until the motor locks up. Odors usually trace to the splash guard. The guard comes out, the chamber gets cleaned and a torn guard gets replaced.
