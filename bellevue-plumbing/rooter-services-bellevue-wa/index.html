    Kitchen Line Rooter Service in Bellevue, WA

    Kitchen lines clog slowly, over months, with grease, soap and food scraps that cool and harden on the pipe wall. We go in through the trap or a cleanout with a medium cable and spiral cutter.     Camera Inspection for Rooter Services in Bellevue, WA

    On a hard or repeat clog, FHN Plumbing sends a camera down after the cable. The monitor shows distance from the cleanout and, with a locator, the depth at that spot.     Hydro-Jetting Services in Bellevue, WA

    Jetting puts water through a nozzle at 3,000 to 4,000 psi and scrubs the pipe wall clean. It strips grease, scale and root hair that a cable slides past. Soft water can be slightly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. The pipe gets a camera check first. Cracked clay or thin cast iron can be hurt by that pressure, so we only jet a line we have seen and trust.

    Sewer Root Removal in Bellevue

    Roots cause more main sewer clogs than anything else on older streets. They work into small gaps at clay and cast iron joints and thicken into mats that snag paper and grease. We cut them back with a root saw on a heavy cable. Plan on rooting every 12 to 24 months.
