    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Bellevue

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. The button pushes a puff of air down a thin tube. That pulse trips a switch in a control box plugged in under the sink, so no live wiring sits anywhere near the water, which is the main reason people like them. If there is a spare faucet hole, we use it, otherwise we drill.

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Bellevue

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty.
