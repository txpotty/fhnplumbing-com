    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Bellevue, WA

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Many rural Washington homes rely on private wells, and some carry iron, manganese or other minerals that call for testing and treatment. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides extra protection.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Bellevue, WA

    Tankless installation costs more upfront than a standard tank because of the unit price, gas line upgrades and venting changes. Over time, lower standby energy losses and a longer expected lifespan make up for part of that cost. Tankless units often last 15 to 20 years with proper descaling, compared with about 8 to 12 years for many tanks in hard water areas.
