    Copper Water Line Installation in Bellevue

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. Street pressure does not bother it. We bed it in sand, keep it away from sharp rock, and sleeve it where it passes through a foundation wall, because concrete and copper rubbing together for twenty years eventually wear a hole.     Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Bellevue

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? We will usually just fix that spot.     Water bubbling up through the lawn or the street side of the sidewalk means a break, not a drip. Close the main shutoff at the house first.     PEX Water Line Replacement in Bellevue

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, connect it with brass or poly fittings that are approved underground, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    The thickest wall belongs to Type K, the usual choice underground. Type L is thinner and mostly used inside the house.
