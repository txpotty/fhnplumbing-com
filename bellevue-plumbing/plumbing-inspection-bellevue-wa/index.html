    Detailed Plumbing Inspections in Bellevue, WA

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Bellevue

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in Washington houses. Common finds include old pipe, stiff shutoffs, tired heaters, slow drains and rooty or sagging sewers. Each gets tested, not guessed at. Catching a corroded fitting or a partial sewer blockage now keeps it from becoming a ceiling stain or a backup on a holiday weekend.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Bellevue, WA

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging. The camera goes in at a cleanout and travels to the main or the tank, recording roots, cracks, offsets and bellies along the way. A locator marks the depth. Crawlspaces and basements are both common in Washington homes, and a damp crawlspace is often the first sign of a drainage or plumbing problem.

    Plumbing Inspection Costs in Bellevue, WA

    A basic plumbing inspection usually costs $150 to $300, and adding a sewer camera usually brings it to $250 to $500. Bigger properties cost more. Next to a surprise sewer replacement at $4,000 to $15,000 or more, an inspection costs little, and the price is set when you schedule.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Bellevue

    Many rural Washington homes rely on private wells, and some carry iron, manganese or other minerals that call for testing and treatment. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. You get options, not a pitch. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.

    Residential Plumbing Inspections in Bellevue

    A full home inspection covers the system from the water meter to the sewer connection. Our plumber looks at all exposed pipe, runs every faucet and toilet and tests the heater, the main valve and the fixture stops. Pressure gets measured too. High pressure gets flagged.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Bellevue, WA

    Washington has two very different climates, with long, wet and mild seasons in the west and hot, dry summers and cold winters in the east. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. Outdoor faucets, exposed pipe, irrigation backflow devices and the heater drain and relief valve all get checked. Insulation gets checked too. Fixing a cracked hose bibb or adding insulation is a small job compared to a burst pipe in the wall behind it.

    Pre-Purchase Inspection With Sewer Video in Bellevue

    Plumbing problems are some of the most expensive surprises after closing. Our pre-purchase inspection covers the supply pipe type, the sewer condition on video, the water heater's age and every fixture. Your agent gets the report. Buyers often use the findings to request repairs or credits, and the sewer video by itself can head off a very costly surprise.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Bellevue

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. We confirm proper venting. Expansion tanks, drain pans and softeners get a look as well, and a quick hardness test tells us whether the softener is actually doing its job or just using salt.
