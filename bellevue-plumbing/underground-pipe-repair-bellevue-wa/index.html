    Pipe Bursting Services in Bellevue, WA

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless technique where a new pipe is pulled through the path of the old, broken pipe, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is highly effective for complete pipe replacement, creating a new, durable pipe of the same or larger diameter. It's a robust solution for severely damaged or collapsed sewer and water lines, offering a long-term fix in Bellevue, WA.

    Pool and Outbuilding Supply Line Repair in Bellevue, WA

    Many properties have buried water lines feeding a pool auto-fill, a guest house, an outdoor kitchen or a detached garage. These lines are usually longer than the main service and harder to locate. We use tracer wire and acoustic equipment to find leaks along the full run. Repairs are made with direct-bury rated pipe and tested before we close up.

    Irrigation and Yard Line Repair in Bellevue

    Drip lines and sprinkler supply pipes are often shallow and easy to break during landscaping or by roots. A broken irrigation main can run up a water bill fast in summer. We repair PVC and poly irrigation mains, replace failed backflow connections and fix the supply line to the valve manifold. We can also reroute exposed lines away from trees.

    Main Water Line Repair in Bellevue

    Your main water line runs from the meter box to the house and is under constant pressure. When it leaks, you may notice a spinning meter dial with all fixtures off, low pressure or a wet spot that never dries. We locate the leak, expose the pipe and splice in a new section with approved fittings. If the line is old galvanized or polybutylene, we will recommend a full replacement before more breaks occur.

    Sewer Line Repair in Bellevue

    Sewer line damage underground can show up as foul odors, slow drains or soggy spots in your yard. We diagnose breaks, blockages or collapses in your main sewer line using HD camera inspections. Our repairs range from targeted patch repairs to full line replacement, frequently using trenchless methods to minimize disruption to your landscaping and concrete in Bellevue.

    Trenchless Underground Pipe Repair in Bellevue

    For homeowners in Bellevue looking to limit disruption to their landscaping, FHN Plumbing offers trenchless underground pipe repair.     Underground Pipe Repair Cost Comparison in Bellevue, WA

    A single point repair on a shallow water line is the most economical option. Pipe bursting or pulling a new line through the old path costs more but avoids open trenching and long-term risk. Full trench replacement is sometimes needed when the old route is blocked or the pipe is too damaged. We explain each method and its cost so you can choose the best option.
