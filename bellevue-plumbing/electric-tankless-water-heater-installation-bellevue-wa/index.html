    Residential Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Bellevue

    Residential electric tankless installs start with counting fixtures that run at the same time. Two showers at 2 gallons per minute each with 50 degree incoming water need roughly a 27 kW unit, and that unit needs three 40 amp double-pole breakers. One bath needs less. We install Rheem, EcoSmart and Stiebel Eltron.

    Rheem Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Bellevue

    Rheem's RTEX line covers most of the whole-home electric tankless units we install. Model choice depends on your panel, since the larger RTEX units need three 40 amp breakers. They mount flat on the wall. We connect water, set temperature and test for leaks.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Bellevue

    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes. Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. We recommend a softener or a scale inhibitor ahead of the unit, and every install gets service valves so the heater can be flushed with a descaling solution once a year. Flushing takes an hour.

    Rheem Electric Tankless Water Heater Services in Bellevue

    Rheem offers electric tankless models from small point-of-use units to 27 kW whole-home heaters. We install and service Rheem units, testing elements, flow sensors and wiring when problems start. Replacement parts are stocked. We help you pick the right size.

    Electric Tankless Installation Cost in Bellevue, WA

    Point-of-use electric tankless costs far less than a whole-home system. Whole-home installs often need multiple circuits and sometimes a panel upgrade, and that electrical work can cost as much as the unit itself. Your quote shows it all. You get a tank versus tankless comparison.

    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless Electric in Bellevue

    Switching from tank to tankless electric usually takes panel work and some re-piping. The higher amperage often means new breakers, heavier wire and sometimes a service upgrade, and the water lines get moved to meet the unit on the wall. We manage both trades. The closet opens up.

    Cost Comparison: Electric Tankless vs. Traditional in Bellevue, WA

    Upfront, electric tankless usually costs more than a tank because of the electrical work. A tankless can outlive two tanks and skips standby loss, which narrows the gap over time. It depends on your use. You get a real comparison first.
