    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Bellevue

    The cost of water heater replacement in Bellevue, WA usually ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation. Tankless units can range from $3,000 to $5,000 or more, depending on the model and needed plumbing modifications. Factors like unit type, capacity, brand and labor complexity influence the final price.

    Upgrading provides more reliable hot water, improved energy efficiency and lower utility bills, better performance and a reduced risk of leaks or breakdowns. Newer models often have helpful features and longer warranties, improving comfort and safety in your home.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but usually have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    Manufacturer tank warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater annually is recommended in most homes. Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is generally soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from damaging heating elements.

    FHN Plumbing installs and services all major brands, including Bradford White, Rheem, AO Smith, Rinnai, Navien and State Water Heaters. We can help you choose the best brand and model that suits your budget and hot water demands.

    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take more time.

    Most new water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components, depending on the model and brand. FHN Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, so your investment is covered.
