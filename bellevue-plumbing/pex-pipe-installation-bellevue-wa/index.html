    A whole-home PEX repipe typically costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on home size, bathroom count and access. Smaller jobs like a single bathroom remodel rough-in often run $800 to $2,500.

    Small repairs are possible for handy homeowners, but a full repipe requires proper sizing, support spacing and code compliance. Mistakes behind walls can cause hidden leaks and failed inspections.

    FHN Plumbing stands behind our PEX pipe installations with a full warranty on both labor and materials. Specific warranty details will be provided with your estimate, but you can be confident your new PEX system is installed correctly and will perform reliably for years to come in your Bellevue home.

    PEX can be used for outdoor applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light, which can break down the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should always be buried or otherwise shielded.

    While PEX is simpler to install than copper, professional installation is highly recommended. Correct sizing, routing and secure connections are crucial for preventing leaks and ensuring system longevity. Improper DIY installation can lead to costly water damage and code violations.

    While PEX installation can look straightforward, professional installation is strongly recommended, especially for whole-home repipes or main line work. Proper tools, techniques and adherence to local plumbing codes are crucial for a leak-free and safe system. DIY mistakes can lead to costly water damage and future repairs, so trusting FHN Plumbing for your Bellevue installation gives you peace of mind.

    PEX piping is designed to be highly durable and has an estimated lifespan of 50 years or more. Its resistance to corrosion, scaling and freeze damage contributes to its long-term reliability, making it a smart investment for your Bellevue home.
