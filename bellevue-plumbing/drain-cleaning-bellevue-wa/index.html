    Tree roots are attracted to the moisture and nutrients inside sewer lines. During dry periods, roots will seek out any tiny crack or loose joint in older pipes to reach water, then grow and expand, causing serious blockages. This is a common issue for homes with mature trees.

    We usually advise against chemical drain cleaners. While they might offer a temporary fix, they can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and are often ineffective against tough clogs like tree roots or heavy grease buildup. They can also pose health risks.

    Caustic and acid cleaners can damage older cast iron, soften plastic joints and harm the person using them. They also rarely clear the full clog. Mechanical cleaning is the safer choice and more effective.
