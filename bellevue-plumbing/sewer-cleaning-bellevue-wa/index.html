    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Cleaning in Bellevue

    Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to thoroughly clean the inside of your sewer pipes, removing grease, sludge, and roots. It is used for stubborn blockages, preventative maintenance, or when traditional snaking is not sufficient to restore full pipe diameter.

    Cleaning does not require a permit. Installing a cleanout or repairing the sewer typically does, and we handle that paperwork.

    Common signs include multiple drains backing up simultaneously, gurgling noises from toilets or drains, foul odors coming from drains, and water pooling in your yard, especially near the sewer line. If you notice any of these in your Bellevue home, it is time to call a professional.

    Drain cleaning typically refers to clearing clogs in smaller branch lines leading from individual fixtures, like sinks or tubs. Sewer cleaning, on the other hand, addresses blockages in the main sewer line that collects wastewater from all household drains and carries it to the municipal system. Sewer clogs affect all drains, while drain clogs are often isolated.

    Most standard sewer cleaning jobs can be done within 1 to 3 hours. However, more severe blockages, such as those caused by extensive tree roots or significant pipe damage, may require more time and possibly additional services.
