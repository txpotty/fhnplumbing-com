    How We Repair Basement Leaks in Bellevue, WA

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Bellevue, WA

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing, cleaning the wall, and applying a membrane with a drainage board over it. It is the most thorough fix and the most disruptive one. Expect a few days of work. Our crew handles the footing drain and its outlet, and a waterproofing partner applies the membrane.

    Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Bellevue

    A floor drain that backs up when the washer drains or during a storm usually points to a blockage downstream or a failed backwater valve. We cable or jet the line, then camera it to confirm. Where the city sewer surcharges in storms, a backwater valve on the building drain keeps sewage from coming up through the floor drain, and it needs its flapper cleaned once a year. Dry traps smell.

    Wall Leak Solutions in Bellevue, WA

    Damp walls without a crack often point to water soaking through porous block or concrete. A simple foil test tells you whether it is seepage or condensation: tape a square of foil to the wall for a day and see which side gets wet. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Seepage needs drainage outside or a channel inside.

    Crack Injection for Basements in Bellevue

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. Ports go along the crack, the face gets sealed, and resin goes in from the bottom up until it fills the full depth. Epoxy adds strength. For a crack that is actively wet, polyurethane works better since it reacts with water.

    Emergency Basement Flooding Response in Bellevue

    A burst pipe or a failed sump during a storm can put inches of water on the floor in minutes. Call us first, then move belongings off the floor. Stay away from wet outlets. Water near an outlet or a gas appliance means stay out until the power is shut off.

    Commercial Basement Waterproofing in Bellevue

    Commercial basements hold inventory, records or mechanical rooms that cannot sit in water. We look at floor drains, elevator pits and sump systems as one system, since a single failed check valve or a clogged pit inlet can flood the whole lower level. Duplex pumps help. A high-water alarm warns staff before the floor gets wet.

    Groundwater Pressure Relief in Bellevue

    High groundwater pushes up through the slab as well as in through the walls. Water rising through floor cracks or around a floor drain during wet weather is the usual sign. Under-slab drain pipe tied to a sump lowers the water level beneath the floor. It is not a quick job.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Bellevue

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. We camera the tile from the sump inlet to see where it is blocked. A jetter usually opens it. If the old clay tile has collapsed, it gets replaced with new perforated pipe.
