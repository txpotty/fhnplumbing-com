    Liberty Pumps Installation in Bellevue, WA

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. Their 250 and 280 series cover most residential lifts, and the vertical float version fits narrow pits where a wide tethered float would rub. Parts are easy to find. A small weep hole in the discharge pipe keeps the pump from air locking.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Bellevue

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. That saves some labor. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Bellevue

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. For those jobs we install duplex systems where two pumps alternate, with a control panel and a high water alarm that can tie into the building monitoring. A flooded elevator pit shuts down a building. Work is scheduled around your operating hours.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Bellevue

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. We install exterior basins with pumps for those spots. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. The outlet sits well clear of the foundation and the property line.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Bellevue

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. Lids close flat over them. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Bellevue, WA

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. People with carpet downstairs buy these. They are worth it anywhere a few inches of water would mean new drywall.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Bellevue, WA

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Bellevue

    Sump pump installation in Bellevue, WA runs about $800 to $2,500 for most homes. The pump type and whether we dig a new pit move the price most. Backups add to it. You get a written price after we look at the space.

    Discharge should end at least ten feet from the foundation, on ground that slopes away. Plenty of cities ban discharge into the sanitary sewer.

    Most standard installs take three to six hours. If we have to cut the slab, dig a new pit and patch the concrete, plan on most of a day, with the patch curing overnight. Swaps go faster.

    Swapping a pump in an existing pit takes about two to three hours. New pit installs can run all day.

    Odd noises, rust, constant cycling or a pump that fails to start are the common signs. Any pump past seven years is worth a closer look, even if it seems fine, because the switch is usually the first part to go. An inspection settles it.

    Grinding, rattling or a pump that runs with no water in the pit are all warning signs. Visible rust, a float that sticks or a pump that cycles every minute during dry weather usually means the end is near. A flooded basement settles it.

    A submersible sits in the pit under water, so it runs quieter and takes no floor space. The pedestal type mounts the motor on a column above the water, which makes it easier to reach for service and usually cheaper to buy, though you will hear it. Either can do the job. The pit and the noise you can live with decide it.

    Plenty of cities require a plumbing permit for new sump pump installs or major replacements. FHN Plumbing handles the permit in Bellevue, WA so the work passes inspection.
