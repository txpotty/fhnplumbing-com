    Slab Leak Repiping in Bellevue

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Steep slopes and wet soils can move over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    Commercial Repipe Services in Bellevue

    Older commercial buildings in Bellevue often have galvanized risers, copper that has pinholed or plastic pipe that no longer meets code. Our crews swap out risers, branches and drains with as little interruption to tenants as the building allows. Shutoffs get scheduled ahead. We plan the work by zone and handle keys, access and quiet hours with your manager.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Bellevue

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Clogged aerators are a clue. New PEX or copper stops the rust, restores pressure and ends leaks at the threads, which is where galvanized fails first. We disconnect and abandon the old pipe where removing it would mean tearing out finished walls.

    Residential Repiping Plans in Bellevue

    Every house is piped a little differently, so a repipe starts with a walk-through. Our estimator counts fixtures, checks access, finds the main and asks about any planned remodels before choosing a route. Then we size the lines. Bigger homes may need a 1-inch trunk so the shower does not drop when the dishwasher fills.

    Commercial Repiping in Bellevue, WA

    Commercial buildings in Bellevue, WA often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day. Our crew breaks commercial repipes into zones, working one riser or suite at a time while the rest of the building keeps water. Night work is possible. We use Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX sized from fixture units and installed to commercial code.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Bellevue

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. Type L tolerates UV, stays rigid and has decades of field history. It costs more. Our plumbers solder joints with lead-free solder or use press fittings, support the pipe at the spacing the code calls for and pressure test the system before any wall is closed.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Bellevue, WA

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. The pipe gets brittle from the inside, and fittings crack, so a home that has had one polybutylene leak is usually at risk everywhere else in the system. Full replacement is the fix. Many insurers ask about it.

    Drain Line Repiping in Bellevue

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Slow drains in several fixtures, sewer odors and repeated clogs in the same line are common signs of a failing drain system. A camera shows the condition. Failing sections are replaced with PVC or ABS sloped and vented to code, which drains faster and resists buildup much better than the pipe it replaces.
