    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Spokane Valley

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. Sizing comes first. Your test result and water use decide the tank size.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Spokane Valley

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. It complements a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Spokane Valley

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. Each install follows the brand's specs for drain, bypass and power, then we program it to your water test. We handle all of it. Existing loops make it faster.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Spokane Valley

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Soft water reaches every fixture. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Spokane Valley, WA

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Spokane Valley, WA

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Newer valves meter actual water use, some connect to Wi-Fi for salt alerts and higher-capacity tanks regenerate less often for larger households. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Spokane Valley

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. That pays back over time. We install several models.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Spokane Valley

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. Every salt-based install includes a sized resin tank, a brine tank, a bypass and a drain to an air gap. Programming is the last step. Regeneration time is usually set for 2 a.m.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Spokane Valley

    Water softener installation in Spokane Valley, WA usually runs $1,500 to $4,000. Where you land depends on the type and size of the system and whether we need to change any plumbing. New loops cost more. We quote after an on-site test.

    Salt-based softeners add a small amount of sodium. Plenty of homeowners add an RO system at the kitchen sink for drinking and cooking.

    A good softener usually lasts ten to fifteen years. Unit quality, how hard your water is and how well the system is maintained all change that, and resin can be replaced to extend it. Yearly checks help.

    Adding salt every 4 to 8 weeks is most of the work. Once a year a plumber should check for salt bridges, worn seals and injector clogs. Do not let the salt run out.
