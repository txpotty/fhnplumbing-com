    Jet Pump System Repair in Spokane Valley, WA

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. FHN Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Spokane Valley operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Spokane Valley, WA

    Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive typically shows a code that guides diagnosis. We troubleshoot drives, sensors and wiring and repair or replace components. These systems need a smaller pressure tank but careful setup.

    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Spokane Valley

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a frustrating problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself. This could be due to a failing pressure switch, a partially clogged well screen, a leak in the supply line, or even a pump that is no longer meeting demand. Our team conducts careful diagnostics to pinpoint the exact cause of low pressure in your Spokane Valley home, delivering a targeted and effective repair solution.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Spokane Valley, WA

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives steadier pressure at the tap.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Spokane Valley, WA

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Heavy, prolonged rain from atmospheric river storms is a major factor in western Washington, raising groundwater and filling crawlspaces and basements. Lightning and power surges frequently damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help reduce repeat failures.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Spokane Valley

    The pressure tank works with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. Issues like a waterlogged tank, sudden pressure drops, or continuous pump operation usually point to a faulty pressure tank. In Spokane Valley, we repair or replace tanks to improve your well system's performance and extend pump life.
