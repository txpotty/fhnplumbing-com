    Root Intrusion Repair in Spokane Valley, WA

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause.     Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Spokane Valley

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease is the usual culprit.     Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Spokane Valley

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Spokane Valley, WA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Waste collects in the dip. Glacial till and dense hardpan are common in western Washington, and they drain poorly enough to keep water against foundations and in crawlspaces. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Spokane Valley

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. The crack lets soil in and sewage out. Most of these are one-day jobs.
