    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Spokane Valley

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Spokane Valley

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Clearances, maximum vent lengths and relief valve rules differ by brand, and warranty claims get denied when an installer skipped them. Our crews install to spec. Your warranty stays intact.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Spokane Valley

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Carbon monoxide is the risk. We follow code on all of it.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Spokane Valley

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. A leak lands on drywall. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Spokane Valley, WA

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Spokane Valley

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. We test draft before leaving. Big families do well with gas.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Spokane Valley

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. Sizing is based on the first-hour rating on the yellow label, which tells you how much hot water the tank can deliver during a busy hour. Gas or electric both work. We connect the fuel, set the vent and haul the old tank away.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Spokane Valley, WA

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Rebates may apply. We will tell you if your space works.
