    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Spokane Valley, WA

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Spokane Valley, WA

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. Guessing costs money. The camera feed shows roots, grease, breaks and bellies in real time, and the locator marks the spot on the ground above.

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Spokane Valley

    Roots come back after cutting. Usually within a year or so. A scheduled cleaning every year or two keeps roots and grease from closing the line and lets you plan around it.

    Commercial Sewer Cleaning in Spokane Valley, WA

    Commercial lines take a beating. Restaurants fight grease every day. In offices and shops, paper and odd objects flushed in restrooms are the usual cause of a stopped lateral, and our heavy jetters handle 6 inch commercial pipe without much trouble.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Spokane Valley

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Both have weak spots. Roots love clay joints, while Orangeburg fiber pipe tends to squash into an oval and eventually collapse, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Spokane Valley, WA

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. Every drain shares that one pipe. We clear it with a sectional cable or a jetter, then camera the line to see whether the cause was roots, debris or a structural problem like a broken joint or an offset section.

    Jetting a Sewer Line in Spokane Valley, WA

    Grease and scale are jetting work. Cables leave them behind. A jetter nozzle with rear-facing jets pulls itself down the line and blasts the buildup off the pipe wall, restoring close to the full diameter of the pipe and slowing the next clog.
