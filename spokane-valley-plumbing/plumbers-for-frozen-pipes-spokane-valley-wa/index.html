    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbers for Frozen Pipes in Spokane Valley

    Costs range from about $200 for simple thawing to $1,500 or more for a burst pipe inside a finished wall. Where the pipe sits matters most. Open crawlspace lines are quick, and ceiling repairs take more time and drywall work.

    No water at one faucet after a cold night is the classic sign. Frost on the pipe confirms it. Feel the pipe where it runs through a cold space, and if one section is much colder than the rest or you can see a bulge, that is likely where the ice is sitting.

    Keep cold air away from the pipe. Insulation and air sealing do most of the work. Close vents, insulate exposed runs, install frost-free hose bibs and add heat cable where needed.

    Pipe location matters more than brand. Copper and galvanized split first. Metal pipe in cold spots splits most, and PEX often survives except at the fittings.

    Many policies cover sudden water damage from a burst pipe, though coverage varies. Read your policy. We provide photos and a detailed invoice to support your claim, and some policies exclude unheated homes.
