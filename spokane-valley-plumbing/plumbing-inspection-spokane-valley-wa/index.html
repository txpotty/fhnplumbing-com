    Water Heater Inspections in Spokane Valley

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. We read the serial number for age, look for rust at the fittings and the base, check the venting and test the T and P relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. Gas heaters get a look at the flame, the drip leg and the vent draft, since a poorly venting heater is a safety issue.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Spokane Valley, WA

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. Old brass connectors, missing shutoffs or drip legs and rust all get noted, and we sweep joints with a gas detector. Leaks get reported immediately. Older homes are where we find the most issues, usually at appliance connectors that have been there since the house was built.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Spokane Valley

    Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is generally soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. Signs include crusted aerators, clogged showerheads, slow toilet fill and heater sediment, and we can test hardness during the visit. You get options, not a pitch. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Spokane Valley

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue. Early slab leak finds save floors and foundations.
