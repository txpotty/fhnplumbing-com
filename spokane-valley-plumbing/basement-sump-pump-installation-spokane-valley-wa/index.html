    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Spokane Valley

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit usually runs $500 to $1,200 including the pump and check valve. A brand new pit through the slab falls between $1,500 and $3,500, and a battery backup adds roughly $600 to $1,500. Most people add the backup.

    Look for a pump that runs nonstop, short cycles, or does not start when the pit fills. Visible rust and a stuck float are other red flags, and if your basement flooded even though you have a pump, the pump or its switch was not up to the job. Age matters too.

    Plan on 4 to 8 hours for a standard install. Digging a new pit and patching the slab can take the whole day, and tricky electrical access or a long discharge run through a finished wall adds time on top of that. You get a time estimate first.

    Grinding or rattling during a cycle is the first warning. Rust on the housing and constant on-off cycling are others, along with a pump that does not start during a heavy rain even though the pit is full to the float. Past ten years, replace it.

    A straight pump replacement usually does not need a permit. New pits, new discharge routes and new circuits may, so we look up the local rules and file for a permit when one is needed.

    Swapping an identical plug-in pump is doable for a handy homeowner. Cutting a pit, wiring a new outlet and routing discharge are not, because a mistake there leads to a cracked slab, a shock hazard, or water pumped right back against the foundation in Spokane Valley. Those parts call for a licensed plumber.

    We strongly recommend professional installation. You are dealing with wiring, pipe and concrete in one job, and a bad seal on the lid, an undersized pit or a discharge that drains toward the house can leave you worse off than having no pump at all. Mistakes show up in storms.

    Mostly cast iron pumps from Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty Pumps and Little Giant. All four have parts at local supply houses, which matters when a switch fails on a Sunday and you want the pump running again without waiting a week for an order to ship. Common models ride on our trucks.
