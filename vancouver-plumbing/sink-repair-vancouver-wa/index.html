    Burst Supply Line Repair in Vancouver

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Then lay towels down. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Vancouver, WA

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Code calls for a trap seal between 2 and 4 inches, and a trap that leaks, sags or was cut down by a past owner can lose that seal and let the smell in. Bad traps get a new matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Vancouver

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and protecting hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. UV light cracks plastic traps and supply lines left outdoors, and dirt from the yard clogs the drain. We fix leaks at the hose bib too. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Vancouver, WA

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We run a camera or a longer cable into the wall arm and look for a sagging line or buildup where the sink ties into the tub drain. Sometimes the vent is blocked. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Vancouver

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down the drain all day, and the trap behind the cabinet door may not get looked at until it overflows onto the carpet. We clear it and add a strainer. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Vancouver

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. When the epoxy under the counter fails, the bowl drops a quarter inch, the caulk line splits, and water from the counter runs straight down into the cabinet. New clips go in and the bowl gets pulled tight. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.
