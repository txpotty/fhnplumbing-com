    Sewer Pipe Relining in Vancouver, WA

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Vancouver.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Vancouver

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's far more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and stopping recurring clogs. This service is particularly helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Vancouver dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with recurring drain issues.
