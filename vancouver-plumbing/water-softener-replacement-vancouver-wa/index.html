    Picking the Right Softener for Vancouver, WA Water

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Vancouver

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico runs them without electricity, using water flow to drive the valve. The price is higher.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Vancouver, WA

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. What they do well is keep scale off pipe walls and heater elements. They need no drain line. Swapping to a salt-based unit is an option too.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Vancouver

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break them up and clean out the mush at the bottom. Cracked tanks and stuck safety floats get replaced. Use pellet salt.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Vancouver, WA

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Washington homes. An old Fleck with discontinued parts usually gets replaced, valve and tank together. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Vancouver

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. Sometimes a carbon filter goes ahead of it.

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in Vancouver

    Restaurants and laundromats run softeners hard, and a commercial unit often needs a 1.5-inch or 2-inch valve and twin tanks to keep up with flow. We size it to peak gallons per minute. We work around your business hours.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in Vancouver, WA

    Swapping a salt-based softener in Washington homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. We pressurize and check every joint. The drain line gets an air gap.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Vancouver, WA

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. It is programmed before we pack up.
