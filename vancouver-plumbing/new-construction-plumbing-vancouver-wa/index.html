    Underground and Under-Slab Plumbing in Vancouver

    Underground work sets the drain, waste and vent layout that every fixture in the house relies on. Steep slopes and wet soils can move over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. Each stub gets measured off the form boards so it comes through the slab right where the wall will be. Two inches off puts a drain under a plate. Before the pour we water test the whole system with a test ball and a standpipe.

    Gas Piping for New Homes in Vancouver

    We size gas piping for ranges, water heaters, furnaces and fireplaces, plus pool heaters and outdoor kitchens. Each appliance gets a shutoff within six feet of it. Sediment traps go at the furnace and water heater inlets, and the whole system holds a test on a gauge for the inspector before the meter is set. The test runs at least ten minutes.

    Residential New Construction Plumbing in Vancouver

    Layout drives cost on a house. Grouping the kitchen, laundry and baths along one wet wall can cut a few hundred feet of pipe out of a plan. We point out those savings during plan review. Moving a toilet on paper is free, and moving it after the pour means a saw and a jackhammer.

    Plumbing Rough-In Services in Vancouver

    Rough-in covers every pipe that ends up inside a wall or floor. We drill joists and studs within the limits the framing code allows, which usually means holes no bigger than 40 percent of a stud's width in a bearing wall. Vents go up through the roof. Every trap arm is sized and sloped for the distance to its vent.

    Rough-In Plumbing Services in Vancouver

    Top-out is the second half of rough-in, after framing. We run supply lines, drains and vents through the walls and attic and set every stub-out to the cabinet drawings. Then the system gets tested. Drains get a water test, filled to the highest fitting, and supply lines hold pressure on a gauge while the inspector walks it.

    Sewer and Drainage Systems in New Builds in Vancouver

    The building drain under a new house usually runs 4-inch PVC at a quarter inch per foot out to the sewer or septic connection. Steep slopes and wet soils can shift over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. Sand or gravel bedding keeps the line on grade while the trench settles. Cleanouts go where code calls for them. A two-way cleanout outside the foundation saves pulling a toilet every time the line needs a cable.

    Gas Line Installation for New Construction in Vancouver, WA

    Gas piping is sized from the total BTU load and the longest run, using the tables in the fuel gas code. An undersized line shows up later as a tankless heater that errors out or a range that will not hold a flame. We install black iron or CSST to the plan. Any CSST run is bonded to the grounding electrode system per the manufacturer.

    Gas Line Installation for New Homes in Vancouver

    Natural gas or propane, the piping has to match the appliance list. Add up a range, a furnace and a tankless heater and you can pass 350,000 BTU, which often means a 1-1/4 inch main. We size off the full list. Stubs for a future grill or fire pit are cheap now.
