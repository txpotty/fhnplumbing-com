    Repiping Work We Handle in Vancouver, WA

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Vancouver

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Clogged aerators are a clue. New PEX or copper stops the rust, restores pressure and ends leaks at the threads, which is where galvanized fails first. Old pipe stays in walls when pulling it would wreck finished surfaces.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Vancouver, WA

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. We replace the full system with PEX or copper. Insurers often ask about it at renewal.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Vancouver, WA

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average house often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 once access and restoration are included. Do the math. By the second or third leak, a repipe usually pays for itself, and we put both options side by side so you can decide.

    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Vancouver, WA

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. For a typical house, copper often runs 20 to 40 percent above PEX, though some buyers and homeowners like the look and feel of metal pipe and are willing to pay for it. Both prices include permits, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    Commercial Repiping in Vancouver, WA

    Commercial buildings in Vancouver, WA often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day. We phase commercial repipes by floor, suite or riser so most of the building stays in service while we work. Night work is possible. Materials are usually Type L copper or commercial PEX, sized to the fixture count and installed to commercial code.

    Residential Repiping Plans in Vancouver

    Every house is piped a little differently, so a repipe starts with a walk-through. We count fixtures, check attic and crawlspace access, look at where the main comes in and ask about plans for future remodels before we draw a route. Then we size the lines. A three-bath house with a tankless heater may need 1-inch main trunk lines to keep showers steady when the washer kicks on.

    Copper Repiping in Vancouver, WA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings. Expect a slightly longer job. Most copper jobs run a day longer than PEX, since each turn needs its own fitting.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Vancouver

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Both still turn up in older homes. We can usually identify it from the pipe at the water heater, and full replacement makes the house easier to insure and sell.
