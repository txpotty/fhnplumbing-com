    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in Vancouver

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon gas water heater typically costs $1,800 to $4,000 installed. Power vent and tankless gas units often cost more due to venting and gas line requirements.

    Installing a gas water heater involves working with natural gas lines, water lines and exhaust venting, which can be dangerous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning or water damage. It is highly recommended to hire a licensed professional for safety and code compliance.

    Rusty water, leaks, pilot problems and slow recovery are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement may be the better option.

    We install all major reputable brands of gas water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A. O. Smith, State Water Heaters and Navien for tankless models. We can help you choose the best brand and model for your particular needs and budget.

    Most new gas water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components. FHN Plumbing also provides a warranty on our installation labor. We will explain both during your consultation.

    Most standard gas water heater installations take between 3 and 5 hours. More complex installations, such as converting to a tankless unit or significant gas line adjustments, might take a full day. We work quickly to minimize disruption to your home.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for gas water heater installation. We pull permits when required and make sure the work passes inspection.

    It's recommended to have your gas water heater professionally serviced annually. Regular maintenance can extend its lifespan, improve efficiency and catch potential issues before they become major problems, ensuring safe operation.
