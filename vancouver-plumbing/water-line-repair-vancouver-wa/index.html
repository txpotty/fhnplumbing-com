    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Vancouver, WA

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a simple spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with actual information.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Vancouver

    Copper pipes are known for their durability and long life, but they can still develop leaks due to corrosion, excessive water pressure, or physical damage. In Vancouver, we often see pinhole leaks in older copper lines. Our skilled plumbers are adept at repairing copper water lines, whether it involves soldering a small patch or replacing a damaged section. We ensure all repairs meet current plumbing standards for a reliable, leak-free connection.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Vancouver, WA

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can work loose those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is in charge of the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve in the same visit.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Vancouver, WA

    A full break can flood a yard, wash out soil under walkways and leave your home without water.     Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Repair in Vancouver

    A spot repair on an accessible service line usually runs $350 to $1,500 depending on depth and access. Full replacement from meter to house usually costs $1,800 to $5,500, and trenchless replacement often falls between $3,000 and $7,000. We give you a written price before starting.

    Many policies do not cover the pipe itself but may cover resulting damage to your home. Some homeowners carry separate service line coverage through their utility or insurer. We provide photos and an itemized invoice to support your claim.

    Common signs include a wet or unusually green patch of yard, a hissing sound near the meter, low pressure at every fixture and a water bill that climbs without explanation. Dirty or rusty water can also signal a damaged line. If your meter moves with all fixtures off, you likely have a leak.

    Most spot repairs are finished in two to four hours. A full replacement usually takes one day, and trenchless jobs are often done in a day as well. Water is typically back on the same day.

    Full service line replacement typically requires a plumbing permit and inspection from your city or county. Small spot repairs often do not. We pull the permit and schedule the inspection when needed.

    Our plumbers are experienced with all common water line materials found in Vancouver, including PEX, copper, PVC, and older galvanized steel pipes. We check the existing material and recommend the best repair or replacement option for durability and compliance with modern standards. We stock parts for all these materials to ensure efficient repairs.

    Trenchless water line repair is a method of repairing or replacing underground pipes without extensive digging. It involves creating small access points and using specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one (pipe bursting) or insert a new lining into the existing pipe (pipe lining). This method saves landscaping and reduces repair time.

    While some very minor, accessible leaks might seem DIY-friendly, we strongly advise against attempting water line repairs yourself, especially for underground or main lines. Improper repairs can lead to greater damage, code violations, and costly future problems. Professional plumbers have the specialized tools, experience, and knowledge of Washington plumbing codes to ensure a safe and lasting repair.
