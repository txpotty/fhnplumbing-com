    Plan on one to three hours. Simple swaps take about an hour, and corroded hardware or new shutoff valves can stretch that to two or three hours. We tell you up front.

    Plenty of homeowners manage it. Seized mounting nuts and corroded shutoffs in a cramped cabinet are what usually make the job harder than people expect it to be. We can do it fast without harming the sink.

    A basic swap is doable with the right tools. The trouble starts under the sink, where seized mounting nuts and corroded shutoffs can turn a short job into a cracked sink or a flooded cabinet. That is a common call for us.

    Worn cartridges and O-rings cause most leaks. Many rural Washington homes rely on private wells, and some carry iron, manganese or other minerals that call for testing and treatment.
