    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Services in Vancouver

    The cost of drain cleaning in Vancouver can vary widely based on the complexity of the clog and the method required. Simple drain snaking might range from $150 to $350, while hydro jetting or main sewer line clearing could be $400 to $800 or more. We provide a detailed estimate after diagnosing the issue.

    Routine drain cleaning and jetting do not require permits. Sewer line repair or replacement usually does, and we handle those permits.

    Hair, grease, soap scum and hard water scale cause most fixture clogs. Main line clogs are often caused by wipes, tree roots or a belly in the pipe.

    Tree roots are naturally drawn to the moisture and nutrients inside sewer lines, and dry spells can make them seek out any available water source. Tiny cracks or loose joints in older sewer pipes provide an entry point, allowing roots to grow and eventually block the line. Crawlspaces and basements are both common in Washington homes, and a wet crawlspace is often the first sign of a drainage or plumbing problem. Shifting soil can also contribute to pipe movement, creating these access points.

    When it comes to drain services, our focus is on clearing and repairing the pipes themselves, rather than specific fixture brands. However, our technicians are familiar with all major plumbing fixture brands like Moen, Delta, Kohler, and American Standard, and can address any issues related to their drain assemblies during service.

    While readily available, we usually advise against using chemical drain cleaners. They can be corrosive, potentially damaging your pipes, especially older ones, and are often ineffective against tough clogs like tree roots or heavy grease. They also pose a risk to your health and the environment. Professional methods are safer and more effective.

    Avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens in sinks and showers to catch hair and food particles, and be mindful of what gets flushed down toilets. Regular preventative maintenance, like an annual professional drain cleaning, can also be highly effective.

    Common signs include slow draining sinks or tubs, gurgling noises from drains, foul odors coming from pipes, water backing up in sinks or toilets, and multiple fixtures draining slowly. If you notice any of these, it's wise to call a professional before the problem escalates to a full blockage or a sewage backup.
