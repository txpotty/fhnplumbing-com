    Gas and Electric Tank Checks in Vancouver, WA

    Gas and electric tanks share the same steel shell, but the parts that wear out are different. Gas units get a burner, thermocouple and vent check. On electric units we meter both elements and check each thermostat terminal for scorching. Both get a flush.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Vancouver, WA

    The anode rod is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the steel tank does not. Many rural Washington homes rely on private wells, and some carry iron, manganese or other minerals that call for testing and treatment. We loosen the hex head with a breaker bar and an impact gun, pull the rod and look at how much core wire is showing. Six inches of bare wire means replace it.

    Standard Tank Flushing in Vancouver

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. Hard water makes it more urgent. We drain, partly refill and drain again until the hose runs clear, sometimes four or five rounds. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Relief Valve Testing in Vancouver

    Temperature and pressure relief valves on residential tanks are typically rated to open at 150 psi or 210 degrees, and they can stick shut after years of mineral buildup. Our tech lifts the lever, lets water flow, then watches it close. If it keeps dripping, we replace it. We also confirm the discharge pipe runs full size to within six inches of the floor or to an approved drain, which is what plumbing codes ask for.

    Rental and Commercial Tank Logs in Vancouver

    Landlords and property managers with a dozen units need to know which tank is next to fail. We log every tank we service. The log shows the serial date, anode condition, the last relief valve test and a rough estimate of years left, so you can plan replacements during a vacancy instead of on a Saturday night. We service 30 to 100 gallon tanks in rentals.

    Electric Thermostat Service in Vancouver

    Each element on an electric tank has its own thermostat, and the upper one carries a reset button for the high-limit switch. When the reset trips, something caused it. We find out why it tripped, which usually comes down to a shorted element or a thermostat stuck closed, before we press it back in. Power stays off at the breaker the whole time.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Vancouver

    Calcium carbonate drops out of hard water every time the tank heats, and over a year it can harden into chunks the size of a thumbnail. That is what makes the popping noise. When a plain flush will not move them, we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems, let it sit and then flush it all out. A clean bottom puts less heat stress on the seam.

    T&P Valve Replacement in Vancouver

    Replacing a relief valve is a short job, but it has to be done right. The new valve must match the tank's BTU or kilowatt rating. We drain the tank below the valve opening, thread in the new one with pipe dope rated for potable water, and reconnect a discharge pipe in copper, CPVC or another approved material. A capped discharge pipe is a serious hazard.
