    Burner and Pilot Light Maintenance in Vancouver, WA

    For gas hot water tanks, proper burner and pilot light operation is key for efficient heating. Our service includes cleaning the burner assembly to remove dust and debris that can impede combustion. We also check and adjust the pilot light, ensuring it ignites reliably. This attention to detail prevents common issues like intermittent hot water or complete heating failure, which can be stressful for homeowners in Vancouver, WA.

    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Vancouver, WA

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial defense against corrosion. Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's badly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Vancouver, WA, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Vancouver

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Vancouver

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Many families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.
