    Pipe Bursting in Vancouver, WA

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is ideal for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It usually requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Vancouver

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot get into. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their shape.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Vancouver, WA

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Washington homeowners.

This involves inserting a high-resolution camera into the pipe to accurately diagnose the problem's exact location and nature. This key step makes sure we choose the most appropriate and effective trenchless method for your Vancouver property, avoiding guesswork.
