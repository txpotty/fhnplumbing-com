    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Leak Repair in Vancouver

    Basement plumbing leak repairs in Vancouver typically cost $200 to $1,500, depending on the pipe and how hard it is to reach. Exposed pipe on an unfinished ceiling is cheap.     Most plumbing leak repairs in a basement are done in one visit. With wall access, plan on two to four hours. Seepage work ranges from half a day for one crack to several days for a perimeter drain.

    For sump pumps we mostly install Zoeller and Liberty, with Wayne for some battery backups. We prefer cast iron pump housings. Injection resins and drain membranes come from commercial suppliers, matched so the parts fit.

    Yes, every repair carries a written workmanship warranty. Sump pumps and parts add the manufacturer's warranty. Seepage warranty terms depend on the repair method, and we spell them out before you sign.

    Crack repairs use two-part polyurethane or epoxy injection resin, depending on whether the crack is actively wet. Polyurethane handles wet cracks. Our interior drains use rigid perforated pipe, which keeps its slope and can be jetted later.

    Tightening a loose fitting or replacing a washer hose is a reasonable DIY task. Cast iron stack cracks are another matter. Slab leaks and sump wiring need a pro, since a mistake can flood the floor or create a shock risk.

    Leak repair stops a specific source, like a cracked pipe or a leaking wall crack. Waterproofing is broader. It handles groundwater all around the foundation and makes sense when water comes in at many points.
