    Water Softener Installs in Vancouver, WA

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Vancouver

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. Sizing comes first. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Vancouver

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. Pairs well with a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Vancouver

    Testing is what makes the settings right. Our crew checks hardness, iron and chlorine, then sets the regeneration and salt dosage to match. Iron adds to the setting. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Vancouver

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. That pays back over time. We install several models.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Vancouver, WA

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. Our crew pulls the old system, checks the loop and drain line and installs a model matched to your water today. We haul away the old one. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Vancouver

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Little upkeep is needed. They suit moderate hardness.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Vancouver

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. We follow the Ecodyne manual for the bypass, drain and overflow connections, then program hardness and regeneration settings for your water. We install store-bought units. Programming is part of the job.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Vancouver, WA

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. The savings add up. We swap it out in a few hours.
