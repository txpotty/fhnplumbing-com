    Copper Leak Repair Jobs We Handle in Vancouver, WA

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Vancouver

    Pinholes start inside the pipe where the copper wall pits and thins. The first sign is often a spot. They are small and easy to miss behind drywall, which is why we use thermal imaging to find them before we open anything. We replace the pitted length with Type L. We look at the copper around it.

    Copper Pipe Joint Leak Repair in Vancouver, WA

    Joints leak from poor original soldering, thermal expansion or a house that settles. Steep slopes and wet soils can move over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. A crusty fitting is the clue. We inspect each joint near the leak for weeping or mineral buildup, and either resolder it properly or swap the fitting for a new one. We test before closing up.

    Split Copper Line Repair in Vancouver, WA

    A copper line can split from a freeze, a settling foundation or a hit from a nail. Water comes fast. Our plumber isolates the water source, cuts out the split and solders in new pipe, while checking nearby pipe for damage from the same cause. We protect the pipe afterward.

    After-Hours Copper Leak Repair in Vancouver

    Copper leaks like to show up at night. We still answer. An after-hours plumber in Vancouver can stop the water, find the leak and fix it the same night using parts already on the truck. Larger repairs get scheduled for morning.

    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Vancouver, WA

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Temperature drives it. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, so we repair leaking hot lines and can add a timer or adjust the pump to cut down on wear. Less run time, less wear.

    Commercial Copper Pipe Leak Services in Vancouver, WA

    In a commercial building, one copper leak can affect several tenants at once. Planning matters. We isolate the smallest zone possible and repair the leak with press fittings or solder, and we schedule the shutdown around business hours so customers and tenants are not affected. Records are available for managers.

    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Vancouver, WA

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and covering hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. Close the main valve. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, so a pipe that froze overnight may start spraying the next afternoon once the house warms up. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Insulation goes on after.

We look at exposed copper for green crust and pitting, test static pressure, check the bond wire at the water heater and main, then scan suspect walls with a thermal camera on homes with a leak history. We write up what we find.
