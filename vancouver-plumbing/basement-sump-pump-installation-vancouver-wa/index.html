    Some lots sit in a high water table and fill a standard pit every few minutes during spring. For those, we step up to a 1/2 or 3/4 horsepower unit in a bigger basin, all sized after we time how fast your pit refills. We use a stopwatch.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Vancouver

    Duplex systems with two pumps and an alternating panel are standard for parking garages and multi-unit buildings with below-grade levels in Vancouver. The panel swaps the lead pump every cycle, so wear stays even. Staff get a high water alarm. We coordinate the wiring with your electrician.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Vancouver

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. The motor stays dry and you can reach it without pulling the pump, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Vancouver homes. They are louder, though.

    Sump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Vancouver

    The basin sets the ceiling on what the pump can do. It goes at the lowest point in the slab, bed it in washed stone, and run the discharge line with a slight pitch to the exit point so nothing sits in the pipe in Vancouver. Nothing should drain back toward the wall.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Vancouver

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. Without house power, a battery backup keeps a second pump going, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We test it under load.
