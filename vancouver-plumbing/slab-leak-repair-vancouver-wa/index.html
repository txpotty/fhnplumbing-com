    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Vancouver, WA

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. The pipe swells and shrinks against rock every time hot water flows, and a recirculation pump keeps that going constantly, thinning the wall. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Vancouver, WA

    Leaks under a load-bearing wall or a kitchen island are hard to reach from inside. Tunneling solves that. A pit next to the foundation lets the crew tunnel under the slab by hand to the pipe, with shoring as needed, so your interior floors and cabinets stay untouched. Many Vancouver, WA homes are good candidates.

    Residential Slab Leak Repair in Vancouver

    Working in an occupied home means planning around your day. Drop cloths go down first. Dust gets contained with plastic sheeting, the cut area is kept small, and the water stays on for the rest of the house while we work on one line, so you can still cook and shower. Vancouver homeowners get a clean site at the end.

    Slab Leak Detection in Vancouver

    The first test is the water meter. Every fixture gets shut off. A meter that keeps turning tells us to isolate hot from cold, test each side and walk the losing line with a ground mic. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Vancouver

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. Rerouting ends that. New PEX runs through the attic and down the walls while the old pipe stays buried, with openings placed behind vanities or in closets.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Vancouver

    Locating comes before cutting. The main tools are a ground microphone and thermal imager. With the mic catching the hiss through the concrete and the camera showing warm water spreading, we can usually narrow the leak to about a foot. We test hot lines, cold lines and drains in your Vancouver home.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Vancouver, WA

    Spot repairs fit leaks in otherwise good pipe. It is a single small opening. We open the floor over the leak, dig to the pipe, replace the bad section and pressure test it, then compact the trench and patch the concrete so your flooring contractor can finish the surface.

    Thermal and Acoustic Tools in Vancouver

    A thermal camera shows the warm streak a hot water leak leaves on the floor. Cold leaks need the mic. On cold lines we rely on an acoustic sensor that listens through the slab, marking the point where the sound is loudest. Both tools ride on every Vancouver truck.
