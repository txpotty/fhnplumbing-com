    Most simple toilet repairs, like a new flapper or fill valve, run roughly $125 to $275 including parts and labor. Resetting a toilet on a new wax ring is typically $175 to $350, and flange repairs can bring the total to $300 to $500. We give you a flat price before starting.

    Repeated toilet clogs can be caused by a variety of factors. These include flushing non-biodegradable items, excessive use of toilet paper, a low-flow toilet design that struggles with waste volume, or more serious issues like a partially blocked drain line, tree root intrusion in the sewer line, or even a damaged or improperly vented main stack.

    If the porcelain is sound and the toilet flushes well, a repair is generally the smart choice. If it uses 3.5 gallons or more per flush, clogs often or has a cracked tank, replacing it with a 1.28 gallon WaterSense model often makes more sense.

    To prevent future toilet problems, avoid flushing non-biodegradable items, use septic-safe toilet paper, and address small issues like a running toilet promptly. Regular plumbing maintenance and annual inspections can also help identify potential problems before they escalate.

    A failed wax ring or a loose, cracked flange is the usual cause. Condensation on the tank can also drip down and look like a base leak. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange and reset it on a fresh seal.
