    Clogged Toilet Solutions in Vancouver, WA

    Paper towels, wipes, dental floss and toys cause most of the repeat clogs we clear, and none of them break down the way toilet paper does. The closet auger usually hooks them and pulls them out.     Commercial Toilet Repair in Vancouver

    Office and retail restrooms take more flushes in a day than a house sees in a month, so the parts wear faster and fail harder.     Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Vancouver

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the china that compresses air with incoming water pressure, and the cartridge inside that vessel eventually wears out. Hardware stores rarely stock those parts. We carry Flushmate cartridges and dual-flush seals for the common TOTO and American Standard models, and we check house pressure, since these units need steady line pressure to flush properly. Low pressure makes them sound weak.

    Flush Mechanism Repair in Vancouver, WA

    A weak flush usually means the tank is not filling to the fill line, the chain has too much slack, or the flapper is closing before the tank empties. We bring the water up to the line stamped on the overflow tube and adjust the chain to about half an inch of slack. Handles break too. A cracked handle arm or a stripped mounting nut takes about five minutes to replace.

    Leaky Toilet Repair in Vancouver

    Food coloring in the tank is the cheapest test we run. If color shows up in the bowl within fifteen minutes without a flush, water is leaking past the flapper or the flush valve seat, and the toilet is quietly refilling itself all day and night. Most of the time a new flapper does it, unless the seat under it is pitted or cracked.

    Running Toilet Repair in Vancouver

    A tank that keeps hissing and refilling every few minutes is almost always losing water past the flapper or overfilling into the overflow tube. It adds up fast. We check the chain, the float height, the flapper and the seat before touching a part, then replace only what failed. Most running toilets are quiet again in about thirty minutes.

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Vancouver, WA

    Needing two flushes on a regular basis usually comes down to scale in the rim jets, a clogged siphon jet, a partial blockage in the trapway, or a flush valve that does not open all the way. A gurgle from a nearby sink or tub when you flush points to a vent problem. We clear the trap with an auger and treat the jets with a descaler.
