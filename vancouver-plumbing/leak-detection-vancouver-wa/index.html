    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Vancouver, WA

    Copper leaks are loud on a ground microphone. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. We handle both every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX are the two leaks we find most.

    Pool Leak Detection in Vancouver

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation likely has a leak somewhere. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, listen for leaks with a hydrophone underwater, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain to find the exact crack or fitting that is losing water.

    Fast Leak Response in Vancouver

    A sudden drop in pressure or water spreading across a floor needs someone fast. Close the main.     Leak Testing for Multifamily and Offices in Vancouver

    Multifamily buildings and office parks have long supply systems where leaks hide for a long time. We isolate each unit or floor, test it and trace leaks with the same acoustic and thermal tools we use in single-family houses. Property managers get a report per unit. Early detection means fewer tenant complaints and fewer insurance claims building-wide.

    Gas Leak Detection in Vancouver

    A faint sulfur smell near the range or furnace needs a professional check. Leave the house if the odor is strong. Our licensed plumbers use a combustible gas detector and soap solution on fittings, appliance connectors and the meter riser, then pressure test the line to find a leak too small to smell, with repairs made to the fuel gas code before the gas comes back on.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Vancouver, WA

    Sprinkler laterals crack and zone valves stick open, and either one can waste thousands of gallons before the bill shows it. We start outside. Our tech tests the irrigation system separately from the house, runs each zone and traces buried lines, since sprinkler leaks are the first thing most water departments suggest checking.

    Gas Line Leak Locating in Vancouver

    Gas leaks are found by test, not by nose. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, then check each joint with a detector and soap solution. We also walk the yard line, since a dead strip of grass over a buried gas pipe often marks a leak that has been there a while. Utility coordination is included.

    Leaks in Walls and Ceilings in Vancouver, WA

    A ceiling stain right below a bathroom usually points to a shower valve, a drain joint or a toilet wax ring. Our job is figuring out which. Thermal cameras show the cool wet area, a moisture meter confirms it and a borescope through a small hole lets us see the actual pipe without opening the whole ceiling.
