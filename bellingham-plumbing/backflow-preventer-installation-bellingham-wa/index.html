    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Bellingham

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a widely used backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Bellingham, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Bellingham

    Most water providers require testable backflow assemblies to be certified at installation and then once a year. Our certified testers check each valve's opening point, relief valve operation and differential pressure. If a device fails, we can repair or rebuild it on the spot with genuine parts. We submit passing results so you avoid compliance letters or service interruption.

    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Bellingham, WA

    Irrigation systems in Bellingham, WA are a common source of backflow, as stagnant water and lawn chemicals can be drawn back into the potable water supply. Installing a dedicated backflow preventer for your sprinkler system is often a local code requirement. We specialize in selecting and installing the correct device, such as a pressure vacuum breaker or atmospheric vacuum breaker, to protect your home's main water line from contaminants introduced by your landscape irrigation.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Bellingham

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the design of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Bellingham

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Residential Backflow Preventer Installation in Bellingham

    For homeowners in Bellingham, protecting your family's drinking water is the top priority. We install many types of backflow preventers, often required for irrigation systems, fire suppression lines or properties with alternative water sources. We handle everything from small residential devices to bigger systems.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Bellingham, WA

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry proven brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.
