    Replacing an Old Kitchen Sink in Bellingham

    Pulling an old cast iron sink takes two people and a putty knife, because they weigh a lot and are usually sealed hard to the counter. We check the cutout first.     Disposal Repair and Replacement in Bellingham, WA

    A disposal that hums but will not spin usually has something wedged in it. We cut the power, work the flywheel loose with a 1/4 inch hex key from underneath, pull out the bottle cap or bone, and press the red reset button before testing it again. Leaking units are another matter. A drip from the bottom of the housing means the internal seal failed, and that unit has to be replaced.

    Disposal Replacement in Bellingham, WA

    Disposals last about eight to twelve years. When the housing starts weeping from the bottom, it is done. We drop the old unit, check the mounting ring, install the new one and reconnect the dishwasher hose with a new clamp so it does not leak at the inlet. The old unit leaves with us.

    Burst Supply Lines in Bellingham, WA

    Supply lines with plastic coupling nuts split more often than braided stainless, especially after years of pressure swings. If one line cracked from age, the other one is about the same age.

    Dishwasher and Ice Maker Lines in Bellingham
