    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacements in Bellingham

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. It cracks the old pipe outward into the soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. This is the fix for collapsed pipe.

    Pipe Bursting in Bellingham, WA

    Two pits are standard for a burst, one at the house end near the foundation and one out near the street or the city tap. The new HDPE pipe is butt-fused into one length first. The winch or hydraulic puller then drags the bursting head and the new pipe through. A 60-foot pull often takes under an hour.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Bellingham, WA

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. That is called channel rot. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, that beats jackhammering.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Bellingham

    Most trenchless sewer jobs in Bellingham, WA fall between $4,000 and $15,000. Length and method drive the price. Depth and access matter too, so a line under a driveway with only one cleanout will cost more than an open-lawn lateral of the same length.

    Workmanship and materials are typically covered for 10 to 20 years, depending on the product and the method. Manufacturers like Perma-Liner and NuFlow back their materials separately. You get both in writing, along with the before and after camera video that shows what was installed and where.

    Pipe bursting uses high-density polyethylene, HDPE, fused into one continuous length with no joints. For lining, it is a felt or fiberglass tube saturated with epoxy resin. Both resist roots and corrosion, and both are rated by their makers for a service life of around fifty years when installed on properly prepared pipe.

    Fifty years is the common design rating for cured-in-place liners and HDPE pipe. Material warranties often run past 25 years. Installation quality is what makes or breaks that number, so we record the prep on camera and keep the footage on file with your warranty.

    Less digging is the big one. There is no trench across the lawn and no slab cut in the driveway, and the new liner or HDPE pipe has no joints for roots to get into, which is a real upgrade over clay or cast iron. Restoration costs drop sharply.

    A rented drum machine might clear the clog for a week or two, but it will not fix a crack, an offset or the roots that keep growing back through a broken joint. Running a cable through fragile pipe can tear it further. Get a camera inspection first.

    Plan on a day for most lining jobs and one to two days for a burst. A dig can take a week. The water is off only during the cure, which is usually a few hours, so most families never leave the house and can use the toilets again by dinner.

    Think about it when the same drain backs up every few months, or when a camera shows roots, cracks or corrosion in a line that still holds its shape. It makes the most sense under hardscape. Cost is a factor too. We quote both ways.
