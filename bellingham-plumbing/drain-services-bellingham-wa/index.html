    Hydro Jetting in Bellingham

    Hydro jetting sends water at up to 4,000 psi through a specialized nozzle that scours the full inside wall of the pipe. Unlike a cable, which punches a hole through the clog, jetting removes grease, scale and fine roots completely. It is the go-to method for restaurant drains and lines that clog again within months. We camera the line first to make sure the pipe is sound enough for jetting. Most residential jetting jobs take about two to three hours.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Services in Bellingham

    The cost of drain services in Bellingham, WA can vary a great deal, typically ranging from $150 for a simple clog removal to over $5,000 for a major sewer line replacement. Factors like the type of blockage, its location, and the method required (for example, snaking versus hydro jetting) all influence the final price. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after an on-site assessment.

    Traditional sewer line replacement can involve digging trenches, which can be messy. However, we also offer trenchless sewer repair options, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, which limit excavation and disruption to your Bellingham property.

    For most homes, a professional drain cleaning every one to two years is enough for preventative maintenance. However, homes with older plumbing, frequent hair or grease buildup, or known tree root issues may benefit from annual cleaning. Businesses, especially restaurants, often require more frequent service.

    A typical drain cleaning for a single clogged fixture can take anywhere from 30 minutes to 2 hours, depending on the severity and location of the clog. More complex issues, like main sewer line clearing or hydro jetting, may require several hours. We provide an expected timeline after our initial assessment.

    Hydro jetting is a thorough drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to clear blockages and scour the inside of pipes. It's often needed for severe clogs, tree root intrusions, grease buildup, or when traditional snaking isn't effective, particularly for main sewer lines.

    Hydro jetting is safe for pipe in sound condition, including PVC and most cast iron. We camera the line first so we do not jet a pipe that is currently cracked or collapsing.

    Most single fixture drain cleanings take under an hour. Main line cleaning and hydro jetting typically take one to three hours.

    Our technicians are experienced with all major brands of plumbing fixtures and drain components, including Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard, and more. We ensure any replacement parts are a proper match and high quality.
