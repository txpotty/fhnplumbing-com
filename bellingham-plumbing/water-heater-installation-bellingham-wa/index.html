    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Bellingham, WA

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank.     Gas Water Heater Installation in Bellingham, WA

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. Expansion tanks go in where code requires.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Bellingham

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Venting is what keeps CO out. We follow code on all of it.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Bellingham

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Plumbers in Washington must hold a state certification, and water heater replacements and repiping usually require a permit. An expansion tank gives that water somewhere to go, so the relief valve stops dripping and pipe joints stop taking pressure spikes every time the burner runs. We set the air charge to match your water pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Bellingham, WA

    Water on the garage floor and no hot water usually means the tank has rusted through. Shut the cold supply valve on top of the tank and turn the gas knob to pilot or flip the breaker, then call us. Common tanks ride on our trucks. Same-day replacement is normal.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Bellingham, WA

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. No venting needed. You get more spots to put it.
