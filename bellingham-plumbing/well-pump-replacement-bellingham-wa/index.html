    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Bellingham, WA

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. Air sneaks in through a pinhole in the suction line, the pump loses prime and the seal burns up running dry. The suction line gets a pressure test. If the well is too deep for a jet pump, we will say so and quote a submersible.

    Constant Pressure Well Pump Installation in Bellingham, WA

    Constant pressure systems hold the house at one pressure, say 50 psi, whether one faucet is open or the shower and the irrigation are running together. A variable frequency drive speeds the pump up and slows it down to match demand. Showers stop going weak.

    Constant Pressure Pump Systems in Bellingham

    Franklin SubDrive and Grundfos SQE are the two constant pressure systems we install most. They get by with a much smaller tank because the drive matches pump speed to demand. The soft start is easier on the motor. The upfront cost is higher.

    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in Bellingham, WA

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They cost more up front. The SQE pairs with a CU 301 controller for constant pressure.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Bellingham
