    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Bellingham, WA

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Soft water can be slightly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. Flush it yearly. We install isolation valves for that.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Bellingham

    Sizing a home unit depends on two figures: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. With a shower at about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen tap a bit under, two showers and a sink together need around 5 or 6 gallons per minute at the needed rise. It shortens the list quickly.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Bellingham

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, because the unit has to sense flow and fire first. A recirc pump on a timer or sensor keeps the hot line primed, cutting the wait at the far sink from about a minute to a few seconds. Less water goes down the drain.
