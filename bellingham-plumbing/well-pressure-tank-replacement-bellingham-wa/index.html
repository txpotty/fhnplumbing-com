    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Bellingham

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Old ones leak at threads. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, and a corroded valve may not open when needed. Whole set, one visit.

    Constant Pressure System Conversion in Bellingham, WA

    Constant pressure systems use a variable speed drive that adjusts pump speed to demand. You get even pressure across fixtures. These systems can run on a smaller tank, but they cost more up front and need a compatible pump and drive. We explain the trade-off.
