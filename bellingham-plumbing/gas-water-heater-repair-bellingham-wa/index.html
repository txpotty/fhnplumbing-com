    Pilot Light Repair in Bellingham

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are completed in about an hour.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Bellingham

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures usually involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are verified before we leave.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Bellingham, WA

    Atmospheric gas water heaters depend on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and verify draft with a test. Proper venting is essential for your family's safety.

    If your gas water heater isn't heating water to the correct temperature, or if it is running too hot, the thermostat or gas valve might be to blame. These components regulate the gas supply and temperature. Our plumbers in Bellingham, WA can test the thermostat for accuracy and inspect the gas valve for any blockages or faults.
