    Slab Leak Detection in Bellingham

    We start by shutting off fixtures and watching the meter to confirm a leak. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to determine which one is leaking. Using acoustic ground microphones, we listen for the sound of escaping water through the concrete. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor surface.

    If multiple slab leaks point to a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution.     Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Bellingham

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method bypasses the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Bellingham with original copper or galvanized plumbing.
