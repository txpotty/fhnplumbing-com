    Softener Brands We Work On in Bellingham

    Most softeners sold in the last 20 years run on a handful of valve platforms, mainly Fleck, Clack, Autotrol and a few proprietary heads. Once you know the valve, the name on the cabinet matters less. Kinetico and Culligan build their own valves, so we check part availability before quoting.

    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Bellingham, WA

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We step it through each cycle by hand and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. A worn piston is a common find.

    Yearly Maintenance Plans in Bellingham, WA

    Plans take the remembering off your plate. You get a reminder call before each yearly visit, and if the control valve fails between visits you get priority booking. Repairs found during a plan visit are billed at a reduced rate.

    Commercial Softener Service in Bellingham

    Commercial units work harder. Restaurants and laundromats can burn through a softener's capacity in a single day, so twin-tank systems swap tanks, keeping one in service while the other regenerates. We set visits around your business hours so the water never goes hard during a rush.

    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Bellingham, WA

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. We empty, scrub and rinse the tank. Then we look for cracks near the overflow elbow and the brine well and refill with fresh salt.

    Repair or Replace a Softener in Bellingham, WA

    New softener systems often run $1,200 to $3,500 installed, while a resin rebed usually lands between $300 and $700. Sound tanks and valves are worth keeping. We recommend a new unit when the valve is obsolete and parts are gone, or when the tank itself is cracked.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Bellingham

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle tend to mean the seal and spacer stack is worn out. On a Fleck 5600 the fix is a seal kit and a new piston, often done in under an hour without pulling the valve off the tank. It costs a fraction of a new control head.

    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Bellingham, WA

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. It does not take much debris to block it. We pull the injector and screen, clean them, and replace the seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1 if they are worn.
