    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Bellingham, WA

    The brine tank is crucial for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can build up, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a frequent issue we address in Bellingham homes.

    Valve and Controller Diagnostics in Bellingham, WA

    The control valve and electronic controller regulate the entire softening process. Our technicians perform diagnostic checks to ensure these components are operating accurately, adjusting programming as needed. We check for wear and tear on moving parts and lubricate them to prevent future issues, keeping your water softener dependable in Bellingham, WA.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Bellingham, WA

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a complete regeneration every time.
