    Slab Leak Reroutes in Bellingham

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab, which costs far less than a repipe and avoids breaking the floor. It suits a first-time leak. If the house has had several leaks, we will walk through why a full repipe usually costs less over time.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Bellingham, WA

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Weak flow and rusty water are common. We remove or abandon the galvanized lines and replace them with PEX or copper, and your water pressure and water quality usually improve the same day the new lines go into service.

    Copper Repiping in Bellingham, WA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity.     PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Bellingham, WA

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. For a typical house, copper often runs 20 to 40 percent above PEX, though some buyers and homeowners like the look and feel of metal pipe and are willing to pay for it. Our quotes for either include permit, fittings, patching and cleanup.
