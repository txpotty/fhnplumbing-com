    Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Bellingham, WA

    PEX rarely fails on its own. When it does, the cause is usually a nail or screw driven through it, a rodent chewing on an attic run, or a fitting that was never crimped all the way. We carry the common sizes. Most emergency PEX repairs in Bellingham, WA come down to a cut and a splice, usually under an hour after the wall is open.

    New Construction PEX Plumbing in Bellingham, WA

    On a new build, FHN Plumbing runs PEX supply lines while the framing is still open in Bellingham, WA. We drill the plates, set the manifold and stub out every fixture to the heights on the plans, then hold a pressure test for the rough-in inspection. Nail plates go on wherever pipe crosses a stud. It keeps the schedule moving.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Bellingham

    Your service line runs from the meter or well to the house, often a foot and a half to three feet underground. A PEX main comes off a coil in one piece, so there are no buried joints between the meter and the foundation wall. Steep slopes and wet soils can move over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. It gets bedded in sand, not rocky backfill. Rock pressing on the pipe is the usual cause of an early failure.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Bellingham, WA

    Gray polybutylene went into millions of homes from the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorinated water breaks the pipe and its plastic fittings down from within, which means it can look perfectly fine until a fitting suddenly fails. Many insurers ask whether you have it. Swapping it for PEX can help with insurance and with resale.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Bellingham

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. After 40 or 50 years the bore is so choked with scale that a half-inch line delivers a trickle, and the water comes out tinted brown after it sits overnight. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. You get real pressure back, and the discolored water goes away with the old pipe.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Bellingham, WA

    Think of a manifold as a breaker panel for water. Each fixture gets its own line from a central header, and each line gets its own valve, so you can shut off one toilet without killing water to the kitchen. It uses more pipe. The trade-off is almost no fittings hidden in the walls and steadier pressure when two showers run at once.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Bellingham, WA

    With PEX-A, the tech slips a ring over the pipe end and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. Joints get time to set before we pressurize.

    Commercial PEX Pipe Solutions in Bellingham, WA

    Apartment buildings, small offices and churches in Bellingham, WA are good candidates for PEX as much as houses can. With a manifold and shutoff for each unit, a leak in one apartment only takes that apartment offline, not the whole riser. Rated walls need firestopping. Where PEX passes through a fire-rated assembly, we use listed firestop sleeves or sealant so the rating holds.
