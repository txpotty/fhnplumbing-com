    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Bellingham

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack with no warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when selling the home.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Bellingham

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a common choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and covering hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during unexpected cold snaps. FHN Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers excellent performance and water quality for your home.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Bellingham, WA

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep appearing. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you weigh the cost of both paths.
