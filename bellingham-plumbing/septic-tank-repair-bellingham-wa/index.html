    Drain Field Clog Remediation in Bellingham

    A clogged drain field is a frequent cause of septic system backups and can be a significant issue for homeowners in Bellingham. This can be caused by excessive solids, tree root intrusion or soil compaction. We offer professional drain field remediation, which may include hydro-jetting the distribution lines, addressing root infiltration or assessing the soil's percolation capacity to restore proper effluent absorption and prevent future saturation.

    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Bellingham

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field quickly. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where appropriate.
