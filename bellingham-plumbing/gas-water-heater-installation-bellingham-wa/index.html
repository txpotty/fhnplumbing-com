    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Bellingham

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Many need no power. The vent goes through a wall with proper clearances.

    Standard Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Bellingham

    Our standard gas tank install starts with removing the old heater. We drain it, disconnect the gas and the vent, carry it out and then install the new tank with fresh connectors, a new drip leg and a properly pitched vent connector. Testing comes last. Value and reliability are why people choose them.

    Propane Water Heater Installation in Bellingham

    Propane homes need heaters set up for propane, not natural gas. Different pressure and orifice size mean either a propane model or a factory conversion kit. We work with your supplier. We test all fittings.

    Brands We Install in Bellingham

    Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith, Navien and Rinnai make up most of the gas heaters we install in Bellingham homes and businesses. We compare warranties, recovery and venting across brands so the choice is easy. Price is part of it. We install all of them.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Bellingham, WA

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Because the exhaust comes out cool and wet, they vent through PVC and need a condensate drain, which changes where the heater can go and what the install involves. Rebates may apply. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Bellingham

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They cost the least, but the vent has to be the right size, slope upward and keep its clearances. We inspect the existing vent. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Bellingham

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. A conversion often means a larger gas line, new venting, a condensate drain and service valves. It takes most of a day. We do all of it to code.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Bellingham, WA

    Gas tankless units fire at up to 199,000 BTU, far more than a 40,000 BTU tank burner. The higher input means a bigger gas line and a dedicated vent, often PVC on condensing models.
