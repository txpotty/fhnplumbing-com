    Trenchless Sewer and Water Line Repair in Auburn, WA

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Auburn

    Tree roots, corrosion and soil movement wreck most sewer lines. Steep slopes and wet soils can shift over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. Small cracks get a sectional liner. For longer damage we line the whole run, and for a collapse we burst in new pipe, without trenching the yard or cutting the drive.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Auburn, WA

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. A winch pulls a bursting head through the line, cracking the old pipe outward into the soil while new HDPE follows right behind it, so the replacement pipe ends up in the same path. HDPE resists corrosion and leaks in Auburn, WA soil.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Auburn, WA

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It can go up a size. Our crew sets an entry pit and an exit pit, then pulls a bursting head and new HDPE through on a hydraulic winch, which replaces the full run in a day or two for most homes. FHN Plumbing fuses the HDPE into one length.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Auburn

    Older Auburn homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. They wear out. Clay joints let roots in and cast iron scales and rusts through, and a liner seals both problems with a new jointless pipe that often outlasts the original material by decades. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Repair Cost in Auburn, WA

    Per foot of pipe, trenchless can cost more than open trenching. Look at the full cost. Once you add restoring driveways, patios, landscaping and irrigation, trenchless is often the better value, and it is faster and far less disruptive for the household. We compare both with real numbers for your property.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Auburn, WA

    Main water lines can also be replaced without a trench. Bursting or boring works. New HDPE or PEX goes in along the old path by bursting, or along a new one by directional boring, leaving lawns and drives alone. The line is pressure tested before service is restored.

    Sewer Lateral Lining in Auburn

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Clay and Orangeburg fail often. Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots over time, so we jet the line clean, inspect it by camera and choose lining or bursting based on what we see. The new line restores full flow and stands up to roots.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Auburn

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Auburn yard.
