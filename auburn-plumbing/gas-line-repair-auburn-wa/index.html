    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Auburn

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. We shut off every appliance and watch the meter's test dial, then close branch valves one at a time to find which section is leaking. A combustible gas detector narrows it further. A spray of soap solution on the fitting shows bubbles at the leak, and we fix that joint or section and retest the whole system.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Auburn

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. We replace them with new coated stainless connectors sized for the appliance and add an accessible shutoff valve if the old one is missing or seized. This is a quick fix. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.

    Gas Shut Off by the Utility in Auburn

    When your utility shuts off gas for a leak, you may be without hot water, heat or cooking until a licensed plumber fixes it. Our plumber finds and fixes the leak, then runs the test your gas company requires before unlocking the meter. The truck carries what we need. We do not leave until the gauge holds steady and the system is ready for the utility.

    Gas Leak Detection in Auburn

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead grass in a stripe across the yard or a gas meter that moves with everything off are the common leak signs. Trust your nose. Our plumbers isolate the system section by section, sweep it with an electronic detector and confirm the exact spot with soap solution. Once we find it, you get a written repair price before work starts.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Auburn, WA

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. Each new appliance gets a properly sized branch, a shutoff you can reach, a drip leg where required and a new coated connector. Leaking existing lines get repaired during the same trip.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Auburn

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Glacial till and dense hardpan are common in western Washington, and they drain poorly enough to keep water against foundations and in crawlspaces. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.

    CSST Repair in Auburn

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Common damage comes from nails, rodents or lightning, and repairs require cutting out the bad section and using fittings from the same manufacturer. Bonding gets checked on every system. A bonding clamp and conductor tie the CSST to the home's grounding system, following the maker's instructions and code.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Auburn, WA

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.
