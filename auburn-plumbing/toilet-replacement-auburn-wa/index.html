    Common Toilet Replacements in Auburn, WA

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Auburn

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips. No plumbing changes are needed. Many people pair it with an elongated bowl and a soft-close seat, and the swap takes the same couple of hours as any other.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Auburn, WA

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are noticeably louder than gravity toilets. Replacement means matching the vessel brand for parts later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Auburn, WA

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for a score of at least 800. A high score means fewer double flushes and fewer clogs, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    Standard Toilet Replacement in Auburn

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. That is when we swap them. We pull the old toilet, scrape the flange and check it for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Auburn, WA

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They cost more and weigh more. A one-piece toilet usually takes two people to carry upstairs, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Auburn, WA

    Late-night failures happen, usually a tank that splits along a seam or a bowl that cracks at the base and keeps seeping no matter how many towels go down. Closing the angle stop stops the water. A replacement can usually go in first thing the next morning, and we will tell you if the floor needs to dry before we set the new one.

    ADA and Commercial Toilet Replacement in Auburn, WA

    Public restrooms need bowls rated for heavy use, and many need to meet ADA height and clearance rules when a fixture is replaced. We confirm the clearances before ordering. Sensor flush valves are an easy add during the swap, and they cut down on unflushed bowls.

    Emergency Toilet Replacement in Auburn

    A toilet that cracked overnight and is still seeping into the floor cannot wait for next week, so we bring a standard round and elongated model on emergency calls. First, close the valve behind the toilet. Most of the time the new toilet goes in that same day, and we check the subfloor for soaked plywood before setting it.
