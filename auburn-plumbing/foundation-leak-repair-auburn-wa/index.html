    Residential Foundation Leak Repair in Auburn, WA

    For a homeowner in Auburn, WA, the first question is usually how bad it is. We answer that with a pressure test. If the gauge drops fast, the leak is large and needs attention this week, but a slow drop over twenty minutes points to a pinhole that gives you time to compare repair options.     Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in Auburn, WA

    Crawl spaces hide leaks from supply lines, drains and AC condensate lines. Sagging, wet insulation is a common clue. Water sitting under the house rots sill plates and floor joists and feeds mold on the subfloor, so we find the source, fix the pipe and check whether the vapor barrier needs replacing. A sump pump may make sense in a Auburn, WA crawl space that floods from outside.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Auburn, WA

    Drain leaks under a slab are harder to find. Drains carry no pressure. A hydrostatic test shows whether the line holds water, and a camera run shows us the cracked fitting, offset joint or roots causing it. Fixes include tunneling, slab cuts or a pipe liner, and we lay out the trade-offs.

    Foundation Leak Detection in Auburn

    Locating comes before anything else. We split the system first. Hot and cold get tested separately, then we trace the losing line and walk it with the ground mic until the hiss is loudest. Thermal imaging helps verify hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.
