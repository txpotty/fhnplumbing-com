    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Auburn

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. Both elements get tested. With power off, we check thermostats and connections.

    Water Heater Maintenance Plans in Auburn, WA

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Auburn

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Yellow or lazy flames mean poor combustion, so we clean the burner and combustion chamber, check the thermocouple or flame sensor, and confirm the vent has a proper draft and tight connections. Loose vents leak CO. The gas line and shutoff get leak tested.

    Commercial Tank and Tankless Service in Auburn, WA

    Commercial heaters work harder than home units.     Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Auburn, WA

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We lift the lever, confirm water discharges and the valve reseats without dripping, and check that the discharge pipe runs full size to an approved spot within a few inches of the floor or outside. Leaky valves get replaced. We check the expansion tank too.

    Water Heater Leak Checks in Auburn, WA

    Small drips become floods. We inspect the supply connections, the relief valve outlet, the drain valve and the tank seams for moisture or rust trails, and we check the drain pan and the floor around the unit for signs of a slow leak. Rust trails mean trouble. Early repairs protect floors and drywall.

    Anode Rod Checks in Auburn

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. Once the rod is eaten down to its core wire, the water goes after the tank itself. It comes out for inspection. Replacing the rod is much cheaper than replacing the heater.
