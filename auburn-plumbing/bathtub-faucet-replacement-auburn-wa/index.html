    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts clamp to copper with a set screw, and threaded ones screw onto a pipe nipple. We check yours before buying. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Wall-mount tub fillers are a popular upgrade. A long-reach filler or a slide-bar handheld changes the tub, and both usually need a new valve or diverter. Rough-in gets planned before trim. The trim goes on last.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Auburn

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting.     Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Auburn, WA

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.
