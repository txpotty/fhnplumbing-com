    Broken Drain Pipe Repair in Auburn, WA

    Broken drain pipe usually comes from settling, roots or age. Sometimes it is a bad glue joint. We find the break with a camera and a locator, then replace that section with new pipe joined by shielded couplings that fit the old and new outside diameters. We test it before closing up.

    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Auburn, WA

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. It clogs with silt. Steep slopes and wet soils can move over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, so we camera the tile, find the failed section and replace it with perforated pipe wrapped in filter fabric and clean gravel. We confirm it drains to daylight or a sump.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Auburn, WA

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. They hide well. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then replace the bad length with pipe that matches what is already there. Repairs meet local plumbing code.

    Tub and Shower Drain Repair in Auburn, WA
