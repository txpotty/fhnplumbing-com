    Dependable Basement Sump Pump Installation Services in Auburn, WA

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Auburn

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a good fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Auburn

    Power outages during heavy storms can render a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump provides crucial protection when the power goes out. We install dependable backup systems that activate automatically, keeping water moving even without electricity. This is an important safeguard for homes in Auburn that lose power during storms, offering peace of mind in severe weather. We tie these systems seamlessly with your existing plumbing.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Auburn, WA

    Power outages can happen, especially during severe weather, and they leave your primary sump pump useless. We supply and install battery backup sump pump systems that switch on automatically if the power goes out, providing continuous protection for your basement in Auburn, WA. River flooding and saturated ground can push water back through sewer lines, so backwater valves and sump pumps are worth considering. These systems include a deep cycle battery and a secondary pump, so your basement stays dry even when the electricity fails.

    Wayne Sump Pump Installation in Auburn, WA

    Wayne sump pumps offer a range of options, from basic utility pumps to advanced systems with built-in alarms and smart features. Our technicians are familiar with installing Wayne pumps, providing efficient water removal for your Auburn, WA basement. We help you select the right Wayne model for your needs and complete a professional installation that gets the most from its performance and lifespan.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Auburn

    Office buildings, parking structures and multi-unit properties with below-grade levels often need duplex sump systems with two pumps and an alternating control panel. The panel switches lead and lag pumps on each cycle so wear stays even, and a high water alarm alerts building staff. We coordinate electrical with your electrician and follow manufacturer specs for basin size and float settings. Property managers get a clear maintenance schedule to keep both pumps exercised.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Auburn, WA

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit is the least expensive option, typically a few hundred dollars plus the pump. Digging a new pit through the slab adds labor for saw cutting, excavation and concrete patching. Adding a battery backup increases the total but often costs less than one cleanup after a flooded basement. We give you an itemized quote so you can compare a basic primary pump against a full primary and backup system before we begin.

    Sump Pump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Auburn

    A complete sump pump system requires a properly installed basin and discharge line. We handle the excavation and placement of the sump basin, setting it at the lowest point of your basement floor so it collects water well. We also install the discharge line, ensuring it is correctly sloped, sealed and routed away from your foundation to a suitable drainage area, so water does not find its way back into your Auburn property.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Auburn

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by finding the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor stays clean and level.

    We recommend having your sump pump inspected and maintained annually, ideally before the rainy season.     Watch for a pump that runs constantly, cycles on and off rapidly, makes grinding noises or never seems to turn on. Rust stains, a stuck float and water sitting above the switch level are also warning signs.
