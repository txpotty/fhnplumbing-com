    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Auburn

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. No water, low pressure or a pump that hums without moving water are the usual signs one has failed. We test the motor from the surface first. When a pull is needed, a hoist brings the pipe up safely so we can replace the motor, the impellers or the check valve.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Auburn, WA

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. We carry the common Franklin parts. Replacing a failed motor usually costs less than a rewind once shipping is counted.

    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Auburn, WA

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. High amp draw points to a weak motor. A clogged intake or a dropping water level shows up as low flow with normal amps, and that changes the repair entirely. Testing first saves a pull.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Auburn

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. When the bladder fails, the tank fills with water and the pump starts and stops every time someone opens a faucet. The bladder, the switch and the tank fittings all get checked. Proper sizing adds years to a pump.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Auburn, WA

    Switch and control repairs often cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500. A submersible pull and replacement runs about $1,800 to $5,000 and up, based on depth and horsepower. An older pump that already needs pulling is often worth replacing on the same trip. You see both numbers.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Auburn

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. Usually the bladder has failed or the tank has lost its air charge. We check the precharge with the tank drained. Set it 2 psi under cut-in, which means about 38 psi for a 40/60 switch.

    Control Box & Electrical Repair in Auburn, WA

    Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. Breaker trips usually come first. With the power locked out, we test and replace the failed parts and tighten every terminal. Wiring meets Washington code before we restart the pump.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Auburn, WA

    Variable speed drives like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by changing motor speed. A fault code on the drive points us in the right direction. We troubleshoot the drive, the pressure sensor and the wiring, then repair or replace the failed part and verify the setpoint. These need a smaller tank.
