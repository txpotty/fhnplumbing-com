    Fill Valve Replacement in Auburn

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank usually points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet much quieter.

    Toilet Flush Mechanism Repair in Auburn, WA

    If your toilet isn't flushing properly, it could be due to issues with the flush chain, handle, flapper, or fill valve. A weak flush may mean the tank isn't filling correctly, while a loose handle might not be engaging the flapper. Our plumbers are skilled at diagnosing these internal tank problems, replacing worn or broken components like flush valves, fill valves, and toilet handles to restore full and effective flushing power.

    Running Toilet Fixes in Auburn

    A toilet that continually runs or refills itself intermittently is not only annoying but also a major water waste. This issue in Auburn is usually caused by a faulty flapper valve, an improperly adjusted float, a worn-out fill valve, or sediment buildup. Our technicians diagnose the specific component causing the problem and replace it with durable, long-lasting parts. We ensure proper alignment and adjustment of all internal mechanisms to restore your toilet's efficient operation and stop unnecessary water consumption.

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Auburn, WA

    The gaskets and seals within your toilet tank and at its base are essential for preventing leaks. Over time, these rubber components can degrade, crack, or become brittle due to age and exposure to water, especially where the water is hard. We specialize in replacing worn-out flapper seals, tank-to-bowl gaskets, and the wax ring at the toilet's base, which is essential for preventing leaks onto your bathroom floor and maintaining a watertight seal.

    Clogged Toilet Solutions in Auburn, WA

    Persistent toilet clogs are a common nuisance in Auburn, WA homes, often caused by non-flushable items or excessive toilet paper. While a plunger can clear minor blockages, persistent clogs suggest a deeper issue in the trap, drain line, or even the main sewer line. Our plumbers use professional-grade augers and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the toughest clogs without damaging your toilet or pipes. We also inspect for potential causes like root intrusion or pipe corrosion, which are sometimes seen in older homes.

    Leaking Toilet Repair in Auburn

    Toilet leaks can cause serious water damage to your bathroom floor, subfloor, and even the ceiling below. Leaks can start from the base of the toilet, the tank, or the supply line. Common culprits include a worn-out wax ring, loose bolts, or cracked porcelain. We thoroughly inspect all potential leak points, replacing seals, tightening connections, or recommending bowl replacement if the porcelain is damaged, preventing further water intrusion.
