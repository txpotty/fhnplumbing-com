    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Auburn

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. That pays back over time. We install several models.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Auburn

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. We size before we sell. Your test result and water use decide the tank size.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Auburn, WA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. FHN Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. They work best on very hard water.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Auburn

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Soft water reaches every fixture. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Auburn, WA

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. The savings add up. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Auburn

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron changes the math. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Water Softener Replacement in Auburn, WA

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Salt use drops. You will notice soft water the same day.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Auburn, WA

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. Installing on an existing loop is faster and cheaper, while adding a new loop means cutting into the main and running pipe, which adds labor and fittings. Carbon tanks cost extra. We put equipment, labor and setup on the quote.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Auburn

    Most installs cost $1,500 to $4,000 including equipment. Adding a new softener loop usually adds $500 to $1,500, depending on how easy it is to reach the main line.

    White scale on faucets, spotty dishes, dry skin and a water heater that struggles are common signs. Testing tells you for sure.

    Some homeowners try it, but we recommend a plumber. A mistake on the bypass, drain or programming means leaks or water that never softens. It also protects the warranty.

    Sized and installed correctly, a softener should not noticeably drop your pressure. If pressure falls after the install, it usually points to an undersized valve or a clogged pre-filter, which our plumbers can fix.
