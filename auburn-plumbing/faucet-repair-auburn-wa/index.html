    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Auburn, WA

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. There are slip-on and threaded spouts.     Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Auburn

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Eastern and mountain areas of Washington freeze regularly and deeply, so insulated pipes and winterized irrigation systems are standard. We replace packing washers and seat washers on standard hose bibs, and we install frost-free sillcocks with the valve seat inside the warm wall when an old one has split. Every new spigot gets a vacuum breaker for backflow protection.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Auburn

    One drip a second adds up to more than 3,000 gallons a year, roughly the water used in 180 showers. It shows up on the bill. The worn washer or cartridge gets tracked down and replace it, then check the cabinet below for swelling or stains from the slow leak.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Auburn

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. If the discs are scratched, the whole cartridge gets replaced, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Leaky Faucet Repair in Auburn

    Most drips come from the part that seals the water inside the faucet, which is a rubber washer on older two-handle faucets and a cartridge or ceramic disc on newer ones. First we identify which type of valve you have. It matters. We carry the common washers, seats and cartridges, so the fix usually happens on the first trip.

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Auburn

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. Hard water speeds it up. The valve body gets cleaned while the old part is out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Auburn, WA

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touch and touchless faucets add a solenoid and batteries to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.
