    Cost-Effective Disposal Repair in Auburn, WA

    Paying half the price of a new disposal for a repair seldom pays off, and we will say so. Plenty of repairs cost little. Resetting an overload, freeing a jam, replacing a switch or resealing a flange usually costs well under the price of a replacement and keeps a younger unit running for years.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Auburn

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. That whine often comes from worn motor bearings, and once bearings start to fail the noise only gets worse until the motor seizes. Odors usually trace to the splash guard. We pull it, clean the chamber walls and replace the guard if it is torn.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Auburn, WA

    The flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube and body are where leaks start. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed, and in that case replacement is the better option. It is not a repairable part.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Auburn

    A metal-on-metal grind may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Failing bearings are not worth fixing.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Auburn

    A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. They are different problems. We check the breaker, the reset button and the switch for silent units, and the flywheel and motor windings for humming ones, then decide if a repair makes sense.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Auburn

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough all point to something inside. Loose mounting rings rattle too. We check the mount, then look for debris, then run the motor empty, since a whine with an empty chamber means the bearings are going.

    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Auburn, WA

    Every brand, from InSinkErator to Waste King and Moen, mounts differently, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit. InSinkErator uses a three-bolt or Quick Lock style ring, while Waste King uses an EZ Mount twist ring that comes off without tools. Common kits ride on our trucks. That keeps most repairs to one visit.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Auburn, WA

    Fine grinding with slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. The trap comes off, the tube and arm get cleared with a properly sized cable, and we confirm the arm drops about a quarter inch per foot.
