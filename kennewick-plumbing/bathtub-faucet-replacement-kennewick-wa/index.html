    Budget Tub Faucet Options in Kennewick, WA

    Not every tub faucet needs a full valve. A cartridge plus a same-brand trim kit can refresh an old faucet for far less than a new valve. That option comes up first. Three-handle faucets are the exception.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Kennewick, WA

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We replace Moen and Delta cartridges or old compression stems and seats, and we grease the O-rings with silicone plumber's grease so the handle turns smoothly for years. Old seats need a seat wrench. About an hour, usually.

    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in Kennewick, WA

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update. Delta Monitor valves take new cartridges from the front, and the MultiChoice body accepts future trim swaps. We stock common Delta cartridges. Older Delta valves sometimes need full replacement.

    Tub Valves in Older Houses in Kennewick, WA

    Repeated repairs add up.     Tub Spout Replacement in Kennewick

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts slide over a 1/2 inch copper stub and lock with a set screw underneath, while threaded spouts screw onto a 1/2 inch pipe nipple, and putting the wrong type on causes leaks behind the wall. The connection gets checked first. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Kennewick

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. We cut out the old valve, sweat or crimp in a single-handle pressure-balance valve, and cover the old handle holes with a remodel plate so the tile stays intact around it. Most need an access panel or a back-wall opening. Tile around the plate stays intact.
