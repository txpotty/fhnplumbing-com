    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Kennewick

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. We jet the full main to the street tap. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Kennewick, WA

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. We jet the line from the kitchen to the interceptor in short passes, which keeps loosened grease from plugging the pipe farther down, and we coordinate with your pump-out company. Quarterly works for most.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Kennewick, WA

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Soft water can be slightly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. A jetter with a scouring nozzle strips that scale from the walls, and on heavily scaled cast iron we may follow up with a descaling chain head to finish the job. The difference shows on camera.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Kennewick, WA

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Heavy, prolonged rain from atmospheric river storms is a major factor in western Washington, raising groundwater and filling crawlspaces and basements. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. We also check the pop-up emitters.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Kennewick, WA

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. Jetting can remove that buildup. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Root Removal Jetting in Kennewick

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. A root nozzle cuts them out. Jetting does not stop roots from returning, because the crack is still there, but it clears the pipe completely and lets us see on camera whether a spot repair or a liner would stop the regrowth.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Kennewick

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. We camera every line before we set up the jetter. Footage shows the material and joint condition, which sets our nozzle and pressure.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in Kennewick, WA

    Retail and office buildings have long horizontal drain runs that collect buildup. Flat runs collect more debris. After-hours jetting keeps equipment out of hallways while tenants are working.
