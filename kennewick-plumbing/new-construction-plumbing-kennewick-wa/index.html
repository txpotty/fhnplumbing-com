    Frequently Asked Questions About New Construction Plumbing in Kennewick

    Price depends mostly on fixture count and finish level.     PEX handles most water supply in new homes. It bends around corners and needs far fewer fittings inside walls than copper does. For drain, waste and vent we use Schedule 40 PVC or ABS, whichever your code and plans call for. We still use copper near water heaters.

    We can run a dedicated return line during rough-in. It gets hot water to the far bathroom in seconds. Compared with a crossover valve at the sink, a dedicated return keeps the cold line cold and works faster. The pump needs an outlet.

    Most jurisdictions inspect plumbing three times. First comes underground, with drains holding a water or air test before the pour. Top-out gets inspected before insulation, and the final comes after fixtures are set and running. Some add a gas test.

    Expect plumbing to touch the schedule three times. Underground takes one to three days. Top-out runs three to five days, then trim needs one or two days after cabinets and tile are done. Inspections add a day each.

    Steep slopes and wet soils can move over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. Ground movement puts stress on pipes under a slab.
