    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Kennewick, WA

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. We check gas pressure with all units firing at once. We take a flow and temperature reading on each heater. Then we compare them.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Kennewick, WA

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. Rinnai LC codes point to scale, Navien E003 is a failed ignition and Noritz 90 points at combustion air or exhaust trouble. First we read the code history. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Kennewick

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate. We clear the condensate trap and check the neutralizer media. On A-series models with the built-in recirculation pump, we test the pump and the buffer tank too.

    What Tankless Maintenance Saves in Kennewick

    Scale on an exchanger acts like insulation. A heavily scaled unit burns more gas per shower than a clean one, and that adds up each month. You get the maintenance price beside the likely repair cost, and the decision is yours.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Kennewick, WA

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. We read gas pressure at the inlet with a manometer while the burner is at full fire and compare it to the rating plate. We check the flue for blockages. You get a written report with the readings, so next year's visit has something to compare against.

    Tankless Vent Inspection in Kennewick

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. Condensing units use PVC or polypropylene vent pipe, pitched back toward the heater so condensate drains. Every joint gets checked, and we test for carbon monoxide by the heater. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Kennewick, WA

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. We start a Noritz tune-up at the exhaust, checking for soot and any blocked termination. Next comes the combustion air intake and the fan. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.
