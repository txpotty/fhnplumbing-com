    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Kennewick, WA

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Commercial Gas Tank Replacement in Kennewick

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Kennewick

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape.     Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Kennewick

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. When one fails twice on a tank that is already 8 or 9 years old, replacing the whole heater is usually cheaper over time. We confirm a dedicated outlet first.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Kennewick, WA

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. They cost more and need a condensate drain and an outlet. Big households see payback sooner.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Kennewick

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. The new tank ties into your existing gas line and flue. Closets need enough combustion air. A tank in a tight closet needs louvered doors or a ducted air supply, and we check that before lighting it.
