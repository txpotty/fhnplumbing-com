We typically use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is a good fit when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Slab Leak Detection in Kennewick

    The cost of slab leak detection in Kennewick, WA typically ranges from $250 to $700, depending on the complexity of the leak and the time required for detection. This cost covers the specialized equipment and expertise needed to accurately locate the leak, which is crucial before any repair work can begin.

    Reroutes and repipes often require a plumbing permit, while small direct repairs may not. Requirements vary by city and county. We take care of permits and inspections when needed.

    Most slab leak detection services can be completed within 1 to 3 hours, depending on the severity and location of the leak. Our goal is always to be as efficient and non-invasive as possible, providing a fast diagnosis so repair work can begin promptly.

    A water line slab leak involves pressurized fresh water escaping from supply pipes, often resulting in higher water bills, warm spots or the sound of running water. A sewer line slab leak involves wastewater leaking from drain pipes, which typically causes foul odors, slow drains or visible sewage seepage, and is not usually pressurized.

    Common pipe materials prone to slab leaks include older copper pipes vulnerable to corrosion from hard water or soil acidity, and sometimes PEX or CPVC if they were improperly installed or exposed to extreme temperatures. Galvanized steel pipes, though less common under slabs today, can also corrode and leak.

    Some homeowner's insurance policies may cover the cost of accessing and repairing the slab leak, especially if the leak was sudden and accidental. However, coverage often varies, and damage caused by long-term neglect might not be covered. We recommend checking your specific policy details or contacting your insurance provider for details.

    Many policies cover the cost to access the leak and repair resulting water damage, though the pipe itself may not be covered. Coverage varies by insurer. We provide photos and a written report for your claim.
