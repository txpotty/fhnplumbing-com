    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Kennewick, WA

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Heat is the reason. Each time hot water runs, the pipe expands against concrete and gravel, and recirculation pumps keep that cycle going all day, which grinds the pipe wall thin at the rub points. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Kennewick, WA

    Direct repair is often the most practical fix for a single leak. We cut a small square. Once the pipe is exposed, we replace the bad section, test the line and backfill in compacted lifts before patching. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Kennewick

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. A reroute stops the cycle. We leave the leaking run in the ground and pull new PEX through the attic and down the walls, cutting openings where they are easiest to patch, often behind a vanity or in a closet.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Kennewick

    Locating comes before cutting. We use a ground mic and a thermal camera. With the mic catching the hiss through the concrete and the camera showing warm water spreading, we can usually narrow the leak to about a foot. We test hot lines, cold lines and drains in your Kennewick home.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Kennewick, WA

    Leaks under a load-bearing wall or a kitchen island are hard to reach from inside. We dig in from outside. A pit next to the foundation lets the crew tunnel under the slab by hand to the pipe, with shoring as needed, so your interior floors and cabinets stay untouched. Many Kennewick, WA homes are good candidates.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Kennewick, WA

    Spot repairs fit leaks in otherwise good pipe. It is a single small opening. The floor gets opened, the pipe replaced and tested, and the trench compacted and patched, ready for your flooring contractor.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Kennewick, WA

    Two or three slab leaks in a few years point to the pipe, not bad luck. A repipe may be cheaper long term. PEX bends with the structure, won't corrode and runs overhead, so a whole-house repipe leaves nothing under the slab. It suits many Kennewick homes.
