    Frequently Asked Questions About Pipe Repair in Kennewick

    A simple accessible leak runs $150 to $450. Hidden pipe costs more. A burst pipe that needs significant access or a long replacement section could run $500 to $2,500 or more, with slab and ceiling leaks at the top of the range. We price it once we know the cause.

    Policies often cover sudden water damage from a burst pipe. Worn pipe is usually excluded. The cost of fixing old or corroded pipe is usually yours, but the resulting damage to the house may be claimable. Check with your agent. We provide documentation.

    A clamp or tape can stop a drip for a day. Temporary fixes fail. Pressurized supply lines need a proper fitting, since a failed patch at full pressure can soak a floor in minutes. Use it only until a plumber arrives.

    Stains on walls or ceilings are the most obvious sign. So does a damp odor. A drop in pressure, the sound of running water when nothing is on, a bill that jumps for no reason, or a meter that moves with every fixture off all mean there is a leak somewhere in the system. Brown water means corrosion.

    A visible leak is often fixed in an hour or two. Walls add time. Burst pipes behind walls or slab leaks can take several hours to a full day, depending on how much access is needed and whether the line has to be rerouted. The quote includes a time estimate.

    Corrosion from the inside of the pipe. Water chemistry drives it. High velocity, excess pressure and stray electrical current from poor grounding can all thin the copper wall until a tiny hole opens, usually on a horizontal run. Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is typically soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. More pinholes tend to follow.

    Clamp-on patches exist for emergencies. They are temporary. On pressurized water lines they rarely hold for long, and a DIY repair that does not meet code can fail inside a wall and cause far more damage than the original leak. Get it fixed properly.

    We use pipe and fittings from major manufacturers. Everything is code-listed.
