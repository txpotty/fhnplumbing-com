    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Kennewick, WA: The Details

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Kennewick

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for tile, laminate and butcher block counters. Usually the fastest upgrade. We check the cutout size, seal the rim and tighten the clips evenly, and in most rentals and older homes the job is done in a couple of hours.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Kennewick, WA

    Every fabricator handles undermount clips a bit differently. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. Either way, the bowl gets braced. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Kennewick

    Composite sinks are heavier than stainless and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. We check the strainer fit first. If the included strainer is too short for the sink wall, we swap in one that fits rather than overtightening and risking a chip around the opening.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Kennewick

    A double bowl drains both sides through one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. We pick the layout around your disposal and dishwasher, since a disposal on one side changes where the tee can go and how much fall each side gets. Heavy-wall fittings resist leaks.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Kennewick

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Kennewick, WA

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and many are deep single bowls with the drain off center. We plan the trap around that offset drain so the disposal fits and the cabinet keeps usable space on one side. Check the faucet reach as well.

    Kitchen Sink Installation Cost Factors in Kennewick, WA

    A drop-in swap costs less than an undermount on stone. Each extra, like a new disposal, a moved drain, a lowered wall stub or replacing galvanized stubs, adds labor and shows up as its own line on the quote. Plumbing is priced apart from the counter.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Kennewick, WA

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, with silicone run around the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, typically for a full day, before loading it with water. Rushing this causes the classic sag. Just an eighth of an inch of sag leaves a gap that catches water and food along the edge.
