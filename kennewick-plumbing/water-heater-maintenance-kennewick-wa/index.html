    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Kennewick

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Kennewick, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's significantly depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing costly tank leaks.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Kennewick

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely and efficiently. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for leaks.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Kennewick

    For electric water heaters, our maintenance service focuses on checking heating elements, thermostats, and electrical connections. We verify proper wiring and test for any short circuits or power problems that could affect performance. Sediment flushing is also performed, just like with gas models, to prevent buildup and protect the lower heating element from burning out prematurely in Kennewick.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Kennewick, WA

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as needed. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water quickly. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.
