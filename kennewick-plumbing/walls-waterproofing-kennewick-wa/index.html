    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Kennewick

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra attention before new tile is set.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Kennewick

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most reliable method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Crawlspaces and basements are both common in Washington homes, and a damp crawlspace is often the first sign of a drainage or plumbing problem. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also added to divert water away.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Kennewick, WA

    Older homes, especially those built before modern waterproofing standards, are particularly prone to water intrusion. Mid-century homes often have galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains, while some 1980s and 1990s homes were plumbed with gray polybutylene. Many older foundations may lack adequate exterior drainage or proper sealing. We specialize in assessing and upgrading waterproofing systems for these properties, often combining exterior excavation with interior drainage solutions. We respect the existing structure while installing modern, effective waterproofing, with attention to the challenges of older brick and stone foundations.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Kennewick, WA

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are common culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry for good.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Kennewick

    Many homes in Kennewick have crawl spaces, which are highly susceptible to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting stops ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an affordable way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Kennewick

    Even minor cracks in foundation walls can allow serious water intrusion. Our services include crack repair using epoxy or polyurethane injection to seal these entry points permanently. This method fills the entire depth of the crack, restoring strength and preventing water penetration. We address both vertical and horizontal cracks, knowing that horizontal cracks can indicate structural issues that need a thorough assessment before sealing. Addressing these early prevents further water damage and expensive future repairs.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Kennewick, WA
