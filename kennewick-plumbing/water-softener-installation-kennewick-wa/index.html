    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Kennewick, WA

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Newer valves meter actual water use, some connect to Wi-Fi for salt alerts and higher-capacity tanks regenerate less often for larger households. The savings add up. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Kennewick

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. Pairs well with a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Kennewick

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. We follow Culligan's requirements for the drain, bypass and electrical, and we program the system for your tested hardness so it does not over-regenerate. Connections and setup included. Existing loops make it faster.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Kennewick, WA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. FHN Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Kennewick, WA

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. Commercial softeners we install use larger resin tanks or twin-tank alternating systems so soft water is available around the clock, even during regeneration. Equipment lasts longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Kennewick

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. We follow the Ecodyne manual for the bypass, drain and overflow connections, then program hardness and regeneration settings for your water. We install store-bought units. Programming is part of the job.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Kennewick, WA

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. They are simple to program, easy to rebuild and parts are stocked almost everywhere, which matters when a seal fails ten years in. We program yours to your water. We confirm the setup before leaving.
