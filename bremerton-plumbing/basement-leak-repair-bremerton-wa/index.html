We quote after testing.

    Check the base of the walls after a heavy rain. Damp bands low on the wall and water at the cove joint are typical. With everything turned off, a moving water meter means a plumbing leak. Note where the water shows.

    Most plumbing leak repairs in a basement are done in one visit. With wall access, plan on two to four hours. Seepage repairs vary more, from half a day for an injected crack to most of a week for a full interior perimeter drain, which includes cutting and removing the slab edge, setting pipe and stone, and pouring new concrete.

    Crack repairs use two-part polyurethane or epoxy injection resin, depending on whether the crack is actively wet. Wet cracks get polyurethane. Our interior drains use rigid perforated pipe, which keeps its slope and can be jetted later.

    Leak repair stops a specific source, like a cracked pipe or a leaking wall crack. Waterproofing is broader. It manages groundwater around the entire foundation with drains, membranes and a sump, and it makes sense when water enters along many points rather than one obvious spot that a single repair can fix.

    Only some repairs need excavation. Outside membranes and new footing drains require digging to the footing. Inside repairs avoid the yard, which helps when a patio or driveway sits against the wall.

    Simple pipe repairs and crack injection usually do not need a permit. Larger work is a different story. Stack replacement, new sumps and backwater valves often need one, and we handle it.
