    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Bremerton

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape.     Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Bremerton, WA

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. A bigger burner needs more gas. We size the gas line before installing, since an undersized line cuts recovery.

    Atmospheric Vent Upgrades in Bremerton, WA

    Flue pipes on older atmospheric heaters rust at the joints and lose their upward slope, and a loose connector can spill exhaust into the closet. Bad vent sections and draft hoods get replaced. Then we test draft with the burner on.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Replacement in Bremerton, WA

    Commercial gas heaters in restaurants and offices often run 75 to 100 gallons at 75,000 BTU or more, and they need a gas line sized for that load. Code is stricter here. We size by peak demand and recovery, not tank volume alone.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Bremerton, WA

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. They cost more and need a condensate drain and an outlet. Payback depends on usage.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Bremerton

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. We disconnect the old unit and reconnect the new one to the existing gas line and flue. Closets need enough combustion air.
