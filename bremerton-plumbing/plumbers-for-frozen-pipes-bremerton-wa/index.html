    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in Bremerton

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Those get heat cable. We replace old constant-wattage tape with listed self-regulating cable.
