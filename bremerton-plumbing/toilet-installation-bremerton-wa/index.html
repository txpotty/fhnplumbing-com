    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. There is no tank to refill.     ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Bremerton

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. It is set that way so a grab bar fits.     Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Bremerton, WA

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Bremerton, WA

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. Suddenly the old flange is below the floor.     High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Bremerton, WA

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Look for the WaterSense label on the box. We steer customers toward models with a MaP score of 800 grams or higher, because a cheap low-flow bowl that needs two flushes saves nothing. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.

    Customer-Supplied Toilet Installation in Bremerton, WA

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Bremerton

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. Below about 20 psi, they flush poorly. We check static pressure at a hose bib before recommending one, and we warn customers about the noise, since the flush is loud enough to hear across the house. They rarely clog.
