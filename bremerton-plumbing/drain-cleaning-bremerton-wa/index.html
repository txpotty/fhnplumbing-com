    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Cleaning in Bremerton

    Most single fixture drain cleanings in Bremerton run about $125 to $275. Main line cleaning from a cleanout typically costs $175 to $450, and adding a camera inspection usually adds $150 to $350.

    Water that drains slowly, gurgling sounds, sewer odors and water backing up into a tub or shower are the most common signs. If more than one fixture is affected, the problem is likely in the main line.

    Most standard drain cleaning jobs, like clearing a kitchen or bathroom sink, can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. More complex main line blockages or jobs requiring hydro jetting might take 2 to 4 hours, depending on accessibility and severity.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens to trap hair and food particles, flush only toilet paper, and consider periodic professional drain maintenance, especially if you have older pipes or large trees nearby.

    A plunger or a small hand auger can handle a simple hair clog near the drain. For deep clogs, main line backups or repeat problems, a pro has the machines and camera to find and correct the real cause.

    Tree roots are attracted to the moisture and nutrients inside sewer lines. During dry periods, roots will seek out any tiny crack or loose joint in older pipes to reach water, then grow and expand, causing serious blockages. This is a common issue for homes with mature trees.

    Hydro jetting is usually safe for most modern plumbing systems. However, it may not suit severely corroded or fragile pipes, which are sometimes found in very old Bremerton properties. We always perform a camera inspection first to check pipe condition and use the safest method.
