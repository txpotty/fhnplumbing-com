    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Bremerton

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller.     Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Bremerton

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to early wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Bremerton residence.
