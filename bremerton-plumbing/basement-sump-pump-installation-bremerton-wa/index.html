    Residential Basement Sump Pump Installation in Bremerton

    A typical house pit holds an 18 by 22 inch basin with a 1/3 horsepower pump, and that covers most homes.     Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Bremerton

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. That keeps it dry and easy to reach, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Bremerton homes. Expect more noise.

    Same-Day Sump Pump Installation in Bremerton

    Same-day emergency installs cover pumps that quit in the middle of a storm. We remove standing water from the pit first, then set a new pump on a new check valve, plug it into a tested GFCI outlet and watch it run through several cycles before leaving your Bremerton basement. Then we talk about a backup.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Bremerton, WA

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. Eastern Washington deals more with drought and heat, while spring snowmelt can still raise creeks and groundwater in low areas. We add a second pump in the same pit with its own float, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak. The main pump keeps doing the daily work.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Bremerton

    Zoeller's M53 Mighty-Mate is the cast iron pump we install more than any other. It is a 3/10 horsepower unit with a vertical float switch, and it moves about 2,580 gallons an hour at 5 feet of lift, enough for a lot of Bremerton basements. Parts are easy to get.

    Quiet Submersible Pumps for Finished Basements in Bremerton

    Finished basements call for a quiet pump and a sealed lid.     Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Bremerton
