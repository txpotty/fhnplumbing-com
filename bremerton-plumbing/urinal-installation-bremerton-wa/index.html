    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Bremerton

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge.     ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Bremerton

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. The flush handle has to be easy to reach. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Bremerton

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. New gaskets always go in. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Bremerton

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. A hairline crack in vitreous china will eventually leak, and a bowl stained by years of hard scale often cannot be cleaned back to white, so a new fixture on the old carrier is usually the fastest fix. Carrier bolts get inspected first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    Urinal Rough-In for New Restrooms in Bremerton

    Tenant improvements need rough-in before the walls close.     Residential Urinal Plumbing in Bremerton, WA

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. The drain is usually 2 inch, the trap is built into the china, the fixture needs a vent within code distance, and the flush valve needs either a 3/4 inch supply or a lower-flow residential valve on 1/2 inch. Your city may want a permit. We check that first.

    Sensor Urinal Installation in Bremerton, WA

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. Battery or hardwired valves get a tuned sensing range and an optional sanitary flush for idle periods. Batteries last a few years. Hardwired units need an electrician first.
