    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Bremerton

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, typically under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. FHN Plumbing installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Bremerton for the best efficiency.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Bremerton, WA

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the complex excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, maintaining compliance with all local Washington ordinances for waste management.
