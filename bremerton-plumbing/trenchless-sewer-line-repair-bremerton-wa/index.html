    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Bremerton, WA

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Washington homeowners.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Bremerton, WA

    Tree and shrub roots seek out moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause repeat backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every couple of months.

    Cost Comparison of Trenchless vs Traditional in Bremerton, WA

    While the upfront cost of trenchless repair might sometimes seem higher than traditional excavation, it often becomes more cost-effective when you count the savings on landscaping, driveway or foundation repair.     Pipe Bursting in Bremerton, WA

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is best for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It typically requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Sectional Point Repair in Bremerton

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.
