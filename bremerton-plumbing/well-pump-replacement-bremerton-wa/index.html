    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Bremerton

    Regular maintenance, such as checking your well's water level, keeping the pressure tank air charge correct and fixing any leaks promptly, can extend your pump's life. Avoiding heavy water use and installing a good quality sediment filter can also reduce wear and tear on the pump.

    Common signs include no water from your faucets, weak or inconsistent water pressure, unusual noises from the well or pressure tank area, short cycling where the pump turns on and off frequently, or cloudy or muddy water.
