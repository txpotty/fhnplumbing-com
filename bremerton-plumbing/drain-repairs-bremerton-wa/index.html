    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Bremerton

    The cost of drain repairs in Bremerton, WA varies widely depending on the nature and severity of the problem.     Most trap and fixture drain repairs take one to two hours. Replacing drain pipe inside a wall or under a floor can take half a day to a full day. We give you a time estimate when we quote the job.

    Drain lines can break or collapse due to several factors, including shifting soil, tree root intrusion, corrosion from age, extreme temperature changes or heavy loads passing over underground pipes. Poor installation or defective materials can also contribute to early failure.

    We work with all standard drain pipe materials found in Washington homes, including PVC, ABS, cast iron and copper. Our repairs and replacements use high-quality, code-compliant materials from reputable manufacturers for durability and longevity.

    FHN Plumbing stands behind our work. We offer a warranty on our drain repair services, which varies depending on the type of repair and parts used. Our technicians will provide details on the specific warranty that applies to your service.

    The length of drain repairs depends entirely on the issue. A simple clog can often be cleared in an hour or two. More involved repairs, like replacing a section of a main sewer line, might take a full day or even several days, especially if permits or extensive digging are involved.

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to clear out stubborn blockages, grease buildup, mineral deposits and even tree roots from your pipes.
