    Backflow Preventer Testing in Bremerton

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers call for annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices.     Water Softener Maintenance in Bremerton

    Water softeners fight hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance prolongs its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing expensive scale damage.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Bremerton

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility right away.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Bremerton, WA

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits significantly from annual maintenance. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to early tank failure.
