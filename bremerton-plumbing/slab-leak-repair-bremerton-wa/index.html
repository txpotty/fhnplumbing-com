    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Bremerton, WA

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. The pipe swells and shrinks against rock every time hot water flows, and a recirculation pump keeps that going constantly, thinning the wall. We repair or reroute the line and can adjust the pump to cut wear.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Bremerton, WA

    Spot repairs fit leaks in otherwise good pipe. It is a single small opening. The floor gets opened, the pipe replaced and tested, and the trench compacted and patched, ready for your flooring contractor.

    Emergency Slab Leak Services in Bremerton

    A burst line under the slab can push water through grout lines in a few hours. Close your main valve. A crew comes out to isolate the broken line, cap it so the rest of the house has water, and make a temporary or permanent repair depending on what we find. That goes for anywhere in Bremerton.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Bremerton

    Locating comes before cutting. We use a ground mic and a thermal camera. The mic picks up the hiss of water escaping through concrete, and the camera shows warm water spreading from a hot line, so together they narrow the leak to a spot about a foot across. We test hot lines, cold lines and drains in your Bremerton home.

    Ground Radar and Acoustic Locating in Bremerton

    Ground-penetrating radar shows what is inside the slab before we cut. It finds rebar and cables. Combined with a listening device, radar finds leaks in any pipe type, even with traffic noise, since radar maps the route and the mic confirms the spot. We use both in Bremerton.

    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Bremerton, WA

    Copper and PEX fail in different ways under a slab. Copper tends to pinhole. Old copper wears through where it touches gravel or reacts with the water, while PEX usually leaks at a fitting that was crimped short or chewed by rodents near an access point. Trucks in Bremerton, WA stock fittings for each type.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Bremerton

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. Watch the bill instead. They show up as a higher bill, a meter that turns with every faucet off, or a patch of carpet that stays damp, and they soak the soil just as much as hot leaks do. Your Bremerton home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and repair or reroute it.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Bremerton

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. Rerouting skips it. We abandon the leaking run in place and install new pipe through walls or the attic, so the problem section is gone from service and the water travels through fresh line that you can reach later. Bremerton homes with original plumbing are good candidates.
