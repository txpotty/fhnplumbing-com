    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron changes the math. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Bremerton, WA

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. They are simple to program, easy to rebuild and parts are stocked almost everywhere, which matters when a seal fails ten years in. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Bremerton

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Programming is the last step. Regeneration time is usually set for 2 a.m.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Bremerton, WA

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. Commercial softeners we install use larger resin tanks or twin-tank alternating systems so soft water is available around the clock, even during regeneration. Equipment lasts longer. We schedule around your hours.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Bremerton

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. That pays back over time. We install several models.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Bremerton

    Water softener installation in Bremerton, WA usually runs $1,500 to $4,000. Where you land depends on the type and size of the system and whether we need to change any plumbing. Loops add cost. You get a written quote after we test.

    A good softener usually lasts ten to fifteen years. Unit quality, how hard your water is and how well the system is maintained all change that, and resin can be replaced to extend it. Service helps.

    White scale on faucets, spotty dishes, dry skin and a water heater that struggles are common signs. A water test confirms hardness.

    Fleck, Culligan, Ecodyne and Kenmore are some of the brands we install. We help you compare models and choose the one that fits your water and budget. Size matters more than brand.

    Three to six hours covers most standard installs. More complex jobs or ones with major plumbing changes can take a full day, and we keep your water off for as short a time as possible.

    Less scale in pipes and appliances is the big one. You also get longer appliance life, lower soap use, softer skin and much easier cleaning. Clothes come out softer.

    Manufacturer coverage on the unit is often 5 to 10 years on major parts. We cover our labor, usually for one year, and install to spec to keep the unit warranty valid.
