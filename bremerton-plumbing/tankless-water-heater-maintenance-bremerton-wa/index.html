    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Bremerton

    Most tankless descaling visits cost $175 to $350, depending on brand and access. Adding an isolation valve kit typically runs $150 to $300 more.

    Neglecting maintenance can lead to several problems, including reduced heating efficiency, higher utility bills, decreased hot water output and eventually, costly breakdowns. Mineral buildup can seriously restrict water flow and damage the heat exchanger, potentially leading to premature unit failure and expensive repairs or replacement.

    Common signs include reduced hot water flow, fluctuating water temperatures, strange noises coming from the unit, or error codes displayed on the control panel. If you notice any of these, it's a strong indication that your tankless water heater in Bremerton is due for professional maintenance.

    The descaling process involves shutting off the unit, isolating the water lines and connecting a circulating pump to flush a specialized acidic solution through the heat exchanger. This solution dissolves mineral deposits like calcium and magnesium, which are common in hard water, improving heat transfer efficiency.

    Most visits take one to two hours, including the descaling cycle. Installing isolation valves on the same trip adds roughly an hour.

    A softener or scale prevention system greatly reduces buildup in the heat exchanger. Some brands even recommend treatment when hardness is high. Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines.

    FHN Plumbing services all major brands of tankless water heaters, including Rinnai, Noritz, Navien, Bosch, State, Rheem and more. Our technicians are trained on the specific maintenance requirements and diagnostics for a wide range of models to provide expert care for your unit.

    Many manufacturers exclude scale damage from heat exchanger warranties. Regular, documented maintenance is the best way to keep your coverage intact.
