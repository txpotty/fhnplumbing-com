    Residential Gas Leak Detection in Bremerton

    Gas Line Replacement and Upgrades in Bremerton, WA

    Adding a gas range or a tankless heater can overload pipe that was sized years ago for just a furnace and a water heater.     Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Bremerton, WA

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. Bonding means a 6 AWG copper wire from the gas piping to the electrical grounding system. Plenty of older installs lack it. We repair CSST only with the matching brand's fittings, because TracPipe and Gastite parts do not interchange. A splice that skips that rule fails inspection.

    Most jurisdictions want a permit for new gas pipe or a moved line, though a straight connector swap often does not need one. We tell you up front which way your job falls.     Appliance Connector Replacement in Bremerton
