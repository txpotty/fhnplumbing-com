    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Bothell

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a good option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer dependable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to verify a proper seal.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Bothell, WA

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Bothell

    FHN Plumbing frequently installs Uponor PEX systems, a leading brand known for high-quality, durable PEX-A piping. Uponor's ProPEX expansion fittings create strong, reliable and leak-resistant connections, ideal for the demands of a home's plumbing. We recommend Uponor for its long track record and longevity, giving Bothell homeowners peace of mind about their plumbing investment.

If you are considering radiant heat for a specific area of your home or business in Bothell, PEX is the pipe to use.

    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Bothell

    A whole-home PEX repipe usually costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on home size, bathroom count and access. Smaller jobs like a single bathroom remodel rough-in usually run $800 to $2,500.

    We make small, targeted openings to run new pipe and patch them when finished. Most homeowners are surprised by how little drywall work is needed.

    Signs you might need PEX repiping include persistently low water pressure, discolored or rusty water, frequent leaks, or old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes. These issues are common in older Bothell homes and point to a failing plumbing system.

    We install Uponor PEX-A along with Viega, Zurn and SharkBite PEX-B systems. We recommend the system that suits your project and budget.

    PEX must be protected from sunlight because UV light degrades it. Outdoors it needs to be buried, sleeved or completely covered.

    PEX can be used for outdoor applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light, which can break down the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should always be buried or otherwise shielded.

    We mainly use high-quality, reputable PEX brands such as Uponor (PEX-A) and Viega (PEX-B), which are known for their durability and reliability. These brands meet or exceed industry standards and are approved for use in Washington plumbing applications, providing a long-lasting and dependable plumbing system for your home.

    PEX piping is designed to be highly durable and has an estimated lifespan of 50 years or more. Its resistance to corrosion, scaling and freeze damage adds to its long-term reliability, making it a sound investment for your Bothell home.
