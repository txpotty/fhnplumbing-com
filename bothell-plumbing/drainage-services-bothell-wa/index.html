    We recommend skipping them. They can eat at old pipe. They rarely remove scale or roots, so the clog comes back, and if the cleaner sits in the trap when a plumber opens it, it can splash and burn, which is why we always ask before we start.

    We install and service Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty pumps. Cast iron models last longest. Battery backups with their own float and an alarm cover power outages and pump failures.

    Drain clearing carries a service guarantee for a set period. Repeat clogs get a return visit. The guarantee assumes the line is sound, so if the camera shows roots through a broken joint or a collapsed section, we tell you before we leave that clearing is a temporary fix.

    Drain cleaning usually means a cable machine with a cutting head that bores through the clog. It is quick and cheap.     They solve different problems. A French drain collects water that soaks into the soil. A channel drain catches surface water sheeting across concrete, like a driveway that slopes toward the garage, and many yards need one of each because the water arrives both ways during a hard storm.

    Clearing a clog does not need a permit. Pipe repairs often require one. Replacing a section of sewer, adding a cleanout, or connecting a new drain to the sewer generally requires a permit and an inspection, and we handle that paperwork and schedule the inspector as part of the job.
