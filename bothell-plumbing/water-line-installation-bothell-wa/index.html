    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Installation in Bothell

    The cost of water line installation in Bothell can vary significantly, typically ranging from $1,500 to $6,000 for a main service line.     Steep slopes and wet soils can shift over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. Soil that shifts and settles puts stress on underground pipes. This is why flexible materials like PEX are often recommended, and why proper bedding and backfill during installation are essential to prevent pipe damage from ground movement.

    A typical residential water line takes one to two days, plus time for utility locates and inspection. Rocky ground or long runs can add a day or two.

    Some homeowners dig their own trench, but connecting to the meter, sizing the pipe and passing inspection are best done by a licensed plumber. A poor joint underground can leak for months before anyone notices.

    Trenching does disturb a narrow path, but we backfill, compact and regrade when we finish. If the route crosses concrete or mature landscaping, we can discuss trenchless options.

    In most communities the homeowner owns the line from the meter to the home, while the utility owns the meter and the line on the street side. We confirm responsibility with your provider before work begins.
