    Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Bothell

    Hard water can lead to mineral buildup, or scaling, in your tankless water heater's heat exchanger, cutting efficiency and eventually causing breakdowns. Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. Our descaling service in Bothell involves flushing the unit with a specialized cleaning solution to dissolve these mineral deposits. This preventative maintenance and repair step is crucial for extending the lifespan of your tankless heater and keeping it at peak performance.
