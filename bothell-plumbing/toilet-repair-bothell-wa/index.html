    Toilet Flush Repair in Bothell, WA

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Bothell, WA

    The rubber parts in a toilet, the flapper, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the bolt washers and the wax ring at the floor, all break down with age and chlorine. They get stiff.     Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Bothell

    Fill Valve Replacement in Bothell

    Old ballcock valves with a brass arm and a big plastic float are loud and slow to refill, and they tend to stick open when scale builds up in the ports. The tank gets noticeably quieter.
