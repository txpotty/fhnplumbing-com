    Snaking involves using an auger to punch a hole through a clog, usually leaving behind some debris.     While hydrojetting provides a thorough clean, some underlying issues like severely damaged or collapsed pipes might cause clogs to return.     Generally, a permit is not required for standard hydrojet plumbing services that involve clearing existing drain or sewer lines within a private property in Bothell.
