    Pressure Switch Replacement in Bothell

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Bothell

    Grundfos pumps are popular for their energy efficiency and advanced technology. Our team is trained in the specifics of Grundfos well pump diagnosis and repair, including their variable speed drive systems. We address typical problems like sensor failures, motor issues, or control system malfunctions. Count on FHN Plumbing for focused service that maintains the performance of your Grundfos well pump in Bothell, WA.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Bothell, WA

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Heavy, prolonged rain from atmospheric river storms is a major factor in western Washington, raising groundwater and filling crawlspaces and basements. Lightning and power surges frequently damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help reduce repeat failures.

    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Bothell

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a stubborn problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself. This could be due to a failing pressure switch, a partially clogged well screen, a leak in the supply line, or even a pump that is no longer meeting demand. Our team conducts careful diagnostics to pinpoint the exact cause of low pressure in your Bothell home, delivering a targeted and effective repair solution.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Bothell, WA

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor.
