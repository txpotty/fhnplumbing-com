    Slow Drain Repair in Bothell

    Gurgling toilets and a tub that drains slow often trace to a plugged roof vent or a sagging drain line. After clearing it, we camera the line and fix whatever is broken. Common fixes include replacing corroded cast iron, cutting out failed ABS joints, rebuilding a broken trap arm and resetting toilets on a new wax ring and flange. No monthly snaking.

    Water Heater Valve and Element Repair in Bothell

    Rumbling or popping from a tank means sediment on the bottom. A rotten-egg smell in the hot water usually means the anode rod is reacting with the water. We replace thermocouples, thermostats, elements, gas valves, anode rods and relief valves from truck stock, which covers the large majority of tank repairs we see in a year. A tank leaking at the seam is a different story. No part fixes that, and we tell you plainly.

    Gas Leak Repair in Bothell

    Rotten-egg odor near the range or a hiss at the meter means get out of the house first. Call the gas utility from outside, then call us for the repair. Our plumbers locate the leak with an electronic detector and soap solution, then replace the failed connector, valve or section of pipe to code. No shortcuts on gas.

    Under-Slab Line Repair in Bothell, WA

    Housing in Washington ranges from early 1900s homes to new construction, with plumbing materials that change a lot from one decade to the next. Copper or PEX under the slab lasts a long time until a fitting or a rubbed spot gives out. We find the leak with acoustic and thermal equipment, then lay out the repair options side by side with a price for each. Epoxy lining is one choice. A reroute through the attic or walls is often the cleaner one.

    Drain Line Repair in Bothell

    Slow drains that come back right after a snake usually mean the pipe itself is damaged. Cracked cast iron or a sagging run will hold debris every time. We run a camera to find the spot, then replace that section or hydro-jet the line if buildup is the only problem. Grease is the usual buildup.

    Drain and Pipe Repair in Bothell

    Hydro-jetting at around 4,000 psi clears grease and scale that a cable snake only pokes a small hole through, which is why the clog keeps coming back. FHN Plumbing follows every jetting job with a camera so you can see whether the pipe is sound. Damaged sections get cut out and replaced in whatever material matches the line. Jetting alone will not fix a broken pipe.

    Water Softener Repair in Bothell, WA

    Many rural Washington homes rely on private wells, and some carry iron, manganese or other minerals that call for testing and treatment. White crust on faucets and spotty glasses are the first signs a softener has stopped working. Most of the time it is a salt bridge or a plugged injector. FHN Plumbing repairs the common brands and tests the water hardness before and after the fix so you can see the number drop. Resin lasts ten to fifteen years.

    Gas and Electric Water Heater Repair in Bothell

    Tank and tankless heaters fail in different ways. FHN Plumbing repairs both, including the major brands, gas and electric. On a tank we check the thermocouple, gas valve, elements and anode rod. Tankless trouble is usually scale in the heat exchanger or a dirty inlet screen, and a descaling flush fixes more of them than new parts do.
