    Backflow Assembly Testing and Rebuilds in Bothell

    Most water providers require annual tests on commercial backflow assemblies, and many send late notices that come with fines. Test early.     Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Bothell

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. We push the camera from a cleanout, note the footage at every break, root mass or sag, and use a locator to mark depth and position on the ground above. You get the video file. That footage helps you budget repairs and explain them to owners or a board.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Bothell, WA

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. Our techs test flue draft, check combustion, measure the temperature at the farthest fixture and flush sediment, which together tell us whether to repair the unit or plan its replacement. Tankless units get a descaling flush. If the building has a recirc loop, we test the pump and check valve too.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Bothell, WA

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. Clogged wall-hung toilets sometimes need the carrier checked, because a loose bowl on a carrier can leak behind the wall. Low pressure on one fixture usually points to a clogged supply stop.
