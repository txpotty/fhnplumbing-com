    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Bothell, WA

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is typically soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. Flush it yearly. We install isolation valves for that.
