    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Bothell

    Soft water can be slightly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. Then we explain your options. A softener, a filter or a scale-reducing system can extend heater and fixture life, and we tell you which one fits the hardness we measured.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Bothell, WA

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. Backflow devices, grease traps, large heaters, flushometers and the main sewer are all part of it. Inspectors like documentation. The written report helps facility managers plan and budget repairs before a health inspector or the water provider writes them up.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Bothell, WA

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging. The camera goes in at a cleanout and travels to the main or the tank, recording roots, cracks, offsets and bellies along the way. A locator marks the depth. Steep slopes and wet soils can move over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Bothell

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in Washington houses. Common finds include old pipe, stiff shutoffs, tired heaters, slow drains and rooty or sagging sewers. We test each one directly. Catching a corroded fitting or a partial sewer blockage now keeps it from becoming a ceiling stain or a backup on a holiday weekend.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Bothell, WA

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. Old brass connectors, missing shutoffs or drip legs and rust all get noted, and we sweep joints with a gas detector. Leaks get reported immediately. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Bothell, WA

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too.
