    The typical lifespan of a traditional tank water heater is about 10 to 15 years, while tankless water heaters can last 20 years or more with proper maintenance.     An expansion tank absorbs excess water pressure created as water heats up inside the tank. Many homes in Bothell, WA, particularly those with closed-loop plumbing systems, are required by code to have an expansion tank to protect the water heater and plumbing system from damage.

    In most areas, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement or installation to confirm the work meets local building and safety codes. Washington adopts the Uniform Plumbing Code statewide with state amendments, and local building departments handle permits and inspections.
