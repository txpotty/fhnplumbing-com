    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Bothell

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. A small pump circulates descaler. We also clean the inlet screen and check venting and gas supply.

    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Bothell

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast. We check the brine level, clean the injector screen, inspect the resin bed for fouling and confirm the regeneration cycle starts and finishes, then test the treated water hardness at a faucet. Resin cleaner helps. Older resin may need replacing.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Bothell, WA

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. Gas tanks get a burner, flue and leak check, and electric tanks get element and thermostat tests. Both get flushed. Scale on the tank floor wastes energy and shortens tank life.

    Water Softener Service in Bothell

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. Salt level, brine tank, injector and a test regeneration all get checked. Hardness gets tested after. A failed softener sends hard water through the house without warning.
