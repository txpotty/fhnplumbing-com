    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Replacement in Bothell

    Shower replacement in Bothell, WA runs from about $1,500 for a basic unit swap to over $10,000 for a custom tiled walk-in. Shower type, materials and plumbing changes drive the price, with slab drain moves adding the most. Tiled showers run higher. We quote after an on-site look.

    Staining and cracks on the pan or walls are early signs. Constant drips, stubborn mold and weak pressure from old pipes all point to replacement. We confirm it on site.

    Kohler, Delta, Moen, American Standard and Sterling cover most of our installs in Washington homes. We can work with a brand you prefer or recommend options that fit your budget, with an eye on parts availability down the road. We care about future parts.

    Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. Scale builds on heads, handles and glass doors over time. Left alone, it cuts water flow, stiffens the valve and dulls finishes, but a water softener or regular cleaning slows it down a lot. Ask us about softeners.

    Trim and showerheads are DIY-friendly. Hidden work like valves and pans is best left to a licensed plumber.

    Labor is guaranteed for a set period. Warranty details vary by material and manufacturer, and we include the full warranty information with your quote so you know what is covered. We answer warranty questions.

    Most replacements wrap up in one to three days. Custom tile showers or jobs with a lot of plumbing rerouting can take three to seven days, especially if we find structural damage once the old shower is out. We give you a timeline up front.

    Watch for leaks that return after repairs. Cracked pans or units, soft floors near the shower, mold that keeps coming back and old valves without scald protection are the other signs.
