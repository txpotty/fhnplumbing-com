    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Lacey

    A high water alarm belongs on every ejector system, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. Audible alarms on their own power or Wi-Fi alarms that text you give warning while the pit is still filling, not after waste reaches the floor. Heavy, prolonged rain from atmospheric river storms is a major factor in western Washington, raising groundwater and filling crawlspaces and basements. Backup power can help.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Lacey, WA

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for a very long time, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. They work well for new systems and for drop-in replacements, since many Zoeller models fit existing pits and discharge piping as is. Parts are easy to find.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Lacey

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Lacey

    Residential ejector systems in Lacey serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. The setup is a sealed basin, a pump, a float switch, a check valve and a vent. Simple parts, exact install. Install mistakes cause more trouble than hardware, so the vent and lid seal get extra attention.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Lacey

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so every fixture drains to a sealed pit instead of flowing by gravity. We cut the slab, trench the drain lines to the basin, set it, run a two-inch vent up through the house to the roof and pipe the discharge to the building drain with a check valve and shutoff. Then we patch the concrete.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Lacey

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. A grinder makes sense when the sewer is distant, uphill or pressurized, such as a pressure sewer where each home pumps into a shared main. They cost more. A standard ejector often covers a basement bath.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Lacey

    Liberty ejector pumps are a common pick on our jobs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models. Liberty sells packaged systems with basin, lid, pump and check valve already matched, which means less field fitting and a listed assembly for inspection. We still add a shutoff.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Lacey, WA

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars send gray water down that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A smaller laundry tray pump or a compact ejector can handle it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer dumps at once, with a lint filter so fibers do not wrap the impeller. Lint kills small pumps.
