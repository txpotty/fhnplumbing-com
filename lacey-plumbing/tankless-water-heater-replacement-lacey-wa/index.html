    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Lacey

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Lacey, WA

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. We replace failed units without shutting down the rest. Hand sinks and dish stations have to hit the temperatures health code requires.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Lacey, WA

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Covers and wiring take a beating outdoors. Hard freezes are infrequent on the west side of Washington, but short cold snaps catch many homes with uninsulated pipes off guard. We check the freeze protection settings and the pipe cover kit on every outdoor swap. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Lacey

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. We replace Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart units. A heat pump water heater is sometimes the better fit, and we will tell you.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Lacey, WA

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Their combi boilers handle both space heat and domestic water. Both are in our wheelhouse.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Lacey, WA

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. The catch is gas, since most tankless units need a 3/4-inch line or bigger plus new venting. We check the meter size and pipe run before quoting, and we cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer needed. Plan on a full day.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Lacey, WA

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Plenty of panels do not have room. We check panel capacity and wire gauge before quoting.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Lacey, WA

    Waiting a minute for hot water at the far bathroom wastes thousands of gallons a year in a long single-story house. Navien A models and Rinnai units with internal pumps can recirculate through a return line or a crossover valve under the farthest sink. We set a schedule. Usually just the morning and evening peaks.
