    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Lacey

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacement every three to five years.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Lacey

    Eastern Washington deals more with drought and heat, while spring snowmelt can still raise creeks and groundwater in low areas.     Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Lacey, WA

    The float switch is the most common reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Lacey, WA

    If your sump pump is acting up, making unusual noises or not turning on, our emergency diagnostic service can quickly identify the problem. We use specialized tools to troubleshoot electrical issues, motor failures and float switch malfunctions. Prompt diagnosis and repair are essential to prevent water buildup, especially during unpredictable rainy seasons in Lacey, WA.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Lacey, WA

    For homes equipped with pedestal sump pumps, FHN Plumbing provides dedicated maintenance services in Lacey, WA. We focus on inspecting the external motor, ensuring proper lubrication and checking the impeller for any obstructions. This type of pump, with its motor above the water, requires specific attention to its shaft and bearings to prevent premature wear and support reliable performance during heavy rains.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Lacey, WA

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Lacey

    Our team is skilled in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in Lacey due to their quiet operation and compact design. Maintenance involves pulling the pump to inspect the watertight seals, clearing the intake screen and checking the internal components for corrosion or wear. We also verify the electrical connections are secure and free from moisture, avoiding electrical hazards and supporting longevity.
