    Repiping Work We Handle in Lacey, WA

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Lacey

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Clogged aerators are a clue. Replacing galvanized with PEX or copper ends the rust, brings pressure back and stops the leaks that start at the threaded joints where galvanized is thinnest. Old pipe stays in walls when pulling it would wreck finished surfaces.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Lacey, WA

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average house often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper costing more. One slab leak with access and repair often costs $1,000 to $4,000. Do the math. By the second or third leak, a repipe usually pays for itself, and we put both options side by side so you can decide.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Lacey, WA

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. Full replacement is the fix. Insurers often ask about it at renewal.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Lacey

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. Type L tolerates UV, stays rigid and has decades of field history. It costs more. We sweat joints with lead-free solder or press them, hang the pipe at proper support intervals and test the whole system before patching.

    Copper Repiping in Lacey, WA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. Our copper work uses thicker Type L pipe, joined with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings, rather than the Type M found in many older houses. Expect a slightly longer job. Most copper jobs run a day longer than PEX, since each turn needs its own fitting.

    Commercial Repipe Services in Lacey

    Older commercial buildings in Lacey often have galvanized risers, copper that has pinholed or plastic pipe that no longer meets code. We replace supply risers, branch lines and drains while keeping offices, shops or apartments running as much as possible. Shutoffs get scheduled ahead. Work is phased by zone, and we coordinate access, keys and noise limits with the property manager.

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Lacey

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more involved. We coordinate with the HOA, schedule water shutoffs that may affect neighbors and protect adjoining units from dust and noise as much as we can. Building-wide jobs go in phases. For larger buildings, we repipe one stack or wing at a time so residents can stay in place.
