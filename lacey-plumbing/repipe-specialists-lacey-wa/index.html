    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Lacey, WA

    Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is generally soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. Water in many areas contains minerals that cause scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can accelerate corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral buildup, ensuring a longer-lasting plumbing system.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Lacey

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab.     PEX Pipe Replacement in Lacey, WA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. FHN Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Lacey, WA, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup.
