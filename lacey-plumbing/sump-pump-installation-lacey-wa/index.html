    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Lacey

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Lacey

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Lacey, WA

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. Undersized pumps get upsized. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Lacey

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. Install time is shorter too. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Lacey, WA

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Lacey, WA

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Lacey, WA

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. Backup and combo units are available too. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.
