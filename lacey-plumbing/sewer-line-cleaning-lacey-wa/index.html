    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Line Cleaning in Lacey

    Sewer line cleaning in Lacey usually costs $200 to $500 with a cable machine, or $400 to $900 with hydro jetting. Adding a recorded camera inspection usually runs $150 to $350.

    The duration of sewer line cleaning varies depending on the severity and accessibility of the blockage. A straightforward clog might take 1 to 2 hours, while more complex issues involving extensive root removal or multiple access points could take 3 to 4 hours or more. We will provide an expected timeline after our initial inspection.

    We firmly advise against chemical drain cleaners for sewer line clogs. These products often contain harsh chemicals that can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and are usually ineffective against severe blockages like tree roots. They can also pose a health risk and make professional cleaning more difficult.

    In most cases, sewer line cleaning methods like snaking and hydro jetting are non-invasive and do not require digging. We access the line through existing cleanouts. Digging is usually only necessary if there is a severe structural issue like a collapsed pipe requiring replacement, which we would identify with a camera inspection first.

    Lines with roots or past clogs often need cleaning every 12 to 24 months. Newer PVC lines without issues may go years without service.

    Root treatment products can slow regrowth after a mechanical cleaning, but they do not remove a root mass. Cleaning first is the essential step.

    Generally, standard sewer line cleaning, like snaking or hydro jetting to clear a clog, does not require a permit in Lacey. However, if the cleaning reveals a need for excavation or significant repair to the sewer line itself, permits from the local building department would likely be required. We handle all necessary permit applications for such projects.

    We use sectional and drum cable machines, hydro jetters and color camera systems with locators from established professional brands.
