    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Lacey, WA

    Submersible sump pumps are designed to sit directly in the sump pit, submerged in water, which makes them quieter and less intrusive. They are powerful and effective for basements that take on a lot of water. Our team in Lacey, WA will install your submersible pump with precision, ensuring proper sealing, discharge piping and electrical connections for quiet, dependable operation, which matters most in finished basements.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Lacey

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which protects the motor. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and protecting hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the discharge.

    If your existing sump pump is old, failing or no longer adequate for your home, FHN Plumbing offers expert primary sump pump replacement.     Sump Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Lacey, WA

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit is the least expensive option, usually a few hundred dollars plus the pump.
