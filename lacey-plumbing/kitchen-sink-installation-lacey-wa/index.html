    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Lacey

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. The bowl is mounted, the rim sealed with silicone and the sink braced while the adhesive sets, normally overnight before it can take a full bowl of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Lacey, WA

    Most new kitchen sinks get paired with a disposal and a dishwasher. We mount the disposal, run the dishwasher drain through an air gap or high loop and install a dedicated hot supply with its own shutoff. One visit costs less than three. Every connection is tested under real use.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Lacey

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and the lower the number, the thicker the steel.
