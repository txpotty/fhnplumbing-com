    Faucet and Disposal Hookup in Lacey, WA

    New sinks often get a new disposal. The disposal goes on a new flange, the dishwasher knockout gets punched, and the outlet lines up above the trap. Forgetting the knockout plug floods the dishwasher. We run both before leaving.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Lacey

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. Getting an undermount centered under a polished cutout so the reveal is even on all sides takes a helper, a few shims and patience before the clips get tightened. A crooked reveal never goes away. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Lacey, WA

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Setting the faucet and drain while the bowl is on sawhorses is faster and seals better than working in the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.
