    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Lacey

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and protecting hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. Packing and seat washers get replaced on standard hose bibs, and we install frost-free sillcocks with the valve seat inside the warm wall when an old one has split. A vacuum breaker goes on every new one.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Lacey, WA

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touchless faucets add a solenoid and a battery pack to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Lacey

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. The hose usually splits near the quick-connect fitting. We replace hoses, spray heads and weights with brand parts, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Lacey, WA

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Check the cabinet floor. New O-rings go on and the mounting nut gets tightened, then reseal the base so water on the counter cannot get under it.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Lacey, WA

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Many brands sell the handle on its own. The replacement handle gets matched to both the stem and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Lacey, WA

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. There are slip-on and threaded spouts. The pipe stub gets measured so we bring the right spout, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Lacey

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. The seals are cheap. If the discs are scratched, the whole cartridge gets replaced, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Lacey, WA

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure. Soft water can be slightly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. We pull the aerator with a key and clean or replace it, then check the cartridge screens. If every fixture is weak, the problem is elsewhere.
