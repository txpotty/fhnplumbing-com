    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. One head works fine off a standard valve, while multiple outlets need a thermostatic valve, volume controls and larger 3/4-inch supply lines. We plan the layout first. The water heater gets checked for capacity too.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Lacey, WA

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Lacey, WA

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves hold a set temperature and add volume control, which works well with rain heads and handhelds. Both prevent scalding. We help you choose based on how many outlets you plan.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Lacey, WA

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. We add solid wood blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare, set the valve within reach of a seat and install a handheld on a slide bar at a height that works seated or standing. No high curbs. ADA layouts are available on request.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Lacey, WA

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. Each valve is set to the depth its trim requires, and we include integral stops whenever the valve supports them. Trim goes on after tile. That timing keeps the trim from getting scratched or caked with thinset while the tile is set.
