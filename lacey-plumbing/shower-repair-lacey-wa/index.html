    Shower Diverter Repair in Lacey, WA

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. Usually a new spout fixes it. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Lacey, WA

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Close the main valve where the water line enters the house, or the shower's own stops if it has them behind an access panel. Next, call us. FHN Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Lacey

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. That spool is supposed to slide back and forth to keep the hot and cold mix even when pressure drops on one side, and scale can freeze it in place. A new cartridge fixes it. If you cannot get the water hot enough, the anti-scald limit stop may just need adjusting.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Lacey

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. We reseal the threads. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Lacey, WA

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. The Moen 1222 runs the Posi-Temp line, and the Delta RP46074 runs the Monitor 13 and 14 series, and both wear out on a schedule you can almost set a calendar by. We stock both. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Lacey

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. A new cartridge usually fixes it. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Lacey

    Temperature that swings or a handle that fights you points to the valve. If the valve body is pitted or parts no longer exist, we swap the valve, often through an access opening from the closet side. The new valve will have scald protection. You can step up to a thermostatic valve for tighter control.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Lacey, WA

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. Cleaning often works. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.
