    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Lacey, WA

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Installation in Lacey

    The cost of water line installation in Lacey, WA varies widely depending on pipe material, PEX or copper, the length of the line, excavation depth and site access.     Steep slopes and wet soils can shift over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. Soil that shifts and settles puts stress on underground pipes. This is why flexible materials like PEX are often recommended, and why proper bedding and backfill during installation are essential to prevent pipe damage from ground movement.

    In most communities the homeowner owns the line from the meter to the home, while the utility owns the meter and the line on the street side. We verify responsibility with your provider before work begins.

    Yes, most jurisdictions require a plumbing permit and inspection for a new or replaced service line. We pull the permit and schedule the inspection as part of our service.

    PEX is flexible, less expensive, resistant to freezing and corrosion, and has fewer fittings, which means fewer potential leak points. Copper is rigid, more expensive, has a proven track record, is naturally antimicrobial and can withstand higher temperatures. Both are solid choices, and the best option depends on your budget, property needs and local water conditions in Lacey.
