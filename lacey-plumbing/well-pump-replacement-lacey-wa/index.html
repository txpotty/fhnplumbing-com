    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in Lacey, WA

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They are pricier than a standard pump. Add the CU 301 controller to an SQE and you get constant pressure.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Lacey, WA

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. A pinhole in the suction pipe lets air in, the pump loses prime, and it runs dry until the seal burns up. We pressure test the line. A well that is too deep for suction needs a submersible, and we will quote that instead.

    Emergency Well Pump Replacement in Lacey

    Dry faucets on a well-only property mean no flushing or cooking, so we treat those as same-day calls whenever the crew and the weather allow. We keep 1/2 and 3/4 HP submersibles on the truck. Splice kits and pressure switches ride along too.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Lacey, WA

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. Deep-well jets with a two-pipe setup can reach further, but they work harder. The common failure is losing prime. Most of the time a bad foot valve or a suction leak is letting water drain back, and we check those before condemning the pump.

    Well Pump Efficiency Upgrades in Lacey, WA

    Older 1 HP pumps running against a 60 psi switch use more power than most people expect, especially on a well with a long horizontal run to the house. Right-sizing the pump or adding a variable-speed drive often trims the electric bill. Not always worth it. We run the numbers with you.

    Submersible Well Pump Replacement in Lacey

    A submersible pump hangs on drop pipe inside a 4-inch or 6-inch casing, often 100 to 400 feet down, so pulling one is a two-person job with a pump hoist. Sand in the impellers and lightning surges are the usual culprits. Before pulling, we test the windings with a megohmmeter. That tells us whether the problem is down the hole or up top.

    Well Pump Sizing in Lacey

    Sizing starts with the well log. The log, or a sounder down the casing, gives us static water level and well depth, and a draw-down test tells us roughly how many gallons per minute the well can actually give. Pumps bigger than the well can supply run dry.
