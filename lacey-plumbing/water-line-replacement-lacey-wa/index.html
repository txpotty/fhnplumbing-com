    PEX Water Line Installation in Lacey, WA

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Eastern and mountain areas of Washington freeze regularly and deeply, so insulated pipes and winterized irrigation systems are standard. PEX can swell a little in a freeze and go back to shape, where copper splits. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and keep it covered from sunlight at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Lacey

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. Street pressure does not bother it. We bed it in sand, keep it away from sharp rock, and sleeve it where it passes through a foundation wall, because concrete and copper rubbing together for twenty years will wear through the pipe. PEX is cheaper.

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Lacey, WA

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some insurers will not write a policy on a house that still has it. Worth replacing.
