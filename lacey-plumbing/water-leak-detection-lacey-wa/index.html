    Slab Leak Detection in Lacey

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often indicated by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. FHN Plumbing uses dedicated acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is essential to preventing major foundation damage.

    Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in Lacey, WA

    Toilets are one of the most common sources of wasted water. A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off can leak silently around the clock. We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are usually quick and save water right away.

    Leak Detection for PEX and Copper Pipes in Lacey

    Whether your plumbing system uses modern PEX tubing or traditional copper pipes, FHN Plumbing has the expertise to detect leaks in both materials. PEX, while flexible, can be susceptible to damage from rodents or improper installation, while copper can corrode over time, especially with hard water. Our tools are effective across many pipe materials, ensuring accurate detection regardless of your home's plumbing type.

    Hidden Wall & Ceiling Leak Detection in Lacey, WA

    Leaks behind walls or in ceilings can cause significant damage before they become visible, leading to stained drywall, peeling paint or a musty odor. These often stem from faulty pipe connections, old galvanized pipes or issues with shower pans and drains. Our technicians use moisture meters and infrared cameras to scan these areas, identifying elevated moisture levels and temperature differences that indicate a hidden leak, minimizing the need for extensive wall demolition.

    Smart Leak Detection Device Installation in Lacey

    Smart shutoff valves like Moen Flo and Phyn monitor water flow and can shut off water on their own when they detect a leak. Small sensors placed near water heaters, sinks and washers send alerts to your phone. We install and set up these systems so you get warnings before small leaks become big ones. Some insurers offer discounts for these devices.
