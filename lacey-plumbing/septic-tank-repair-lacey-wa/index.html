    Common causes of septic tank failure include infrequent pumping, which lets solids accumulate, tree root intrusion into pipes or the tank, excessive water usage overloading the system, driving heavy vehicles over the drain field and using harsh chemicals that kill beneficial bacteria. Dense, clay-rich soils can also add to drain field issues.

    FHN Plumbing services and repairs all major septic tank brands and types, including concrete, fiberglass and plastic tanks and components from manufacturers like Infiltrator Systems, Presby Environmental and Advanced Drainage Systems.     The length of a septic tank repair depends on the complexity of the issue. Simple clog removals might take a few hours, while more extensive repairs like drain field work could span several days. We will provide an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.

    Key signs include slow drains, gurgling toilets, sewage odors inside or outside your home, oddly lush or soggy spots in your yard near the septic tank or drain field, and sewage backups into your home.     Frequent causes of septic tank failure include improper waste disposal, such as flushing non-biodegradable items, lack of regular pumping, tree root intrusion, heavy vehicle traffic over the drain field and poor soil drainage.
