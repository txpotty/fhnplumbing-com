    Effluent Pump Replacement in Lacey, WA

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. A pump that hums but moves no water has a stuck impeller or a bad capacitor. Our tech pulls the old unit and checks the discharge before the new one goes in. Floats get replaced at the same time.

    Septic Code and Permit Work in Lacey, WA

    New tanks and new fields need a permit from the local health authority in most of the country, along with any change to the system's layout. We draw the site plan and file the paperwork, then book the inspector before anything is covered. Small fixes like a tee usually skip the permit. The inspector's sign-off goes in your file.

    Septic Maintenance and Small Repairs in Lacey

    A sludge judge, a long clear tube, tells us how full the tank really is. We measure sludge and scum on every maintenance visit, and pumping is due once solids fill roughly a third of the tank. Filters get rinsed. Small cracks around the riser get sealed before groundwater finds them.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Lacey

    A tank that never fills to the outlet, or one that fills slowly with groundwater after rain, has a leak. We pump it down and look at the walls and seams with a light, and dye flushed down a fixture shows us where the water moves. Hairline cracks get a hydraulic cement patch. Split tanks get replaced.

    Drain Field Line Jetting in Lacey

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping. A jetter goes into each lateral from the D-box or an end cleanout, and what washes back shows how plugged it was. Roots get cut. If the soil itself has sealed, jetting will not fix it, and we say so.

    Camera Inspection of Septic Baffles in Lacey, WA

    Some tanks have no riser, so we run a push camera through the small inspection port to see the baffles. That saves digging up the whole lid. A cracked or missing outlet tee means we dig at the outlet end alone, then cut in a new PVC tee and an effluent filter housing. Concrete lids get a riser while they are open.
