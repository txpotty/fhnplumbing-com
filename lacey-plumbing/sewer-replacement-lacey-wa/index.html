    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Lacey, WA

    Where applicable, we offer trenchless sewer repair methods like pipe bursting. This technique involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, simultaneously breaking the existing pipe apart. It's a less disruptive option that requires only two small access points, saving your landscaping and driveway from extensive excavation. Trenchless methods are ideal for certain types of pipe damage and property layouts in Lacey.

    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Lacey, WA

    Many older homes in Lacey, WA still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time. When we perform a sewer replacement, we often suggest upgrading to modern, durable materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene). These materials are more flexible, resistant to corrosion and roots, and have a much longer lifespan, providing a major upgrade to your home's plumbing infrastructure.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Lacey, WA

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the best option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench once it passes inspection. This method allows full correction of slope issues.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Lacey

    For severely damaged or collapsed sewer lines, traditional trenching is often the most reliable solution. This involves excavating a trench along the sewer line's path to allow for direct access and replacement of the entire pipe. While more invasive, it provides a complete overhaul and is often necessary for older homes with clay or cast iron pipes that have reached the end of their lifespan. We ensure careful planning and execution to minimize disruption to your yard.
