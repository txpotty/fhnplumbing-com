    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Lacey, WA

    Pipe bursting needs two pits. We dig a launch pit near the house and a receiving pit near the main. A cone-shaped head is pulled through the old line, breaking it outward, while fused HDPE pipe follows right behind it, so the new line takes the same path without a trench. Driveways stay put.

    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Lacey, WA

    Original sewer pipe in older homes is usually clay, cast iron or Orangeburg. Clay joints open up and let roots in, cast iron scales and cracks from the inside, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid 1900s, squashes into an oval over time. Replacement means PVC or HDPE.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Lacey, WA

    Trenching lets us fix grade. That is its big advantage. When an old line has a sag that holds water and solids, bursting would just follow the same sag, while a trench lets us lay new pipe at the right pitch. Bellies disappear.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Lacey

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. A small excavator does most of it, with hand digging near other lines. The old pipe comes out, the trench bottom gets a bed of gravel or sand, and new 4-inch PVC goes in at a steady quarter inch of fall per foot, checked with a level before backfill. Then the camera.

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Lacey

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. Inspectors check all three in an open trench. We set fall at a quarter inch per foot unless the run requires otherwise, place cleanouts at the house and at major direction changes, and use pipe and fittings listed for underground drainage. Paperwork stays on file.

    Commercial Sewer Line Replacement in Lacey

    Commercial laterals are often 6-inch pipe and carry grease or heavier flows. There is often a grease trap or interceptor to work around. We schedule the work around your hours, set up temporary bypass pumping when the building needs to stay open, and coordinate with the city on any work at the tap. Weekend starts are common.

    Residential Sewer Replacement in Lacey, WA

    Most house laterals run 30 to 100 feet. Some are two feet down, some are eight. We measure both before quoting, check whether the run passes under a driveway, porch or tree, and plan the method around what sits on top of the pipe, so the estimate you sign is the price you pay.

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Lacey, WA

    Clay pipe came in short sections with a joint every two or three feet. That is a lot of joints for roots to find. Orangeburg is worse, since the tar-paper wall softens and deforms under the weight of the soil, and both are good candidates for bursting because they break apart easily. Fast pulls.
