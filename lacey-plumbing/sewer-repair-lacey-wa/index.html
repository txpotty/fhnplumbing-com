    Sewer Repair Methods for Lacey, WA Homes

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Lacey, WA

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. Not every line qualifies, and we will say so.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Lacey

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. Dirt falls in through the crack. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then run water through it and check it on camera before backfill. Figure on a single day for a typical spot repair.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Lacey, WA

    A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Sewer gas follows soon after. Bird nests, leaves and ice can block a roof vent, and older galvanized or cast iron vent stacks crack inside the walls where nobody sees them, so we check the vent any time the drains act up but the main line looks clean. Fixes range from a quick clearing to a new section of stack.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Lacey

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Small defects turn into big backups. We repair larger diameter lines, add two-way cleanouts so future maintenance is easy, and give managers recorded footage for owners, insurers and their own files. Night and weekend work is available.

    Sewer Repair for Commercial Properties in Lacey, WA

    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. A closed kitchen loses revenue by the hour. For restaurants, strip centers and apartment buildings in Lacey we schedule repairs after hours where we can, set up temporary bypass pumping when the line has to stay in service, and coordinate with property managers on tenant notices. We repair a lot of grease-clogged outlet lines.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Lacey, WA

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, depending on how many joints are affected and how far apart they sit. One or two joints is a small job. Ten or twelve usually justifies lining the run.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Lacey, WA

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Lining costs more per foot. It avoids excavation, though, and on long runs under driveways or patios the savings on concrete and landscaping often outweigh the higher price of the liner itself. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. You get each option in writing with a number attached.

    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Lacey, WA
