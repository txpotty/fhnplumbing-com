    Drain Vent Repair in Lacey, WA

    Gurgling and slow draining often mean the vent is blocked. Air cannot get in behind the water. Without air, draining water pulls the trap seal along with it and lets sewer gas into the room, which is why we inspect the roof opening and the vent piping inside the walls. Many vent fixes take under an hour.

    Tub and Shower Drain Repair in Lacey, WA

    Hair, soap scum and minerals narrow bath drains over time. Gaskets fail too. We replace worn pop-up assemblies, broken tub shoes and leaking shower drain bodies, often working from inside the tub so the ceiling underneath stays intact. When access is needed, we keep the opening small.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Lacey, WA

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. They hide well.
