    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Lacey

    A standard single tank softener replacement usually costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can add to the cost.

    Most softener replacements take two to four hours. New loops or drain lines can take a bit longer.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is generally not required for a direct like-for-like water softener replacement if no significant changes to the existing plumbing are made. However, if new water lines or drain lines are being installed or relocated, a permit may be required. We will advise you on any permitting requirements.

    Most softeners last 10 to 15 years. Resin and valves wear faster with very hard or chlorinated water.

    Most standard residential water softener replacements can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. This includes disconnecting the old unit, installing the new one, making plumbing connections and testing the system. More complex installations might take a bit longer.

    Many systems include 5 to 10 years on the valve and longer coverage on tanks. We also provide a labor warranty.
