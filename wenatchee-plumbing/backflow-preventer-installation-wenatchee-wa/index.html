    Above-ground assemblies bake in summer sun and can freeze on a cold night.     Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Wenatchee

    A double check valve assembly, or DCVA, uses two spring-loaded checks in series with four test cocks. It is the usual pick for low-hazard lines such as many fire sprinklers, and because it does not discharge water, it can go in a vault where local rules allow it, sized so it does not rob your system of pressure in Wenatchee. Test cocks stay reachable.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Wenatchee

    Testable assemblies need certification at install and usually once a year after that. We file the passing report.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Wenatchee, WA

    High-hazard connections in Wenatchee, WA call for a reduced pressure zone assembly. That means chemical feed systems and medical equipment, where a backflow event could put something toxic in the supply, so the RPZ adds a relief valve between two checks that dumps water if either check leaks. It has to drain somewhere.

    Backflow Preventer Installation Cost Comparison in Wenatchee, WA

    A residential PVB is the least expensive device we install. The first test is included.
