    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Wenatchee

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. For slab houses, new PEX-A runs through the attic and drops down interior walls, so none of it sits under the floor. PEX handles a freeze better than copper. Expansion fittings keep the bore full size, which helps pressure at the last fixture on the line.

    Why Polybutylene Fails in Wenatchee

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. Hot water speeds it up. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    Repairs on polybutylene are temporary by nature, because the pipe next to the repair is the same age. We still make them. We cut out the failed piece and splice in PEX with listed fittings, stopping the leak and giving you time to plan the full job.
