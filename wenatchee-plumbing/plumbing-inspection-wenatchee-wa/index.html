    Plumbing Inspection Work in Wenatchee, WA

    Residential Plumbing Inspections in Wenatchee

    A full home inspection covers the system from the water meter to the sewer connection. We check every visible pipe for corrosion and leaks, run each fixture for flow and drainage and test the water heater, the main shutoff and the angle stops. Pressure gets measured too. We note anything above 80 psi.

    Water Heater Inspections in Wenatchee

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. We read the serial number for age, look for rust at the fittings and the base, check the venting and test the T and P relief valve. We check tankless units for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Wenatchee, WA

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. We look at backflow assemblies, grease interceptors, commercial water heaters, flush valves and the building sewer. Inspectors like documentation. The written report helps facility managers plan and budget repairs before a health inspector or the water provider writes them up.

    Commercial Property Plumbing Checks in Wenatchee

    Businesses in Wenatchee use our inspections to plan maintenance instead of reacting to breakdowns. Backflow devices, heaters, flush valves, floor drains, trap primers and the sewer all get checked. Downtime costs money. A written report with ranked priorities lets you schedule the repairs after hours or on a weekend instead of closing during business hours.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Wenatchee

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. Backdrafting heaters can push combustion gases indoors, which is why we test the vent and report problems before we leave.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Wenatchee, WA

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging. We push the camera in from a cleanout, watch for cracks, roots, offsets and low spots and record the whole run to the street or septic tank. We mark problem spots from above. Glacial till and dense hardpan are common in western Washington, and they drain poorly enough to keep water against foundations and in crawlspaces.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Wenatchee, WA

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one. Some need replacing soon. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.
