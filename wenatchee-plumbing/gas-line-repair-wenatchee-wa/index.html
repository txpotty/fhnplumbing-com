    What Our Gas Line Plumbers Fix in Wenatchee, WA

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Wenatchee, WA

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. Nails, rodents and lightning can all damage CSST, and older yellow-jacket tubing must be bonded to the home's grounding system to lower arc risk. It takes about an hour. Damaged runs get spliced with the manufacturer's fittings, and we confirm the bond on the way out. Heavy, prolonged rain from atmospheric river storms is a major factor in western Washington, raising groundwater and filling crawlspaces and basements.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Wenatchee, WA

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. After the coating breaks down, corrosion starts, and constant moisture from irrigation or poor drainage makes it worse. We call 811 before digging. The new line is polyethylene with tracer wire, or coated steel where it has to be exposed, and we backfill and clean up the yard when the test passes.

    Gas Shut Off by the Utility in Wenatchee

    When your utility shuts off gas for a leak, you may be without hot water, heat or cooking until a licensed plumber fixes it. We respond quickly, locate the leak, make the repair and run the pressure test the utility needs to see before it will restore service. Most are one-visit repairs. We do not leave until the gauge holds steady and the system is ready for the utility.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Wenatchee

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. With every appliance off, a moving test dial on the meter tells us gas is escaping somewhere, and then we isolate branches to narrow it down. A combustible gas detector narrows it further. Soap solution at the suspect fitting confirms it with bubbles, and then we repair the joint, replace the section or swap the connector before testing again.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Wenatchee, WA

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Wenatchee

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Steep slopes and wet soils can move over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Wenatchee, WA

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. A pool heater can pull 250,000 BTU or more, which can rob gas from the furnace and water heater if the line was sized for a grill. Sizing is the first thing we check. New and repaired lines get tested, buried to the required depth and laid with tracer wire.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Wenatchee

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. We add up the BTU load of every appliance on the system, measure the longest run and pick a pipe size that keeps pressure up at the farthest appliance when everything runs together. Then we route it, tie it in and test it.
