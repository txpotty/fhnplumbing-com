    Full Home PEX Repiping in Wenatchee

    If your home has old, corroded or leaking pipes, a full home PEX repipe can drastically improve your plumbing system. We remove outdated galvanized, copper or polybutylene pipes and replace them with durable PEX, upgrading your entire water delivery system. This prevents future leaks, improves water pressure and eliminates the rust-colored water often seen with older piping in Wenatchee.

    PEX Main Water Line Installation in Wenatchee

    The main water line brings water into your home, and a failing one can cause major issues. We provide professional PEX main water line installation, replacing old, damaged lines with durable PEX. This upgrade improves water flow, reduces the risk of future leaks and offers superior resistance to ground shifting and corrosion. Our team handles proper trenching and the connection to your municipal supply.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Wenatchee, WA

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, similar to a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to balance cost and performance.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Wenatchee

    PEX is also a premier choice for hydronic heating systems, including radiant floor heating. Its ability to withstand high temperatures and resist corrosion makes it perfect for circulating hot water. If you are considering radiant heat for a specific area of your home or business in Wenatchee, PEX is the pipe to use.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Wenatchee

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer dependable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to confirm a proper seal.
