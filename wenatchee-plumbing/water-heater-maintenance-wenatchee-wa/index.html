    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Wenatchee

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Wenatchee, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's significantly depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing costly tank leaks.

    Maintenance Cost Versus Replacement in Wenatchee, WA

    A water heater tune-up costs a fraction of a new unit, which can run well over a thousand dollars installed. Regular flushing and anode replacement can help a tank reach the high end of its expected lifespan. Tankless units in particular can last many years with proper descaling. If your heater is near the end of its life or leaking from the tank, we will tell you plainly. We help you weigh repair against replacement with real numbers.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Wenatchee

    For electric water heaters, our maintenance service focuses on checking heating elements, thermostats, and electrical connections.     Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Wenatchee

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for any leaks.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Wenatchee, WA

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building unsafe pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge where installed.
