    Tankless Descaling and Service Work in Wenatchee, WA

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Wenatchee

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is your heat exchanger. Calcium and magnesium drop out on the hottest tube walls first and narrow the passages. A 45 to 60 minute circulation with descaling solution dissolves it.

    What Tankless Maintenance Saves in Wenatchee

    Scale on an exchanger acts like insulation. A heavily scaled unit burns more gas per shower than a clean one, and that adds up each month. You get the maintenance price beside the likely repair cost, and the decision is yours.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Wenatchee

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. Solution goes from a bucket into the cold port, out the hot port and back to the bucket. After the cycle we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in Wenatchee

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. They see heavy use, and scale builds faster than at home. We descale each heater in the bank, check the cascade controller and confirm the load is shared evenly across every unit. Quarterly service is common.

    Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in Wenatchee, WA

    Built-in recirculation pumps and buffer tanks get hot water to the tap faster, but they also push more water through the exchanger every day, so those units scale sooner. The recirc loop gets flushed with the exchanger. Then we check whether the system uses a crossover valve or a dedicated return line and set the schedule to match.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Wenatchee

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. The secondary exchanger produces acidic water. We clear the condensate trap and check the neutralizer media. A-series models with built-in recirculation also get a pump and buffer tank check.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Wenatchee

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. On units with an air intake filter, dust and lint load it up and the burner runs rich or throws a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Wenatchee

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. Gas connections get a leak test, and we work the pressure relief valve. The air filter comes out and the igniter gets a look. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.
