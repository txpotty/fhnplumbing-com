    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Wenatchee

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. Never mix the two.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin Installation in Wenatchee, WA

    The basin holds everything, and a 30-gallon size is common for a single basement bathroom. A larger basin means longer, less frequent cycles that spare the motor and switch, so we go bigger when the pit takes a laundry or several fixtures. Every lid gets sealed.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Wenatchee, WA

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin costs the least, often done in a few hours. A full new system with basin, floor excavation, roof vent, electrical and alarm costs more, but it is usually the only way to add a below-sewer bath besides a macerator. We price both options.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Wenatchee

    Macerating systems like Saniflo let you add a toilet and sink without breaking the concrete floor. The unit sits behind the toilet, grinds the waste and pumps it through a small discharge line, often three-quarter inch or one inch, up to the nearest drain, which makes it a good fit for a small basement half bath. Venting still applies.

    Emergency Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Wenatchee

    An ejector failure often starts with the high water alarm, followed by slow drains and a smell near the pit. Stop all use of the basement fixtures and call, because once a plumber is on site most emergency replacements take just a few hours. We carry common pumps.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Wenatchee

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. A grinder makes sense when the sewer is distant, uphill or pressurized, such as a pressure sewer where each home pumps into a shared main. Grinders run pricier. A standard ejector often covers a basement bath.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Wenatchee

    Liberty ejector pumps show up on a lot of our installs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models. Liberty sells packaged systems with basin, lid, pump and check valve already matched, which means less field fitting and a listed assembly for inspection. A full-port valve gets added.

    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Wenatchee, WA

    Businesses in Wenatchee, WA with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house. Commercial setups often run duplex pumps with an alternating panel, so one pump can fail and the other keeps the restrooms open. Downtime costs money. Each pump can carry the load alone.
