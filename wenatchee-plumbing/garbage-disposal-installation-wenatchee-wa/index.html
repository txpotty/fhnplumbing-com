    Standard Disposal Installation in Wenatchee

    Most households in Wenatchee do well with a 1/3 to 1/2 horsepower continuous-feed unit.     Disposal Upgrades in Wenatchee, WA

    Stepping up from an old 1/3 HP unit to a quieter model is a common request. Old drain arms often need replacing. Rust works from inside galvanized pipe.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Wenatchee, WA

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. Heavy-wall fittings last longer.
