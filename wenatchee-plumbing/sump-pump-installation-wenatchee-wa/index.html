    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Wenatchee, WA

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. Finished basements are the usual reason. They are worth it anywhere a few inches of water would mean new drywall.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Wenatchee

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. Install time is shorter too. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Wenatchee

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Rentals need these. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Wenatchee

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Lots that slope toward the house need this most. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Wenatchee

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Wenatchee, WA

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. Our trucks carry 1/3 and 1/2 HP submersibles, check valves and fittings, so in most homes we can pull the old pump and set a new one in one visit. Figure on two hours for a typical swap. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Wenatchee, WA

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Wenatchee

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. A flooded elevator pit shuts down a building. Work is scheduled around your operating hours.
