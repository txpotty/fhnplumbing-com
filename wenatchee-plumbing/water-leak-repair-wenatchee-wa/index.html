    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Wenatchee

    Slab leaks are common where soil shifts and puts stress on pipes embedded in concrete foundations. Steep slopes and wet soils can shift over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. We use non-invasive acoustic and thermal imaging equipment to pinpoint the exact location of the leak beneath your home's foundation in Wenatchee. Our repair options range from targeted slab penetration to re-routing the affected line, minimizing disruption and protecting your foundation from further damage.     The cost of water leak repair in Wenatchee, WA can vary significantly depending on the leak's location, severity, and the type of pipe involved.     Water pipes can leak due to several factors including age and corrosion, shifting soil that stresses pipes, high water pressure, and freezing temperatures that can cause pipes to burst. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and covering hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. Hard water can also contribute to scale buildup and gradual pipe degradation.

    Repeat pinholes usually come from water chemistry, high water pressure or thin-walled copper. We test your pressure and inspect the regulator, and we discuss repiping if leaks keep returning.

    Common signs of a hidden water leak include unexplained spikes in your water bill, persistent sounds of running water when no fixtures are in use, visible water stains on walls or ceilings, musty odors, and soggy spots in your yard. For slab leaks, you might notice warm spots on your floor or cracks in your foundation.

    The length of a water leak repair depends on its complexity. Simple fixture repairs might take an hour or two, while burst pipe repairs or PEX line fixes could take half a day. More involved repairs, like a slab leak, might require a day or two for detection and repair, particularly if access is difficult.
