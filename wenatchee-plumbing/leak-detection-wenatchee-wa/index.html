    Fixture and Appliance Leaks in Wenatchee, WA

    One running toilet can waste hundreds of gallons daily. Worn faucet cartridges, softener valves and water heater connectors add more.     Irrigation Leak Detection in Wenatchee, WA

    Sprinkler laterals crack and zone valves stick open, and either can lose thousands of gallons before the bill shows it. Check the yard first. Our tech tests the irrigation system separately from the house, runs each zone and traces buried lines, because many water providers say to rule out irrigation first when a summer bill jumps.

    Leak Testing for Multifamily and Offices in Wenatchee

    Multifamily buildings and office parks have long supply systems that let leaks hide for months. We isolate each unit or floor, test it and trace leaks with the same acoustic and thermal tools we use in single-family houses. Property managers get a report per unit. Early detection means fewer tenant complaints and fewer insurance claims building-wide.

    Slab Leak Detection in Wenatchee

    Concrete slab homes have supply lines buried in sand below the floor, and a pinhole there can run for months before anyone notices. A warm tile is the classic sign. We pressure test the hot and cold sides separately, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak through the concrete and confirm with a thermal camera when the leak is on the hot side.

    Leaks in Walls and Ceilings in Wenatchee, WA

    A ceiling stain right below a bathroom usually points to a shower valve, a drain joint or a toilet wax ring. Our job is figuring out which. Thermal cameras show the cool wet area, a moisture meter confirms it and a borescope through a small hole lets us see the actual pipe before any big opening is made.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Wenatchee, WA

    A cracked drain line leaks wastewater into the soil, which can show up as a sewer smell in the yard, a soggy patch or slow drains. Cameras find these. Our tech runs a camera from the cleanout to the city tap, recording cracks, offset joints and root entry in PVC, clay and cast iron, so you see the exact distance and depth of the break.

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Wenatchee, WA

    On a ground microphone, copper leaks are easy to hear. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. Both show up on our schedule every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX top the list of what we find.

    Preventive Leak Inspections in Wenatchee, WA

    Checking for leaks once a year catches the small stuff. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves. Older homes with galvanized pipe or original water heater connectors get the most out of this, since those parts tend to fail all at once. Short visit, big savings.
