    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Wenatchee

    Most standard gas or electric tank replacements cost $1,400 to $3,000 including the heater, labor and common code upgrades. Attic installs, larger tanks or relocations can raise the cost to $3,000 to $4,500.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line changes.

    Leaks at the base, rusty water, rumbling noises, inconsistent hot water and an age over 10 years are common signs. A leaking tank should be replaced right away.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater once a year is recommended in most homes. Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is generally soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from damaging heating elements.

    Most new water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components, depending on the model and brand. FHN Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, so your investment is protected.

    Upgrading provides more reliable hot water, improved energy efficiency and lower utility bills, better performance and a reduced risk of leaks or breakdowns. Newer models often have helpful features and longer warranties, improving comfort and safety in your home.
