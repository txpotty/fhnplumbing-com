    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Wenatchee

    Many water utilities install pressure regulators or backflow devices that create a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure spikes can strain your tank and fixtures. Code commonly requires a thermal expansion tank on these systems. We install a properly sized expansion tank and pre-charge it to match your water pressure. This simple add-on can help your new heater last longer.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Replacement in Wenatchee

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon gas water heater replacement usually runs $1,800 to $3,200 installed, including haul-away and basic code upgrades. Larger 75 gallon or power vent units usually cost $3,000 to $4,800. We give a firm price before we start.

    If your home has a pressure regulator or backflow device, heated water has nowhere to expand. An expansion tank absorbs that pressure and helps protect the heater and fixtures.

    Tank warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We add our own labor warranty on the installation.

    Atmospheric vent water heaters use natural convection to vent combustion gases up a chimney or flue. Power vent models use an electric fan to force gases out, allowing for more flexible venting options and often higher efficiency due to cleaner combustion.

    FHN Plumbing installs and services all major brands of gas water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A. O. Smith, State and more. We help you choose the best brand and model to suit your needs and budget.

    Replacing a gas water heater involves working with gas lines, water connections and venting, which can be dangerous if not done correctly. It is strongly recommended to hire a licensed professional like FHN Plumbing for safety and code compliance.

    Regular maintenance, such as annual flushing to remove sediment and checking the anode rod, can greatly extend your gas water heater's lifespan. Proper sizing and professional installation also play a big role.

    Most standard gas water heater replacements in Wenatchee can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. More complex installations, such as converting to a different type of unit or significant plumbing changes, may take longer.
