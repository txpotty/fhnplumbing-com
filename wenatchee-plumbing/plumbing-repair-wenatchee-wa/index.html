    Gas and Electric Water Heater Repair in Wenatchee

    Tank and tankless heaters fail in different ways. FHN Plumbing repairs both, including the major brands, gas and electric. On a tank we check the thermocouple, gas valve, elements and anode rod.     Well Pump Repair in Wenatchee, WA
