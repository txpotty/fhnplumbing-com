    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Wenatchee

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Wenatchee

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. A wall bracket lagged into blocking carries the weight, and if the wall has none, we open it and add a board. Drywall anchors do not cut it. A loose basin cracks the trap.

    Sink Replacement and Upgrades in Wenatchee

    Upgrading the sink is a cheap way to refresh a room. A same-size drop-in swap usually takes a morning, and a deeper bowl may only need a new trap. Countertop changes add time. We tell you which one you are looking at.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Wenatchee, WA

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. The supply stops and the trap sit right behind the column, so we center the drain stub, use chrome or polished brass trap parts, and set the stops tight to the wall where the pedestal can hide them. The wall needs solid blocking. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Utility Sink Installation in Wenatchee

    Laundry and garage tubs take dirty water and a lot of it. The tub goes on a solid stand or bracket with a 1-1/2 inch trap, plus a faucet tall enough to fill a bucket. Soft water can be slightly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. A cleanout plug goes on the trap.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Wenatchee, WA

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.
