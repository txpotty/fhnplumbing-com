    Pipe Bursting Services in Wenatchee, WA

    For severely damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, breaking apart the old pipe at the same time. This provides a completely new, durable pipe, often HDPE (high-density polyethylene), which resists corrosion and leaks. It's an effective long-term fix for extensive issues in Wenatchee, WA.

    Pipe Bursting in Wenatchee, WA

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting needs an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Wenatchee

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Wenatchee

    CIPP lining is a popular trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one.
