    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbers for Frozen Pipes in Wenatchee

    Thawing an accessible frozen pipe in Wenatchee usually costs $150 to $400, and burst pipe repair typically runs $300 to $1,000. Prevention costs less. Insulating a crawlspace, adding heat cable to the worst run, and swapping outdoor faucets for frost-free models usually costs a few hundred dollars, which is less than a single burst pipe repair.

    A short, exposed section usually thaws in 30 to 60 minutes. Pipes inside walls take more time. Lines in walls or plastic pipe can take a few hours with gentler heat.

    Pipe location matters more than brand. Metal pipe cracks more easily. Older galvanized steel and copper in uninsulated attics, crawlspaces and outside walls are the most likely to split, while PEX in the same spot may freeze but often survives, though its fittings can still fail.

    Thawing and small pipe repairs usually do not need a permit. Larger jobs can. Repiping, rerouting or replacing a backflow device may need one, which we handle.

    You can open the faucet and gently warm an exposed pipe with a hair dryer. Open flame is never safe here. Hidden pipes are better left to us, since a hidden split can flood the wall as it thaws.

    Open the faucet on that line and leave it open. It gives melting ice somewhere to go. Turn off the main water valve if you can reach it, warm the space with a space heater kept at a safe distance, and call us so we can thaw the line and check it under pressure before the water goes back on.

    Check which fixtures work. Ice is usually between a working fixture and a dead one. If only the kitchen sink is out and it sits on an outside wall, the ice is almost certainly in that wall or the crawlspace below it, and a thermal camera confirms the spot without cutting.

    A new freeze is not a warranty issue, but it is a warning sign. That pipe is still exposed. We will look at why it froze again and quote insulation, heat cable or a reroute into a heated space, because patching the same exposed run every winter costs more than fixing the cause once.
