    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. It still fails.     Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Frozen water cracks it. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many burst pipes are found hours after the cold snap. We repair the split and add insulation. We can add heat cable where needed.
