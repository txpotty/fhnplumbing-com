    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Wenatchee

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in older homes and rusts from the inside until flow drops and leaks start at threaded joints. Repairs are possible, but every disturbed fitting can start a new leak. We replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric unions to stop galvanic corrosion. For homes with widespread galvanized piping, we will quote a full repipe so you can weigh both options.

    Leaky Pipe Repair in Wenatchee, WA

    Even a small leak can lead to mold growth, wood rot and higher water bills over time. FHN Plumbing provides precise leaky pipe repair services for all types of plumbing systems in Wenatchee. We use modern leak detection equipment, including acoustic sensors and thermal imaging, to pinpoint the exact source of the leak without unnecessary demolition. Once located, we offer targeted repairs, from patching minor pinhole leaks to replacing corroded sections of pipe, for a watertight seal and long-term reliability.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair in Wenatchee, WA

    Slab leaks are especially challenging when the soil under a home shifts and stresses the pipes below it. Crawlspaces and basements are both common in Washington homes, and a damp crawlspace is often the first sign of a drainage or plumbing problem. Our plumbers are experts in diagnosing and repairing slab leaks beneath your home's concrete foundation. We use non-invasive methods like electronic leak detection to find the exact location, then offer solutions such as direct access repair, rerouting the line or trenchless techniques to fix the pipe with minimal impact on your home.

    PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Wenatchee

    PEX piping is a popular, flexible and freeze-resistant alternative to traditional copper or PVC, often used in newer construction and remodels in Wenatchee. Our team is skilled in PEX pipe repair, addressing crimp failures or accidental punctures. We also specialize in PEX pipe installation, a reliable, corrosion-resistant and cost-effective solution for re-piping projects that delivers a leak-free plumbing system for years to come.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Wenatchee

    Drain pipes made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet. We locate the leak, replace the damaged section and test the drain under full flow. We also check for proper slope and venting while the line is exposed.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Wenatchee

    Pinhole leaks form when copper pipe wall thins from the inside, often due to water chemistry, high velocity or poor grounding. They start as tiny drips that stain drywall or ceilings. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new pipe or a PEX transition. If we find several pinholes in the same area, we will talk with you about repiping that section.

    PEX and Copper Pipe Repair in Wenatchee, WA

    Whether your home has modern PEX plumbing or traditional copper pipes, FHN Plumbing has the expertise to perform effective repairs. PEX is flexible and resistant to freezing, while copper is durable and long-lasting. Our plumbers are skilled in repairing both materials, using specific techniques and fittings designed for each type. We address issues like crimp failures in PEX or pinhole leaks in copper, using materials suited to Washington climate.

    Pipe Repair vs Repipe Cost in Wenatchee, WA

    A single pipe repair is much less expensive than a whole-house repipe and makes sense when the rest of the system is in good shape. If you are seeing repeated leaks, low pressure or rusty water, the total cost of patching can quickly exceed repiping.     Frequently Asked Questions About Pipe Repair in Wenatchee

    The cost of pipe repair in Wenatchee, WA varies widely depending on the type of pipe, the location of the damage and the extent of the repair. Minor leak repairs can range from $150 to $500, while more complex issues like burst pipes or slab leaks might cost between $800 and $5,000 or more, especially if excavation is required. We provide a detailed estimate after a full assessment.

    Most pipe repairs take one to three hours. Repairs requiring wall or ceiling access may take longer, and drywall repair is typically a separate step after the plumbing is done.

    Preventive measures include insulating pipes in unheated areas, maintaining proper water pressure, addressing hard water issues that can lead to mineral buildup and regular plumbing inspections. For homes in Washington, ensuring proper drainage around foundations can also prevent soil shifts that damage underground pipes.

    Pinhole leaks usually result from water chemistry, high water velocity, excessive pressure or electrical grounding issues that corrode the pipe from the inside. Hard water and chlorine treatment can also contribute. Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines.

    Freezing temperatures are a leading cause of burst pipes, especially when uninsulated pipes are exposed to sustained cold. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and covering hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. Other causes include extreme water pressure, corrosion from aging pipes, shifting foundations and physical damage from construction or tree roots.

    For minor repairs like patching a small leak, a permit is usually not required. However, for significant pipe replacements, especially for main water lines, sewer lines or extensive repiping projects, a plumbing permit from the city of Wenatchee may be necessary to meet local building codes. FHN Plumbing will handle all necessary permits on your behalf.

    While temporary fixes like clamps or tape might stop a small drip, they are rarely long-term solutions and can fail, leading to bigger problems. Professional plumbers have the tools, expertise and materials to properly diagnose the cause of the leak and perform a durable, code-compliant repair. For your safety and to prevent further damage, it's always best to call a professional for pipe repair.

    The length of a pipe repair depends on the complexity of the issue. A simple visible leak might be fixed within an hour or two. More involved repairs like burst pipes behind walls or slab leaks can take several hours to a full day, sometimes requiring multiple visits if specialized parts are needed.
