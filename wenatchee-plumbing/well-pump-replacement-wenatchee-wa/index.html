    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Wenatchee

    The cost of well pump replacement in Wenatchee, WA can vary widely, usually ranging from $1,500 to $5,000 or more. This price depends on factors like the type of pump, submersible or jet, well depth, horsepower and any additional components needed like pressure tanks or control boxes. We provide a detailed, upfront estimate after diagnosing your situation.

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up from shallower wells, usually less than 25 feet. Submersible pumps are placed directly inside the well casing, submerged in water, and are used for deeper wells, pushing water up to the surface. Each has its own applications and benefits.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 1 to 5 years. We also provide a labor warranty.

    Permit requirements vary by county. We follow local requirements for well work.

    Most well pumps last 8 to 15 years. Sediment, hard water and frequent cycling can shorten lifespan.

    New well pumps usually come with a manufacturer's warranty, usually ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. FHN Plumbing also stands behind our installation work with our own labor warranty. We will discuss warranty details for your chosen pump during the quoting process.

    FHN Plumbing installs and services a wide range of trusted well pump brands known for their durability and performance. These commonly include Grundfos, Franklin Electric, Goulds Pumps and Sta-Rite. We can recommend the best brand and model to suit your well's needs.

    The lifespan of a well pump varies greatly depending on usage, water quality and pump type, but most last between 10 and 20 years. Regular maintenance and proper sizing can extend its life, but eventually wear and tear will call for replacement.
