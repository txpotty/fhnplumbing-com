    Softener Systems We Replace in Wenatchee, WA

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Wenatchee

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. It mounts on the main line after any filter and needs no electricity or drain. Magnets and clamp-on gadgets are a different story.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Wenatchee, WA

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Low-sodium diets are another reason.

    Residential Water Softener Replacement in Wenatchee

    Scale in the water heater is the expensive part of hard water. A tank water heater on hard water can lose a noticeable share of its efficiency as scale builds on the bottom, and tankless units scale up even faster. Soft water prevents it.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Wenatchee, WA

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Washington homes. An old Fleck with discontinued parts usually gets replaced, valve and tank together. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Wenatchee, WA

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. The valve's age decides it. New resin under a tired valve rarely lasts.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Wenatchee

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico runs them without electricity, using water flow to drive the valve. They cost more.

    Whole-Home Softener Placement in Wenatchee, WA

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. We typically leave the hose bibs on hard water. No sense spending salt on the garden.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Wenatchee, WA

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. We set it before we leave.
