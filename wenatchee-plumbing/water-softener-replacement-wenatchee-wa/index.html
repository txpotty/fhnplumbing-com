    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Wenatchee

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water all day and night. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but avoid hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Wenatchee, WA

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more cost effective than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes better sense.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Wenatchee, WA

    Fleck is a well-known brand for reliable water softener control valves, found in many residential and commercial systems across Washington. If your Fleck-controlled softener is beyond repair or simply outdated, FHN Plumbing can properly replace the entire unit. We know many Fleck models and can recommend an equivalent or upgraded system that fits cleanly with your existing plumbing in Wenatchee.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Alternatives in Wenatchee, WA

    Some homeowners prefer a salt-free scale reduction system that uses template-assisted crystallization media. These systems do not remove hardness minerals but change them so they are less likely to stick. They require no drain and no salt. We explain the differences plainly, since they work differently than true softeners. They can be a good fit when drain access is limited or sodium is a concern.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Wenatchee, WA

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and effective solution for hard water in Washington. When replacing these units, we carefully disconnect the old brine tank and resin tank, then install the new components.     Fleck Water Softener Replacement in Wenatchee, WA

    Fleck is another leading brand known for durable water softener components. When your Fleck system needs replacing in Wenatchee, WA, our plumbers are experienced with these units.     Metered Softener Replacement in Wenatchee

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first month.
