    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Wenatchee, WA

    Foundation Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Wenatchee, WA

    Direct repairs are often the lowest upfront cost but involve breaking concrete and may need flooring repairs.     Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Wenatchee

    Slab leaks are notorious for being difficult to detect, often showing quiet signs like warm spots on the floor or a sudden jump in your water bill. FHN Plumbing uses acoustic and infrared technology to accurately locate these hidden leaks under your concrete foundation. Once identified, we offer tailored repair solutions, whether it's a spot repair, re-routing, or pipe bursting, to restore your plumbing system in Wenatchee without extensive damage to your property.

    Water Line Reroute in Wenatchee

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This avoids breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for easy patching.

    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in Wenatchee, WA

    Crawl space foundations can suffer leaks from plumbing, groundwater, or HVAC condensation. These leaks can lead to wood rot, mold, and structural damage if not addressed quickly. Our team carefully inspects the crawl space and identifies the source of water intrusion. We then implement solutions such as pipe repair, sump pump installation, or vapor barrier replacement to dry out and protect your crawl space in Wenatchee, WA from future moisture issues.

    Foundation Leak Detection in Wenatchee

    Accurate detection is the most important step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to verify a leak exists. Then we use acoustic listening equipment and electronic line tracing to find the exact spot beneath the foundation. Thermal imaging helps verify hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Exterior Foundation Leak Solutions in Wenatchee

    Many foundation leaks originate from exterior factors like poor drainage, overflowing gutters, or saturated soil. We assess your home's exterior to identify these culprits. Our solutions include grading adjustments, French drain installation, and downspout extensions to move water away from your foundation. Proper exterior water management is key for preventing hydrostatic pressure buildup against your foundation walls in Wenatchee and keeping water out.
