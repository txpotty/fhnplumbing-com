    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Wenatchee, WA

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can work loose those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is responsible for the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve in the same visit.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Wenatchee, WA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option more and more used in homes across Wenatchee, WA. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact. Our team is fully experienced in PEX repair, using specialized crimping tools and fittings to make strong, leak-proof connections. We make sure your PEX system continues to perform well.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Wenatchee

    Low water pressure can be a frustrating symptom of an underlying water line problem, such as blockages, leaks, or a failing pressure regulator. In Wenatchee, if your water pressure has dropped suddenly, it's crucial to have your main water line inspected. We diagnose the cause of low pressure, whether it's mineral buildup restricting flow or a hidden leak, and perform the needed repairs or replacements to restore strong, consistent water pressure throughout your home.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Wenatchee

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Wenatchee

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a particularly challenging issue. Newer homes in Wenatchee usually use PEX supply lines, and many older houses have been partially repiped over the years. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to major structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to pinpoint slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak promptly in Wenatchee is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Wenatchee

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We confirm the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to find the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before we backfill.
