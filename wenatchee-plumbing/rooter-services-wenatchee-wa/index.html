    Sewer Root Removal in Wenatchee

    Roots cause more main sewer clogs than anything else on older streets. They work into small gaps at clay and cast iron joints and thicken into mats that snag paper and grease. We cut them back with a root saw on a heavy cable. Most of these lines need cutting every year or two.

    Kitchen Line Rooter Service in Wenatchee, WA

    Kitchen lines clog slowly, over months, with grease, soap and food scraps that cool and harden on the pipe wall. Access is through the trap arm or a cleanout, using a medium cable and a spiral cutter. Heavy grease gets a jetting pass. The jet washes the walls clean, which keeps the line open much longer than the cable alone.

    Residential Drain Cleaning in Wenatchee

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Wenatchee, WA

    Jetting puts water through a nozzle at 3,000 to 4,000 psi and scrubs the pipe wall clean. Grease, scale and fine roots that a cable rides over come off with the water. Many rural Washington homes rely on private wells, and some carry iron, manganese or other minerals that call for testing and treatment. We camera the line first. Pressure that high can damage cracked clay or thin cast iron, so we jet only after we have looked at the pipe.
