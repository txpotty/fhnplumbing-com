    Hydro-Jetting Services in Wenatchee, WA

    For the most stubborn clogs and extensive pipe buildup, FHN Plumbing offers hydro-jetting services in Wenatchee, WA. Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. Hydro-jetting cleans pipes more thoroughly than traditional snaking, restoring them to near-original condition and reducing future clogs.
