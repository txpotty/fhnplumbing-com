    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Wenatchee

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. We pump descaler through the service valves. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Scheduled Service for Businesses in Wenatchee

    Commercial plumbing takes a beating. We service large water heaters, restroom fixtures, backflow preventers and kitchen drain lines on a set schedule, so failures are caught on a quiet Tuesday morning instead of a busy Friday night. Inspections go smoother. You get records for every visit.

    Water Softener Service in Wenatchee

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. Salt level, brine tank, injector and a test regeneration all get checked. We test the water afterward. A failed softener sends hard water through the house without warning.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Wenatchee, WA

    Heavy, prolonged rain from atmospheric river storms is a major factor in western Washington, raising groundwater and filling crawlspaces and basements. A sump pump sits idle for months and then has to work hard all at once, so we test the float switch, clean debris out of the pit and confirm the discharge line runs clear to daylight. The check valve gets a look. Battery backups get tested too.

    Water Heater Flush and Inspection in Wenatchee

    Popping or rumbling means sediment. Hard water drops minerals that settle on the bottom of the tank, where they trap heat, waste fuel and make that crackling sound every time the burner or elements fire. Flushing clears it. Tankless units get a descaling flush through the service valves with a pump and descaler. Records support warranty claims.

    Commercial and Priority Maintenance in Wenatchee, WA

    Restaurants, offices and apartments need plumbing that works every day. Scheduled service covers drains, heaters, backflow and restrooms, with priority emergency response for clients. Reports come every visit. Use them for planning.

    Gas Line and Pipe Material Checks in Wenatchee, WA

    Gas leaks are the one item we never skip. Visible gas piping, connectors and shutoffs get checked, and old flex connectors get flagged. We note pipe types. Older pipe types get flagged with repipe choices.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Wenatchee

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Many rural Washington homes rely on private wells, and some carry iron, manganese or other minerals that call for testing and treatment.
