    Galvanized Steel Pressure Tank Upgrades in Wenatchee

    Many older homes in Wenatchee still have traditional galvanized steel pressure tanks, which can corrode over time and lose their air charge. We offer professional upgrades from these older tanks to more modern and durable bladder-style tanks.     Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Wenatchee

    The tank tee is the manifold that connects the tank, switch, gauge, relief valve and drain. Galvanized tees corrode and choke flow, especially with sandy or mineral-heavy water. We replace them with brass or stainless steel tank tees that hold up much longer. A new liquid-filled gauge gives accurate readings, and a fresh relief valve protects against a stuck switch. These small parts make a big difference in reliability.

    Large Capacity Tanks for Ranch and Commercial Wells in Wenatchee

    Properties with livestock, irrigation or more than one home on a well need more storage than a single residential tank. We install multiple tanks manifolded together or large capacity models over 100 gallons. Proper sizing keeps big three-phase pumps from cycling too often. We also check the check valves and pressure ratings on the distribution lines. Work is scheduled to keep water downtime brief for animals and occupants.

    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Wenatchee

    Bladder tanks hold water inside a flexible butyl bag surrounded by pressurized air. When the bag ruptures, water fills the whole tank, there is no air cushion and the pump short cycles. A quick test is pressing the air valve on top, since water spitting out means the bladder has failed. We replace the tank, set the new pre-charge and confirm the pump runs a full cycle. Most bladder tank swaps take about two to three hours.

    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Wenatchee

    When your well pressure tank shows signs of trouble, it is important to determine if a repair is practical or if full replacement is necessary. Small air leaks or issues with the air valve might be repairable. However, if the tank itself is corroded, has a ruptured diaphragm, or is waterlogged, replacement is almost always the more economical and reliable option. Our technicians in Wenatchee provide an straightforward assessment, explaining the pros and cons of repair versus a complete well pressure tank replacement, so you can make an informed decision.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Wenatchee, WA

    Diaphragm tanks like the Amtrol Well-X-Trol use a fixed membrane that separates air above from water below. They are durable, but the steel shell can rust through at the base after years of condensation in a hot, damp equipment space. We replace corroded diaphragm tanks and check the stand and fittings under them. The new tank is leveled and supported so it does not stress the piping. This restores steady pressure to every faucet.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Wenatchee

    Most well pressure tank replacements cost $900 to $2,200 installed, depending on tank size and whether the switch and tank tee are replaced. Large capacity or multi-tank setups can run $2,500 or more. We give a firm price before starting.

    Both bladder and diaphragm tanks use an air cushion to pressurize water, but they differ in how the water and air are separated. Bladder tanks have a replaceable rubber bladder that holds the water, completely isolating it from the air. Diaphragm tanks use a fixed rubber diaphragm. Bladder tanks are generally preferred for their longevity and ease of maintenance.

    If your switch is more than a few years old or the contacts are pitted, replacing it with the tank is low-cost insurance. It also lets us match the switch settings to the new tank.

    Common signs include your well pump cycling on and off very often (short-cycling), fluctuating water pressure at your faucets, no water coming from the tap, or a waterlogged tank when you tap on it. Visible rust or leaks around the tank are also clear indicators.

    Most pressure tank replacements take two to four hours. Adding a constant pressure system or multiple tanks can take the better part of a day.

    FHN Plumbing offers a solid warranty on both the well pressure tank itself (as per manufacturer's warranty) and our installation labor. We stand behind our work to ensure your complete satisfaction and peace of mind.

    A like-for-like tank replacement often does not require a permit in most counties. Electrical changes or new piping may, and we check local rules for you.

    We install Amtrol Well-X-Trol, Flexcon and Water Worker tanks. All three are easy to source and backed by solid manufacturer warranties.
