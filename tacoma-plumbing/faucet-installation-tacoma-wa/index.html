    How We Install Faucets in Tacoma, WA

    Commercial-Style Kitchen Faucets in Tacoma

    Commercial-style spring spout faucets are tall. Check the clearance to any shelf or window sill above the sink, since many stand 20 inches or more before you order. Any extra sink hole can hold a soap pump or side sprayer. The hose weight goes where the spout docks cleanly.

    Kitchen Faucet Installation in Tacoma

    Pull-down sprayers get yanked a thousand times a year. So we set the hose weight where the spout retracts cleanly and check that the hose does not snag on the shutoffs or the disposal when it pulls back, which is where most early kitchen leaks start. We connect new braided lines to both stops. Before we pack up, we run both sides for several minutes and check the cabinet floor with a dry paper towel.

    Vessel Sink Faucets in Tacoma, WA

    Vessel sinks sit high, so the faucet has to sit higher. We use either a tall deck faucet or a wall spout, sized so water hits near the drain and not the rim where it splashes onto the counter. Reach matters as much as height. Too short and you bang your knuckles on the bowl.

    Touchless Faucet Installation in Tacoma

    Touchless faucets run on a solenoid valve. The control box mounts under the sink, up off the cabinet floor where a small leak will not soak it, and it runs on batteries or a plug-in transformer. Mineral scale can stick a solenoid open, so we add the inline strainer the maker supplies. Sensor range gets tested last.

    Utility Sink Faucet Installation in Tacoma, WA

    Utility tubs see hot water and harsh cleaners. We install heavy-duty faucets with brass bodies and a hose-thread spout, mounted tight to the tub or the wall so they do not wobble when a hose is pulled. The spout gets a vacuum breaker. We check these joints twice, since a garage tub can drip for months before anyone notices.

    Brand-Specific Faucet Installation in Tacoma

    Every brand mounts a little differently. Moen and Delta each use their own quick-connect hose fitting, and Kohler often ships a different mounting bracket. Following the maker's sheet step by step keeps the warranty good. Grohe and Brizo come across our trucks most weeks too.

    Commercial Faucet Installation in Tacoma

    Commercial restrooms need faucets that hold up to hundreds of uses a day. We install sensor faucets and wrist-blade handles for hand sinks, plus pre-rinse units in commercial kitchens, all to current accessibility rules. Work can happen after closing. We send the fixture specs to your property manager for the inspector.

    Outdoor Faucet and Hose Bib Installation in Tacoma

    Hose bibs get more abuse than any indoor faucet. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and protecting hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. We match the hose bib to that, using a frost-free sillcock with its seat set back inside the heated wall wherever freezing is a real risk. Every hose bib needs a vacuum breaker under code. Most frost-free models include one, so the backflow protection is built right into the body.
