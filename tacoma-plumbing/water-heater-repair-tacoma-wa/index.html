    Gas Control and Thermocouple Repair in Tacoma

    Thermocouples, flame sensors, igniters and gas control valves cause most gas heater failures. We test each one, clean the burner and pilot assembly and check draft at the vent. A pilot that lights and then goes out a few seconds later usually points to a weak thermocouple or a clogged flame arrestor screen. Most are done in under two hours.

    Pilot Light Repair in Tacoma

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Less often it is a dirty flame arrestor screen choking off combustion air. We test the thermocouple output in millivolts. If it reads fine, the gas control valve is next.

    Electric Water Heater Repair in Tacoma

    Two elements and two thermostats run a standard electric tank, and any one of the four can quit without warning. Lukewarm water that runs out fast usually means the lower element. We test resistance with a meter. The replacement parts ride on the truck, which keeps it to one visit.
