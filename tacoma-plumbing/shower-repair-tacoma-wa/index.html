    Leaking Shower Valve Repair in Tacoma

    A shower that drips after it is turned off typically has a worn cartridge or seals. Hard water scale scratches cartridge surfaces and breaks down O-rings faster than soft water does. We replace the cartridge with a genuine part and lubricate the valve with silicone grease. This stops the drip and makes the handle turn smoothly again.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Tacoma, WA

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This restores full flow to the showerhead.

    Clogged Shower Drain Solutions in Tacoma, WA

    Slow or standing water in your shower is a clear sign of a clogged drain. These clogs are typically caused by a buildup of hair, soap scum, and sometimes mineral deposits. Our team uses professional drain snakes and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the most stubborn blockages without damaging your pipes. We also offer advice on preventive measures to keep your shower drains flowing freely.

    Tub and Shower Combination Repair in Tacoma, WA

    Many homes in Tacoma, WA feature tub and shower combination units, which means their plumbing is connected. Issues like faulty diverter valves, shared drain clogs, or a leaky tub spout can affect the entire unit. Our plumbers are adept at diagnosing and repairing problems specific to these combination fixtures, ensuring both your tub and shower function perfectly. We handle all components, from the faucet to the overflow drain.

    Leaky Shower Head Repair in Tacoma

    A dripping shower head wastes water and can be very annoying. Our plumbers can quickly identify the cause, whether it is a worn-out washer, O-ring, or a faulty cartridge. We carry common replacement parts on our trucks to ensure a fast fix. We will stop the leak, saving you money on your water bill and preventing potential water damage in your bathroom.

    Faulty Shower Valve Repair in Tacoma

    Problems with your shower's water temperature or pressure often point to a malfunctioning mixing valve or cartridge. These components control the hot and cold water flow. Our technicians are skilled in diagnosing valve issues and can repair or replace them, ensuring you get consistent water temperature and pressure every time you shower. We work with all major valve brands.

    Showerhead and Arm Repair in Tacoma

    Dripping showerheads, leaking arm connections and broken handheld hoses are frequent problems. We clean or replace showerheads, reseal shower arms and install new handheld hoses and holders. We can also add a slide bar for a handheld shower. Hard water clogs nozzles, so we recommend heads with flexible rubber nozzles that are easy to clean.

    Low Water Pressure in Shower Repair in Tacoma, WA

    Experiencing weak water flow in your shower can be frustrating. Low water pressure can be caused by various issues, including mineral buildup in the shower head, a faulty pressure balancing valve, or even problems with your home's main water line. We diagnose the specific cause and implement targeted repairs to restore strong, consistent water pressure to your shower in Tacoma.

    Most common shower repairs, like fixing a leaky faucet or clearing a drain clog, can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex issues, such as replacing a shower valve that requires wall access, might take half a day or longer. We aim for prompt service to minimize disruption.

    A stuck or worn pressure-balance spool often causes temperature swings when other fixtures are used. Replacing the cartridge or valve usually solves it.
