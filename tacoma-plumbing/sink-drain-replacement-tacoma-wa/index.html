    Pop-Up Drain Replacement in Tacoma

    Lift-rod pop-ups are the most common bath drain. We pull the old body, clean the flange seat down to bare porcelain, reset the new flange in putty or silicone, and adjust the clevis so the lift rod opens and closes the stopper with one easy motion. We match the faucet finish. Overflow-style drains are standard.

    Hard-Water Drain Damage Repair in Tacoma, WA

    Many rural Washington homes rely on private wells, and some carry iron, manganese or other minerals that call for testing and treatment. Scale grows inside pop-up bodies and on stopper rods until the linkage seizes. Once it locks, someone forces the lift rod, the pivot snaps, and the stopper ends up stuck down for good, and by then the scale has also narrowed the tailpiece enough to slow the drain. We replace the whole scaled assembly. Smooth PVC sheds buildup better.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Tacoma

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. The putty under a 3-1/2 inch strainer dries out, the locknut rusts, and water seeps down the outside of the tailpiece where it looks like the trap is leaking even when it is not. Strainer and trap get swapped as a pair. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Utility Sink Drain Replacement in Tacoma

    Laundry tubs see lint and paint water. Over time that wears the thin plastic drain and trap on a utility sink until it cracks, and lint from the washer standpipe often builds up in the trap so badly that it never drains at full speed again. A 1-1/2 inch trap with a cleanout plug goes in. A lint trap on the washer hose helps.

    Trap Arm and Wall Drain Repair in Tacoma, WA

    Leaks right at the wall point to the trap arm or stub. A cracked trap arm or a stub rusted through behind the escutcheon will drip down the inside of the cabinet back panel, where you only notice it when the wood swells or the cabinet smells musty. We cut back to solid pipe. Then new PVC gets tied in.

    Drain Replacement vs Repair Costs in Tacoma, WA

    A single washer is cheaper today. On a drain more than fifteen years old, though, the next joint often fails within months, and paying for two or three service calls costs more than replacing the whole assembly in one visit. We show you both prices. Your call.

    Code Updates During Drain Replacement in Tacoma

    Old drains sometimes break current rules. S-traps and drum traps were common decades ago, and when we replace the drain we bring it up to a vented P-trap with the right trap arm length. An AAV may be allowed where a vent is missing. Local rules decide.
