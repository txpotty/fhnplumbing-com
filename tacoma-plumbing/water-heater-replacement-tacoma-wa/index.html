    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Tacoma

    The cost of water heater replacement in Tacoma, WA typically ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation. Tankless units can range from $3,000 to $5,000 or more, depending on the model and necessary plumbing modifications. Factors like unit type, capacity, brand and labor complexity influence the final price.

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith water heaters. Each offers reliable performance and a range of warranties.

    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take longer.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater once a year is recommended in most homes. Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from damaging heating elements.

    Most new water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components, depending on the model and brand. FHN Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, so your investment is protected.

    Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times.     Leaks at the base, rusty water, rumbling noises, inconsistent hot water and an age over 10 years are typical signs. A leaking tank should be replaced right away.
