    Residential Gas Leak Detection in Tacoma

    A gas leak in a house rarely sits where the smell is strongest, because gas drifts along joists and collects in closets and basements.     Underground Gas Line Repair in Tacoma

    Yard lines run from the meter to the house, and often on to a detached garage, a pool heater, a grill or an outdoor kitchen. After an 811 locate, we excavate, cut out the failed run and lay polyethylene with a tracer wire beside it. Sand goes around the new pipe before backfill, per Tacoma trench requirements.

    Commercial Gas Leak Services in Tacoma

    Restaurant kitchens and small shops run more appliances on bigger pipe, often 1-1/4 inch or larger, fed from a meter with its own regulator.     Outdoor Gas Line Repair in Tacoma

    Grills, fire pits, pool heaters and patio heaters often sit at the end of a long buried line that nobody has looked at since it went in. We pressure test the outdoor branch on its own, locate the leak and replace the run with polyethylene and a tracer wire. Above-ground stub-ups get a fresh riser and a shutoff valve.
