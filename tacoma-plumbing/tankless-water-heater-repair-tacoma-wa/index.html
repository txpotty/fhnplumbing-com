    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Tacoma, WA

    Rinnai units are common, and scale causes most of their service calls. The LC code is the unit's own warning that the exchanger needs a flush.     Commercial Tankless Repair in Tacoma, WA

    Hotels and gyms draw hot water from open to close, so their tankless racks scale up two or three times faster than a home unit.     Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Tacoma

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, and they hold temperature off thermistor readings. A dirty inlet screen or a failing sensor shows up as no hot water, temperature swings or a unit that drops out mid-shower. Our tech cleans the screen, checks sensor values in the service menu and tests thermistor resistance against the chart. Usually under an hour.

    Navien's E016 is a heat exchanger overheat, and it usually traces to scale or a backed-up condensate line.     Electric Tankless Element Repair in Tacoma, WA
