    Severe storms can knock out power right when your pump is needed most. Battery backup pumps take over automatically when the main pump fails or power goes out.     Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Tacoma

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit typically costs $600 to $1,500. Installing a new pit with slab cutting and excavation often runs $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups typically add $500 to $1,500.

    In many cities, a plumbing permit is typically required for new sump pump installations or major replacements. This ensures the work meets local building and plumbing codes. FHN Plumbing handles all necessary permitting in Tacoma, WA to make sure your installation is fully compliant.

    It's recommended to have your sump pump inspected and cleaned annually, especially before the rainy season. This maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning the pit and testing the discharge line to ensure it's free of obstructions. Regular checks prevent unexpected failures.

    A simple replacement usually does not need a permit. New pits, electrical work or discharge connections may require one, and we will let you know.

    Most standard sump pump installations can be completed within 3 to 6 hours. However, if a new sump pit needs to be excavated, or if complex plumbing and electrical work is required, the process might take a full day. We always aim for efficient service with little disruption to your Tacoma home.

    Strange noises, constant running, failure to turn on and visible rust are common signs. Testing your pump before storm season helps catch problems early.

    A submersible sump pump sits inside the sump pit, making it quieter and more powerful, ideal for heavy use. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to reach for maintenance and often more affordable, though it can be louder.
