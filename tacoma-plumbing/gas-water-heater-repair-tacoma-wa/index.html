    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Tacoma

    Odd noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can add to sediment accumulation.     Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Tacoma

We always provide a detailed, upfront estimate once we diagnose the problem.

    Replacing a part like a thermocouple or gas valve usually does not. Replacing the water heater or altering gas piping or venting often requires a permit, which we handle.

    Watch for a lack of hot water, inconsistent water temperature, a pilot light that won't stay lit, unusual noises like rumbling or popping, a rotten egg smell, which can point to a gas leak, or visible leaks around the tank. Any of these call for a professional inspection.

    If you smell gas, leave your home right away, do not turn any lights or appliances on or off, and call your gas company's emergency line and then FHN Plumbing from a safe location. This points to a potentially dangerous gas leak that requires immediate professional attention.

    Because of the dangers that come with natural gas and possible carbon monoxide poisoning, gas water heater repairs should always be handled by a licensed professional. DIY attempts can be dangerous and may void warranties or violate local Tacoma, WA plumbing codes.

    Many Honeywell and White-Rodgers valves use flash codes to report problems like a weak thermopile or a tripped sensor. We read the code and test the matching part.

    Regular maintenance, such as annual flushing of the tank to remove sediment, inspecting the anode rod and checking the pressure relief valve, can greatly extend the life of your gas water heater and prevent common issues. Ask about a maintenance plan with FHN Plumbing.

    While some minor issues might seem DIY friendly, working with gas appliances carries serious safety risks, including gas leaks and carbon monoxide poisoning.
