    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Bowl Replacement in Tacoma

    Toilet bowl replacement typically costs $250 to $550, including the matched bowl, new seals, hardware and labor. Specialty or discontinued colors can increase parts cost. We compare that against full replacement so you know the smartest option.

    We install and replace all major toilet bowl brands, including American Standard, Kohler, Toto, Delta, Gerber, and more. We can help you select a durable and efficient model that fits your needs.

    In many cases, if only the bowl is damaged, such as with a crack, we can replace just the toilet bowl itself, assuming a compatible tank is available and in good condition. However, if the tank is also damaged, or if you're looking for an upgrade in efficiency or style, a full toilet replacement might be a more cost-effective option.

    Water-efficient toilets, often labeled WaterSense, use 1.28 gallons per flush (GPF) or less, compared to older models that can use 3.5 GPF or more. They save a large amount of water and can lower your utility bills, which benefits any household.

    A wax ring is a crucial seal located at the base of your toilet bowl, creating a watertight barrier between the toilet and the drain pipe. It prevents water from leaking onto your floor every time you flush. When replacing a toilet bowl, installing a new wax ring is a must for a leak-free and odor-free installation.

    Replacement bowls carry the manufacturer warranty, and we guarantee our installation workmanship. If a leak traces back to our work, we fix it.

    Usually, replacing an existing toilet with a new one of similar type does not require a permit in Tacoma. However, if you are relocating the toilet or making significant changes to the plumbing system, a permit might be necessary. We can advise you on local code requirements.

    With the matching bowl on hand, the job typically takes 1 to 2 hours. If the bowl must be ordered, it may take a few days to arrive.
