    Descaling Tankless Units in Tacoma

    Error codes often mean scale. Tankless heaters are especially sensitive to hard water, and once scale coats the heat exchanger, flow drops, the unit overheats and the control board shuts it down to protect itself. A pump kit handles it. Most makers recommend descaling at least yearly with hard water.

    Tankless Descaling in Tacoma, WA

    Scale is the tankless unit's main enemy. Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is generally soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. We close the isolation valves, connect a small pump and circulate descaling solution through the heat exchanger for the time the manufacturer recommends, then flush it with clean water. The inlet screen gets cleaned too. We also check the burner and venting.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Tacoma, WA

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We lift the lever, confirm water discharges and the valve reseats without dripping, and check that the discharge pipe runs full size to an approved spot within a few inches of the floor or outside. Leaky valves get replaced. Expansion tanks get an air charge check.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Tacoma

    Anode Rod Checks in Tacoma

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. We pull it and look. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.
