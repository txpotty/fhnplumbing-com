    Water Softener Maintenance in Tacoma, WA, Part by Part

    Yearly Water Softener Tune-Ups in Tacoma

    A tune-up starts with two hardness readings and ends with a full manual regeneration that we watch from start to finish. Between those, the salt level, brine well and float all get a look. It takes about an hour on most homes.

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Tacoma

    Resin does the actual softening. Each bead trades sodium for calcium and magnesium until it runs out of capacity. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to see whether the bed still holds its rated capacity.

    Softener Brands We Work On in Tacoma

    Most softeners sold in the last 20 years run on a handful of valve platforms, mainly Fleck, Clack, Autotrol and a few proprietary heads. Once you know the valve, the name on the cabinet matters less. Kinetico and Culligan use their own designs, so we check part availability before quoting.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Tacoma

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle tend to mean the seal and spacer stack is worn out. On a Fleck 5600 the fix is a piston and seal kit swap, often done in under an hour without pulling the valve off the tank. It costs a fraction of a new control head.

    Hardness Testing at the Tap in Tacoma

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. We take the raw sample from an unsoftened hose bib or with the softener on bypass. The softened sample comes from a kitchen faucet.

    Resin Rebedding in Tacoma

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. A resin cleaner is the first thing we try. If the bed is spent, we drain the tank, pull the old resin and refill it with fresh high-capacity resin, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Tacoma, WA

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. It coats the beads orange and blocks the exchange sites. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we shorten the time between regenerations so the iron does not build back up.

    Service for Most Softener Brands in Tacoma, WA

    Culligan, Kinetico, EcoWater, Morton and the store brands all use different valves, and parts for some of them only come through the dealer. Seal kits for the common valves ride on the truck. For a proprietary unit, you hear up front whether a part needs to be ordered and how long it usually takes.
