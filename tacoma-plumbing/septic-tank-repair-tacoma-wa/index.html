    Frequently Asked Questions About Septic Tank Repair in Tacoma

    The cost of septic tank repair in Tacoma, WA can vary a lot, typically ranging from $300 for minor issues like a broken baffle to $5,000 or more for major problems such as drain field remediation or tank replacement. Factors include the nature of the problem, required excavation and parts needed. FHN Plumbing provides upfront, clear pricing after a thorough on-site diagnosis.

    Typical signs include sewage odors around the tank or drain field, slow drains or backed-up toilets, lush green patches of grass over the drain field, gurgling sounds from pipes and standing water in your yard. If you notice any of these, call FHN Plumbing promptly.

    If your septic tank is overflowing, immediately stop all water use in your home to prevent further spillage. Avoid contact with the wastewater and call FHN Plumbing at (425) 491-3131 for emergency service. We will dispatch a technician to assess the situation and begin repairs as quickly as possible.

    FHN Plumbing services and repairs all major septic tank brands and types, including concrete, fiberglass and plastic tanks and components from manufacturers like Infiltrator Systems, Presby Environmental and Advanced Drainage Systems. Our technicians are experienced with many septic system designs, from conventional gravity systems to aerobic treatment units.

    The duration of septic tank repair depends on the complexity of the issue. Our technicians will provide an estimated timeline during the diagnosis.

    We install effluent pumps from Zoeller, Liberty, Goulds and similar quality brands, along with matching float switches and alarm panels.

    Soil type has a major effect on septic system performance. Clay soils drain slowly, making drain fields more prone to failure, while very sandy or rocky ground can pass effluent too quickly. Our plumbers understand these local soil conditions.
