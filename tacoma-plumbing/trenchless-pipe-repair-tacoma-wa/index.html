    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Tacoma

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Tacoma yard.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Tacoma, WA

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. The old pipe gets split. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Tacoma, WA soil.

    Sectional Point Repair in Tacoma

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. That calls for a sectional liner. We position a short resin-soaked sleeve over the damaged area with a camera, inflate it and let it cure, so you pay for a few feet of liner instead of the whole run. It suits isolated damage in Tacoma laterals.
