    Hydro-Jetting Services in Tacoma, WA

    A jetter pushes water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and pulls itself down the pipe. The spray cleans the pipe all the way around. Cables punch a hole through a clog, but a jetter washes away the grease, scale and soft roots that cause the next clog to form. We use it on repeat offenders.

    Bathroom Drain Clog Clearing in Tacoma, WA

    Hair, soap scum and toothpaste bind together into a mat that sits on the pop-up stopper. Most sink clogs live there. We pull the stopper and clean it, then clear the trap and cable the drain arm if the flow has not come back. A drain screen helps a lot.

    Main Sewer Line Clog Clearing in Tacoma, WA

    Several drains backing up at once usually means the clog is in the main. It needs a bigger machine. We find the cleanout, relieve the pressure slowly and then run a large cable with a cutting head. We camera the line once it flows.

    Toilet Clog Repair in Tacoma

    A toilet clog usually sits in the trapway, the S-shaped channel inside the bowl. A plunger does not always reach. We use a closet auger with a rubber sleeve, so the cable clears the trapway and the bowl stays unmarked. Toys and wipes come out this way. If the auger finds nothing, the clog is past the toilet and we check the main.

    Kitchen Sink Clog Repair in Tacoma

    Kitchen sinks clog from grease that cools and hardens on the pipe wall. Then food scraps and soap pile on. We pull the trap, run a cable through the trap arm and down the branch line to the main stack, and check the disposal for a jam or worn impeller. A heavy grease line may need a quick jet afterward.

    Laundry and Utility Drain Clogs in Tacoma, WA

    A washing machine dumps up to 20 gallons in a couple of minutes. Fibers and lint go with the water. Over months that lint packs the standpipe and the branch line behind it, so the first sign is often water spilling out of the standpipe mid-cycle. A lint filter on the hose helps. Some laundry lines share the kitchen branch, so we check both.

    Emergency Clogged Drain Service in Tacoma

    Backups seem to happen right before guests arrive. We get it. Our emergency technicians come with full drain equipment, and when several fixtures are backing up at once we go straight to the main line because that is almost always where the clog is. Stop running water until we get there.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Clogs in Tacoma

    Cooking grease, coffee grounds and starchy food stick to the walls of a kitchen drain and harden. The pipe shrinks over time. We remove the trap, cable the line to the stack and jet it if the grease is thick. Scrape grease into the trash.
