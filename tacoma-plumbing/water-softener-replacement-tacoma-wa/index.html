    Brine Tank Replacement in Tacoma

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore steady brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the right salt type for your system.

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in Tacoma

    Businesses in Tacoma often rely on heavy-duty water softening systems to protect equipment and maintain product quality.     Metered Softener Replacement in Tacoma

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Tacoma

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and effective solution for hard water in Tacoma. These systems use ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium minerals, which are well known for causing scale buildup. We replace old, inefficient salt-based units with modern, high-efficiency models that use less salt and water. Our plumbers ensure proper brine tank installation and regeneration settings for strong performance.

    Emergency Water Softener Replacement in Tacoma

    While not always an emergency, a sudden water softener failure can cause immediate issues with hard water. If your system leaks significantly or stops working entirely, FHN Plumbing can provide prompt emergency water softener replacement in Tacoma. We move quickly on urgent situations to minimize disruption and protect your home from hard water damage.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Tacoma, WA

    For homeowners in Tacoma, WA looking for an alternative to traditional salt-based systems, salt-free water conditioners offer a different approach. These units, sometimes called descalers, condition water to prevent mineral scale without removing the minerals or adding salt. While they do not produce soft feeling water, they reliably protect pipes and appliances from scale. We can replace your existing salt-free system or help you switch from a salt-based unit.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Tacoma, WA

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more economical than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes more sense.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Tacoma

    The cost of water softener replacement in Tacoma, WA can vary widely, typically ranging from $1,500 to $4,000. This depends on the type and size of the new system, labor costs and any necessary plumbing changes. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after assessing your needs.

    Many systems include 5 to 10 years on the valve and longer coverage on tanks. We also provide a labor warranty.

    We test your hardness in grains per gallon and estimate daily water usage. That determines the resin capacity needed.

    While it might seem like a straightforward task, water softener replacement involves working with water lines, drain connections and sometimes electrical components. Improper installation can lead to leaks, inefficient operation or even water damage. Hiring a professional means the job is done correctly, safely and to code.

    Most softener replacements take two to four hours. New loops or drain lines can take longer.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had significant mineral buildup restricting flow. However, a water softener's main job is to remove hardness minerals, not to boost pressure. If low water pressure is your main concern, we can investigate other causes.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is usually not required for a direct like-for-like water softener replacement if no significant changes to the existing plumbing are made. However, if new water lines or drain lines are being installed or relocated, a permit may be required. We will advise you on any permitting requirements.

    With proper maintenance, most quality water softeners can last between 10 and 15 years, and sometimes even longer. Factors like water hardness, usage volume and the quality of the original installation can influence its lifespan. Regular servicing helps extend the life of your unit.
