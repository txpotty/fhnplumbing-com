    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Tacoma

    Grit gets in. The small screen on the cold inlet catches sand and loose scale after a water main repair. On units with an air intake filter, dust and lint load it up and the burner runs rich or throws a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Tacoma

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. Solution goes from a bucket into the cold port, out the hot port and back to the bucket. After the cycle we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Tacoma

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is the exchanger. Minerals settle on the hottest tube walls first, and the passages get narrower. We dissolve it with 45 to 60 minutes of pump-driven descaling solution.

    Service Valve Kit Installs in Tacoma, WA

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. We add a pair of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines. Most kits include a relief valve port as well. Future flushes then take minutes to set up.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Tacoma, WA

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. We check gas pressure with all units firing at once. Each heater gets its own flow and temperature reading. Then we compare them.

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Tacoma

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. The trap gets cleaned and the media checked. We replace spent media. An outdoor run of condensate line gets checked for support and protection.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Tacoma

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. The secondary exchanger produces acidic water. We clear the condensate trap and check the neutralizer media. A-series models with built-in recirculation also get a pump and buffer tank check.

    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in Tacoma

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. They see heavy use, and scale builds faster than at home. Each heater gets descaled, and we check that the controller spreads the load evenly. Quarterly service is common.
