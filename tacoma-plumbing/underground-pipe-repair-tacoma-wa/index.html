    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Tacoma

    A single repair on a buried water line typically costs $800 to $2,500 including locating and excavation. A new service runs higher. Main water service replacement typically runs $2,500 to $6,500, and sewer lateral repairs range from $1,500 to $5,000 depending on how deep the pipe sits. You get the price on paper.

    Water lines get PEX or Type K copper. Both are burial rated. Sewer lines get Schedule 40 PVC, with local code and the old pipe deciding the details. We match transitions carefully.

    Acoustic ground microphones pick up the sound of escaping water. Tracer gas helps too. Hydrogen-nitrogen gas pushed into the line rises through the soil at the leak, and electronic locators trace the exact pipe route so we know where to listen. The mark is usually very close.

    Wet spots or extra-green grass are the classic signs. The water bill often tells you first. A sudden drop in pressure, a sewage or sulfur smell in the yard, or gurgling drains may mean a broken pipe underground on the supply or drain side. Check the meter with everything off.

    Every repair is warrantied. We write the warranty down. Our workmanship warranty covers the repair, while the parts carry separate manufacturer coverage. We explain it before we start.

    Many utilities offer a one-time leak adjustment. It is worth asking. Most want proof that the leak was repaired promptly, so we hand you paperwork showing what was fixed and when. Rules differ by provider.

    A spot repair is usually a few hours. Trenchless jobs take about a day. A full sewer replacement with excavation can take two to five days, depending on length, depth, inspection timing and how much surface restoration the job needs. You get the timeline with the quote.

    It depends on your policy and the cause. Check what is excluded. Sudden and accidental damage is sometimes covered, but normal aging and long-term leaks generally fall outside coverage. Ask your insurer directly. We provide documentation for the claim.
