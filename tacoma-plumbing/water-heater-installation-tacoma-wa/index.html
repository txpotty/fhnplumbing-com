    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Tacoma

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank water heater typically costs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless installations often cost $3,000 to $6,500 depending on gas line and venting needs.

    Many tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, though hard water can shorten that. Regular flushing and anode rod checks help lengthen life.

    The average lifespan of a traditional tank water heater is about 10 to 15 years, while tankless water heaters can last 20 years or more with proper maintenance. Factors like local water quality, maintenance frequency and usage habits can all affect the actual lifespan of your unit. Regular flushing can help extend its life.

    In most areas, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement or installation to ensure the work meets local building and safety codes. Plumbers in Washington must hold a state certification, and water heater replacements and repiping usually require a permit. FHN Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and inspections in Tacoma as part of our service.

    Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional inspection.

    An expansion tank absorbs excess water pressure created as water heats up inside the tank. Many homes in Tacoma, WA, especially those with closed-loop plumbing systems, are required by code to have an expansion tank to protect the water heater and plumbing system from damage.

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve or check valve, an expansion tank is usually required. It protects your water heater and pipes from pressure spikes.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency.
