    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Everett

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Check the heater first. It also shows up under sinks, at the main and through garage walls, and we follow it into the attic to see how much of the house it feeds.

    Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Everett

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. Watch the water meter. With every fixture off, a spinning leak indicator on the meter means water is going somewhere, and we use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to find it without opening walls at random. Once we find it, a temporary fix buys time to schedule the repipe.

    Why Polybutylene Fails in Everett

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is typically soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. Heat makes it worse. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Everett

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. It is rigid, handles heat well and has decades of history in homes. It costs more. We review your water and any history of copper pinholes nearby before recommending it over PEX.

    Polybutylene Leak Repair in Everett

    Repairs on polybutylene are temporary by nature, because the pipe next to the repair is the same age. We still make them. When a poly line leaks, we cut out the bad section and splice in PEX with approved transition fittings, which stops the leak and gives you time to schedule a full replacement on your own terms.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Everett, WA

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Faster install means lower labor cost. Attic PEX stays out of UV, clear of flues and can lights and supported as the maker specifies, which protects the pipe and the warranty.
