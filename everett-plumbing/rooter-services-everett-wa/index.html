    Clearing a clog in Everett does not need a permit. Repairs are another matter. If the camera shows broken pipe that needs a dig, a new cleanout or a sewer replacement, a plumbing permit is usually required. We pull it and schedule the inspection for you.

    Most single-drain clogs take 1 to 3 hours from arrival to flow test. A root-packed main or a job that adds jetting can take most of a day. Access is the wild card. With no usable cleanout, we pull a toilet to get in, and that adds about an hour.

    Clay or older cast iron lines with trees nearby usually need rooting every 12 to 24 months. Newer PVC lines may go years without it. A camera helps set the schedule. How quickly roots return after the last cut tells us if yearly or every-other-year service fits your line.
