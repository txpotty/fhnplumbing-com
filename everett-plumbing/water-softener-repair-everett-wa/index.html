    Resin Testing and Replacement in Everett

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Everett

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. New resin restores full capacity.
