    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout.     Bathroom Sink Repair in Everett, WA

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Everett

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and protecting hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage.
