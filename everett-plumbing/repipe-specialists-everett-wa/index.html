    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Everett, WA

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average home often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper typically costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 including access and restoration. After two or three leaks, a repipe often pays for itself. We give you a side-by-side comparison so you can make the call.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Everett, WA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in Everett, WA. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a dependable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Everett

    Copper piping offers excellent durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Everett. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team carefully solders each joint, ensuring a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions. Copper also naturally inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Everett

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to four days.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Everett

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Crawlspaces and basements are both common in Washington homes, and a wet crawlspace is often the first sign of a drainage or plumbing problem. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Everett, WA

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are known for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply. If your home in Everett, WA has polybutylene, a full replacement is essential to prevent catastrophic water damage. FHN Plumbing offers specialized polybutylene plumbing replacement, carefully removing the old, brittle pipes and installing a reliable new system.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Everett

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell later. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us which type you have.

    Drain Line Repiping in Everett

    While often overlooked, your home's drain lines are just as crucial as your supply lines. Old cast iron or corroded drain pipes can lead to frequent clogs, slow drainage, and even sewage backups. FHN Plumbing provides comprehensive drain line repiping in Everett, replacing failing waste pipes with new, smooth PVC or ABS lines. This supports efficient waste removal and prevents future drainage problems, protecting your home from foul odors and unsanitary conditions.
