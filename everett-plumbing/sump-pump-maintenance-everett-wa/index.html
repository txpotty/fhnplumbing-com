    Wayne Sump Pump Service in Everett, WA

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. We check that intake and the float mechanism for buildup. Any brand can rattle its discharge fittings loose over time. Easy fix.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Everett, WA

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Each start heats the motor windings. Our plumbers replace worn valves with quieter spring-loaded types and check the pipe for cracks and loose couplings out to where it leaves the house. The water should exit well away from the slab or foundation so it does not soak back toward the pit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Everett, WA

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. We check the battery with a load tester, not just a voltmeter. Then we unplug the main pump and let the backup empty the pit on its own. Alarms get tested too.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Everett

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. We pump the basin down and scoop out the sediment by hand. With the pit clean, the float swings without catching. The pump runs cooler too.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Cost in Everett, WA

    A new sump pump, installed, often runs $400 to $1,200 depending on horsepower, pit work and whether you add a battery backup. Routine maintenance costs a small fraction of that. Most pumps last seven to ten years with care. You get a straight answer on whether your pump has life left or should be replaced.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Everett

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. Even so, we look for cracks in the casting and make sure the float moves freely. Common Zoeller parts ride on our trucks.

    Emergency Sump Pump Service in Everett

A burned motor means we swap in a new pump on the spot and leave the pit protected.

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out.
