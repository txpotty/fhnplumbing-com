    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a popular choice in many contemporary Everett bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out.     Leaky Faucet Repair in Everett

    A dripping faucet is more than just an annoyance, it's a waste of water and money. Fixing a leak quickly is one of the simplest ways to conserve water at home. Our plumbers can identify the cause, whether it's a worn-out washer, O-ring, or a corroded valve, and replace the failed components.     Ball Faucet Repair in Everett, WA

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's probably time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are typically the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Everett

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and protecting hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. A leaking hose bib can waste a lot of water over a full season of yard use.

    If water keeps running from the tub spout while the shower is on, the diverter is worn. We replace diverter spouts, stems and gates and repair tub faucet drips.     Dripping Faucet Solutions in Everett

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Everett
