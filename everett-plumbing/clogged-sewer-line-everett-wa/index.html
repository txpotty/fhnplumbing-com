    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Everett, WA

    One slow drain is a branch clog. Several slow drains is the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, so the pattern of slow drains tells us where to go in. Starting in the right place matters.

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Everett

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. Cabling alone is not enough. Those lines are usually coated in grease or scale, and a cable just cuts a small hole that closes back up. Jetting removes the coating down to the pipe wall. It lasts much longer.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Everett

    A sewer camera is a waterproof head on a push rod with a locator transmitter built in. It shows us everything. Cracks, offsets, sags, roots and heavy scale all show up on the screen, while the locator marks the spot and depth from above. That keeps digging to a minimum. You get a copy of the footage.

    Commercial Sewer Line Clearing in Everett, WA

    A backed up sewer at a restaurant or office means closing the doors. Every hour costs money. FHN Plumbing clears commercial mains with heavy sectional machines and a high-flow jetter, scheduling the work for off hours when that helps. Kitchen mains need jetting.

    Tree Root Invasion Removal in Everett

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture in the soil. They only need a tiny opening. Once inside, they grow into a dense mat that catches toilet paper and waste until the whole line stops flowing. We cut them out with a root saw on a sectional machine, and check the joints on camera afterward. Lining or replacement seals the joints for good.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Everett

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That is the difference. Water at thousands of psi cuts roots, grease and scale, and the rear-facing spray carries all of it downstream as the nozzle pulls itself along the line. We camera the pipe first so jetting only goes into lines that can take it. Many lines stay clear for years after.
