    What a Water Heater Tune-Up Covers in Everett, WA

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Everett

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. We test both elements. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.

    Descaling Tankless Units in Everett

    Error codes often mean scale. Scale in the exchanger cuts flow and trips the unit's safety shutoff. A pump kit handles it. Most makers recommend descaling at least yearly with hard water.

    Tune-Up Versus Replacement in Everett, WA

    A tune-up costs a fraction of a new heater. Maintenance helps a tank reach its full rated life and helps tankless units last much longer. Sometimes replacement makes sense. You see both prices side by side.

    Anode Rod Checks in Everett

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. We pull it and look. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.

    Water Heater Problem Diagnosis in Everett, WA

    Something sounds or feels wrong. Rumbling, short hot water or a wet pan get diagnosed on the spot, with a straight answer on repair or replace. We lay out choices. No pressure either way.

    Commercial Tank and Tankless Service in Everett, WA

    Commercial heaters work harder than home units. High demand speeds up scale and wear, so commercial units need service on a tighter schedule. We service them regularly. We keep your business supplied.

    Yearly Service Plans in Everett, WA

    Forgetting is the main reason tanks go unflushed. A yearly plan puts the visit on the calendar, sends a reminder a week ahead, and gives you priority scheduling and repair discounts if the heater ever acts up between visits. We track it for you. It pays off over time.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Everett, WA

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We test the valve, confirm it reseats and check that the discharge pipe is routed correctly. We swap bad ones. Expansion tanks get an air charge check.
