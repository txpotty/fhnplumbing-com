    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Everett

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. FHN Plumbing carefully installs various models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Everett home.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Everett

    Hard water can damage plumbing and appliances, leading to expensive repairs. Our residential water softener installation services are designed to protect your home's entire plumbing system. We assess your water usage and hardness levels to recommend the ideal system size and type, delivering strong performance for your Everett household. From initial consultation to final setup, we handle every detail with precision.

    Softener Loop Installation in Everett

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service simple.
