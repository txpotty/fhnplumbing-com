    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Everett, WA

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is generally soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.
