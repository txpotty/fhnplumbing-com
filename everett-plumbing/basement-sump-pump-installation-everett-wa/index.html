    New Sump Pit Excavation in Everett

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by finding the low point where water collects. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor remains clean and level.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Everett

    The cost for basement sump pump installation in Everett, WA usually ranges from $800 to $2,500, depending on the type of pump, pedestal or submersible, whether a new pit needs to be excavated and whether a battery backup is included. We provide itemized estimates after an on-site assessment.

    We recommend having your sump pump inspected and maintained annually, ideally before the rainy season. Regular maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning debris out of the pit, testing the check valve and confirming the discharge line is clear. This helps prevent unexpected failures and lengthens the life of your pump.

    We install a wide range of well-built, reliable sump pump brands, including Zoeller, Wayne, Little Giant and Liberty Pumps. We can help you choose the best brand and model for your budget and water management needs in your Everett home, considering horsepower and features.

    Watch for a pump that runs constantly, cycles on and off rapidly, makes grinding noises or never seems to turn on. Rust stains, a stuck float and water sitting above the switch level are also red flags.

    Replacing a pump generally does not need a permit, but new pits, new discharge routes or new electrical circuits may. We check local requirements and pull permits when they are needed.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 3 to 5 years on quality cast iron pumps. We also back our installation labor, and we will walk you through the specific coverage for the model you choose.

    We regularly install Zoeller, Liberty Pumps and Wayne because they offer cast iron housings, reliable switches and good parts support. We will fit the brand and model to your pit size and budget.

    Usually, standard home insurance policies do not cover a new sump pump installation or repairs unless the damage was caused by a sudden, accidental event like a burst pipe, rather than gradual wear and tear or groundwater seepage. Some policies offer separate water backup or sump pump failure endorsements for an extra premium. It is best to check with your insurance provider.
