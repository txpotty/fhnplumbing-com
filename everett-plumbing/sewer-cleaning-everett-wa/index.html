    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Roots love clay joints, while Orangeburg fiber pipe tends to squash into an oval and eventually collapse, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Shop and Restaurant Sewers in Everett

    Busy buildings clog more often. Volume is the reason. We bring larger jetters for 4 and 6 inch commercial lines and can work before opening or after closing, so the cleaning does not shut down a lunch rush or a full day of business.

    Video Inspection Before Cleaning in Everett, WA

    Some lines need a look first. Old pipe especially. Seeing the pipe first lets us pick the cheapest method that will work, and avoid damaging a line that is already failing.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Everett, WA

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Mid-century homes often have galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains, while some 1980s and 1990s homes were plumbed with gray polybutylene. Scale builds on top while the bottom channels out. Because a heavy cutter can punch through weak iron, we use chain descalers and a lower-pressure jet, then check the wall thickness on camera.

    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Everett, WA

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. One pipe, many fixtures. We clear it with a sectional cable or a jetter, then camera the line to see whether the cause was roots, debris or a structural problem like a broken joint or an offset section.

    Scheduled Sewer Cleaning in Everett, WA

    History predicts the next backup. We set a cleaning interval. Homes with past root problems, older cast iron or a lot of kitchen grease do well on a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the growth or buildup closes the line.
