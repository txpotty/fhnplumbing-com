    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Everett, WA

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is known for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Many insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Everett

    Kitchen and bathroom remodels are a great time to replace old supply lines with PEX. Additions and guest suites get correctly sized lines tied into your existing system. We coordinate with your contractor to keep the project schedule moving.

    PEX is also a premier choice for hydronic heating systems, including radiant floor heating.     PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Everett, WA

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Everett, WA

The total depends on the number of bathrooms, the home's size and how accessible the attic and walls are. Drywall patching and texture matching are sometimes included or quoted separately. We give you a detailed estimate so you can compare PEX-A, PEX-B and copper options side by side.

    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Everett

    A whole-home PEX repipe usually costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on home size, bathroom count and access. Smaller jobs like a single bathroom remodel rough-in usually run $800 to $2,500.

    PEX offers several advantages over copper, including lower material and installation costs, greater flexibility and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Unlike copper, PEX does not form pinhole leaks over time due to water chemistry, making it a more durable choice for many Everett homes. It also reduces water hammer noise.

    We install Uponor PEX-A along with Viega, Zurn and SharkBite PEX-B systems. We recommend the system that fits your project and budget.

    While PEX is simpler to install than copper, professional installation is strongly recommended. Correct sizing, routing and secure connections are essential for preventing leaks and ensuring system longevity. Improper DIY installation can lead to costly water damage and code violations.

    Signs you might need PEX repiping include persistently low water pressure, discolored or rusty water, frequent leaks, or old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes. These issues are typical in older Everett homes and point to a failing plumbing system.

    The timeline for PEX pipe installation depends on the size and complexity of the project. A small repair or extension might take a few hours, while a complete whole-home repipe for an average-sized house in Everett typically takes 2 to 5 days. We work efficiently to limit disruption to your daily routine.

    Properly installed PEX is expected to last several decades. Manufacturers commonly offer 25 year warranties when their pipe and fittings are used together.

    PEX resists scale and corrosion, flexes with temperature changes and has far fewer joints. Copper is very durable but prone to pinhole leaks in some water conditions and costs more to install.
