    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Pasco, WA

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Soft water can be slightly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.
