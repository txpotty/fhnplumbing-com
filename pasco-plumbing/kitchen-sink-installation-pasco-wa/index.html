    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Pasco, WA: Style by Style

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Pasco, WA

    A new sink is the cheapest time to add a disposal. The disposal mounts to the new flange, the trap is set to its outlet height and the dishwasher plug gets knocked out for the hose. You also need power. We check for a switched outlet in the cabinet.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Pasco, WA

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, with silicone run around the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, typically for a full day, before loading it with water. Rushing this causes the classic sag. A sink that drops even an eighth of an inch opens a gap where water and crumbs collect along the counter edge.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Pasco, WA

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the sink has to be in place before the counter is templated. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. That avoids an uneven reveal. A cast iron apron sink can weigh 150 pounds or more, so two of us show up.

    Kitchen Sink Installation Cost Factors in Pasco, WA

    Undermount and farmhouse installs cost more than a drop-in swap. Each extra, like a new disposal, a moved drain, a lowered wall stub or replacing galvanized stubs, adds labor and shows up as its own line on the quote. Plumbing is priced apart from the counter.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Pasco, WA

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. Either way, the bowl gets braced. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.

    Restaurant Sink Installation in Pasco

    Restaurants, cafes and commissary kitchens in Pasco get their commercial sinks installed by FHN Plumbing. Hand sinks need hot and cold. Three-compartment, prep and hand sinks go in with the floor sinks, indirect drains and grease trap tie-ins the inspector checks.

    Stainless Sinks With Sound Pads in Pasco

    Cheap stainless bowls are noisy when dishes hit them or the disposal runs. Better sinks come with rubber pads and an undercoating, and if yours does not, we can add a coat of spray undercoating before we set it. It is a small upgrade. After the sink is in place the underside is hard to get at, which is why we do it while the bowl is still sitting out.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Pasco

    Composite sinks are heavier than stainless and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. We check the strainer fit first. If the included strainer is too short for the sink wall, we swap in one that fits rather than overtightening and risking a chip around the opening.
