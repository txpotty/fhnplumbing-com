    Storm Drain Repair in Pasco, WA

    Yard and storm drains carry rain away from the foundation. Heavy, prolonged rain from atmospheric river storms is a major factor in western Washington, raising groundwater and filling crawlspaces and basements. Leaves and silt clog them. We clear the line with a jetter, camera it to look for crushed or separated sections, and replace damaged corrugated or PVC pipe so water flows to the outlet instead of pooling against the house. We check the discharge end every time.

    Laundry and Floor Drain Repair in Pasco

    A washer dumps a lot of water fast. Small standpipes overflow. We correct standpipe height, replace cracked laundry box drains and add venting where it is missing, and for garage and utility floor drains we restore the trap seal so sewer odor stays in the pipe. We fix primers that keep those seals full.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Pasco, WA

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. Cracks start along the bottom. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, and we replace the bad length with plastic pipe joined to the old iron with shielded couplings. If the whole stack is failing, we price it separately.

    Tub and Shower Drain Repair in Pasco, WA

    Hair, soap scum and minerals narrow bath drains over time. Drain seals wear out. We replace worn pop-up assemblies, broken tub shoes and leaking shower drain bodies, and failed drain gaskets can usually be replaced from above without cutting into the ceiling below. Any access hole is kept small and neat.

    Clogged Drain Repair in Pasco

    Grease, hair, food scraps and mineral scale cause most clogs. Repeat clogs are a warning.     Every drain in the house empties into one building drain. Trouble there affects everything.     Emergency Drain Repairs in Pasco, WA

    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Pasco, WA

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. It clogs with silt. Steep slopes and wet soils can shift over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, so we camera the tile, find the failed section and replace it with perforated pipe wrapped in filter fabric and clean gravel. We confirm it drains to daylight or a sump.

    Cleaning removes a blockage from a sound pipe. Repair fixes the pipe. A crack, a sag, a broken fitting or a missing vent will keep causing clogs no matter how often the line gets cleaned, so a drain that clogs repeatedly usually needs repair rather than more snaking. A camera tells you which.

    PVC, ABS, cast iron, galvanized and copper drain all show up in Washington homes. We work on all of them. Joining new plastic to old metal takes a shielded transition coupling sized to the outside diameter of each pipe, because a mismatched coupling is the most common reason a drain repair drips. We stock the usual couplings.

    Hydro-jetting scours the pipe with water at several thousand psi. It cleans wall to wall. A snake only punches a hole through a clog, while a jetter removes grease, scale and fine roots from the full circumference of the pipe. Fragile pipe gets checked before jetting.

    Most trap and fixture drain repairs take one to two hours. Wall work takes longer. Replacing drain pipe inside a wall or under a floor can take half a day to a full day, depending on how much finish material has to come out. We give you a time with the quote.
