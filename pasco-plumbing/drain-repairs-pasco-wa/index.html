    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Pasco

    The cost of drain repairs in Pasco, WA can vary widely, typically ranging from $150 for a simple clog removal to over $3,000 for complex main sewer line repairs. Factors like the type of drain, severity of the issue and access to the pipes all affect the final price. We provide a detailed estimate after a thorough inspection.

    A dry trap, a cracked drain fitting, a failed wax ring or a blocked vent can all let sewer gas into a room. We locate the source with a smoke test or visual inspection and seal it.

    Drain lines can break or collapse due to several factors, including shifting soil, tree root intrusion, corrosion from age, extreme temperature changes or heavy loads passing over underground pipes. Poor installation or defective materials can also lead to early failure.

    Repeat clogs usually come from grease buildup, hard water scale, poor pipe slope, a missing vent or a damaged pipe that catches debris. A camera inspection shows which cause you are dealing with so it can be fixed permanently.

    While chemical drain cleaners are widely available, we usually advise against using them. They often contain harsh chemicals that can damage your pipes, especially older ones, and are typically only a temporary fix. For effective and safe clog removal, professional methods are always the better choice.

    Harsh chemical cleaners can soften older ABS glue joints and corrode metal drain parts with repeated use. They also rarely clear grease or scale fully. A mechanical auger or hydro-jet is safer and more effective.

    Tightening a loose slip nut or replacing a plastic trap is a reasonable DIY job. If the leak is from a cracked tailpiece, a corroded metal drain or inside a wall, a plumber can make a lasting repair and check for hidden damage.

    Minor fixture drain repairs usually do not require a permit. Replacing drain stacks, relocating drains or adding new lines often does, depending on the city. We handle permits when they apply.
