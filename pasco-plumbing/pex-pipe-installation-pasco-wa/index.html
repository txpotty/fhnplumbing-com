    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Pasco

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the distinct advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, usually using crimp or compression fittings. Our plumbers in Pasco can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Pasco

    When your main water line from the street to your home needs replacement, PEX is an smart option. Its flexibility allows for easier trenchless or minimal-dig installations, limiting disruption to your yard in Pasco. PEX main lines handle ground movement and resist corrosion better than rigid pipe. Steep slopes and wet soils can move over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Pasco, WA

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper repipes because the material is less expensive and installs faster. The total depends on the number of bathrooms, the home's size and how accessible the attic and walls are. Drywall patching and texture matching are sometimes included or quoted separately. We give you a detailed estimate so you can compare PEX-A, PEX-B and copper options together.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Pasco, WA

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is known for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Pasco

    The cost of PEX pipe installation in Pasco can range from $2,500 to $15,000 or more, depending on the size of your home, accessibility and whether it is a full repipe or a partial replacement.     PEX offers several advantages over copper, including lower material and installation costs, greater flexibility and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Unlike copper, PEX does not form pinhole leaks over time due to water chemistry, making it a more durable choice for many Pasco homes. It also cuts down on water hammer noise.

    Whole-home repipes usually require a plumbing permit and inspection. We handle the permit process as part of the project.

    Properly installed PEX is expected to last several decades. Manufacturers often offer 25 year warranties when their pipe and fittings are used together.

    We make small, targeted openings to run new pipe and patch them when done. Most homeowners are surprised by how little drywall work is required.

    Most whole-home PEX repipes take two to four days. Larger homes or tight attic access can take a bit longer.

    PEX is a good fit for a wide range of climates because of its flexibility and resistance to freezing. Constant moisture west of the mountains promotes mold, rot and corrosion around leaks, so small drips should not be ignored. While rigid pipes can burst when water freezes, PEX can expand and contract, greatly lowering the risk of burst pipes during cold snaps.
