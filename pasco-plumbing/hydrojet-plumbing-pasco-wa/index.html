    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Pasco

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. We camera every line before we set up the jetter. The video shows the pipe material, the condition of each joint, and any cracks, offsets or sags, which tells us the nozzle and pressure to use or whether we should not jet that line at all.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Pasco

    Slow drains in several rooms usually mean buildup in a main or branch line. We jet the main from the cleanout and the branches from roof vents or from fixture access points as needed for that layout. Most homes take two to three hours.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in Pasco, WA

    Retail and office buildings tend to have long drain runs where buildup settles. Flat runs collect more debris. After-hours jetting keeps equipment out of hallways while tenants are working.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Pasco

    Preventive jetting pays off for lines that clog more than once a year. Camera findings set the interval. A line with heavy grease might need jetting every six months, while a sewer with a few root entry points might only need it every 18 to 24 months, and the footage tells us which.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Pasco

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain is a main line blockage. Stop using water first. We open the line, jet out the cause, and camera it to see if a repair is needed.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Pasco, WA

    Restaurant kitchen lines carry hot grease that hardens as it cools. Much of it coats the pipe before it ever reaches the interceptor. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Commercial Hydrojet Plumbing in Pasco, WA
