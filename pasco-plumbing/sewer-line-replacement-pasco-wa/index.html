    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Pasco, WA

    Concrete is expensive to replace. Bursting lets us run new HDPE under a driveway, patio or mature tree by pulling it through the path of the old pipe from a launch pit to a receiving pit.     Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Pasco

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. Where we can, the sod is cut and set aside before the digging starts. The inspector sees open pipe. Backfill goes in compacted lifts so the trench line does not sink into a ditch a year later.

    Two holes instead of one long trench. Pipe bursting drags a cone-shaped head through the old line, cracking it outward while new fused HDPE follows right behind.
