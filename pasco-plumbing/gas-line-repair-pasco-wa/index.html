    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Pasco

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged.     Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Pasco, WA

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas.     Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Pasco, WA

    Propane Gas Line Service in Pasco, WA
