    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Pasco

    Many rural Washington homes rely on private wells, and some carry iron, manganese or other minerals that call for testing and treatment. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. Then we explain your options. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Pasco

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked.     Water Heater Safety Inspection in Pasco
