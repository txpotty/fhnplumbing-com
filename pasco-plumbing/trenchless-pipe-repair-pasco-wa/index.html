    Tree roots, corrosion and soil movement wreck most sewer lines. Steep slopes and wet soils can move over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints.     Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Pasco

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Prep is half the job. Before lining, Picote heads mill out roots and scale, and after curing they reopen the lateral connections so each drain flows. Good prep makes a good liner.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Pasco, WA

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Pasco, WA soil.

    Sewer Lateral Lining in Pasco

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Clay and Orangeburg fail often. Over time, clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots, so we jet, scope and then pick lining or bursting. The new line restores full flow and stands up to roots.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Pasco

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Pasco yard.
