    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Pasco

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. A burst fitting in an attic or wall can cause major damage in minutes.     Copper Pipe Replacement in Pasco

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. It is rigid, handles heat well and has decades of history in homes. It costs more. Some water can pinhole copper over time, so we look at your water and any past leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Pasco

    Most poly replacements in Pasco fall between $4,000 and $15,000 or more. Size drives the price. A small single-bath home with easy attic access sits at the low end, while a large two-story house with several bathrooms, or one where we choose copper, sits at the high end. The quote comes in writing at the visit.

    We commonly use Uponor PEX-A and Type L copper with Viega ProPress or soldered fittings. Your house and water decide which we suggest.

    Both are good choices. PEX is cheaper. PEX is flexible, handles freezing and movement better and installs faster, while copper is rigid, tolerates heat and sunlight and has a long track record, so the right answer depends on budget, water and preference.

    Look for gray, flexible plastic pipe at your water heater, under sinks or near the main shutoff, often stamped PB2110. It is most common in homes from the late 1970s to mid 1990s.

    Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. Hard water does not cause poly to fail the way chlorine does. Scale builds inside. Scale buildup can reduce flow and add stress to pipe that is already brittle, so in hard water areas we often suggest adding a softener loop during the replacement.

    Replacing a whole supply system takes special tools, code knowledge and real repipe experience. It is not a weekend job. DIY mistakes can leave leaks hidden in walls, fail inspection or void the pipe warranty, which is why we recommend leaving it to licensed plumbers.

    Properly installed PEX usually lasts 25 to 50 years. Copper can last longer. In water that does not attack it, Type L copper can last 50 years or more, and both are major upgrades over poly.

    In most cases, yes, a plumbing permit is required for full repiping in Pasco. We take care of it. We handle the application, the open-wall inspection and the final sign-off, leaving no paperwork for you.
