    Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Pasco

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and proven solution for hard water in Pasco.     Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Pasco, WA

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more cost effective than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes better sense.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Pasco

    A standard single tank softener replacement typically costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can raise the cost.

    Many systems include 5 to 10 years on the valve and longer coverage on tanks. We also provide a warranty on our labor.

    We test your hardness in grains per gallon and estimate daily water usage. That tells us the resin capacity needed.

    To extend the life of your new water softener, use the correct type of salt, if applicable, and keep the brine tank filled. Regular maintenance, such as cleaning the brine tank and periodic inspections by a professional, can also help ensure its longevity and efficiency.

    Spots on dishes, scale on faucets, dry skin and increased soap use can point to a failing softener. A softener that no longer uses salt or keeps flooding the brine tank may also need replacement.

    Most standard residential water softener replacements can be finished within 3 to 5 hours. This includes disconnecting the old unit, installing the new one, making plumbing connections and testing the system. More complex installations may take a bit longer.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had heavy mineral buildup restricting flow. However, a water softener's main job is to remove hardness minerals, not to boost pressure. If low water pressure is your main concern, we can look into other causes.

    New water softeners typically come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years on different components, such as the control valve and resin tank. FHN Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work for your peace of mind. Exact warranty details will be provided with your quote.
