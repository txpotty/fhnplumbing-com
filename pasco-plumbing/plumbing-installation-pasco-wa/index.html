    What We Install in Pasco, WA

    Water Softener Installation in Pasco, WA

    Many rural Washington homes rely on private wells, and some carry iron, manganese or other minerals that call for testing and treatment. Scale shows up first as white crust on faucet aerators and a rumbling water heater. We test hardness in grains per gallon at the kitchen tap, then size a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner to your household use. The bypass valve matters. With it, the softener can be serviced while the house still has water.

    PEX Repiping in Pasco

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. It runs quieter than copper, with less water hammer banging in the walls. Freeze damage is less likely too. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and protecting hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. We use fittings matched to the tubing brand.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Pasco

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. Repiping in PEX stops the cycle. We run either a manifold with home runs to each fixture, which balances pressure, or a trunk-and-branch layout when the walls make home runs impractical. We cut small, clean access holes and patch them, or hand off to your drywall contractor if you prefer.

    Water Heater Replacement in Pasco

    Most tank heaters give out between 8 and 12 years, sooner with heavy sediment. Our stock covers 40 and 50 gallon tanks and 75 gallon units for larger families, and each install includes new connectors, a code relief line and a vent check. We also check whether you need an expansion tank. Most installs finish in one visit.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Pasco, WA

    PEX bends around corners, shrugs off corrosion and needs far fewer fittings than copper. On a whole-house repipe that means fewer joints hidden behind drywall and fewer places for a slow leak to start rotting a stud. It can also stretch when water freezes. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and protecting hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. Stretch or not, we still insulate runs in exterior walls and crawl spaces.

    Backflow Preventer Installation in Pasco

    Irrigation systems and fire sprinkler lines are the usual reasons a home needs a backflow preventer. It keeps fertilizer-laced lawn water from being pulled back into the drinking supply when a main break or hydrant flush drops pressure. The type matters. We pick the assembly for the hazard level and local requirement and test it on install day.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Pasco, WA

    Navien and Rinnai tankless units hang on a wall and free up the floor space a tank used to take. Most gas units need upsized gas piping and a new vent run in stainless or PVC. Electric models need amperage. We size by gallons per minute and the temperature rise from your incoming water. Washington has two very different climates, with long, wet and mild seasons in the west and hot, dry summers and cold winters in the east. Isolation valves go on every unit for descaling.

    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Pasco, WA

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. We size every run from the appliance BTU ratings and total pipe length, and use black iron or CSST depending on the path. Code requires CSST to be bonded. Without that bond, a lightning strike nearby can arc through the tubing and start a fire.
