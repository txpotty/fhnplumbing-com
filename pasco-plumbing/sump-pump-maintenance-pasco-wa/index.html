    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Pasco

    FHN Plumbing is proficient in servicing Zoeller sump pumps, a widely trusted brand known for its durability. Our maintenance includes specific checks for Zoeller models, such as inspecting the cast iron housing for cracks, ensuring the motor is running smoothly and testing the float switch assembly for correct activation. We stock common replacement parts for efficient repairs, keeping your Zoeller pump in peak condition.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Pasco

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last longer.

    Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Pasco

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. We test duplex systems, alternating controls and high-water alarms on a scheduled basis. Maintenance logs help property managers stay on top of inspections.

    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Pasco

    Regular annual maintenance is crucial for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage. We also test the system's operation by filling the pit with water to ensure it activates correctly and pumps water away efficiently, protecting your Pasco home from unexpected flooding.

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Pasco

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacement every three to five years.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Pasco, WA

    Many Pasco, WA homeowners rely on battery backup sump pumps for protection during power outages. FHN Plumbing provides thorough maintenance for these critical systems, including testing the battery's charge and health, inspecting the backup pump's operation and checking the charging system. This keeps your home protected even when the primary power source fails, a common concern during severe storms.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Pasco, WA

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in Pasco, WA.
