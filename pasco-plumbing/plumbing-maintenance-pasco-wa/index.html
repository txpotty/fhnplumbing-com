    Water Heater Maintenance in Pasco

    Regular water heater maintenance is crucial to preventing sediment buildup. Many rural Washington homes rely on private wells, and some carry iron, manganese or other minerals that call for testing and treatment. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, improves efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Pasco

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is especially important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and possible breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.
