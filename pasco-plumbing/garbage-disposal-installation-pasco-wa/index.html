    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Pasco

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first.     Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Pasco, WA

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Pasco, WA

    A continuous-feed unit needs a switch, which a batch-feed kitchen may not have. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. An air button skips the wall wiring. The drain usually stays the same.

    InSinkErator Disposal Installation in Pasco, WA

    InSinkErator uses the Quick Lock mount on most current models, with a ring we snug down using the wrench in the box. Badger units are the simple single-stage line, while Evolution models add grind stages and insulation that make them noticeably quieter in an open kitchen. Cords are often sold separately. We bring the right cord kit for the model.

    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Pasco

    Batch-feed disposals only run when the stopper lid is in place, which adds safety for families with kids. Because they need no wall switch, they suit kitchens where a new switch would mean cutting drywall or a tile backsplash. Mounting and drains are standard.

    Disposal Rough-In for New Construction in Pasco

    Rough-in decides where the outlet and drain end up before the cabinets go in. For a kitchen with a disposal, we usually set the drain stub lower than a plain sink would need, so the trap can sit below the disposal outlet with room for proper fall. The electrician adds the switched outlet. We mark its spot so it clears the trap.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Pasco, WA

    A 1 HP disposal earns its keep in a busy kitchen, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model. We confirm the circuit can carry the motor and the drain can handle the flow, because a strong unit pushing into a flat trap arm will still clog. These units weigh more. They get supported during mounting.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Pasco, WA

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. Pressing the button sends a pulse of air through a small tube to a control box under the sink, so there is no electricity at the button and no need to drill for a wall switch. Islands use them often.
