    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Pasco

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. They clog on a schedule you can almost set your watch by. We open the line with a sectional cable, then jet the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so the grease does not just close back up in a week. Trap primers get checked too.

    Grease Trap and Interceptor Repair in Pasco, WA

    Food service sites need a grease trap or interceptor to keep fats, oils and grease out of the public sewer, and pretreatment inspectors check them. Odor is the first warning. We repair broken baffles, replace cracked lids and outlet tees, jet the inlet lines and confirm the unit is sized for your fixture count and flow rate. Slow sinks and overflow usually point to a full or damaged trap. We remind you to keep the pump-out log current for the inspector.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Pasco

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. We push the camera from a cleanout, note the footage at every break, root mass or sag, and use a locator to mark depth and position on the ground above. The video is yours to keep. That footage helps you budget repairs and explain them to owners or a board.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Pasco

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. We test RPZ, double check and PVB assemblies with calibrated gauges and send the results straight to the water provider. A failed test usually means new rubber and springs, not a whole new assembly. We carry Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits for that reason.

    Commercial Leak Detection in Pasco, WA

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. We shut fixtures off zone by zone and watch the meter, then use acoustic sensors, thermal cameras and tracer gas to find leaks in walls, ceilings and buried service lines. Running toilets count too. One stuck flush valve can waste several thousand gallons a week.

    Grease Trap Installation in Pasco

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Under-sink traps work for small kitchens, while larger operations need in-ground interceptors of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We work from your kitchen plan, set the unit at the right depth and vent it so it passes the pretreatment inspection on the first try.

    Commercial Fixture and Faucet Repair in Pasco, WA

    Commercial faucets and toilets are built heavy, but they still wear. Cartridges leak. We repair and replace sensor faucets, metering faucets, wall-hung toilets, mop sinks and three-compartment sinks, and we stock parts for Sloan, Chicago Faucets, Zurn and T and S Brass. Low-flow aerators cut water use without slowing hand washing. Most fixture calls are done in under two hours.

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Pasco, WA

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while.
