    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Spokane

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Both have weak spots. Roots love clay joints, while Orangeburg fiber pipe tends to squash into an oval and eventually collapse, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Spokane, WA

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. A crack stays a crack. When a line keeps backing up, we put the cost of regular cleaning next to the cost of a repair so you can see which makes sense, with the numbers in writing.

    Root Masses in Spokane Sewer Laterals

    Mature trees and old clay laterals do not mix. Roots find the joints. Our root cutting heads attach to the sectional cable and saw through the mass in passes, and a camera run afterward shows how much growth is left and where the roots are coming in.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Spokane, WA

    Grease hardens as it cools. Then it narrows the pipe. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.

    Scheduled Sewer Cleaning in Spokane, WA

    History predicts the next backup. We set a cleaning interval. Homes with past root problems, older cast iron or a lot of kitchen grease do well on a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the growth or buildup closes the line.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Spokane, WA

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. We would rather look than guess. The camera feed shows roots, grease, breaks and bellies in real time, and the locator marks the spot on the ground above.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Spokane, WA

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Housing in Washington ranges from early 1900s homes to new construction, with plumbing materials that change a lot from one decade to the next. Scale builds on top while the bottom channels out. Because a heavy cutter can punch through weak iron, we use chain descalers and a lower-pressure jet, then check the wall thickness on camera.
