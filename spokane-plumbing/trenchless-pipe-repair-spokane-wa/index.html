    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Spokane, WA

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It also upsizes lines. With an entry and exit pit dug, the winch pulls the head and new HDPE through, replacing the run in a day or two in most homes. FHN Plumbing fuses the HDPE into one length.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Spokane

    Older Spokane homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. Both fail with age. Clay joints let roots in and cast iron scales and rusts through, and a liner seals both problems with a new jointless pipe that often outlasts the original material by decades. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Spokane

    Not every pipe needs lining end to end. Short damage gets a short liner. When damage is limited to a few joints or a short section, a sectional liner costs less than a full-length one and goes in quickly, but we confirm the rest of the line is sound by camera before recommending it.

    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Spokane

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Picote milling heads clear roots, scale and old fittings out of the pipe before lining, and after the liner cures, they cut open the lateral connections so every drain in your home flows again. Good prep makes a good liner.

    Sectional Point Repair in Spokane

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. A spot liner fixes that. Guided by camera, a short liner is placed over the bad section and cured, so you pay for a few feet instead of the full line. It suits isolated damage in Spokane sewer lines.
