    Mixing Valve Repair in Spokane

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall.     Hot Water Pressure Problems in Spokane, WA

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead.     Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Spokane, WA

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Spokane, WA
