    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Spokane

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Crawlspaces and basements are both common in Washington homes, and a damp crawlspace is often the first sign of a drainage or plumbing problem. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Spokane, WA

    Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is typically soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. Water in many areas contains minerals that cause scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can accelerate corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral buildup, ensuring a longer-lasting plumbing system.
