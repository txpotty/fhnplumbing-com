    Well Pump & Pressure Tank Replacement in Spokane

    Rural homes around Spokane that run on a well go dry the moment the pump stops. The old pump comes up, wire and drop pipe get checked, and the new pump goes in at the right depth, often with a new tank and switch too. Water is usually back that day.

    Water Heater Replacement in Spokane

    Water heaters in Spokane that leak from the tank, rumble on every heat cycle or run out of hot water halfway through a shower are usually cheaper to replace than to keep repairing. Tank leaks are not repairable. We match the fuel and size, update the venting and add an expansion tank or drain pan if code calls for one. Hot water is back by evening.

    Water Softener Replacement in Spokane, WA

    Softeners wear out as the resin breaks down and the control valve stops cycling properly. Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is typically soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. Once scale returns, dishes spot or salt stops dropping, we test hardness, size a new unit and plumb it with a bypass so the house keeps water during service. New valves use less salt.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Spokane, WA

    Switching to tankless during a replacement is common in Spokane, WA, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. The gas draw can be four times a 40-gallon tank, so the meter line usually gets upsized, and the venting moves from B-vent to stainless or PVC. Both are on the quote.

    Repair vs Replacement Cost Comparison in Spokane, WA

    A common rule of thumb is to replace when a repair costs more than half the price of new, or when the part is near the end of its life. Take a 12-year-old heater with a bad gas valve, where the valve alone costs a third of a new unit and the tank is already near the end. We price both.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Spokane

    A well pump that clicks on and off every few seconds usually points to a failed pressure tank. When the bladder inside the tank ruptures, the tank fills with water, loses its air cushion and forces the pump to start every time someone opens a tap, which burns out pump motors years early. Replacing the tank stops it. The pressure switch gets checked too.

    Pipe Replacement & Whole-Home Repiping in Spokane, WA

    Homes in Spokane, WA with galvanized steel or polybutylene usually show it the same ways: low pressure, rusty water and leaks that keep coming back. Repiping swaps every supply line from the main to each fixture for PEX or copper, usually in two to four days with water restored most nights. We patch every opening.

    Water Line Replacement in Spokane, WA

    A leaking water service line runs your meter all day and night and can soften the soil next to the foundation. In Spokane, WA, we replace old galvanized, polybutylene and failing copper service lines with PEX or copper from the meter to the house and add a new full-port main shutoff at the foundation wall.
