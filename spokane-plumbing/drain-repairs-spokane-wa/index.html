    Drain Repair Options for Spokane, WA Homes

    Storm Drain Repair in Spokane, WA

    Yard and storm drains carry rain away from the foundation. Eastern Washington deals more with drought and heat, while spring snowmelt can still lift creeks and groundwater in low areas. Leaves and silt clog them. We clear the line with a jetter, camera it to look for crushed or separated sections, and replace damaged corrugated or PVC pipe so water flows to the outlet instead of pooling against the house. A clean outlet matters too.

    Recurring Clog Repair in Spokane

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Cleaning will not stop it. The usual suspects are a trap arm with too little slope, a branch line with a belly, scale built up inside old metal pipe or a fitting with a rough edge that snags paper, and most of them are obvious once the camera or a pulled trap shows the inside. We repair the part that catches debris.

    After-Hours Drain Repairs in Spokane, WA

    Backups and burst drain pipes do not keep business hours. Our phones stay on. An after-hours plumber in Spokane can clear a main line backup, stop an active leak and make a permanent repair on the spot when the truck has the right parts, which it usually does. Larger repairs go on the next morning's schedule.

    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Spokane, WA

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. Fine soil plugs it over time. Crawlspaces and basements are both common in Washington homes, and a wet crawlspace is often the first sign of a drainage or plumbing problem. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, so we camera the tile, find the failed section and replace it with perforated pipe wrapped in filter fabric and clean gravel. We confirm it drains to daylight or a sump.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Repair in Spokane, WA

    Kitchen drains take grease, food and soap scum. They clog the most. We clear the branch, replace corroded or cracked tailpieces, fix leaking slip nuts and reset traps that were installed too low or too high for the trap arm to drain properly. We also check the disposal and dishwasher connections.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Spokane, WA

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. They hide well. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then replace the bad length with pipe that matches what is already there. Every repair is done to code.

    Drain Vent Repair in Spokane, WA

    Gurgling and slow draining often mean the vent is blocked. The drain cannot breathe. Without air, draining water pulls the trap seal along with it and lets sewer gas into the room, so we check the roof vent for nests or debris and look for cracked or disconnected vent pipe in the walls. Clearing it is often quick.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Spokane, WA

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. The bottom goes first. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, and we replace the bad length with plastic pipe joined to the old iron with shielded couplings. Full stack replacement gets a separate quote.

    Local Drain Repairs in Spokane, WA

    Our Spokane crew knows the housing stock here. That speeds things up. Older neighborhoods tend to have cast iron stacks and galvanized branches, while newer homes run ABS or PVC, so we show up with the right cutters, couplings and fittings for the pipe we expect to find.

    Local matters on drain work because water quality and pipe age change from town to town. Scale builds faster in some areas. We know which parts of town see the most buildup, and that shortens the time it takes to find a repeat clog's cause.
