    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Spokane

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We test the meter, check pressure loss and inspect for warm spots, damp baseboards and mildew. On slab homes we can follow up with acoustic and thermal equipment to pinpoint leaks. Catching a slab leak early limits damage to floors and foundations.

    Water Heater Inspection & Safety in Spokane

    A vital part of our plumbing inspection includes a thorough check of your water heater, whether it's a traditional tank or tankless unit. We inspect for leaks, sediment buildup, anode rod condition, pressure relief valve operation, and proper ventilation for gas units.
