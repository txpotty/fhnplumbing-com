    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Kirkland, WA

    Direct repair is often the most practical fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. Once the pipe is exposed, we replace the bad section, test the line and backfill in compacted lifts before patching. The concrete is finished level with the floor.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Kirkland

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. The new pipe runs through the attic. For older Kirkland homes, this works well with aging copper or galvanized pipe, since it bypasses every foot of buried line on that run. We cut small openings at fixture walls.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Kirkland, WA

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out.
