    PEX Pipe Replacement in Kirkland

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a popular choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and protecting hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during sudden cold snaps. FHN Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials.
