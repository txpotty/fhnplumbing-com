    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Kirkland

    Regular annual maintenance is key for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage. We also test the system's operation by filling the pit with water to confirm it activates correctly and pumps water away efficiently, protecting your Kirkland home from unexpected flooding.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Kirkland

    River flooding and soaked ground can push water back through sewer lines, so backwater valves and sump pumps are worth considering. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Kirkland, WA

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil near the slab or foundation.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Kirkland

    Sump Pump Cleaning and Debris Removal in Kirkland
