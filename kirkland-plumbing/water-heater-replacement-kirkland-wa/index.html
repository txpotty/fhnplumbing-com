    What Goes Into a Water Heater Swap in Kirkland, WA

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Kirkland, WA

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Kirkland, WA homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Every joint gets checked.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Kirkland

    We use Bradford White often on our jobs because the tanks hold up and the parts are easy to find. When we replace one, we match the fuel, size and venting, check whether the old warranty might still cover the tank, and register the new unit so the warranty is on file from day one. We file the warranty.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Kirkland

    Electric water heaters in Kirkland usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Hot water is back the same day.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Kirkland, WA

    A leaking tank can empty itself onto the floor faster than most people expect. If water is coming from the heater, shut off the cold supply valve on top, turn the gas valve to off or flip the breaker, and open a hot tap to relieve pressure, then call and we will try to have a plumber and a new tank at your home that day. Do not wait it out.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Kirkland, WA

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith on most tank jobs. Bradford White is trade-only and durable, Rheem offers range and parts, and AO Smith brings plenty of sizes, so the pick comes down to fit, warranty and stock. All three hold up.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Kirkland, WA

    Ruud and Rheem come out of the same company and share many parts. Our technicians install both, and since the parts overlap, a Ruud thermostat or element is easy to source when a repair makes more sense than a full replacement on a newer tank. Repair stays an option.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Kirkland

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. Since tankless can pull four times the gas of a tank, the meter line usually gets bigger, and the old vent is swapped for stainless or PVC. You see both numbers.

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Kirkland

    Standard tank heaters are still in most homes, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. A leak at the base, loud popping or rusty hot water means it is time, and a like-for-like or resized swap usually takes two to four hours. Hot water by evening.
