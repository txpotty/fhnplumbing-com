    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Kirkland

    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace failed parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are correct.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Kirkland, WA

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and confirm proper drainage. PVC venting is also checked for slope and sagging.
