    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Kirkland

    Simple trap or slip-joint repairs typically cost $150 to $350. Replacing a section of drain pipe usually runs $400 to $1,200, and cast iron stack replacement can range from $2,000 to $6,000 depending on access. We provide a written price before we start.

    Drain cleaning removes a blockage from an otherwise sound pipe. Drain repair fixes the pipe itself, such as a crack, bad slope, broken fitting or missing vent. Many recurring clogs need repair, not more cleaning.

    Tightening a loose slip nut or replacing a plastic trap is a reasonable DIY job. If the leak is from a cracked tailpiece, a corroded metal drain or inside a wall, a plumber can make a lasting repair and check for hidden damage.

    Minor fixture drain repairs usually do not require a permit. Replacing drain stacks, relocating drains or adding new lines often does, depending on the city. We handle permits when they apply.

    While chemical drain cleaners are widely available, we generally advise against using them. They often contain harsh chemicals that can damage your pipes, especially older ones, and are typically only a temporary fix. For effective and safe clog removal, professional methods are always recommended.

    Drain lines can break or collapse due to several factors, including shifting soil, tree root intrusion, corrosion from age, extreme temperature changes or heavy loads passing over underground pipes. Poor installation or defective materials can also lead to early failure.

    Harsh chemical cleaners can soften older ABS glue joints and corrode metal drain parts with repeated use. They also rarely clear grease or scale fully. A mechanical auger or hydro-jet is safer and more effective.

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to clear out stubborn blockages, grease buildup, mineral deposits and even tree roots from your pipes. It is often used for severe clogs or as a preventive measure to thoroughly clean drain lines, leaving them almost like new.
