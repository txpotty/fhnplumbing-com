    Basement Wall Sealing in Kirkland, WA

    Basement wall sealing is a critical part of walls waterproofing. Washington has two very different climates, with long, wet and mild seasons in the west and hot, dry summers and cold winters in the east. We apply high-quality sealants to porous concrete and masonry walls to prevent moisture from wicking through and causing dampness, mold, or musty odors. This service can be performed alone or as part of a larger waterproofing system, offering a drier, healthier environment in your Kirkland, WA basement.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Kirkland, WA

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to verify the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test proves the pan is watertight before tile.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Kirkland

    Even small cracks in your foundation walls can allow water to seep into your basement or crawl space. Our walls waterproofing service includes foundation crack repair using specialized epoxy or polyurethane injection techniques. These materials fill and seal cracks, preventing future water entry and often strengthening the foundation. Addressing cracks promptly in Kirkland homes is crucial to avoid larger, more costly structural issues.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Kirkland

    Restaurants, gyms, salons and offices often have restrooms, locker rooms and kitchens with constant water exposure.     Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Kirkland, WA

    Waterproofing a shower or wall costs much less than repeated drywall, cabinet and flooring repairs. Ignoring moisture also invites mold, which can be expensive to remediate. A typical shower wall waterproofing project falls in the low thousands, while exterior block sealing varies with wall size. We give you a written quote so you can compare options clearly.

    Drainage System Integration in Kirkland, WA

    Lasting walls waterproofing often requires a solid drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Kirkland, WA properties, so water is efficiently managed and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Kirkland

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra reinforcement before new tile is set.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Kirkland

    Even minor cracks in foundation walls can allow serious water intrusion. Our services include crack repair using epoxy or polyurethane injection to seal these entry points for good. This method fills the entire depth of the crack, restoring strength and preventing water penetration. We address both vertical and horizontal cracks, knowing that horizontal cracks can signal structural issues that need a thorough assessment before sealing. Addressing these early prevents further water damage and expensive future repairs.
