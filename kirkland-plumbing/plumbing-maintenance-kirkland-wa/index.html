    Sump Pump Testing in Kirkland, WA

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. We pour water into the pit to make sure the float lifts and the pump kicks on, check the discharge line for clogs or freezing risk, and inspect the check valve so water does not drain back in.     Gas leaks are the one item we never skip. Visible gas piping, connectors and shutoffs get checked, and old flex connectors get flagged. We note pipe types. Polybutylene or old galvanized supply lines go on the report with repipe options.

    Gas Connector and Shutoff Checks in Kirkland

    Gas feeds water heaters, furnaces, ranges and dryers. Visible piping and connections get leak tested, shutoffs get turned, and old flex connectors get replaced. Gas work is done to code. If you ever smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility from outside.

    Drain Cleaning as Maintenance in Kirkland

    Slow drains rarely fix themselves. Camera checks and cleaning on problem lines keep grease and roots from causing a holiday backup. We jet greasy lines. Root-prone lines go on a schedule.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Kirkland

    Tankless units need descaling. A small pump circulates descaler. We also clean the inlet screen and check venting and gas supply.
