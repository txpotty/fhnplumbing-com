    Leaking Toilet Repair in Kirkland

    Toilet leaks can cause serious water damage to your bathroom floor, subfloor, and even the ceiling below. Leaks can originate from the base of the toilet, the tank, or the supply line. Common culprits include a worn-out wax ring, loose bolts, or cracked porcelain. We thoroughly inspect all potential leak points, replacing seals, tightening connections, or recommending bowl replacement if the porcelain is damaged, preventing further water intrusion.

    Clogged Toilet Solutions in Kirkland, WA

    Persistent toilet clogs are a common nuisance in Kirkland, WA homes, often caused by non-flushable items or excessive toilet paper. While a plunger can clear minor blockages, persistent clogs indicate a deeper issue in the trap, drain line, or even the main sewer line. Our plumbers utilize professional-grade augers and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the toughest clogs without damaging your toilet or pipes. We also inspect for potential causes like root intrusion or pipe corrosion, which are sometimes seen in older homes.

    Toilet Flush Mechanism Repair in Kirkland, WA

    If your toilet isn't flushing properly, it could be due to issues with the flush chain, handle, flapper, or fill valve. Our plumbers are skilled at diagnosing these internal tank problems, replacing worn or broken components like flush valves, fill valves, and toilet handles to restore full and effective flushing power.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Kirkland, WA

    Water around the base typically means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange.
