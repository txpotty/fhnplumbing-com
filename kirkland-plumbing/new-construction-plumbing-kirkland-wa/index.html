    Rough-In Plumbing Services in Kirkland

    Top-out is the second half of rough-in, after framing. Supply lines, drains and vents go through the framing and up to the roof, with stub-outs set to the cabinet drawings. Then the system gets tested. Drains get a water test, filled to the highest fitting, and supply lines hold pressure on a gauge while the inspector walks it.

    Water Heater Installation for New Builds in Kirkland

    Picking the water heater during design saves a gas line reroute later. A 50-gallon tank covers most three-bedroom homes. Tankless units suit bigger houses but need a larger gas line and their own vent route, both planned at rough-in. Expansion tanks and drain pans go in where code requires.

    Gas Piping for New Homes in Kirkland

    We size gas piping for ranges, water heaters, furnaces and fireplaces, plus pool heaters and outdoor kitchens. A shutoff valve goes in within six feet of each appliance. Sediment traps go at the furnace and water heater inlets, and the whole system holds a test on a gauge for the inspector before the meter is set. The test runs at least ten minutes.

    Fixture Installation and Finishing in Kirkland, WA

    Final trim is the visible part, so we take our time with it. Caulk lines matter here. Toilets go down level and tight to the floor, faucets get set before the backsplash where we can, and every supply line gets checked for leaks after an hour of use. Final inspection follows once fixtures are in.

    Rough-In Plumbing Services in Kirkland

    Rough-in covers every pipe that ends up inside a wall or floor. We drill joists and studs within the limits the framing code allows, which usually means holes no bigger than 40 percent of a stud's width in a bearing wall. Vents go up through the roof. Each fixture gets a trap arm sized and sloped to its vent distance.
