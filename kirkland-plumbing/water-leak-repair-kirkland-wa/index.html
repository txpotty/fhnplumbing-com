    In-Wall Leak Repair in Kirkland, WA

    Leaks inside walls show up as bubbling paint, soft drywall, baseboard stains or a musty smell. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to find the wet area and the leaking pipe. Repairs usually involve replacing a pinholed copper section or a failed PEX fitting. We cut a clean access opening that is simple to patch.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Kirkland

    Copper pipes in some homes develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity or poor original installation. Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. One pinhole frequently signals others nearby. We replace the damaged section with new copper or PEX and inspect the surrounding piping. If pinholes keep showing up, we discuss a partial or full repiping.
