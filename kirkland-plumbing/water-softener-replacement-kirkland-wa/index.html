    Metered Softener Replacement in Kirkland

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Kirkland

    The cost of water softener replacement in Kirkland, WA can vary widely, usually ranging from $1,500 to $4,000. This depends on the type and size of the new system, labor costs and any necessary plumbing changes. We provide clear, upfront pricing after assessing your needs.

    We install systems with Fleck, Clack and Kinetico valves, among others. These brands have good parts availability and reliable performance.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had heavy mineral buildup restricting flow. However, a water softener's main job is to remove hardness minerals, not to boost pressure. If low water pressure is your main concern, we can look into other causes.

    Salt-free conditioners reduce scale buildup but do not remove hardness minerals. A true softener removes them, which is better for soap and fixtures.

    Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is typically soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. Hard water contains dissolved minerals like calcium and magnesium. These minerals can lead to scale buildup in pipes, reduced efficiency of water heaters and appliances, spots on dishes, and dry skin and hair. A working water softener is essential for combating these issues in Kirkland homes.

    New water softeners typically come with two types of warranties.
