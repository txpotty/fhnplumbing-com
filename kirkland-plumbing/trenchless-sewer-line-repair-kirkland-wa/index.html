    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Kirkland, WA

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Washington homeowners.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Kirkland, WA

    Tree and shrub roots go after moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause repeat backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every couple of months.

    Sectional Point Repair in Kirkland

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are a good fit on otherwise healthy lines.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Cost in Kirkland, WA

    Point repairs often cost $1,500 to $3,500, while full lining of a residential lateral commonly runs $4,000 to $12,000 depending on length and diameter. Pipe bursting typically ranges from $5,000 to $15,000. Traditional excavation can be cheaper upfront on short, shallow runs, but restoring concrete and landscaping can quickly close the gap. We price both options so you can decide.

    Pipe Bursting in Kirkland, WA

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is ideal for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It typically requires an entry pit and an exit pit.
