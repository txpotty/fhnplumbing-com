    Commercial Urinal Installation in Kirkland

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use.     Drainage and Venting for Urinals in Kirkland, WA

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Urine leaves a hard mineral buildup inside the trap and the drain line, especially on waterless units and low-flow fixtures that send less water through, so we slope the line properly and add a cleanout where we can. Venting stops gurgle and odor. We tie in within code distance.

    Urinal Brands We Install in Kirkland, WA

    Brand choice is mostly about parts support. We recommend brands whose valves and cartridges are easy to get locally, so repairs never wait. Each of those brands is routine for us. Spec sheets come first.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Kirkland, WA

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. The volume matches the fixture rating. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    ADA Urinal Installation in Kirkland

    Accessible urinals start at the carrier. We set the carrier so the finished rim lands at 17 inches or lower, keep the clear floor space free of partitions and door swings, and mount the flush handle or sensor where a seated user can reach it. Measurements get checked twice. Inspectors measure too.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Kirkland

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Cleaning staff need a quick lesson. We show them how.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Kirkland, WA
