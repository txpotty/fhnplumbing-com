    PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Kirkland

    PEX comes in coils up to several hundred feet. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, because one coil covers the whole run without a joint. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Kirkland

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. Scale and corrosion are not a problem with it. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, connect it with brass or poly fittings that are approved underground, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Kirkland

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? We will usually just fix that spot. We tell you which camp you are in and show you the pipe we cut out.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Kirkland, WA

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Plumbing code caps house pressure at 80 psi. A quarter turn and the water is off.
