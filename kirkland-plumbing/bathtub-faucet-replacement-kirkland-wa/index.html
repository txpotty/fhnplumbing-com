    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Kirkland

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. A single-handle valve often takes new trim and a cartridge from the front, while a three-handle valve usually has to be cut out of the wall and replaced with a modern pressure-balance body. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Kirkland, WA

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. On a single-handle setup the diverter is usually the lift gate in the spout itself, and on a three-handle faucet it is the middle stem, and both wear out until water splits between the spout and the head. A new diverter spout takes minutes. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Kirkland

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. Replacement parts stop it. Cracked bodies get a new valve.
