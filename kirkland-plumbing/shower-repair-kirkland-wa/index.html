    Shower Valve Replacement in Kirkland

    If you are experiencing inconsistent water temperature, low water pressure, or difficulty turning your shower on or off, the shower valve may be failing. A faulty mixing valve can lead to scalding or freezing water, which is a safety hazard. We can replace old or broken shower valves with new, reliable models, including pressure-balancing and thermostatic options for improved safety and comfort. This often involves reaching the valve behind the shower wall.

    Showerhead and Arm Repair in Kirkland

    Dripping showerheads, leaking arm connections and broken handheld hoses are frequent problems. We clean or replace showerheads, reseal shower arms and install new handheld hoses and holders. We can also add a slide bar for a handheld shower. Hard water clogs nozzles, so we recommend heads with flexible rubber nozzles that are easy to clean.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Kirkland, WA

    Weak shower pressure is often caused by mineral buildup in the showerhead or flow restrictor. It can also come from a clogged cartridge or a failing pressure regulator. We clean or replace the showerhead, check the cartridge and test whole-home pressure when needed. Most low-pressure issues are fixed fast.

    Leaky Shower Faucet Repair in Kirkland
