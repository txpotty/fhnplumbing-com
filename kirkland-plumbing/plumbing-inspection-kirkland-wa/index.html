    Water Heater Inspections in Kirkland

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. We check tankless units for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Kirkland, WA

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. We run a high-resolution camera from a cleanout to the city connection or septic tank. Every offset, belly, root intrusion and break shows up with its depth and location, and the recording is yours to keep.

    Residential Plumbing Inspections in Kirkland

    A full home inspection covers the system from the water meter to the sewer connection. We check every visible pipe for corrosion and leaks, run each fixture for flow and drainage and test the water heater, the main shutoff and the angle stops. Pressure gets measured too. We note anything above 80 psi.

    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Inspections in Kirkland

    A house can pass a general inspection and still have a sewer full of roots. Our report tells buyers what pipe the house has, what the sewer looks like and how old the heater is, while there is still time to act. Findings help with negotiations. Galvanized supply lines, a cracked clay sewer or a water heater past fifteen years are all things a seller can credit for or fix before closing.

    Leak Detection and Assessment in Kirkland, WA

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. We shut everything off, check the meter's leak dial and then follow the leak with acoustic equipment and thermal imaging. Catching it early keeps it small. Steep slopes and wet soils can shift over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Kirkland, WA

    Constant moisture west of the mountains encourages mold, rot and corrosion around leaks, so small drips should not be ignored. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. We look at hose bibbs, exposed pipe in garages and crawlspaces, irrigation backflow devices and the water heater's relief valve and drain. We look at pipe insulation. Swapping a cracked outdoor faucet or insulating a pipe costs little next to a burst line in the wall.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Kirkland

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan. We check gas units for draft. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Kirkland, WA

    Older homes in Kirkland often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. Our camera shows each defect, and the onboard counter tells us exactly how far from the cleanout it sits. Then we locate it. Knowing the depth and location means a repair estimate can be accurate, and it helps decide between a spot repair, a liner or a full replacement.
