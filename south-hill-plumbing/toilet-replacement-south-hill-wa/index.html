    Toilet Flange Repair During Replacement in South Hill

    Pulling the old toilet is the only chance to see the flange, and a cracked plastic ring or a rusted cast iron flange will ruin a new install within a year. We check it every time. A split ring gets a stainless repair ring, while a flange that sits below the finished floor gets a spacer or extender so the seal compresses properly.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in South Hill, WA

    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more.     Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in South Hill, WA

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Measure to the closet bolts, not the baseboard. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. An offset flange can correct an inch or two without moving the drain.
