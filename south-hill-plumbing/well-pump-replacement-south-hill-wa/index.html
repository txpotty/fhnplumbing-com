    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in South Hill

    Well pump replacement usually costs $1,800 to $4,500 depending on well depth and pump size. Deep wells or constant pressure systems can cost $4,000 to $8,000.

    We install Grundfos, Goulds and Franklin Electric pumps. These brands are reliable and widely supported.

    Common signs include no water from your faucets, weak or inconsistent water pressure, unusual noises from the well or pressure tank area, short cycling where the pump turns on and off frequently, or cloudy or muddy water. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional for an inspection.

    Most new well pumps come with a manufacturer's warranty, usually ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. FHN Plumbing also stands behind our installation work with our own labor warranty for complete peace of mind.

    If your pressure tank is old or waterlogged, replacing it at the same time can protect the new pump. A failed tank causes rapid short cycling, which is one of the most frequent reasons submersible motors burn out early.

    Regular maintenance, such as checking your well's water level, keeping the pressure tank air charge correct and fixing any leaks promptly, can extend your pump's life. Avoiding excessive water use and installing a good quality sediment filter can also reduce wear and tear on the pump.

    FHN Plumbing installs and services a wide range of reputable well pump brands known for their durability and performance. These commonly include Grundfos, Franklin Electric, Goulds Pumps and Sta-Rite. We can recommend the best brand and model to suit your well's needs.

    It is often a good idea to consider replacing your pressure tank at the same time as your well pump, especially if both are of similar age. A new pressure tank can improve the efficiency and lifespan of your new pump by reducing cycling. It also saves on labor costs to do both jobs at once.
