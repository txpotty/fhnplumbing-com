    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in South Hill, WA

    The gaskets and seals within your toilet tank and at its base are essential for preventing leaks. Over time, these rubber components can degrade, crack, or become brittle due to age and exposure to water, especially where the water is hard. We focus on replacing worn-out flapper seals, tank-to-bowl gaskets, and the wax ring at the toilet's base, which is essential for preventing leaks onto your bathroom floor and maintaining a watertight seal.

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in South Hill, WA

    If your toilet needs two flushes or clogs often, the problem could be scale in the rim jets, a partial blockage in the trapway or a flush valve that does not open fully. We auger the trap, check the vent if gurgling is present and clean the jets with a descaling treatment. Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you honestly if replacement is the better fix.

    Fill Valve Replacement in South Hill

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank usually points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet noticeably quieter.

    Running Toilet Fixes in South Hill

    A toilet that continually runs or refills itself intermittently is not only annoying but also a major water waste. This issue in South Hill is typically caused by a faulty flapper valve, an improperly adjusted float, a worn-out fill valve, or sediment buildup. Our technicians diagnose the specific component causing the problem and replace it with high-quality, long-lasting parts. We ensure proper alignment and adjustment of all internal mechanisms to restore your toilet's efficient operation and stop unnecessary water consumption.
