    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in South Hill

    Polybutylene's inherent weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is generally soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already weakened. Understanding this failure mechanism is crucial for diagnosing issues and recommending the most effective and permanent replacement strategy for your home.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in South Hill

Our team specializes in full home repiping using PEX (cross-linked polyethylene), a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant material. PEX is an excellent choice for Washington homes, as it resists freezing better than rigid pipes and expands to handle temperature changes. We carefully plan the repipe to minimize disruption, often running new lines through attics or existing wall cavities, delivering a complete and long-lasting upgrade for your entire plumbing system.

    Yard Service Line Replacement in South Hill, WA
