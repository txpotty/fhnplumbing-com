    Pilot Light and Burner Issues in South Hill

    A frequent problem with gas water heaters is a pilot light that won't stay lit or a burner that fails to ignite. This can be caused by a faulty thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube or issues with the gas control valve. Our technicians in South Hill are adept at diagnosing these components and performing the needed repairs, whether it is cleaning, adjusting or replacing parts. We ensure proper gas flow and ignition for consistent hot water.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in South Hill, WA

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame signals a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats more quickly and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes dropping from the flue.

    Pilot Light Repair in South Hill

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Thermostat & Gas Valve Repair in South Hill, WA

    If your gas water heater isn't heating water to the correct temperature, or if it is overheating, the thermostat or gas valve might be to blame. These components control the gas supply and temperature. Our plumbers in South Hill, WA can test the thermostat for accuracy and inspect the gas valve for any blockages or faults. We carry a range of replacement parts for various brands, allowing for fast and efficient repairs.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in South Hill

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it senses vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and shut down the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as needed.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in South Hill

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures often involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are checked before we leave.
