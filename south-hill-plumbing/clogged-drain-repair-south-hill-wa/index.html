    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in South Hill

    Clearing a single sink, tub or shower drain typically costs $125 to $300. Harder clogs that need a larger machine or access through a roof vent run $250 to $450. Hydro jetting a heavily scaled or greasy line usually costs $350 to $800.

    Gurgling sounds, water backing up into a different fixture and frequent clogs in several drains signal a deeper issue. Sewage odors or water around a floor drain are also warning signs. These often mean the main line needs attention.

    While chemical drain cleaners are readily available, we generally advise against them. They often contain harsh chemicals that can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and can be harmful to your health and the environment. Professional plumbers use safer, more effective methods.

    We rely on professional equipment from trusted brands like Spartan Tool, RIDGID, and Gorlitz. These tools are known for their reliability and effectiveness in diagnosing and clearing various types of drain clogs, supporting a high standard of service.

    While chemical drain cleaners are readily available, FHN Plumbing generally advises against them for homes in South Hill. They often provide only temporary relief, can damage pipes over time, and pose environmental and health risks. Professional tools like augers or hydro-jetting are safer and more effective.

    Most common household clogs, like those in sinks or showers, can be cleared within one to two hours. More severe blockages, such as main sewer line clogs requiring camera inspection and hydro-jetting, might take a few hours to a full day, depending on the complexity.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down kitchen drains, use drain screens to catch hair and food particles, and flush only toilet paper. Regular professional drain maintenance, especially in areas with hard water, can also help limit mineral buildup.

    You should call a professional if a plunger or simple home remedies don't work, if you experience multiple clogged drains, sewage smells, or water backing up.
