    Budget Tub Faucet Options in South Hill, WA

    Not every tub faucet needs a full valve. A new cartridge and a matching trim kit from the same brand can make a twenty-year-old single-handle faucet work and look nearly new for a fraction of what a valve replacement costs. We offer that option first. Three-handle faucets are the exception.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in South Hill

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Supplies have to rise through the floor, from a slab cut or a crawlspace, with a bracket anchored below. Deck fillers mount on the tub deck. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in South Hill, WA

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Volume and temperature often get separate handles. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in South Hill

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. Single-handle valves can often be updated from the front, while three-handle valves usually get cut out and replaced. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in South Hill

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. We cut out the old valve, sweat or crimp in a single-handle pressure-balance valve, and cover the old handle holes with a remodel plate so the tile stays intact around it. Most need an access panel or a back-wall opening. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in South Hill

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. New internals end the drip. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in South Hill, WA

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update. Delta Monitor valves take new cartridges from the front, and the MultiChoice body accepts future trim swaps. Popular Delta cartridges are on the truck. Older Delta valves sometimes need full replacement.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in South Hill

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. A worn 1222 causes drips or weak hot water, and replacing it takes about an hour with a puller. Older Moentrol valves take another cartridge. We carry both.
