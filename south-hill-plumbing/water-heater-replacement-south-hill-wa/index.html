    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in South Hill

    Most standard gas or electric tank replacements cost $1,400 to $3,000 including the heater, labor and typical code upgrades. Attic installs, larger tanks or relocations can raise the cost to $3,000 to $4,500.

    While it might seem tempting, water heater replacement involves gas lines, high voltage electricity, water pressure and heavy equipment. It is a complex job best handled by licensed professionals to ensure safety, proper installation and compliance with local plumbing codes, preventing potential leaks or hazards.

    Leaks at the base, rusty water, rumbling noises, inconsistent hot water and an age over 10 years are typical signs. A leaking tank should be replaced right away.

    FHN Plumbing installs and services all major brands, including Bradford White, Rheem, AO Smith, Rinnai, Navien and State Water Heaters. We can help you choose the best brand and model that suits your budget and hot water demands.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line changes.

    Upgrading provides more reliable hot water, improved energy efficiency and lower utility bills, better performance and a reduced risk of leaks or breakdowns. Newer models often have advanced features and longer warranties, improving comfort and safety in your home.

    Manufacturer tank warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but often have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.
