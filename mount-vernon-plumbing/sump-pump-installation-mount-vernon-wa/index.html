    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Mount Vernon

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Rentals need these. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Mount Vernon, WA

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Mount Vernon

    A pump swap in an existing pit usually runs $600 to $1,500. New pits, with saw cutting, excavation and a concrete patch, run about $1,500 to $4,000. Add $500 to $1,500 for a backup.

    A submersible sits in the pit under water, so it runs quieter and takes no floor space. The pedestal type mounts the motor on a column above the water, which makes it easier to reach for service and usually cheaper to buy, though you will hear it. Both work. The pit and the noise you can live with decide it.

    Not every home needs one. Homes with basements, crawl spaces, sunken patios or yards that collect runoff get the most from a pump, since storm water has nowhere else to go.

    For some homeowners, swapping a pump in a working pit is doable. Anything involving a new pit, a new discharge route or new wiring is a job for a professional.

    Discharge should end at least ten feet from the foundation, on ground that slopes away. Plenty of cities ban discharge into the sanitary sewer.

    Manufacturer warranties usually run three to five years, longer on premium models. We also back our workmanship.

    Permits depend on the scope of the job and the local building department. Washington adopts the Uniform Plumbing Code statewide with state amendments, and local building departments handle permits and inspections. FHN Plumbing pulls any permit the job needs in Mount Vernon.
