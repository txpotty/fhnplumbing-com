    Pipe Bursting Services in Mount Vernon, WA

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. The old pipe gets split. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE resists corrosion and leaks in Mount Vernon, WA soil.

    Pipe Bursting in Mount Vernon, WA

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. The head fractures the old pipe as it travels, with new HDPE trailing behind, fused into one length with no joints.

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter.     The old pipe gets cleaned first. Then the liner goes in. We inflate an epoxy-soaked liner against the wall and cure it with heat or UV, leaving a smooth pipe with no joints. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.
