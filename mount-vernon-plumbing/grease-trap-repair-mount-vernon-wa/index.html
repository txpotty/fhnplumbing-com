    Damaged Baffle Repair in Mount Vernon

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. Once one cracks or falls loose, the trap is just a box that grease passes through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. Plastic units get a factory replacement part. Steel traps with a rusted-out baffle may need a fabricated piece, and sometimes a new trap is the cheaper answer.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Mount Vernon, WA

    Lids crack when someone sets a stockpot on them or a dolly rolls across a floor-mounted unit. A cracked lid lets odor out and lets mop water in. We replace the lid and gasket with the manufacturer part, and on in-floor units we check that the lid sits flush so nobody catches a toe or a cart wheel on it. While the lid is off, we look at the baffles too.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Mount Vernon, WA

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. On the inlet side, the tee directs incoming water down under the floating grease. The outlet tee draws water from low in the tank, well under the grease cap, so if it breaks off or someone removes it during pumping, floating grease walks right out to the sewer. New tees go in as Schedule 40 PVC, cut to the drop length the manufacturer or local code specifies.

    Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in Mount Vernon

    A sewer smell near the trap means gas is getting out somewhere it should not. The usual suspects are a torn lid gasket or a floor drain trap that has dried out. We check venting on the fixture side too, because a sink with no vent can siphon its own trap seal and let sewer gas back into the kitchen. Pumping the trap helps for a week, but it will not fix a gasket that no longer seals.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Mount Vernon

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. A crumbling wall lets grease slip into the second compartment and out the outlet. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Either way, it needs a full pump-out first.

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Mount Vernon, WA

    A wet floor around an under-sink trap usually points to a failed lid gasket or a loose inlet coupling. Steel trap bodies also rust through at the bottom seams after enough years of hot, acidic water. We dry the area, fill the trap and watch where the water shows up. Sometimes it is a $15 gasket. An opened seam is another story, and we will say up front if a patch will not hold.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Mount Vernon, WA

    Lines downstream of the trap should stay clean if the trap is working. When we find grease past the outlet, the trap is not doing its job. We jet the downstream run to the property line or the sewer tap, then camera it to confirm the pipe is clear and to document any cracked or sagging sections for a repair quote.
