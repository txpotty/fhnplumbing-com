    Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in Mount Vernon, WA

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. Either the installer left the defaults in, or the family got bigger and the settings stayed put. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used rather than on a fixed timer schedule.

    Hardness Testing at the Tap in Mount Vernon

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. We take the raw sample from an unsoftened hose bib or with the softener on bypass. The softened sample comes from a kitchen faucet.

    Commercial Twin-Tank Maintenance in Mount Vernon

    Salons, car washes, hotels and restaurants put far more gallons through a softener than any house does, and a hard-water day can mean spotted glassware or a scaled ice machine. Twin-tank and progressive-flow systems need closer checks on the meter and the tank changeover. Visits run monthly or quarterly, depending on volume.

    Softener Upkeep for Restaurants and Shops in Mount Vernon

    Ice machines, espresso machines, dish machines and boilers all scale up fast on hard water, and equipment warranties often require treated water. Each visit includes a hardness test, logged for your warranty file. Brine tank cleanouts come around more often on these systems.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Mount Vernon

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle tend to mean the seal and spacer stack is worn out. On a Fleck 5600 the fix is a seal kit and a new piston, often done in under an hour without pulling the valve off the tank. Cheaper than a new head.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repairs in Mount Vernon, WA

    A bypass valve that does not seat fully lets raw water blend into the softened supply, and the hardness test is usually the first clue. We rebuild or replace the bypass and check the drain line for kinks. Code requires an air gap at the drain.

    Service for Most Softener Brands in Mount Vernon, WA

    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Mount Vernon, WA

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We advance it manually through each cycle and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. A worn piston is a common find.
