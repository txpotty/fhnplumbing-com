    Grease Trap Repair in Mount Vernon, WA

    Cracked lids, missing baffles and rusted inlet tees all let grease pass straight through a trap. We inspect each part during cleaning and replace what has failed, in most cases right then.     Dishwasher and Ice Maker Lines in Mount Vernon

    A dishwasher needs a hot supply with its own shutoff, a drain to the disposal or a wye tailpiece, and either an air gap at the sink or a high loop under the counter, depending on local code.
