    French Drain Installation in Mount Vernon

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects groundwater and carries it away. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so silt does not clog it. The pipe needs at least a 1 percent slope to a daylight outlet, a dry well or a sump, and the outlet end needs a grate so animals do not nest in it.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Mount Vernon

    A hydro jetter sends water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full pipe wall. Unlike a cable, the jet cleans the whole pipe wall, not just a hole through the clog. Restaurants need it most. We camera the line first to make sure the pipe is sound enough to jet.

    Sump Pump Services in Mount Vernon, WA

    A sump pump protects a basement or crawlspace from groundwater.     Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in Mount Vernon

    Most basic drain clearing in Mount Vernon, WA runs $150 to $500. A main line costs more than a sink. Hydro jetting runs higher than cabling because of the equipment and time, and sewer line repair can range from about $1,500 for a spot repair to well over $8,000 for a full replacement with digging.

    A single fixture drain usually takes about an hour. Plan on one to two hours for a main line. Camera work adds 30 to 60 minutes, and a buried or missing cleanout adds more.

    Clearing a clog does not need a permit. Pipe repairs often require one. Replacing a section of sewer, adding a cleanout, or connecting a new drain to the sewer generally requires a permit and an inspection, and we handle that paperwork and schedule the inspector as part of the job.

    We install and service Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty pumps. Cast iron models last longest. Battery backups with their own float and an alarm cover power outages and pump failures.

    Roots grow toward the water and nutrients inside sewer pipe. A hairline crack is enough. Once a root tip gets in through a cracked joint or loose seal, it grows into a mass that catches paper and grease, and clay and older concrete pipe with many joints are the most vulnerable.

    Drain cleaning usually means a cable machine with a cutting head that bores through the clog. It works fast and costs less. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the whole pipe wall clean of grease, scale and roots, so it lasts longer but costs more and needs a pipe that is in good enough shape to take the pressure.

    Slow sinks and gurgling drains are the early signs. A sewer smell in the yard or basement comes next. In the yard, standing water a day after rain or washed-out mulch means the water has nowhere to go.

    Call when more than one drain is slow, or when a toilet gurgles while the washer drains. That usually means the main line. Outside, call when water stands in the yard for more than a day after rain, when a downspout overflows the gutter in every storm, or when water shows up in the crawlspace or basement after a heavy rain.
