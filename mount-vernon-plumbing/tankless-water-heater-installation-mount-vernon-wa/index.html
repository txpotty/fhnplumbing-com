    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Mount Vernon, WA

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. Flush it yearly. Service valves make it easy.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Mount Vernon, WA

    Upfront, a tankless install in Mount Vernon, WA costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. The gap shrinks over time.
