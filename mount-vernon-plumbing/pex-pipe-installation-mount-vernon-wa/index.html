    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Mount Vernon, WA

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Mount Vernon

    When your main water line from the street to your home needs replacement, PEX is an smart option. Its flexibility allows for easier trenchless or minimal-dig installations, limiting disruption to your yard in Mount Vernon. PEX main lines handle ground movement and resist corrosion better than rigid pipe. Steep slopes and wet soils can move over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Mount Vernon

    If your home has old, corroded or leaking pipes made of galvanized steel or polybutylene, a whole-home PEX repipe is an effective solution.     PEX is also a leading choice for hydronic heating systems, including radiant floor heating.     PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Mount Vernon

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Mount Vernon can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Mount Vernon, WA

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to weigh cost and performance.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Mount Vernon, WA

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper repipes because the material is less expensive and installs faster. The total depends on the number of bathrooms, the home's size and how accessible the attic and walls are. Drywall patching and texture matching are often included or quoted separately. We give you a detailed estimate so you can compare PEX-A, PEX-B and copper options side by side.
