    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Mount Vernon, WA

    Direct repair is often the most efficient fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. Once the pipe is exposed, we replace the bad section, test the line and backfill in compacted lifts before patching. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Mount Vernon, WA

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. Each time hot water runs, the pipe expands against concrete and gravel, and recirculation pumps keep that cycle going all day, which grinds the pipe wall thin at the rub points. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Mount Vernon, WA

    Copper and PEX fail in different ways under a slab. Copper tends to pinhole. Copper wears through at rub points or from water chemistry, and PEX usually leaks at a poorly crimped fitting or where a rodent got to it. Trucks in Mount Vernon, WA stock fittings for each type.
