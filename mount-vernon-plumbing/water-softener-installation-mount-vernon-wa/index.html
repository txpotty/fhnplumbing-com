    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Mount Vernon

    Water softener installation in Mount Vernon, WA usually runs $1,500 to $4,000. System type, brand, capacity and how much plumbing work the install needs all move the price within that range. New loops cost more. We quote after an on-site test.

    Salt use depends on hardness, water use and how efficient the softener is. On average a household of four goes through 40 to 80 pounds a month, less with a metered unit. Metering cuts salt use.

    Ten to fifteen years is typical for a quality softener. Unit quality, how hard your water is and how well the system is maintained all change that, and resin can be replaced to extend it. Yearly checks help.

    Manufacturer coverage on the unit is often 5 to 10 years on major parts. We cover our labor, usually for one year, and install to spec to keep the unit warranty valid.

    Adding salt every 4 to 8 weeks is most of the work. A yearly checkup with a plumber catches worn seals, salt bridges in the brine tank and clogged injectors before they cause hard water. Keep the salt level up.

    Some areas require a plumbing permit for this kind of work. FHN Plumbing will tell you if you need one in Mount Vernon and can help with the application.

    With a loop in place, some homeowners can do it, but air gaps, bypass valves and programming matter. Hiring a plumber avoids leaks and poor results.

    Sized and installed correctly, a softener should not noticeably drop your pressure. A pressure drop after install usually means the valve is too small or a filter is clogged.
