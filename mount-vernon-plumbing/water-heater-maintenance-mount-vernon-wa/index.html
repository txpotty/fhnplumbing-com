    Anode Rod Checks in Mount Vernon

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. It comes out for inspection. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.

    Tank Flush and Inspection in Mount Vernon

    Sediment forms from minerals in the water. We flush the tank, test the relief valve, check the anode and look over each fitting. Efficiency comes back. Recovery speeds up.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Mount Vernon
