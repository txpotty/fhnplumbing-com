    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Mount Vernon

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. Older homes often need this on at least one section. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Mount Vernon

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease is the usual culprit. We jet the line, camera it, and repair the weak section with options that limit how long you are closed, such as overnight work or temporary bypass pumps that keep the building running. A two-way cleanout usually goes in at the same time.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Mount Vernon, WA

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, based on the number of bad joints and the spacing between them. One or two joints is a small job. Ten or twelve usually justifies lining the run.

    Sewer Repair for Commercial Properties in Mount Vernon, WA

    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. A closed kitchen loses revenue by the hour. For restaurants, strip centers and apartment buildings in Mount Vernon we schedule repairs after hours where we can, set up temporary bypass pumping when the line has to stay in service, and give managers a clear window to share with tenants. Grease trap outlet lines are one of our most common commercial repairs.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Mount Vernon

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We camera first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Mount Vernon

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, so any excavation stays small and targeted. Older homes in Mount Vernon often hide two or three problems in one line, and the video catches all of them.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Mount Vernon, WA

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. Every drain backs up. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and we locate the break and its depth before we start digging. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Mount Vernon

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. Over decades it goes oval and blisters. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, so by the time a camera shows widespread damage, spot repairs are a poor investment. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.
