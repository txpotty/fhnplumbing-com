    Venting and Draft Repair in Mount Vernon, WA

    Atmospheric gas water heaters rely on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and verify draft with a test. Proper venting is essential for your family's safety.

    Burner Assembly and Venting System Repair in Mount Vernon

    A malfunctioning burner assembly can result in insufficient heating or unusual noises. We closely inspect the burner for blockages, corrosion or wear. We also check the venting system for proper draft and obstructions, which is essential for safe operation and preventing carbon monoxide buildup in your Mount Vernon home. Proper venting is a key safety consideration.

    Pilot Light and Ignition System Repair in Mount Vernon

    A common culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system. Our technicians are experienced in diagnosing issues like a dirty pilot tube, thermocouple failure or a bad igniter. We carry common replacement parts on our service vehicles to make for a quick fix and restore your hot water flow promptly.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Mount Vernon

    Strange noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can add to sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, restoring efficiency and quiet operation.

    Thermostat and Gas Valve Repair in Mount Vernon, WA

    If your gas water heater isn't heating water to the correct temperature, or if it is running too hot, the thermostat or gas valve might be to blame. These components regulate the gas supply and temperature. Our plumbers in Mount Vernon, WA can test the thermostat for accuracy and inspect the gas valve for any blockages or faults. We carry a range of replacement parts for various brands, allowing for fast and efficient repairs.

    Pilot Light Repair in Mount Vernon

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.
