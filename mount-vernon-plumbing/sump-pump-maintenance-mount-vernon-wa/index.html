    River flooding and soaked ground can push water back through sewer lines, so backwater valves and sump pumps are worth considering.     Emergency Sump Pump Maintenance in Mount Vernon

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Mount Vernon, WA

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get hung up.

    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Mount Vernon

    Regular annual maintenance is crucial for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage. We also test the system's operation by filling the pit with water to ensure it activates correctly and pumps water away efficiently, protecting your Mount Vernon home from unexpected flooding.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Mount Vernon, WA

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil near the slab or foundation.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Mount Vernon, WA

    A power outage during a heavy storm can render your primary sump pump useless. Our maintenance service includes thorough testing of your battery backup sump pump system. We check the battery charge, verify the charger's function and ensure the backup pump activates when the main power is interrupted. This dual protection is vital for homes in Mount Vernon, WA where power interruptions can coincide with severe weather.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Mount Vernon

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to early wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Mount Vernon residence.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Cost in Mount Vernon, WA

A new installed sump pump often runs $400 to $1,200 depending on horsepower and backup options. Most pumps last seven to ten years with care.
