    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Repair in Mount Vernon

    The cost of faucet repair in Mount Vernon can range from $120 to $350, depending on the type of faucet, the specific parts needed, and the difficulty of the repair. A simple washer replacement will be on the lower end, while a cartridge replacement in a high-end faucet might be more. We provide a written estimate before any work begins.

    While some minor leaks, like a simple washer replacement on a compression faucet, can be a DIY project, most modern faucets involve specific tools and intricate parts like cartridges or ceramic discs. Incorrect repairs can lead to further damage, more significant leaks, or voided warranties. It's often best to call a professional for a proper and lasting fix.

    If the faucet body is solid and parts are available, a repair is usually worth it. If it is corroded, cracked or discontinued, a new faucet is often the better choice.

    Many brands, including Moen and Delta, offer limited lifetime warranties on cartridges and finishes for the original owner. We can help you check whether parts might be covered.

    Generally, simple faucet repairs like replacing internal components, handles, or the entire faucet (if it's a like-for-like replacement) do not require a permit in Mount Vernon. However, if the repair involves altering the existing plumbing lines or significantly changing the fixture type, a permit might be necessary. Our plumbers know local codes and will let you know if a permit is required.

    Our plumbers are skilled at repairing all major faucet brands commonly found in Washington homes, including Delta, Moen, Kohler, American Standard, Pfister, and many others. We keep a stock of universal and common brand-specific parts to expedite repairs.

    Faucet repair involves fixing the existing fixture by replacing specific worn or broken internal components like washers, O-rings, or cartridges. Faucet replacement means removing the old faucet entirely and installing a brand new one. We recommend replacement when a faucet is badly damaged, very old, or if you simply want an upgrade.

    Simple cartridge swaps can be a DIY job if you identify the correct part. Stuck cartridges, corroded valve bodies and leaks under the sink are better handled by a plumber.
