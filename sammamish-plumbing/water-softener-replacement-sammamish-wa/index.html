    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Sammamish

    A standard single tank softener replacement usually costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can add to the cost.

    Most softener replacements take two to four hours. New loops or drain lines can take longer.

    The right size depends on your household's daily water usage and the hardness of your water in grains per gallon. Our technicians will perform a water test and calculate the appropriate softening capacity in grains per day for your Sammamish home, so the system keeps up.

    We install systems with Fleck, Clack and Kinetico valves, among others. These brands have good parts availability and proven performance.

    We test your hardness in grains per gallon and estimate daily water usage. That tells us the resin capacity needed.

    New water softeners usually come with two types of warranties. There is a manufacturer's warranty on the equipment itself, which can range from 5 to 10 years or more for tanks and control valves, and an installation warranty from FHN Plumbing. We stand behind our work with a solid warranty for your peace of mind.

    Salt-based softeners remove hardness minerals, calcium and magnesium, through ion exchange, truly softening the water. Salt-free conditioners do not remove minerals but change their crystal structure so they do not stick to surfaces. That prevents scale buildup without actually softening the water.

    Most softeners last 10 to 15 years. Resin and valves wear faster with very hard or chlorinated water.
