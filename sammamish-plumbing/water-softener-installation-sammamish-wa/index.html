    We install softeners with Fleck and Clack control valves and other reliable brands. We recommend systems based on performance and serviceability.

    Softeners remove hardness minerals using salt, while conditioners change how minerals behave to reduce scale. Softeners are more effective for very hard water.

    A properly sized and installed water softener should not noticeably affect your water pressure. If you experience a noticeable drop in pressure after installation, it could indicate an issue with the system or installation, which our plumbers can diagnose and resolve.
