    Expansion Tank Installation in Sammamish, WA

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. The tank soaks up that extra volume. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Sammamish

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. We install commercial tanks over 100,000 BTU and racked tankless systems, with the venting and gas supply those loads require. Downtime costs you money. Installs can happen after close.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Sammamish, WA

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. Closets and attics get a pan. Quiet and simple.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Sammamish

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. Ceilings are at stake. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Sammamish

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. We install a new flex connector with a sediment trap, check vent pitch and draft and test every joint with a gas leak detector. Carbon monoxide is the risk. All work follows local code.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Sammamish, WA

    Up front, a tank heater costs less than tankless. Gas tankless installs often run higher because of the bigger gas line and new venting, and attic jobs, relocations or code upgrades add labor on top of that. Your quote lists permits. We compare operating costs so you can choose with real numbers.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Sammamish, WA

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. They do not need a vent. That makes placement easier.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Sammamish, WA

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Some qualify for tax credits. We will tell you if your space works.
