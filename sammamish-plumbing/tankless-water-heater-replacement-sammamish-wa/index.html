    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Sammamish

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Washington has two very different climates, with long, wet and mild seasons in the west and hot, dry summers and cold winters in the east. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Sammamish, WA

    Converting from a 40 or 50 gallon tank to tankless frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss in a garage or closet. The catch is that tankless units often need a larger gas line, often three quarter inch or bigger, plus new venting. We evaluate your meter size and pipe run before quoting so there are no surprises mid-job. We also cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer used.     Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Sammamish, WA

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can wear out plastic covers and wiring over time. Disconnecting hoses, insulating crawlspace pipes and protecting hose bibs before the first freeze prevents most winter damage. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to guard the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.
