    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Sammamish

    Underground leaks hide well. Many people find out from the bill before they see anything in the yard.     Emergency Water Line Repair in Sammamish, WA

    First thing on a burst line is shutting the water off, at the house valve if it works and at the curb stop if it does not. Free-running water washes soil out from under walks and slabs fast. FHN Plumbing sends a plumber with a curb key, a pump and repair couplings. We can usually stop it within the hour. If the repair will run long, a temporary bypass keeps water flowing to the house during the repair.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Sammamish

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. PEX usually fails at a fitting. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, and for PEX we use the matching crimp or expansion tool so the joint is made the way the manufacturer intends.

    Commercial Water Line Repair in Sammamish, WA

    Commercial service lines are often 1 1/2 to 4 inch pipe feeding a meter bank, a backflow assembly and sometimes a fire line. A break shuts the business down. FHN Plumbing can schedule repairs early in the morning or after closing, and we bring the larger fittings and couplings on the truck. We also arrange the backflow test and inspection that most water providers require after work on the commercial side of the meter.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Sammamish, WA

    A full break can flood the yard and leave the house dry at the same time. Shutting the water off comes first. Same-day repairs are the norm. We photograph the damage for your insurance or HOA.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Sammamish

    A meter that moves with every fixture off is the clearest sign you have. Confirming it takes about ten minutes. The house shutoff gets closed, the dial gets watched, and continued movement tells us the leak is in the buried line. Ground microphones find the spot. A repair coupling or a new length of pipe goes in, and then we pressure test it.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Sammamish

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Newer homes in Sammamish typically use PEX supply lines, and many older houses have been partially repiped over the years. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. You get a price on every option.
