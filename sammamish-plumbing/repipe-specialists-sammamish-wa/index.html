    Repiping Work We Handle in Sammamish, WA

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Sammamish

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. It holds up well, does not mind sunlight in an exposed run and has a long track record behind it. It costs more. We sweat joints with lead-free solder or press them, hang the pipe at proper support intervals and test the whole system before patching.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Sammamish, WA

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. Full replacement is the fix. Insurers often ask about it at renewal.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Sammamish, WA

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Rust-colored water in the morning, poor pressure upstairs and dripping threaded joints all point to failing galvanized. New PEX or copper takes its place. Owners usually notice stronger flow the first day.

    Copper Repiping in Sammamish, WA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. Our copper work uses thicker Type L pipe, joined with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings, rather than the Type M found in many older houses. Expect a slightly longer job. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a day longer than PEX, mostly because every turn needs a fitting.

    Commercial Repiping in Sammamish, WA

    Commercial buildings in Sammamish, WA often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day. Our crew breaks commercial repipes into zones, working one riser or suite at a time while the rest of the building keeps water. Night work is possible. Materials are usually Type L copper or commercial PEX, sized to the fixture count and installed to commercial code.

    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Sammamish, WA

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. For a typical house, copper often runs 20 to 40 percent above PEX, though some buyers and homeowners like the look and feel of metal pipe and are willing to pay for it. Our quotes for either include permit, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Sammamish, WA

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Because PEX flexes, it absorbs some of the shock that causes water hammer, and long runs with few fittings mean fewer places for a leak to start inside a wall. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.

We match the texture. We match knockdown, orange peel or smooth walls, so the patches are ready to prime and paint.
