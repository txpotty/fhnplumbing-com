    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Sammamish

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Crawlspaces and basements are both common in Washington homes, and a wet crawlspace is often the first sign of a drainage or plumbing problem. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Sammamish

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save you money.

    Copper Repiping in Sammamish, WA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Common signs you need repiping include persistent low water pressure throughout your home, discolored or rusty-looking water, frequent pipe leaks, visible corrosion on exposed pipes, and a metallic taste or odor in your water.
