    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Sammamish, WA House

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Service in Sammamish, WA

    Higher BTU inputs on commercial units mean bigger gas lines and larger vent sizes, plus a permit and inspection in most jurisdictions. We handle all of it. Downtime stays short.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Sammamish

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. A second blower failure on an older tank is usually the point where a new heater makes more sense. A dedicated outlet gets confirmed first.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Sammamish, WA

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Sammamish, WA

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. Those burners need more gas. We check the meter and pipe size first, because a starved burner never reaches its rated recovery.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Sammamish

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter. New venting is part of it. Before quoting, we measure pipe runs and check the meter rating, which keeps the price from moving later.

    Atmospheric Vent Upgrades in Sammamish, WA

    Flue pipes on older atmospheric heaters rust at the joints and lose their upward slope, and a loose connector can spill exhaust into the closet. We replace damaged sections and the draft hood if needed. Then we test draft with the burner on.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Sammamish, WA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. They need a 120-volt outlet nearby. We run the vent within the maker's length and elbow limits.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Sammamish, WA

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. The longer warranty usually means a bigger anode. We help you pick.
