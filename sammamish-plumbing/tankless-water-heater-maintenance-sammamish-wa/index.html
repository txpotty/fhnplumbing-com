    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Sammamish

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system completely. Restored flow means steadier temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Sammamish

We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is shielded where it runs outdoors.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Sammamish

    The cost of tankless water heater maintenance in Sammamish usually ranges from $150 to $300, depending on the unit's condition, age and brand. This generally includes flushing, descaling and a complete inspection. More extensive repairs or part replacements would be quoted separately after diagnosis.

    Rinnai shows LC codes, Navien may show E003 or E030 and Noritz often shows 90 or 99 when scale or airflow is a problem. These codes typically clear after proper cleaning.

    Usually, a permit is not required for routine tankless water heater maintenance, such as flushing and inspections. However, if major repairs or component replacements that alter the original installation are needed, a plumbing permit from Sammamish may be necessary to ensure compliance with local building codes.

    Most tankless water heater manufacturers recommend annual maintenance to support good performance and extend the unit's lifespan. In areas with harder water, more frequent checks, perhaps every 6 to 9 months, might be worth it to prevent excessive scale buildup.

    A softener or scale prevention system greatly reduces buildup in the heat exchanger. Some brands even recommend treatment when hardness is high. Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines.

    A standard tankless water heater maintenance service, including flushing and inspection, usually takes 1 to 2 hours. The exact time can vary depending on the unit's condition, the amount of scale buildup and any unexpected issues encountered during the service.

    Once a year is standard, and homes with very hard water and no softener may need it every six to nine months. Heavy users and commercial units need it more often.

    The descaling process involves shutting off the unit, isolating the water lines and connecting a circulating pump to flush a mild acidic solution through the heat exchanger. This solution dissolves mineral deposits like calcium and magnesium, which are common in hard water, restoring heat transfer efficiency.
