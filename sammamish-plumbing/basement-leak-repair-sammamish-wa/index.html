    Supply Line Leak Repair in Sammamish

    Water lines that run along basement ceilings and walls can leak at fittings, valves or pinholes. Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. Water chemistry affects how fast copper corrodes, and older galvanized lines frequently rust from the inside. We locate the leak, replace the damaged section with copper or PEX and pressure test the repair. If the line shows widespread wear, we'll tell you whether a partial repipe makes more sense.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Sammamish

    Foundation cracks are a common entry point for basement water leaks, especially in homes built on soil that swells and shrinks. Steep slopes and wet soils can shift over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. We use epoxy and polyurethane injection techniques to seal these cracks, preventing water infiltration and reinforcing your foundation. Our methods are tailored to the specific type and size of the crack, ensuring a durable repair.

    Water Heater and Appliance Leak Repair in Sammamish, WA

    Water heaters, softeners and laundry machines are often installed in basements, and their leaks pool quickly. A dripping T&P valve, a corroded tank bottom or a split washer hose are frequent culprits. We repair or replace the failed part and install drain pans and leak sensors to catch future problems early. If a tank has rusted through, we can replace it the same day in most situations.

    Hydrostatic Pressure Management in Sammamish, WA

    Heavy rain can lead to significant hydrostatic pressure against basement walls, forcing water through concrete. FHN Plumbing installs interior and exterior drainage systems, including French drains and weeping tile systems, to relieve this pressure. We also implement sump pump systems to efficiently remove collected water, keeping your basement dry.
