    Downspout and Roof Drain Lines in Longview, WA

    Many homes connect downspouts to buried pipe that carries water away from the foundation. These lines clog with roof granules, leaves and nests, then back up and dump water at the wall. Keeping water away from the slab helps protect against soil movement and cracking.
