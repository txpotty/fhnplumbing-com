    New Sump Pit Installation in Longview

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a dependable place to collect and remove water.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Longview, WA

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and repair. They are often a good choice for shallow sump pits or areas where space is tight. Our team is experienced in installing these pumps, making sure of proper pit depth and discharge line setup for optimal performance in Longview, WA.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Longview

    A high water alarm lets you know when water rises above normal levels. Smart monitors can send alerts to your phone when the pump runs or fails. We install alarms and Wi-Fi monitors on new and existing pumps. These devices are especially useful for vacation homes and rental properties. Early warnings can prevent major water damage.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Longview

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly inside the sump pit, running quietly and taking up less space. They are usually more powerful and durable, making them a popular choice for many homeowners. We handle the complete installation of submersible units, including proper sealing and electrical connections, tailored to the particular needs of your Longview property.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Longview, WA

    Sump pumps often sit idle for long stretches between storms, which can lead to stuck floats and seized motors. We replace old pumps with new models and inspect the pit, check valve and discharge line. Upgrading to a higher capacity pump may help if your old one could not keep up. We test the new pump by filling the pit with water. Replacement before the rainy season is a smart move.
