    Old Toilet Upgrades in Longview, WA

    Bowls from before 1994 were built for 3.5 gallon flushes or more, and pairing one with a new low-flow tank does not work because the trapway and rim are sized for the larger volume.     Changing Toilet Bowl Styles in Longview

    Elongated bowls are about two inches longer than round ones and more comfortable for most adults, but they eat into the clear space code requires in front of the toilet. Small bathrooms may need a compact elongated model. We measure the space before recommending one, so the door still closes.

    Bowl Replacement Versus Full Toilet Replacement in Longview, WA

    Bowl-only swaps make sense when the tank is newer and the bowl is still made, since a matched bowl often costs $100 to $350 while a new basic toilet starts around $200.     Tank-to-Bowl Leak Repair and Replacement in Longview
