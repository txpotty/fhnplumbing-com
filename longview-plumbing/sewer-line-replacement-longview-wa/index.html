    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Longview

    Cast iron drains in older homes corrode from the inside, especially along the bottom of the pipe. Over time this leaves cracks, holes and a rough surface that snags waste. Replacing the cast iron sewer outside the home is often the first step. We can also evaluate under-slab cast iron and discuss lining or rerouting options. New PVC or HDPE will not rust or scale the same way.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Longview

    Many older homes in Longview may have sewer lines made from outdated materials like clay or cast iron, which are prone to cracking, corrosion, and root invasion. We offer upgrades to newer, more durable materials such as PVC or HDPE (high-density polyethylene). These materials stand up to corrosion, roots, and shifting soil, giving your sewer system a much longer lifespan.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Line Replacement in Longview

    For significant damage or certain pipe layouts, traditional trenching may be the most effective method. This involves digging a trench to access and replace the damaged sewer line directly. Our team plans the excavation to minimize disruption to your landscape, using the right equipment for efficient, precise digging. We make sure of proper grading and backfilling to prevent future issues and restore your property.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Longview, WA

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement swaps out pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is a great fit when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in Longview and can often be completed faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.

    Partial Sewer Line Repair in Longview, WA

    Not every damaged sewer needs full replacement. If the camera shows a single break or one root-damaged joint, a spot repair may fix the problem. We locate the exact spot and depth, excavate a small area and replace that section. This costs far less than replacing the whole lateral. We only recommend it when the rest of the line is in sound condition.

    Permits and Code Compliance in Longview, WA

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Longview, WA

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the proper grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we account for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we cover it.
