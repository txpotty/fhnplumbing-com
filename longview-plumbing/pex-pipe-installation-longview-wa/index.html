    Uponor PEX System Installation in Longview

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool and a cold-expansion ring. The tech expands the pipe and ring together, pushes the fitting in, and the plastic shrinks back around the barb over the next few seconds, tighter than when it started. No crimp tool to calibrate.     PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Longview, WA

    With PEX-A, the tech slips a ring over the pipe end and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting is pushed in while the pipe is open, and the plastic's memory shrinks it back around the barb, sealing tighter during the first minute. Cold weather slows it.     PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Longview

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Longview, WA
