    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Longview, WA

    If the camera shows a sound pipe with a grease or root clog, cleaning is usually all you need. Cracked, offset or collapsed pipe will keep clogging no matter how often it is cleared.     Main Line Hydro Jetting in Longview

    Hydro jetting uses water at thousands of psi through a specialized nozzle to cut roots, grease and scale. It cleans the full diameter of the pipe rather than just boring a hole through the clog. We check the pipe condition with a camera first so jetting is only used on lines that can handle it. Many homeowners find that jetting buys them years before another clog.

    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Longview

    Roots from thirsty trees and shrubs seek out moisture and push into sewer pipe joints. Clay and older cast iron pipe are especially prone to root intrusion because of their many joints. We cut roots with a sewer machine fitted with root saws and then camera the line to see how much damage remains. For ongoing root problems, we can recommend a root treatment schedule or lining.
