    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Longview, WA

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. We replace the pump and prime it properly. Then we watch the gauge through a full cycle.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Longview, WA

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. Three-wire pumps have a control box with a start capacitor and relay, and those parts fail far more often than the motor itself. We test them first. Swapping a capacitor costs a fraction of pulling a pump.

    Pulling a Submersible Pump in Longview

    Pulling a submersible means lifting the pump and every length of drop pipe out through the pitless adapter, sometimes hundreds of feet of it. On the way up, we look at the torque arrestor and the splices. Figure on one day.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Longview, WA

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. Air sneaks in through a pinhole in the suction line, the pump loses prime and the seal burns up running dry. We pressure test the line. If the well is too deep for a jet pump, we will say so and quote a submersible.

    Constant Pressure Well Pump Installation in Longview, WA

    Constant pressure systems hold the house at one pressure, say 50 psi, whether one faucet is open or the shower and the irrigation are running together. The VFD changes the motor speed to match how much water you are using. No more pressure dip mid-shower.

    Constant Pressure Pump Systems in Longview

    Franklin SubDrive and Grundfos SQE are the two constant pressure systems we install most. They get by with a much smaller tank because the drive matches pump speed to demand. Soft starts also cut wear on the motor. The upfront cost is higher.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Replacement in Longview

    Franklin Electric builds a large share of the submersible motors sold in the US, including ones sitting under other brands' pump ends. Replacement control boxes are easy to source. We match the motor and the wire gauge to the depth of your well.

    Sandy Wells and Submersible Pumps in Longview

    Sand kills submersibles early. A well that pumps sand will wear impellers down in a few years, so we check for grit in the old pump and talk about setting the new one higher off the bottom or adding a sand separator. Pump setting depth matters.
