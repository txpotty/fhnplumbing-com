    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Longview

    The cost of water heater replacement in Longview, WA typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of unit (gas, electric, tankless or hybrid), its capacity and any necessary plumbing modifications. Our team will provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your needs and existing setup.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but usually have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times. Our technicians will calculate the right GPM (gallons per minute) for tankless or gallon capacity for tank models so you never run out of hot water, especially in multi-bathroom Longview homes.

    Tank water heaters often last about 8 to 12 years, and hard water and sediment buildup can shorten that. Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is generally soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. Annual flushing and anode rod checks can help lengthen that life.

    Most new water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components, depending on the model and brand. FHN Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, so your investment is covered.

    Tankless offers endless hot water and a longer lifespan but costs more upfront. We can compare both options based on your household and budget.

    Leaks at the base, rusty water, rumbling noises, inconsistent hot water and an age over 10 years are typical signs. A leaking tank should be replaced right away.

    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take longer.
