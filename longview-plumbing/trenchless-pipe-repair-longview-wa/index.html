    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Longview

    CIPP lining is a proven trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is well suited for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Longview.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Longview, WA

    Main water lines can also be replaced with trenchless methods. We can pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through the old path using bursting or a directional boring tool. This avoids digging across lawns, driveways and walkways. The new line is pressure tested before service is turned back on.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Longview

    We use industry-leading trenchless technologies and brands like Perma-Liner for reliable pipe repair in Longview. Perma-Liner systems are well known for their durability and effectiveness in creating long-lasting, structural pipe repairs. Their CIPP products are designed to hold up to harsh conditions, offering a robust solution for your underground plumbing.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Longview

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Longview, WA

    For severely damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, breaking apart the old pipe at the same time. This provides a completely new, durable pipe, often HDPE (high-density polyethylene), which resists corrosion and leaks. It's an effective long-term fix for extensive issues in Longview, WA.

    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Longview

    We use trusted brands like Perma-Liner and Picote for our trenchless pipe repair projects. Perma-Liner offers robust CIPP lining systems known for their durability and versatility, capable of addressing many pipe sizes and conditions. Picote tools provide precision pipe cleaning and cutting, important for preparing pipes for lining or bursting. These tools and materials make our repairs not only minimally invasive but also of high quality and longevity for homes in Longview.

    Pipe Bursting in Longview, WA

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting needs an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.
