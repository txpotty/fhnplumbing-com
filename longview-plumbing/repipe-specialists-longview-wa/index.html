    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Longview, WA

    Many older homes in Longview, WA still have galvanized steel pipes, which are vulnerable to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup. This leads to low water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. Our team specializes in the complete removal and replacement of these outdated systems with modern PEX or copper piping. This upgrade not only resolves existing problems but also greatly improves water quality and flow, boosting your home's value and plumbing reliability for decades to come.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Longview

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Steep slopes and wet soils can shift over time, which stresses buried sewer and water lines and sometimes separates joints. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Longview, WA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. FHN Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Longview, WA, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also noticeably dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.
