    Undermount Sink Installation in Longview, WA

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, with silicone run around the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, usually overnight, before loading it with water. Rushing this causes the classic sag. A sink that drops even an eighth of an inch opens a gap where water and crumbs collect along the counter edge.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Longview, WA

    A fireclay apron sink sits on a frame inside the cabinet, not on the countertop. We build that frame from 2x4s or a steel bracket, set the sink dead level and leave the right reveal for the stone shop. Drain placement is planned early. A lot of them drain toward the back, so the trap layout has to match.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Longview

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for tile, laminate and butcher block counters. Usually the fastest upgrade. After checking the cutout, we seal the rim and draw the clips down evenly, and in older homes and rentals it is often a two-hour job.

    Same-Day Sink Replacement in Longview, WA

    A sink with a crack through the bowl or a strainer that has rusted out needs replacing, not patching. We stock the most common double-bowl stainless sizes, so a same-day swap is often possible, and we replace the strainers and trap at the same time. We swap the old stops as well.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Longview, WA

    A new sink is the cheapest time to add a disposal. The disposal mounts to the new flange, the trap is set to its outlet height and the dishwasher plug gets knocked out for the hose. You also need power. We check for a switched outlet in the cabinet.

    Composite Sink Installation in Longview

    Granite and quartz composite sinks resist scratches on the surface but can chip at the drain hole if a strainer is overtightened. We tighten by hand first.
