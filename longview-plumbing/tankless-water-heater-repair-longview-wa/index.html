    Ignition Failure Repair in Longview, WA

    If your tankless unit will not fire, the cause may be a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. We test each component and measure gas pressure at the inlet. Undersized gas lines are a frequent hidden cause when a tankless unit replaced a tank. We correct the issue so the unit lights every time.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Longview

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, restricting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves and run a descaling solution through the exchanger. Annual descaling is wise for most homes on hard water.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Longview

    When your tankless unit fails to produce hot water, it is often due to issues with the ignition, gas supply or a clogged heat exchanger. Our technicians in Longview will thoroughly diagnose the root cause, whether it is a faulty igniter, a pressure sensor fault or mineral buildup. We carry common replacement parts for quick fixes, restoring your continuous hot water supply efficiently. We also check for proper venting, which is essential for the safe operation of your unit.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Longview, WA

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and verify proper drainage. PVC venting is also checked for slope and sagging.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Longview

    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace faulty parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are accurate.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Repair in Longview

    Navien units are known for their advanced condensing technology but can sometimes experience issues with their circulation pump or exhaust systems. FHN Plumbing provides dedicated Navien tankless water heater repair in Longview, addressing common problems like ignition failures or condensate drain blockages.
