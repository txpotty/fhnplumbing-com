    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Longview, WA

    Replacing an old tankless unit tends to go faster than a conversion.     Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Longview, WA

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Longview, WA homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. A whole-home electric unit draws heavy amperage. Some need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, and a 100-amp panel often cannot carry that on top of the air conditioner and range. Panel capacity gets checked up front.

    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Longview

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job since the tank's location may not suit the new unit. A garage or outside wall near the gas meter can shorten the gas run and simplify venting, but it may lengthen the hot water run to the bathrooms. Both distances matter. A far bath may need recirc.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Longview

    Conversions in Longview tend to run a full workday. We drain and remove the tank, upsize and test the gas, run a new vent through a wall or roof, then mount, plumb and fire the unit with service valves. You shower that night. The inspector comes after.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Longview

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, because the unit has to sense flow and fire first. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. It saves water too.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Longview, WA

    Businesses in Longview, WA with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank. One unit can fail and the rest still carry the load. We size the rack by peak demand. Commercial installs usually need a larger gas service and a mechanical permit.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Longview, WA

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install both condensing and non-condensing Rinnai models, and the right one depends on where the vent can go and how much you want to spend up front. Non-condensing units vent in stainless. Condensing models use PVC vent and a condensate drain.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Longview, WA

    Upfront, a tankless install in Longview, WA costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. The gap shrinks over time. Lower standby loss, a lifespan of up to 20 years against about 10 for a tank, and parts that can be replaced instead of the whole unit all count toward the total cost of ownership. We price both side by side.
