    PEX Pipe Installation in Longview

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a common choice for modern pipe replacement projects in Longview due to its flexibility, durability, and resistance to corrosion and freezing. It's also less expensive to install than copper. Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. FHN Plumbing carefully installs PEX piping for full home repipes or targeted section replacements, providing a reliable and long-lasting plumbing solution that handles local water conditions well.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Longview

    Slab leaks, common in homes built on concrete foundations, can be damaging and difficult to detect. These leaks often stem from corroded or damaged pipes embedded within or under your home's slab. FHN Plumbing uses advanced leak detection technology to locate the exact source, then expertly repairs or reroutes the compromised pipe, often using durable PEX to bypass the slab entirely, preventing future issues in the same spot.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Longview, WA

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep coming back. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you compare the cost of both paths.
