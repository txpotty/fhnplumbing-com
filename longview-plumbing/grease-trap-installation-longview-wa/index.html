    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Longview

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, usually under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. FHN Plumbing expertly installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Longview for the best efficiency.

    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Longview, WA

    Correct sizing and smart placement are critical for a grease trap's effectiveness. Installing a unit that is too small will lead to frequent clogs and overflow, while an oversized one can be an avoidable expense. Our plumbers meticulously calculate the required capacity based on your kitchen's water flow, fixture count, and operating hours, ensuring optimal performance for your Longview, WA business.

    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Longview

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with limited space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.

    Grease Trap Installation Cost in Longview, WA
