    Tankless water heaters need specialized maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is especially important where the water is hard.     Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Shoreline, WA

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance.
