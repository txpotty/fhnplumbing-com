    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Shoreline

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. That pays back over time. Several models are available.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Shoreline, WA

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Water Softener Replacement in Shoreline, WA

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Metered valves save salt. You will notice soft water the same day.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Shoreline

    Water softener installation in Shoreline, WA usually runs $1,500 to $4,000. System type, brand, capacity and how much plumbing work the install needs all move the price within that range. New loops cost more. You get a written quote after we test.

    Most homeowners are better off hiring a pro. A wrong connection can leak for weeks or leave the water just as hard as before. Professional work gets sizing, connections and code right.

    Manufacturer coverage on the unit is often 5 to 10 years on major parts. Our installation workmanship is usually covered for a year, and we install to the manufacturer specs so their warranty stays valid.
