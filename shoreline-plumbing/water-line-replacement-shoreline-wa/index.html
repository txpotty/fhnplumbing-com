    Type L vs. Type K Copper in Shoreline, WA

    The thickest wall belongs to Type K, the usual choice underground. Type L is lighter and mostly goes indoors. Some codes allow L for burial, but we stick with K for service lines because a thicker wall buys more years against corrosive soil and stray rocks. Same fittings, different pipe.

    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Shoreline

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? A repair coupling is often the right call. We tell you which camp you are in and show you the pipe we cut out.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Shoreline, WA

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Pulling suits softer pipe like poly or copper.     PEX Water Line Installation in Shoreline, WA
