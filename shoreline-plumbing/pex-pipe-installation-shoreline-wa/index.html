    What Goes Into a PEX Install in Shoreline, WA

    PEX Main Water Line Installation in Shoreline

    The main from the meter to the house feeds every fixture you own. A leaking old service line shows up as a soggy patch in the yard, a higher water bill and weak pressure everywhere. New PEX goes in its own trench. At the foundation we install a new full-port ball valve as the main shutoff.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Shoreline, WA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. All three are code-listed for drinking water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Shoreline

    Radiant floor loops are PEX too, but a different kind you drink from. Heating loops need oxygen-barrier PEX, which has a coating that keeps oxygen from migrating through the wall and rusting the boiler, the pump and any iron in the system. Loop length matters as well. Too long and the far end runs cold.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Shoreline

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. After 40 or 50 years the bore is so choked with scale that a half-inch line delivers a trickle, and the water comes out tinted brown after it sits overnight. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. Pressure comes back at the fixtures and the rust stops.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Shoreline, WA

    Think of a manifold as a breaker panel for water. Each fixture gets its own line from a central header, and each line gets its own valve, so you can shut off one toilet without killing water to the kitchen. It uses more pipe. What you get back is very few hidden fittings and steadier pressure when two people shower at the same time.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Shoreline, WA

    Price on a repipe comes down to a few things: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX usually beats copper on price because the pipe costs less and a crew finishes in two to four days, not a full week. Drywall patching may be separate. We price PEX-A, PEX-B and copper side by side when you ask.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Shoreline, WA

    With PEX-A, the tech slips a ring over the pipe end and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting is pushed in while the pipe is open, and the plastic's memory shrinks it back around the barb, sealing tighter during the first minute. Cold weather slows it.
