    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Shoreline

    Most jetting jobs in Shoreline, WA run $300 to $800. The pipe and access drive it. Short, accessible lines cost less, and long mains with heavy roots or scale cost more, and you get a quote after the camera inspection.

    Typically 1 to 3 hours. Inspection time is included. The first camera pass, the jetting itself and the final inspection all fit in that window for most homes, while commercial jobs or a badly blocked main may need more time.

    Small rental jetters exist. They lack real power. Professional jetters are far more powerful and take training to run safely, and misuse can damage pipe, injure the user or simply fail to clear the blockage.

    Buildup responds best. Grease, scale and sludge. Soap scum, hair mats and fine roots that have worked into the sewer also come out under high pressure, since the jet scours the entire pipe surface instead of just one path.
