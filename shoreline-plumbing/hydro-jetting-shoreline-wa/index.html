    High-Pressure Water Jetting for Drain Cleaning in Shoreline

    High-pressure water jetting, or hydro jetting, is a non-invasive method that uses strongly pressurized water to clear and clean pipes. Our equipment in Shoreline can generate water pressures up to 4,000 PSI, powerful enough to break apart stubborn blockages and scour pipe walls clean without chemicals. This method is effective for various pipe materials, from PVC to cast iron, and is an eco-friendly choice for deep drain cleaning.

    Hydro Jetting for Hard Water Scale in Shoreline

    Soft water can be mildly corrosive to copper over time, which sometimes shows up as pinhole leaks in older supply lines. Even moderate mineral content can lead to scale buildup in pipes over many years. This scale can restrict flow and trap other debris, leading to clogs. Hydro jetting is very effective at breaking down and flushing out these hard mineral deposits, restoring the full diameter of your pipes. This is a better method compared to chemical treatments for scale removal.

    Residential Sewer Line Jetting in Shoreline

    Main sewer lines in homes are usually 3 or 4 inches and can collect years of grease, soap, mineral scale and roots. We feed jetting hose through a cleanout and work it toward the street, cleaning in passes. Rear-facing jets pull the nozzle forward while forward jets break up heavy blockages. The water carries debris down to the city main. A camera run afterward shows the pipe walls clean and clear.

    Hydro Jetting vs. Snaking Cost Comparison in Shoreline, WA

    While snaking might be cheaper upfront for simple clogs, hydro jetting offers a more thorough and long-lasting solution, often saving money in the long run for Shoreline, WA residents. Snaking typically punches a hole through a blockage, leaving residue behind, which can lead to recurring clogs. Hydro jetting cleans the entire pipe, preventing future issues and reducing the frequency of service calls, making it a more economical choice for persistent drain problems.

    Hydro Jetting Before Pipe Lining in Shoreline, WA

    Cured-in-place pipe lining needs a clean surface to adhere and seal correctly. We jet the host pipe to remove roots, scale and debris, then camera it to confirm it is ready. Any heavy obstructions are cut out before the liner is installed. Skipping this step can lead to wrinkles or poor bonding. Proper prep is part of why lined pipe can last many years.

    Scale Removal in Cast Iron Pipes in Shoreline

    Cast iron drains in older homes build up rust tubercles and mineral scale, and hard water speeds that process. Jetting with the right nozzle can strip much of this buildup and bring back flow. We keep pressure in a safe range for aged iron and watch the camera for thin spots. This cleaning is also the standard prep step before pipe lining. Clean pipe walls help a liner bond correctly.
