    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Shoreline

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Shoreline yard.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Shoreline, WA

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It can go up a size. With an entry and exit pit dug, the winch pulls the head and new HDPE through, replacing the run in a day or two in most homes. FHN Plumbing joins the new pipe by heat fusion.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Shoreline, WA

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. The old pipe gets split. A winch pulls a bursting head through the line, cracking the old pipe outward into the soil while new HDPE follows right behind it, so the replacement pipe ends up in the same path. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Shoreline, WA soil.
