    Smart Toilet Installation in Shoreline, WA

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most need a GFCI outlet close enough for the cord. We run the water side and mount the unit, and if a new outlet is needed we bring in a licensed electrician rather than splicing anything ourselves. Then we test every button.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Shoreline

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. The flange should sit on top of the finished floor. An early set means the tile goes around the flange, leaving it low, and that calls for an extender ring later. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Shoreline

    A cracked tank or a bowl leaking at the base cannot wait for next week, especially in a house with one bathroom. We keep common two-piece toilets on the truck.     Toilet Replacement and Upgrades in Shoreline, WA

    Swapping an old 3.5 gallon toilet for a modern one is the easiest water savings in the house. Pulling the old one takes around twenty minutes. Most of the remaining time goes into cleaning the flange and setting the new bowl on shims so it does not rock on a floor that has settled over the years. We haul the old one away.

    Commercial Restroom Toilets in Shoreline, WA

    Restaurants and offices get heavy-duty fixtures and accessible stalls built to code. A closed restroom costs a business money. Work gets scheduled before opening or after close, with fixtures staged on site, so each stall is out of service only briefly. We work with your GC on inspections.

    Toilet Brands We Install in Shoreline

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Each one has its own parts quirks. Some one-piece TOTO models mount with a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler toilets use a tank-to-bowl gasket that most hardware stores do not carry, so we stock both. We keep those parts on hand.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Shoreline, WA

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. On cast iron, the bolt slots rust out first. Plastic flanges crack from overtightened bolts, and a stainless repair ring screwed over the old one usually gives a solid anchor again. Seal goes on last.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Shoreline, WA

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Check for the WaterSense label. A MaP rating of 800 grams or better is what we look for, since a weak low-flow toilet that needs double flushing saves no water at all. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.
