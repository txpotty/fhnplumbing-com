    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Shoreline

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system thoroughly. Restored flow means steadier temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Commercial Tankless System Checks in Shoreline, WA

    Commercial tankless water heaters face higher demand and require more frequent, rigorous maintenance. Our service for businesses in Shoreline includes detailed inspections of multiple units, checking for proper gas pressure, water flow rates and exhaust venting.     Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Shoreline

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is protected where it runs outdoors.
