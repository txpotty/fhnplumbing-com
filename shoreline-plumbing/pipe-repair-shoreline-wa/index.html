    Pinhole Leak Repair in Shoreline

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water quality is often behind it. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, which we fix by replacing that length with new pipe. One usually means more are coming.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Shoreline

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair in Shoreline, WA

    Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Shoreline

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Ice splits the pipe.     PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Shoreline, WA

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Both have weak points. Brittle CPVC needs a gentle hand.
