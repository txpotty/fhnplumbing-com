    Precise Slab Leak Detection Services in Shoreline, WA

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Shoreline, WA

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are harder to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know where to listen.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Shoreline

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs nonstop and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before marking the spot.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Shoreline, WA

    Sewer line issues under a slab can also look like slab leak symptoms. We use high-resolution video cameras to inspect the interior of your drain lines, identifying cracks, blockages or separations. This is important if the issue isn't a pressurized water line, helping us determine if a sewer line repair is needed instead for your Shoreline home.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Shoreline

    A slab leak that is flooding floors or causing water to bubble up through tile needs quick attention. We respond quickly, help you shut off the water and isolate the leaking side if possible. That often lets you keep partial water service until the repair. Then we locate and fix the leak.

    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Shoreline

    Our team uses electronic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation. This non-invasive method helps us avoid unnecessary concrete breaking, saving you time and money on repairs. It's particularly effective for copper and PEX lines commonly found in Shoreline homes.
