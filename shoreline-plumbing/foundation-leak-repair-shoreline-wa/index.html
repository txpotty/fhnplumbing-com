    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Shoreline, WA

    Many older homes in Shoreline, WA have copper water lines beneath their foundations, which can corrode over time due to soil conditions or water chemistry. Municipal water west of the mountains in Washington is generally soft, while supplies on the drier east side tend to run harder. When these copper pipes develop pinhole leaks or cracks, they need specialized repair. Our technicians fix or replace sections of damaged copper piping, providing a durable seal that withstands pressure and ground movement and helps prevent future leaks.

    Water Line Reroute in Shoreline

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This avoids breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for simple patching.

    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Shoreline, WA

    In some cases, the best way to repair a foundation leak is by tunneling beneath the slab. This method allows our plumbers to reach the damaged pipe directly without breaking through your home's interior flooring. FHN Plumbing is experienced in safe and efficient tunneling, limiting disruption to your Shoreline, WA home and ensuring a complete repair of the damaged pipe section.

    Foundation Leak Detection in Shoreline

    Accurate detection is the key step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to confirm a leak exists. Then we use acoustic listening equipment and electronic line tracing to find the exact spot beneath the foundation. Thermal imaging helps confirm hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.
