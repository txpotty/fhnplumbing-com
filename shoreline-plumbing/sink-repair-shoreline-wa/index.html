    Hair Clog Clearing in Shoreline

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. First the stopper comes out and gets cleaned, then a hand auger goes through the trap and wall arm, with no chemicals. We flush the overflow channel while we are there. That slot smells when it fills with slime.

    Emergency Sink Repair in Shoreline, WA

    Shut the angle stops first. Turn both oval handles under the sink clockwise until they stop, and if they spin freely or keep weeping, go to the main shutoff and close it before calling us. We send active leaks to the front of the line. The plumber brings stops and supply lines to stop it on the first visit.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Shoreline, WA

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. Most single-faucet pressure problems are a packed aerator screen, and after that we look at the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Whole-house low pressure is another call. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Shoreline

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. Older compression faucets need a washer and seat, and newer cartridge or disc faucets need the cartridge itself or its seals. Cartridges for Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler are on the truck. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Shoreline

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Next, put towels on the floor. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Shoreline, WA

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A supply line that bursts at night can put several gallons a minute on the floor, so we answer after hours and send a plumber with the parts to stop it. While you wait, shut off the valves under the sink. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Shoreline, WA

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. In kitchens it is cold grease and food, and in bathrooms it is hair knotted on the stopper and glued with soap scum. A hand auger does most of the work. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Shoreline, WA

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Most codes want 2 to 4 inches of trap seal, and a leaking or cut-down trap can lose it and let sewer odor into the house. We replace bad traps with a matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.
