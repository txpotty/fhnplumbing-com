    Tankless Units We Install in Shoreline, WA

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Shoreline, WA

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. Hard freezes are occasional on the west side of Washington, but short cold snaps catch many homes with uninsulated pipes off guard. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Shoreline, WA

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Plenty of panels do not have room. Panel size and wire gauge get checked first.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Shoreline, WA

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. The catch is gas, since most tankless units need a 3/4-inch line or bigger plus new venting. Before quoting, we check the meter and pipe run, and afterward we cap the old flue and seal the roof. Plan on a full day.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Shoreline

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Otherwise one unit does all the work. We also set a descaling schedule.

    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Shoreline, WA

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Over 20 years it can come out close, since a tankless can outlast two tanks and skips standby loss, but the payback depends on how much hot water your house uses. You see both quotes.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Shoreline, WA

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. Expect a bigger gas line, a new vent run and a wall-mounted spot for the heater. We cap or remove the old flue.

    Navien Venting and Install in Shoreline

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. Our vent runs stay within spec. Go past them and the warranty suffers.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Shoreline

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. Most need at least a 3/4-inch line. Pipe length and meter capacity get checked against the sizing tables, and we confirm pressure at full fire. An undersized line causes ignition faults.
